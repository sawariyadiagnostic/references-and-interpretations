## 2. Pre-Analytical Specimen Collection, `2–7 Day` Abstinence & Transit Rejection Matrix (`WHO 2021`)

### 2.1 Mandatory Patient Preparation & On-Site Collection Protocol
1. **Strict Sexual Abstinence Window (`2 to 7 Days` — Optimally `3 to 5 Days`):**
   - **Why $< 2\text{ Days}$ Fails:** Depletes epididymal sperm reserves and seminal vesicle fluid, **falsely lowering Semen Volume, Sperm Concentration, and Total Sperm Count**.
   - **Why $> 7\text{ Days}$ Fails:** Accumulation of senescent, apoptotic spermatozoa in the cauda epididymis **falsely lowers Progressive Motility (`PR`), Vitality, and Normal Morphology**, and increases Sperm DNA Fragmentation (`DFI`)!
2. **On-Site Collection in a Private Laboratory Room:**
   - Collect by **masturbation** directly into a **pre-weighed, sterile, wide-mouthed, non-spermicidal polypropylene container** pre-warmed to $20\text{–}37^\circ\text{C}$.
   - **Forbidden Lubricants & Condoms:** **NEVER use ordinary latex condoms** (contain spermicidal talc/lubricants/nonoxynol-9 that kill $100\%$ of sperm within minutes!), **saliva**, soap, or petroleum jelly.
3. **Completeness of Ejaculate (The Critical First Prostatic Fraction):**
   - The **first $30\%$ of the ejaculate** consists of prostatic fluid containing **$> 70\%$ of the most actively motile spermatozoa**, whereas the final $70\%$ is alkaline gelatinous seminal vesicle fluid (`fructose + semenogelin`).
   - **If the patient loses the initial drops (`Incomplete Collection`):** Sperm concentration and progressive motility are severely underestimated, and $\text{pH}$ rises! The lab **must record completeness on the intake form** and request a repeat sample after $2\text{–}7\text{ days}$ if the first fraction was lost.
4. **Home Collection Emergency Exception & Temperature Shock Shield:**
   - If collected off-site due to inability to produce at the lab, the container must be held **against the body ($25^\circ\text{C}\text{–}37^\circ\text{C}$)** and delivered within **strictly $\le 30\text{ to }45\text{ minutes}$** (recording exact time of ejaculation). Exposure to winter cold ($< 20^\circ\text{C}$) or summer heat ($> 40^\circ\text{C}$) causes irreversible **cold-shock immobilization** (falsely mimicking Asthenozoospermia/Necrozoospermia).

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP (`WHO 6th Edition 2021` & `ISO 23162:2021` Step-by-Step)

### 3.1 Step 1 — Immediate $37^\circ\text{C}$ Incubation, Liquefaction & Gravimetric Volume
1. Place the pre-weighed container immediately on a gentle orbital shaker inside a **$37^\circ\text{C}$ incubator** for **$15\text{ to }30\text{ minutes}$** until the semenogelin coagulum is completely liquefied by prostatic **PSA (`KLK3` serine protease)**.
   - *If Non-Liquefied at $60\text{ Minutes}$:* Record **Delayed Liquefaction**; mechanically pass $6\text{–}10\text{ times}$ through a blunt $18\text{-G}$ needle or digest $1:1$ with **$10\text{ IU/mL}$ Bromelain** in Dulbecco's PBS at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $10\text{ min}$ (apply $2\times$ dilution factor to concentration; note that bromelain alters motility!).
2. **Gravimetric Volume Measurement (`WHO 2021 Mandate` — Never Pour into Pipettes/Cylinders!):**
   - Transferring viscous semen into graduated cylinders leaves $0.3\text{–}0.8\text{ mL}$ adhered to the cup walls! Weigh the sealed container with semen on an analytical balance, subtract the empty pre-weighed container tare weight, and assume seminal density $= 1.0\text{ g/mL}$:
     $$\text{Semen Volume (mL)} = \text{Weight}_{\text{Full Container (g)}} - \text{Weight}_{\text{Empty Container (g)}}$$
3. **Viscosity & `pH` (Read Within $30\text{–}60\text{ Minutes}$):**
   - Aspirate into a wide-bore $1.5\text{ mm}$ plastic pipette and allow to drop by gravity: normal semen falls in discrete drops; **Abnormal Hyperviscosity** forms a thread **$> 2.0\text{ cm}$ long**.
   - Measure **$\text{pH}$** on narrow-range test paper ($\text{pH } 6.0\text{–}10.0$, calibrated against pH buffers): Normal **$\ge 7.2$** ($7.2\text{–}8.0$).

### 3.2 Step 2 — Phase-Contrast Motility Assessment at $37^\circ\text{C}$ ($\ge 400$ Spermatozoa in Duplicate Wet Preparations)
1. Within **$30\text{ to }60\text{ minutes}$** of ejaculation, dispense **$10.0\text{ }\mu\text{L}$** of well-mixed semen onto a pre-warmed ($37^\circ\text{C}$) clean glass slide and cover with a **$22 \times 22\text{ mm}$ coverslip** (creating an exact **$20.0\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ chamber depth** that allows unrestricted rotational swimming). Make **two independent wet preparations**.
2. Wait $60\text{ seconds}$ for drift to stop, then examine at **$200\times$ or $400\times$ Phase-Contrast (`Ph2`)** on a $37^\circ\text{C}$ heated stage. Count **$\ge 200$ spermatozoa per replicate ($\ge 400$ total)** across $\ge 5$ fields using the **WHO 6th Edition (2021) 4-Category Motility Classification** *(note: WHO 6th Ed restored the distinction between Rapid [`a`] and Slow [`b`] Progressive Motility!)*:
   - **Grade `a` — Rapid Progressive Motility:** Moving actively, linearly or in a large circle, at speed **$\ge 25\text{ }\mu\text{m/s}$** ($\ge 5\text{ head-lengths/second}$) at $37^\circ\text{C}$.
   - **Grade `b` — Slow Progressive Motility:** Moving forward linearly/in large circles at **$5 \text{ to } < 25\text{ }\mu\text{m/s}$** ($1\text{ to } < 5\text{ head-lengths/second}$).
   - **Total Progressive Motility (`PR` = Grade `a` + Grade `b`):** Decision Limit **$\ge 30\%$** (with Rapid Grade `a` $\ge 25\%$ clinically desirable).
   - **Grade `c` — Non-Progressive Motility (`NP`):** Flagellar beating/twitching in place or swimming in tight circles ($< 5\text{ }\mu\text{m/s}$) without net forward progression (**Total Motility `PR + NP` $\ge 42\%$**).
   - **Grade `d` — Immotility (`IM`):** Complete absence of tail movement.

### 3.3 Step 3 — Vitality Testing (`Eosin-Nigrosin` — Mandatory Whenever `PR < 40%`)
- **Principle:** Distinguishes **Immotile-Dead Sperm (Necrozoospermia — ruptured plasma membrane allows red Eosin Y entry)** from **Immotile-Live Sperm (Ultrastructural Flagellar Defect / Kartagener Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia — intact membrane excludes Eosin, appearing white against dark purple Nigrosin background)**.
- Mix $50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ semen with $50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ Eosin-Nigrosin ($1\%$ Eosin Y + $10\%$ Nigrosin), wait $30\text{s}$, smear, air-dry, and count $200$ sperm at $1000\times$ oil: **White Head = Live**; **Pink/Red Head = Dead** (Normal **$\ge 54\%$ Live**).

### 3.4 Step 4 — Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer Sperm Concentration & Total Sperm Number
1. Using a **positive-displacement pipette** (air-displacement pipettes under-aspirate viscous semen by $15\%\text{–}30\%$!), dilute **$50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ well-mixed semen** into **$950\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ ($1:20$ dilution)** of **WHO Sodium Bicarbonate-Formalin Immobilizing Fixative** ($50\text{ g }\text{NaHCO}_3 + 10\text{ mL }35\%\text{ Formalin}$ per $\text{L}$).
2. Load **both Chamber A and Chamber B** of a $100\text{ }\mu\text{m}$-deep Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer, incubate $10\text{–}15\text{ minutes}$ in a humidified Petri dish so all sperm settle to the grid floor, and count **$\ge 200$ spermatozoa per chamber ($\ge 400$ total)** (counting only intact sperm with both a head and tail; isolated pinheads/tails are recorded separately).
3. Verify that $|N_1 - N_2|$ falls within the **95% Poisson Binomial Acceptance Limit** ($\le \approx 2\sqrt{N_1 + N_2}$), then calculate:
   $$\text{Sperm Concentration } (10^6/\text{mL}) = \frac{\text{Total Sperm Counted } (N_1 + N_2)}{\text{Volume of Grids Counted (nL)}} \times \text{Dilution Factor } (20) \times 10^{-3}$$
   $$\text{Total Sperm Number per Ejaculate } (10^6/\text{ejaculate}) = \text{Sperm Concentration } (10^6/\text{mL}) \times \text{Semen Volume (mL)}$$
4. **Mandatory $3,000 \times g \times 15\text{ Minute}$ Centrifuged Pellet Protocol Before Reporting `Azoospermia` (`WHO 2021`):**
   - If **zero spermatozoa** are seen in the fresh wet mount, **NEVER report Azoospermia immediately!** Centrifuge the entire liquefied ejaculate at **$3,000 \times g$ for $15\text{ minutes}$**, decant supernatant (save for **Seminal Fructose Test**), and examine the entire resuspended pellet across two $22 \times 22\text{ mm}$ coverslips ($\sim 400$ fields):
     - **Rare Spermatozoa Found in Pellet Only:** Report as **`Cryptozoospermia`** (patient can undergo **ICSI using ejaculated sperm** without needing surgical testicular TESE biopsy!).
     - **Zero Spermatozoa in Entire $3,000\times g$ Pellet:** Report as **`Azoospermia`** and reflex immediately to **Seminal Fructose Test**!

### 3.5 Step 5 — Kruger / Tygerberg Strict Morphology ($1000\times$ Oil Immersion) & Peroxidase Leukocytes
- **Tygerberg Strict Criteria (`Normal >= 4%`):** A spermatozoon is classified as **Normal** ONLY if **ALL four regions** (Head, Acrosome, Midpiece, and Principal Piece/Tail) are simultaneously normal:
  1. **Head:** Smooth, regularly contoured oval; Length **$3.7\text{–}4.7\text{ }\mu\text{m}$**, Width **$2.5\text{–}3.2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$** (Length:Width ratio $1.3\text{–}1.8$); **Acrosomal region** well-defined, comprising **$40\%\text{–}70\%$ of head area** with $\le 2$ small vacuoles ($< 20\%$ head area) and zero post-acrosomal vacuoles.
  2. **Midpiece:** Slender, axially attached, $\sim 1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ wide, length $\sim 1.5\times$ head length; residual cytoplasmic droplet $< \frac{1}{3}$ normal head size.
  3. **Principal Piece (Tail):** Uniform caliber, thinner than midpiece, uncoiled, length $\sim 45\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ ($\sim 10\times$ head length).
- **Round Cells Differentiation (`Leukocytes` vs `Immature Germ Cells`):** On wet mount, both neutrophils and spermatids appear as "round cells". Whenever round cells exceed $\ge 1.0 \times 10^6/\text{mL}$, perform the **Ortho-Toluidine / Benzidine Peroxidase Stain**: **Brown-stained cells = Peroxidase-Positive Polymorphonuclear Leukocytes (`Normal < 1.0 Million/mL`)**; **Unstained cells = Immature Spermatogenic Germ Cells**.

## 6. 🚨 NABL & `CLSI GP47` Critical / High-Risk Alerts & Post-Vasectomy Clearance Criteria

1. **Post-Vasectomy Persistent Motile Spermatozoa Alert (`AUA` / `WHO 2021`):**
   - Post-vasectomy semen analysis (`PVSA`) is performed at **$12\text{ weeks}$ AND $\ge 20\text{ ejaculations}$** post-surgery on a fresh specimen within $1\text{ hour}$ of collection.
   - **Clearance Confirmed:** Complete **Azoospermia** OR **Rare Non-Motile Spermatozoa ($\le 100,000/\text{mL}$ with $0\%$ motility)**.
   - **CRITICAL ALERT:** Presence of **ANY Motile Spermatozoa** (even a single motile sperm!) or $> 100,000/\text{mL}$ non-motile sperm indicates **Vasectomy Failure / Early Vasal Recanalization** $\rightarrow$ Immediate Pathologist verification & direct notification to Surgeon/Urologist (patient must continue contraception!).
2. **Newly Diagnosed `Azoospermia` (`0` Sperm in $3,000\times g$ Pellet):**
   - Mandatory Pathologist double-verification of the $3,000\times g$ centrifuged pellet + immediate **Seminal Fructose** & $\text{pH}$ check before releasing report.
