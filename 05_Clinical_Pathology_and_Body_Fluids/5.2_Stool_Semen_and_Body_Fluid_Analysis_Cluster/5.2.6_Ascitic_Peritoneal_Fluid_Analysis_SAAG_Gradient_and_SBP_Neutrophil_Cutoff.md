## 2. Pre-Analytical Paracentesis Collection, Paired Serum & Bedside Blood Culture Inoculation (`AASLD` / `EASL`)

1. **Mandatory 4-Tube + Simultaneous Paired Serum Protocol:**
   - **Tube 1 — `K2-EDTA` (Lavender Top, $3\text{–}5\text{ mL}$):** Mandatory for **Total `WBC`, `RBC`, and Absolute `PMN` (Neutrophil) Count**. Plain tubes clot exudative ascitic fluid, invalidating the SBP neutrophil count!
   - **Tube 2 — Plain Sterile Tube ($5\text{–}10\text{ mL}$) + Fluoride Tube ($2\text{ mL}$):** For Ascitic **Albumin, Total Protein, LDH, Glucose, Amylase, Triglycerides, Bilirubin, and Enzymatic `ADA`**.
   - **Mandatory Paired Venous Serum Tube (Collected Within $\pm 1\text{–}2\text{ Hours}$ of Paracentesis):** Without a simultaneous **Serum Albumin**, the **Serum-Ascites Albumin Gradient (`SAAG`)** cannot be calculated! *(Avoid calculating SAAG immediately after an IV Human Albumin infusion; wait $24\text{h}$ or note post-infusion state).*
   - **Tube 3 — Heparinized Container ($50\text{–}100\text{ mL}$) for Malignant Cytology & Cell Block.**
   - **Tube 4 — Bedside Inoculation of $10\text{ mL}$ Ascitic Fluid Directly into Aerobic & Anaerobic Automated Blood Culture Bottles (`SOP 6.1.6`):**
     - **Why Bedside Blood Culture Bottle Inoculation is Mandatory (`AASLD`):** Spontaneous Bacterial Peritonitis (`SBP`) has an extremely low bacterial concentration ($\sim 1\text{ organism/mL}$). Sending ascitic fluid in a plain sterile syringe/cup to the lab yields only **$40\%\text{–}50\%$ culture positivity**, whereas bedside inoculation of $10\text{ mL}$ into **BACTEC / BacT/ALERT blood culture bottles increases culture yield to $> 80\%\text{–}90\%$**!

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP: `SAAG` Equations, Traumatic Tap `PMN` Correction & Low-Albumin Precision

### 3.1 Calculation of the Serum-Ascites Albumin Gradient (`SAAG` — Starling Oncotic-Hydrostatic Principle)
*(Note: The obsolete "Exudate vs Transudate" classification based solely on Ascitic Total Protein $\ge 2.5\text{ g/dL}$ had only $56\%$ accuracy in ascites and was **officially replaced worldwide by `SAAG` [$97\%$ accuracy]** per AASLD & EASL guidelines!)*
$$\text{SAAG (g/dL)} = \text{Serum Albumin (g/dL)} - \text{Ascitic Fluid Albumin (g/dL)}$$
- **Analytical Requirement for Low Ascitic Albumin ($< 1.0\text{ g/dL}$):** In decompensated cirrhosis, ascitic albumin is frequently **$0.2\text{ to }0.8\text{ g/dL}$**. Standard serum BCG albumin methods must be verified for linearity down to **$0.1\text{ g/dL}$** on the **Bonavera Chem 400 / 200** (or switched to low-range BCG / immunoturbidimetric urine-CSF albumin mode when Ascitic Albumin $< 0.5\text{ g/dL}$) so `SAAG` is never miscalculated!
- **Hyperglobulinemia Correction (When Serum Globulin $> 5.0\text{ g/dL}$):** High serum globulins exert oncotic pressure that narrows `SAAG`:
  $$\text{Corrected SAAG} = \text{Uncorrected SAAG} \times 0.016 \times \left(\text{Serum Globulin in g/dL} + 2.5\right)$$

### 3.2 Calculation of Absolute Polymorphonuclear Leukocyte Count (`Absolute PMN`) & Traumatic Tap Correction
1. Perform a manual **Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer** Total Nucleated Cell (`WBC`) and Erythrocyte (`RBC`) count on the `K2-EDTA` tube, followed by a **100-Cell Cytospin Romanowsky Differential** to determine `% Polymorphonuclear Neutrophils (% PMN)`:
   $$\text{Absolute PMN Count } (\text{cells}/\mu\text{L}) = \text{Total Ascitic WBC } (\text{cells}/\mu\text{L}) \times \left(\frac{\%\text{ Neutrophils}}{100}\right)$$
2. **Mandatory Correction for Bloody / Traumatic Paracentesis (`Ascitic RBC >= 10,000 cells/uL`):**
   - Peripheral blood entry introduces circulating neutrophils ($\sim 1\text{ PMN per }250\text{ RBCs}$). Whenever Ascitic $\text{RBC} \ge 10,000/\mu\text{L}$, calculate the **Corrected Absolute `PMN` Count**:
     $$\text{Corrected Ascitic PMN } (\text{cells}/\mu\text{L}) = \text{Measured Ascitic PMN} - \left(\frac{\text{Ascitic RBC Count } (\text{cells}/\mu\text{L})}{250}\right)$$
   - *(Note: If the Corrected `PMN` is negative because peripheral neutrophils lysed after entering the peritoneal cavity, report `< 0` [Negative for SBP]).*

| **LOW GRADIENT**<br>**`SAAG` $< 1.1\text{ g/dL}$** ($< 11\text{ g/L}$) | **2B. Low Ascitic Protein ($< 2.5\text{ g/dL}$):** | - **Nephrotic Syndrome** *(Severe hypoalbuminemia in both serum and ascites)*<br>- **Protein-Losing Enteropathy / Severe Kwashiorkor Malnutrition** |

### 5.2 Classification of Peritoneal Infection: `SBP` vs `Runyon's Criteria` for Secondary Hollow-Viscus Perforation
> [!CAUTION]
> **Life-Saving Surgical Distinction (`SBP` vs Secondary Bacterial Peritonitis):** Treating **Spontaneous Bacterial Peritonitis (`SBP`)** with an exploratory laparotomy causes $> 80\%$ mortality in cirrhotics, whereas failing to perform an urgent laparotomy for **Secondary Bacterial Peritonitis (perforated peptic ulcer, appendicitis, or diverticulitis)** causes **$\sim 100\%$ mortality**! Use **Runyon's Triad** on the **Bonavera Chem 400** to distinguish them immediately:

| Peritoneal Infection Variant | Absolute `PMN` Count | Ascitic Culture (`6.1.6`) | **Runyon's Biochemical Triad (`Protein`, `Glucose`, `LDH`)** | Clinical Management |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Classic Spontaneous Bacterial Peritonitis (`SBP`)** | **$\ge 250\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$** | **Monomicrobial** (*E. coli, Klebsiella pneumoniae, Streptococcus pneumoniae*) | Protein $< 1.0\text{ g/dL}$, Glucose $\ge 50\text{ mg/dL}$, LDH $<$ Serum ULN | **Medical:** Immediate IV 3rd-Gen Cephalosporin (Cefotaxime/Ceftriaxone) + IV Albumin ($1.5\text{ g/kg}$ Day 1, $1.0\text{ g/kg}$ Day 3 to prevent Hepatorenal Syndrome). |
| **2. Culture-Negative Neutrocytic Ascites (`CNNA`)** | **$\ge 250\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$** | Negative *(prior antibiotics or low inoculum)* | Same as classic `SBP` | **Treat identically to classic `SBP`!** |
| **3. Monomicrobial Non-Neutrocytic Bacterascites (`MNB`)** | **$< 250\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$** | Positive (Single organism) | Normal | Repeat paracentesis if asymptomatic; treat as `SBP` if symptomatic (fever/abdominal pain/encephalopathy). |
| **4. Secondary Bacterial Peritonitis (Gut Perforation / Abscess — `Runyon's Criteria`)** | **$\ge 250\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$** *(Often $> 5,000\text{–}10,000/\mu\text{L}$)* | **Polymicrobial** (Gram-negative rods + Enterococci + Anaerobes + *Candida*) | **Meets $\ge 2\text{ of }3$ Runyon's Criteria:**<br>1. **$\text{Ascitic Total Protein} > 1.0\text{ g/dL}$**<br>2. **$\text{Ascitic Glucose} < 50\text{ mg/dL}$** ($2.8\text{ mmol/L}$)<br>3. **$\text{Ascitic LDH} > \text{Upper Limit of Normal for Serum LDH}$**<br>*(Plus: $\text{Ascitic CEA} > 5\text{ ng/mL}$ or $\text{Ascitic ALP} > 240\text{ U/L}$)* | **SURGICAL EMERGENCY:** Immediate Anaerobic Coverage (Metronidazole) + Upright Abdominal CT / Water-Soluble Contrast Study + Surgical Evaluation! |

## 6. 🚨 NABL & `CLSI GP47` Critical ("Panic") Values & 30-Minute Read-Back Protocol

1. **Ascitic Fluid Absolute `PMN` Count $\ge 250\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$ (Corrected for RBCs):** **Spontaneous or Secondary Bacterial Peritonitis (`SBP`)** $\rightarrow$ **Immediate ($\le 15\text{–}30\text{ min}$) Telephone Read-Back** to attending Physician/Hepatologist/ICU (every hour of delay in empirical antibiotic initiation increases in-hospital mortality by $\sim 3.3\%$!).
2. **Positive Ascitic Gram Stain (or Polymicrobial Organisms + Runyon's Criteria Positive):** **Suspected Hollow-Viscus Perforation (Secondary Peritonitis)** $\rightarrow$ Immediate **15-minute STAT read-back** to Surgery/Hepatology.
3. **Chylous Ascites ($\text{Triglycerides} > 200\text{ mg/dL}$), Pancreatic Ascites ($\text{Amylase} > 1,000\text{ U/L}$), or Biliary Perforation ($\text{Ascitic Bilirubin} > 6.0\text{ mg/dL}$ and $>$ Serum Bilirubin):** Immediate **30-minute read-back**.

## 7. Pathophysiological Correlations & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Why Cirrhotic Patients with Tuberculous Peritonitis May Show a Falsely Low Ascitic `ADA` ($< 30\text{–}40\text{ U/L}$):** In non-cirrhotic patients, **Ascitic `ADA` $\ge 36\text{–}40\text{ U/L}$** has $> 95\%$ sensitivity and $98\%$ specificity for **Tuberculous Peritonitis**. However, in **patients with co-existing Liver Cirrhosis (`SAAG >= 1.1 g/dL` + low ascitic total protein)**, impaired T-cell responsiveness and transudative dilution lower the `ADA` level—in cirrhotic ascites, a **lower cutoff of Ascitic `ADA` $\ge 27\text{–}30\text{ U/L}$** (combined with lymphocytic predominance and laparoscopic peritoneal biopsy / MGIT culture) should be used!
