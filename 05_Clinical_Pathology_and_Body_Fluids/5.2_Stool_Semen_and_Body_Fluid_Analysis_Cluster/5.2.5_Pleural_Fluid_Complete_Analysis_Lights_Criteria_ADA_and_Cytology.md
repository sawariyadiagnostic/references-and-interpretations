## 2. Pre-Analytical Multi-Tube Thoracentesis Collection, Paired Serum & Stability Matrix

### 2.1 Mandatory 4-Tube + Paired Venous Blood Collection Protocol (`CLSI H56-A` / `C49-A` / `BTS`)
To achieve $98\%$ diagnostic sensitivity across Light's Criteria, cytology, and microbiology, thoracentesis fluid ($20\text{–}50\text{ mL}$) must be distributed immediately at the bedside into **4 dedicated sterile tubes** PLUS a **simultaneous paired venous blood draw**:
1. **Tube 1 — `K2-EDTA` (Lavender Top, $3\text{–}5\text{ mL}$) for Total & Differential Cell Count:**
   - **Critical Rule:** Exudative pleural fluids are rich in fibrinogen and **clot rapidly in-vitro inside plain syringes/tubes**, trapping $50\%\text{–}90\%$ of leukocytes and rendering the `WBC` count falsely low! Always invert the EDTA tube $8\text{–}10\text{ times}$ immediately upon collection.
2. **Tube 2 — Plain / Clot-Free Sterile Tube ($5\text{–}10\text{ mL}$) + Sodium Fluoride Tube ($2\text{ mL}$) for Biochemistry (`Light's Criteria` & `ADA`):**
   - Used for Pleural Total Protein, Albumin, LDH, Enzymatic **ADA**, Amylase, Triglycerides, and Cholesterol; Sodium Fluoride inhibits leukocytic/bacterial glycolysis for accurate **Pleural Glucose**.
   - **Mandatory Paired Serum Tube ( drawn within $\pm 1\text{ hour}$ of Thoracentesis):** Required to measure **Serum Total Protein, Serum Albumin, and Serum LDH** on the **Bonavera Chem 400** for exact calculation of **Light's Criteria** and the **Serum-Pleural Albumin Gradient (`SPAG`)**!
3. **Tube 3 — Heparinized / Citrated Container ($20\text{–}50\text{ mL}$) for Malignant Cytology & Cell Block** *(and Tube 4 Sterile Screw-Cap / Bedside Blood Culture Bottle for Gram/ZN/CBNAAT/Culture — `SOP 6.1.6`)*.
4. **Anaerobic Heparinized ABG Syringe (for Pleural `pH` in Parapneumonic Effusion):**
   - Aspirate $1\text{–}2\text{ mL}$ of non-purulent pleural fluid anaerobically into a pre-heparinized blood gas syringe (expelling all air bubbles) and analyze within **$\le 30\text{ minutes}$** on the ABG analyzer. *(Exposure to room air allows dissolved $\text{CO}_2$ to escape, **falsely raising Pleural $\text{pH}$ by $+0.05\text{ to }+0.15\text{ units}$**; residual lidocaine anesthetic in the thoracentesis syringe **falsely lowers Pleural $\text{pH}$ to $< 7.0$**!).*

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP (`CLSI H56-A` Cytology & `Bonavera 400/200` Chemistry)

### 3.1 Physical & Centrifuged Supernatant Evaluation (Chylothorax vs Pseudochylothorax vs Hemothorax)
- **Milky / Chylous Turbid Fluid:** Centrifuge at $1,000 \times g$ for $10\text{ minutes}$:
  - *Turbidity clears completely into a cell pellet:* **Empyema** (massive neutrophils/pus).
  - *Supernatant remains milky white:* Measure **Pleural Fluid Triglycerides & Cholesterol** on **Bonavera Chem 400**:
    - **Chylothorax (Thoracic Duct Disruption — Lymphoma / Trauma / Filariasis):** **$\text{Pleural Triglycerides} > 110\text{ mg/dL}$** ($1.24\text{ mmol/L}$), Cholesterol $< 200\text{ mg/dL}$, Chylomicrons present on lipoprotein electrophoresis / Sudan III stain.
    - **Pseudochylothorax (Cholesterol Effusion — Chronic Rheumatoid / Chronic TB Pleurisy):** **$\text{Pleural Cholesterol} > 200\text{ mg/dL}$** ($5.18\text{ mmol/L}$), Triglycerides $< 50\text{ mg/dL}$, **rhomboid Cholesterol Crystals with notched corners** under microscopy.
- **Bloody / Hemorrhagic Fluid (Traumatic Tap vs True Hemothorax vs Malignant Effusion):**
  - **Traumatic Tap:** Blood streaks clear progressively from Tube 1 $\rightarrow$ Tube 3; fluid clots rapidly.
  - **True Hemothorax:** **$\text{Pleural Fluid Hematocrit} \ge 50\%$ of Simultaneous Peripheral Blood Hematocrit** (requires immediate tube thoracostomy!).

### 3.2 Manual Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer Count & Cytospin Differential (`CLSI H56-A`)
1. Mix the `K2-EDTA` pleural fluid gently. Load undiluted fluid (or $1:10$ / $1:20$ dilution in isotonic saline if turbid; **NEVER use Turk's Glacial Acetic Acid diluting fluid on mucoprotein-rich serous fluids**, as acetic acid precipitates serous mucin into a **"Rivalta clot"** that traps all WBCs!) into both chambers of an Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer.
2. Count all 9 large squares ($0.9\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ per chamber):
   $$\text{Cells}/\mu\text{L} = \frac{\text{Total Cells in 9 Large Squares}}{9 \times 0.1\text{ }\mu\text{L}} \times \text{Dilution Factor}$$
3. Prepare **Cytospin / Sediment Smears**: Stain 2 slides with **May-Grünwald-Giemsa / Leishman** (air-dried) for **100-Cell Differential Leukocyte Count** (Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Eosinophils, Macrophages/Mesothelial cells) and 2 slides wet-fixed Immediately in **$95\%$ Ethanol** for **Papanicolaou Stain** (malignant nuclear chromatin detail).

### 3.3 Biochemical Analysis on `Bonavera Chem 400 / 200` (Light's Criteria & Enzymatic `ADA`)
- Centrifuge plain/fluoride tube at $1,500 \times g$ for $10\text{ minutes}$ and assay clear supernatant for **Total Protein, Albumin, LDH, Glucose, and Enzymatic Adenosine Deaminase (`ADA`)**:
  $$\text{Adenosine} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{\text{ADA}} \text{Inosine} + \text{NH}_3 \quad \xrightarrow{\text{PNP + Xanthine Oxidase + Peroxidase (550 nm)}} \quad \text{Quinoneimine Dye}$$

## 5. Multi-Stratified Diagnostic Decision Cutoffs (`Light's Criteria`, `SPAG` & `ADA`)

### 5.1 Light's Criteria & The Diuretic "Pseudo-Exudate" Correction (`SPAG` / `NT-proBNP`)
| Classification Criterion | **Transudate** *(All 3 Light's Criteria Negative)* | **Exudate** *(Meets $\ge 1$ of 3 Light's Criteria — Sensitivity $98\%$, Specificity $83\%$)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pleural Protein $\div$ Serum Protein Ratio** | **$\le 0.50$** *(Or Fluid Protein $< 3.0\text{ g/dL}$ if unpaired)* | **$> 0.50$** *(Or Pleural Protein $> 3.0\text{ g/dL}$ if serum unavailable)* |
| **2. Pleural `LDH` $\div$ Serum `LDH` Ratio** | **$\le 0.60$** | **$> 0.60$** |
| **3. Absolute Pleural `LDH`** | **$\le \frac{2}{3}\text{ (67\%)}$ of Serum `LDH` Upper Limit of Normal** | **$> \frac{2}{3}\text{ (67\%)}$ of Serum `LDH` Upper Limit of Normal** *(e.g., $> 160\text{–}200\text{ U/L}$)* |
| **Diuretic Pseudo-Exudate Reclassification (`SPAG` & `SPPG`)** | In **Congestive Heart Failure (`CHF`)** or **Cirrhosis** patients receiving **Loop Diuretics (Furosemide)**, water reabsorption concentrates pleural protein/LDH into the borderline exudate range ($15\%\text{–}25\%$ false-positive exudates!).<br>**Reclassify as True Transudate IF:**<br>- **$\text{Serum Albumin} - \text{Pleural Albumin (`SPAG`)} > 1.2\text{ g/dL}$**, OR<br>- **$\text{Serum Protein} - \text{Pleural Protein (`SPPG`)} > 3.1\text{ g/dL}$**, OR<br>- **Pleural / Serum `NT-proBNP` $\ge 1,500\text{ pg/mL}$ (`Snibe MAGLUMI X3`)**. | True Exudates exhibit **$\text{SPAG} \le 1.2\text{ g/dL}$** and **$\text{SPPG} \le 3.1\text{ g/dL}$**. |

## 7. Pathophysiological Correlations & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Why Direct Pleural Fluid `AFB` Smear (`ZN`) & `CBNAAT` Have Low Sensitivity in Tuberculous Pleurisy:** Tuberculous pleural effusion is primarily a **delayed-type hypersensitivity (`Type IV`) reaction** to subpleural caseous rupture liberating mycobacterial antigens into the pleural space, rather than massive bacillary replication. Consequently, direct **ZN Smear sensitivity is $< 5\%\text{–}10\%$**, **CBNAAT/GeneXpert Ultra sensitivity is $\sim 30\%\text{–}50\%$**, and **Liquid MGIT Culture sensitivity is $\sim 40\%\text{–}60\%$** (whereas **Thoracoscopic Pleural Biopsy achieves $> 90\%$ sensitivity**). Therefore, a **Lymphocytic Exudate ($> 75\%$ lymphocytes, $< 5\%$ mesothelial cells) with Pleural $\text{ADA} > 40\text{ U/L}$** remains the cornerstone biochemical criterion for Tuberculous Pleurisy in India even when pleural ZN/CBNAAT are negative!

PLEURAL FLUID COMPLETE ANALYSIS — LIGHT'S CRITERIA, ADA & CYTOLOGY INTERPRETATION

1. LIGHT'S CRITERIA (TRANSUDATE VS EXUDATE DIFFERENTIATION):
   A pleural effusion is classified as an EXUDATE if >=1 of the following 3

   (a) Pleural Fluid Protein / Serum Protein Ratio > 0.50 (or Fluid Protein >3.0 g/dL)

   - Lymphocyte-Predominant (>50-75%): Tuberculous Pleuritis (supported by

     pH <7.20, Glucose <40 mg/dL, or LDH >1,000 U/L indicates complicated

3. MANDATORY CYTOLOGY & MICROBIOLOGY MEDICO-LEGAL SHIELD:
   A single negative pleural fluid cytology smear has ~60% sensitivity and does
   not exclude pleural malignancy (repeat tap, Cell Block IHC, or thoracoscopic
   biopsy is advised if suspicion persists). Similarly, negative pleural ZN stain
   does not exclude Tuberculous Pleurisy.
   *Not valid for medico-legal purposes (CPA 2019 / ISO 15189:2022).*
