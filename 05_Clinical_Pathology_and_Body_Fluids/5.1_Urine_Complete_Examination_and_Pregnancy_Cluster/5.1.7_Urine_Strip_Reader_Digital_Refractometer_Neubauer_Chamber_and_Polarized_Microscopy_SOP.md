## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Clinical Pathology Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the operation, daily calibration, and preventive maintenance of all analytical equipment in the **Department of Clinical Pathology, Urinalysis, Andrology (`WHO 6th Ed.`) & Body Fluid Cytology**:
   - **Semi-Automated / Automated Urine Strip Reflectance Photometer** ($470, 525, 565, 625, 840\text{ nm}$ LEDs) & Desiccant-Canister Lot Verification.
   - **Clinical Refractometer / Digital Specific Gravity (`SG`) Meter** (`CLRW` Zero-Point $1.000$ & $5.0\%\text{ w/v NaCl}$ $1.022$ Span Verification).
   - **Swinging-Bucket Urine Sediment Centrifuge** ($400 \times g$ for $5\text{ min}$ `CLSI GP16-A3` RCF Nomogram Verification & Standardized $12:1$ Concentration Factor).
   - **Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer** (`0.100 mm` depth, $9\text{ mm}^2$ grid for `CSF / Serous Body Fluids` & `WHO 6th Ed. Semen Concentration`), **$37.0^\circ\text{C}$ Semen Liquefaction Incubator & Stage Warmer**, and **Phase-Contrast / First-Order Red ($\lambda$-Plate) Compensated Polarized Microscopy** (`Synovial MSU vs CPPD Crystals` `SOP 5.2.8`).
2. **NABL 112 Specific Requirement (`Clinical Pathology`):**
   - Mandates **Daily 2-Level Liquid Urine Dipstick + Microscopic Sediment Control (`Level-1 Normal` & `Level-2 Abnormal`)**, daily verification of refractometer calibration against distilled water ($1.000$) and known standard ($1.015\text{–}1.025$), documented reconciliation whenever chemical strip and microscopic sediment disagree (`Erythrocyte Lysis vs Myoglobinuria`; `Leukocyte Esterase vs Pyuria`), and **`WHO 6th Edition (2021)` duplicate $200\text{-sperm}$ Poisson binomial agreement checks** in Semen Analysis.

## Section 2: Urine Dipstick Reflectance Reader & Strip Canister Moisture-Protection Protocol
1. **Daily Optical Calibration Strip (`Grey Calibration Strip`) Verification:**
   - Every morning at $08:00\text{ AM}$ (and weekly/monthly per manufacturer), insert the **Factory Reusable Grey Optical Reference Calibration Strip** into the Urine Strip Reader carriage.
   - Verify reflectance `%R` across all LED wavelengths (`Blue 470 nm`, `Green 525 nm`, `Amber 565 nm`, `Red 625 nm`, `IR 840 nm`) matches the calibration card within **$\pm 1.5\%\text{ R}$**.
2. **Daily 2-Level Commercial Liquid Urinalysis Control (`Bio-Rad Liquichek Urinalysis Control Level 1 & Level 2`):**
   - Test **Level-1 (Normal / All Negative, $\text{pH } 6.0$, $\text{SG } 1.010$)** and **Level-2 (Pathological Positive for Glucose, Protein, Blood, Leukocyte Esterase, Nitrite, Ketones, Bilirubin, Urobilinogen, $\text{pH } 7.5\text{–}8.0$, $\text{SG } 1.025$)** every morning before patient testing, and on every new canister/lot opening.
3. **Strip Canister Desiccant & Oxidation Prevention (`CRITICAL`):**
   - Remove only the exact number of strips required for immediate testing and **recap the desiccant-lined canister within $< 10\text{ seconds}$**.
   - Exposure to ambient humidity/fumes degrades the **Glucose Oxidase**, **Diazo Nitrite/Bilirubin**, and **Tetramethylbenzidine (`TMB`) Peroxidase** pads (causing false-negative Nitrite/Glucose or false-positive Blood!). Write **"Date Opened"** and **"30-Day Open-Canister Expiry"** on every canister.

## Section 3: Refractometer Specific Gravity (`SG`) Calibration & Urine Centrifuge ($400 \times g$) Standardization
1. **Daily 2-Point Refractometer / Digital `SG` Calibration (`CLSI GP16-A3`):**
   - **Zero-Point (`SG = 1.000`):** Place $1\text{–}2\text{ drops}$ of Type-I `CLRW` Distilled Water at $20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$ on the prism; verify reading is **$1.000 \pm 0.001$** (adjust calibration screw if needed).
   - **Span-Point (`SG = 1.022 ± 0.001`):** Place $1\text{–}2\text{ drops}$ of **$5.0\%\text{ w/v}$ Aqueous $\text{NaCl}$ Standard** ($5.00\text{ g}$ dried $\text{NaCl}$ in $100\text{ mL}$ volumetric flask) $\to$ Must read **$1.022 \pm 0.001$** (or **$3.0\%\text{ w/v NaCl} = 1.015 \pm 0.001$**).
   - **Solute Correction Rule for Refractometry (`SOP 5.1.2`):** Unlike the ionic dipstick pad (which measures only ionic strength and ignores glucose/protein), a refractometer measures total refractive index $\to$ **Subtract $0.004$ for every $1.0\text{ g/dL}$ Glucose** and **subtract $0.003$ for every $1.0\text{ g/dL}$ Protein**!
2. **Standardized Urine Sediment Centrifugation ($400 \times g$ for $5\text{ Minutes}$ — Swinging-Bucket Rotor):**
   - Per `CLSI GP16-A3`, spinning urine above $> 500 \times g$ shatters fragile hyaline and cellular casts!
   - Calculate the exact `RPM` for the urine centrifuge's swinging-bucket rotor radius ($r_{\text{cm}}$) to achieve exactly **$\text{RCF} = 400 \times g$**:
     $$\text{RPM} = \sqrt{\frac{400}{1.118 \times 10^{-5} \times r_{\text{cm}}}} \quad \left(\text{e.g., for } r = 14.0\text{ cm} \implies \mathbf{1,600\text{ RPM for } 5\text{ min}}\right)$$
   - Pour exactamente **$12.0\text{ mL}$** well-mixed urine into a graduated conical Kova-type tube, spin at **$400 \times g$ for $5\text{ min}$** (without mechanical brake), decant supernatant leaving exactly **$1.0\text{ mL}$** ($12:1$ concentration), resuspend gently, and load **$20\ \mu\text{L}$** onto a standardized slide under a $22 \times 22\text{ mm}$ coverslip.

## Section 4: Improved Neubauer Chamber, $37^\circ\text{C}$ Andrology Stage Warmer & Compensated Polarized Microscopy SOP
1. **Improved Neubauer Hemocytometer (`0.100 mm` Depth; $1\text{ Large Corner Square} = 1\text{ mm}^2 \times 0.1\text{ mm} = 0.1\ \mu\text{L}$):**
   - **Body Fluid Cell Count (`CSF`, `Pleural`, `Ascitic`, `Synovial`, `Pericardial` — `SOP 5.2.5` to `5.2.9`):**
     - Load both Upper (`Chamber A`) and Lower (`Chamber B`) grids using optically flat thick quartz hemocytometer coverslips (verified by **Newton's iridescent interference rings** on the side rails!).
     - Duplicate counts between Chamber A and Chamber B must agree within the **Poisson $95\%$ Confidence Limit** ($|A - B| \le 2\sqrt{A + B}$).
     - **Mandatory Biohazard Decontamination:** Never reuse a Neubauer chamber without soaking in **$1.0\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite (`10,000 ppm` available chlorine) for $15\text{ minutes}$** (or $2\%$ Glutaraldehyde for $30\text{ min}$), followed by `CLRW` water rinse and $70\%$ `IPA` drying (`CSF` Creutzfeldt-Jakob / Meningococcal / TB precaution!).
2. **`WHO 6th Edition (2021)` Andrology ($37.0^\circ\text{C}$ Incubator, Stage Warmer & Positive-Displacement Pipette):**
   - Verify the **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$ Semen Liquefaction Incubator** and **Microscope Heated Stage ($37.0^\circ\text{C}$)** every morning before performing Progressive Motility (`PR %`) grading (`SOP 5.2.4` — a cold slide at $22^\circ\text{C}$ artificially depresses rapid progressive motility `Class a` by $> 30\%$!).
   - Use a **Positive-Displacement Pipette (`50 uL`)** for viscous semen dilution ($1:20$, $1:5$, or $1:2$ in formal-saline fixative).
3. **First-Order Red ($\lambda = 530\text{ nm}$) Compensated Polarized Microscopy (`SOP 5.2.8` Synovial Fluid):**
   - Cross the Polarizer and Analyzer at $90^\circ$ (dark extinction field) and insert the **First-Order Red Compensator ($\lambda$-Plate)** at $45^\circ$ (rose-magenta background) with the slow vibration axis arrow ($\gamma$) marked:
     - **Monosodium Urate (`MSU` — Gout):** Needle-shaped crystals show **Strong Negative Birefringence** (**Yellow** when parallel `||` to $\gamma$-axis; **Blue** when perpendicular $\perp$).
     - **Calcium Pyrophosphate Dihydrate (`CPPD` — Pseudogout):** Rhomboid crystals show **Weak Positive Birefringence** (**Blue** when parallel `||` to $\gamma$-axis; **Yellow** when perpendicular $\perp$).
