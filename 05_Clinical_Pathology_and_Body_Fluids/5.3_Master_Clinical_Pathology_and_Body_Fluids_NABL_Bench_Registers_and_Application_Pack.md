## Section 3: Mandatory Urine Chemical Dipstick vs. Microscopic Sediment Reconciliation Rules (`NABL 112`)
Before releasing any Complete Urine Examination (`CUE`, `5.1.1`) report in the LIMS, the bench technologist and signatory pathologist must verify concordance across the following **5 Mandatory Chemical-vs-Microscopic Cross-Checks**:

| Discordance Scenario | Underlying Pathophysiological or Pre-Analytical Cause | Mandatory Bench Verification & Report Comment (`SOP 5.1.1`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Dipstick `Blood 2+/3+` Positive, but Microscopy shows `0–2 RBCs/HPF`** | (1) **Hypotonic / Alkaline Urine Lysis of RBCs** ($\text{SG} \le 1.007$ or $\text{pH} \ge 7.5$ or $> 2\text{h}$ delay), (2) **Intravascular Hemolysis (`Hemoglobinuria`)**, or (3) **Rhabdomyolysis (`Myoglobinuria` — check Serum `CK` `2.5.11`!)** | Inspect sediment by phase-contrast for "ghost RBC membranes"; correlate with Urine `SG`/`pH`, plasma color, and Serum `CK`/`LDH`. Append `[STAMP-URINE-LYSIS-MYO]`. |
| **2. Microscopy shows `> 10 RBCs/HPF`, but Dipstick `Blood` is `Negative`** | High urinary **Ascorbic Acid (`Vitamin C > 25 mg/dL`)** inhibiting the pseudoperoxidase `TMB` redox reaction on the strip | Check Vitamin C history / strip pad; release the true microscopic RBC count and flag Ascorbic Acid chemical interference. |
| **3. Dipstick `Protein` is `Negative/Trace`, but `SSA` Turbidity / Myeloma Suspected** | Dipstick `TBPB` "Protein Error of Indicators" detects **exclusively negatively charged Albumin** and misses positively charged **Monoclonal Free Light Chains (`Bence-Jones` $\kappa/\lambda$) and Tubular Globulins** | Perform **Sulfosalicylic Acid (`SSA 3%`) Turbidity + Heat Test (`5.1.5`)** and recommend **24h Urine `UPEP / IFE` + Serum Free Light Chains (`2.6.3`)**. |
| **4. Microscopy shows `Sterile Pyuria (> 10 Pus Cells/HPF)` with `Nitrite Negative` & Routine Culture Negative** | (1) **Genitourinary Tuberculosis (`Renal TB`)**, (2) *Chlamydia / Ureaplasma / Mycoplasma*, (3) Partially treated UTI, (4) Interstitial Nephritis / Nephrolithiasis, or (5) Non-nitrate-reducing *Enterococcus / S. saprophyticus / Pseudomonas* | Recommend **3 Consecutive Early-Morning Urine `AFB` / `CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra` (`6.1.7` / `6.1.8`)** and clinical correlation. |
| **5. Dipstick `Leukocyte Esterase 2+/3+` Positive, but Microscopy shows `0–2 Pus Cells/HPF`** | **Leukocyte Lysis** in hypotonic/alkaline urine or delayed transport ($> 2\text{ hours}$ at ambient room temperature) | Report lysed leukocyte esterase positivity and recommend fresh clean-catch specimen examination within $\le 1\text{ hour}$. |
