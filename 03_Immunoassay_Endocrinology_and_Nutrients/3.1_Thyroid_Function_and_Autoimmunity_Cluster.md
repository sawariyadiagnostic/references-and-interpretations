## 🏛️ 3.1a — MASTER PANEL: Complete Thyroid Function Profile (`T3 / FT3`, `T4 / FT4`, `TSH`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — COMPLETE THYROID FUNCTION TEST (TFT) MASTER PANEL]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
The Thyroid Profile evaluates the **Hypothalamic–Pituitary–Thyroid (HPT) negative-feedback axis**. Because Pituitary **$\text{TSH}$** responds logarithmically to minute changes in circulating free thyroid hormones (a $2\text{-fold}$ change in $\text{Free T4}$ induces a $100\text{-fold}$ inverse shift in $\text{TSH}$), **$\text{TSH}$ is the most sensitive first-line marker** of primary thyroid dysfunction, while **$\text{Free T4 (FT4)}$** and **$\text{Free T3 (FT3)}$** measure the biologically active, unbound hormone fractions ($0.03\%$ of $\text{T4}$ and $0.3\%$ of $\text{T3}$).

**Integrated HPT Axis Pattern Recognition & Smart LIMS Reflex Matrix:**
| `TSH` | `Free T4` / `Total T4` | `Free T3` / `Total T3` | Primary Clinical Interpretation | Automated LIMS Reflex / Cascade Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Elevated $\uparrow$**<br>*($> 10.0\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$)* | **Low $\downarrow$** | **Low or Normal** | **Overt Primary Hypothyroidism:** Hashimoto’s autoimmune thyroiditis, post-thyroidectomy/radioiodine, severe iodine deficiency, or lithium/amiodarone toxicity. | **Auto-Reflex:** `FT4` + `Anti-TPO` + Fasting Lipid Profile (screen for secondary hypercholesterolemia). |
| **Elevated $\uparrow$**<br>*($4.5\text{–}9.9\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$)* | **Normal** | **Normal** | **Subclinical Hypothyroidism:** Confirm with repeat testing in 6–12 weeks; treatment guided by `Anti-TPO` positivity, pregnancy/infertility, goiter, symptoms, or age $< 65\text{y}$. | **Auto-Reflex:** `FT4` + `Anti-TPO` (if not performed in last 12 months). |
| **Suppressed $\downarrow$**<br>*($< 0.1\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$)* | **Elevated $\uparrow$** | **Elevated $\uparrow$** | **Overt Primary Hyperthyroidism (Thyrotoxicosis):** Graves’ disease, Toxic Multinodular Goiter, Toxic Adenoma, Destructive Thyroiditis, or **Excess Levothyroxine Dose**. | **Auto-Reflex:** `FT4` + `FT3` + `TRAb` (to distinguish Graves’ disease from destructive thyroiditis). |
| **Suppressed $\downarrow$**<br>*($< 0.1\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$)* | **Normal** | **Elevated $\uparrow$** | **$\text{T}_3$-Toxicosis:** Early Graves’ disease, autonomous toxic nodule, or iodine-deficient hyperthyroidism. | **Auto-Reflex:** `FT3` + `TRAb`. |
| **Low $\downarrow$**<br>*($0.1\text{–}0.39\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$)* | **Normal** | **Normal** | **Subclinical Hyperthyroidism**, **1st-Trimester Pregnancy** ($\text{hCG}$-mediated $\text{TSH}$ suppression), recent recovery from thyrotoxicosis, or high-dose glucocorticoids/dopamine. | **Auto-Reflex:** `FT4` + `FT3`; check pregnancy status in reproductive-age females. |
| **Low or Inappropriately Normal** | **Low $\downarrow$** | **Low $\downarrow$** | **Central (Secondary/Tertiary) Hypothyroidism** (Pituitary/Hypothalamic lesion) OR **Non-Thyroidal Illness Syndrome (NTIS / Sick Euthyroid)** in ICU/hospitalized patients. | **Auto-Reflex:** `8:00 AM Cortisol` + `ACTH` + `Prolactin` (CRITICAL: rule out adrenal insufficiency prior to starting $\text{LT}_4$!). |

## 🛡️ 3.1c — Master Pre-Analytical, Biotin, Macro-TSH & Drug Interference Shield

1. **Diurnal Variation ($\text{TSH}$):** Serum $\text{TSH}$ exhibits **$30\%\text{–}50\%$ circadian variation** (peak 23:00–04:00, nadir 14:00–17:00). Always collect serial samples at the same morning window (07:00–10:00 AM).
2. **Pre-Dose Levothyroxine ($\text{LT}_4$) Phlebotomy Rule:** Draw blood **before** ingesting the morning $\text{LT}_4$ tablet; serum $\text{FT4}$ rises transiently by **$15\%\text{–}20\%$ for $4\text{–}6\text{ hours}$ post-dose**.
3. **Biotin (Vitamin $\text{B}_7$) Interference Shield:** Intake of $> 5\text{–}10\text{ mg/day}$ Biotin causes **falsely suppressed $\text{TSH}$/$\text{Tg}$ (sandwich)** and **falsely elevated $\text{FT4}$/$\text{FT3}$/$\text{TRAb}$ (competitive)** on streptavidin-biotin platforms, mimicking Graves' disease. Enforce a **48–72 hour Biotin washout**. Note: Direct ABEI-labeled magnetic microbead assays on Snibe Maglumi X3 without streptavidin-biotin capture are resistant, whereas streptavidin-coupled kits require washout.
4. **Macro-TSH & Heterophile Shield:** Persistently elevated $\text{TSH}$ ($10\text{–}100\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$) with normal $\text{FT4/FT3}$ in an asymptomatic patient warrants **PEG 6000 precipitation** (post-PEG recovery $< 25\%$ confirms benign high-molecular-weight $\text{IgG-TSH}$ **Macro-TSH** complex).
5. **Multi-Jurisdictional Medico-Legal Disclaimer:** *Processed under ISO 15189:2022 & NABL quality governance. All immunoassays are subject to biological variation and rare endogenous antibodies. Correlate with clinical phenotype and neck ultrasonography. Not valid for medico-legal purposes under CPA 2019 unless authenticated under formal chain-of-custody.*
