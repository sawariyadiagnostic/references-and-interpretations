## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, `CLSI GP41` Order-of-Draw, Patient Preparation, Kinetic Washout & Quantitative `HIL` Interference Matrix

### 2.1 Specimen Collection, Biobanking & Cervical Lymph Node Washout (`FNA-Tg`) Protocols
1. **Venous Blood Specimen Collection:**
   - **Primary Specimen:** Serum collected in **Gold-Top SST** (silica clot activator with polymer barrier gel) or **Red-Top Plain Vacutainer** (`CLSI GP41-Ed7` Draw Order #4).
   - **Tube Inversion & Clotting:** Invert gently $5\text{–}8\text{ times}$ immediately post-draw; allow upright clotting for $20\text{–}30\text{ minutes}$ at room temperature ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$).
   - **Centrifugation:** Centrifuge at **$1,500\text{–}2,000 \times g$ for $10\text{ minutes}$** in a temperature-controlled swing-bucket centrifuge ($20\text{–}24^\circ\text{C}$).
   - **Minimum Volume Requirements:** Adult: $0.5\text{ mL}$ cell-free serum ($2.5\text{ mL}$ whole blood) to permit paired `Serum Tg`, `Anti-Tg`, and `TSH` testing plus archive biobanking. Pediatric: $150\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ serum ($0.6\text{ mL}$ whole blood in Microtainer SST).
2. **Cervical Lymph Node Fine-Needle Aspiration Washout Fluid (`FNA-Tg`):**
   - **Indications:** Evaluation of suspicious cervical lymphadenopathy (microcalcifications, rounded configuration, cystic changes, chaotic vascularity, or loss of fatty hilum on Doppler ultrasound) in pre-operative DTC or post-thyroidectomy surveillance.
   - **Collection Protocol:** Following standard fine-needle aspiration of the target lymph node (using a $23\text{G}$ to $25\text{G}$ needle) and expulsion of cellular material onto glass slides for cytology, the aspirating needle and hub are immediately rinsed with **$1.0\text{ mL}$ of sterile physiological normal saline ($0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$)** directly into a sterile polypropylene cryovial.
   - **Laboratory Processing:** Centrifuge the washout vial at $1,500 \times g$ for $5\text{ minutes}$ to precipitate red cells and tissue fragments. The clear supernatant is analyzed directly using the high-sensitivity `Tg` assay.
   - **Diagnostic Superiority:** Overcomes false-negative cytology caused by acellular cystic fluid, which occurs in up to $40\%$ of cystic lymph node metastases in papillary thyroid carcinoma.
3. **Mandatory Patient Preparation & Kinetic Washout Rules:**
   - **Rule 1 — Thyroid Biopsy / Palpation Washout ($\ge 14\text{ to }21\text{ Days}$):** Fine-needle aspiration biopsy (`FNAB`), core needle biopsy, neck surgery, or vigorous palpation disrupts thyroid follicular architecture, dumping massive intrafollicular colloid `Tg` boluses into systemic circulation (acute surges $> 500\%$). Sampling must be deferred for $\ge 14\text{ days}$.
   - **Rule 2 — Post-Thyroidectomy & Post-$^{131}\text{I}$ Ablation Clearance Interval ($\ge 6\text{ to }12\text{ Weeks}$):** Serum `Tg` has an in-vivo elimination half-life of **$t_{1/2} \approx 65\text{ hours}$ ($\sim 2.7\text{ days}$)**. Post-total thyroidectomy clearance requires $5\text{ to }6$ half-lives ($\ge 4\text{ to }6\text{ weeks}$) to reach baseline nadir. Following radioactive iodine ($^{131}\text{I}$) remnant ablation, radiation-induced cytolytic lysis causes transient colloid leakage; post-ablation baseline `Tg` must NOT be assessed before **$6\text{ to }12\text{ weeks}$**.
   - **Rule 3 — TSH-Suppressed (Basal) vs TSH-Stimulated Status Documentation:** Every `Tg` test requisition and laboratory report must explicitly document the patient's endocrine metabolic state:
     - **Basal / TSH-Suppressed `Tg`:** Patient on stable Levothyroxine ($\text{LT}_4$) therapy with target $\text{TSH} < 0.1\text{–}0.5\text{ mIU/L}$.
     - **Stimulated `Tg` (rhTSH):** Recombinant human TSH (*Thyrogen*, thyrotropin alfa $0.9\text{ mg}$ IM on Day 1 and Day 2; venous blood drawn on **Day 5**, $\sim 72\text{ hours}$ post-second injection).
     - **Stimulated `Tg` (THW):** Thyroid Hormone Withdrawal ($\text{LT}_4$ stopped for $4\text{–}6\text{ weeks}$ or $\text{LT}_3$ stopped for $2\text{ weeks}$) to achieve endogenous $\text{TSH} > 30\text{ mIU/L}$.
   - **Rule 4 — Mandatory Paired `Anti-Tg` Co-Ordering:** Sandwich CLIA `Tg` is subject to complete false-negativity in the presence of anti-thyroglobulin autoantibodies (`TgAb`). An order for Serum `Tg` automatically initiates quantitative `Anti-Tg` testing (`SDL-SOP-IMM-3.1.8`) on the identical aliquot.
   - **Rule 5 — High-Dose Biotin Washout ($\ge 48\text{ to }72\text{ Hours}$):** Patients consuming $> 5\text{ mg/day}$ biotin (nutritional supplements or neuro-therapies) must abstain for $\ge 48\text{ hours}$ prior to phlebotomy.

### 2.3 Specimen Multi-Temperature Stability & Mandatory Oncology Biobanking Protocol
- **Ambient ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$):** $24\text{ hours}$ (capped, protected from light).
- **Refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$):** $7\text{ days}$ in primary tube gel or polypropylene secondary tube.
- **Frozen ($-20^\circ\text{C}$):** $6\text{ months}$ (non-defrosting freezer).
- **Deep Ultra-Frozen ($-80^\circ\text{C}$):** $\ge 2\text{ years}$ (validated stability; maximum 2 freeze-thaw cycles).
- **Mandatory Oncology Biobank Rule (`NABL 112` / `ATA 2015` Recommendation 62C):** For all differentiated thyroid cancer surveillance patients, a minimum **$300\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ serum archive aliquot** is stored at $-80^\circ\text{C}$ for $12\text{ months}$. In the event of assay kit reformulation, analyzer recalibration, or platform changeover, the frozen prior specimen is retrieved and re-assayed in parallel with the current specimen to prevent spurious clinical recurrence alarms driven by inter-assay calibration shifts.

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Reaction Stoichiometry, `CLSI` Verification, `LoQ` Metrology & High-Dose Hook Effect Protection

### 3.1 Reaction Stoichiometry & Flash Chemiluminescence Detection Physics (`Snibe Maglumi X3`)
1. **Reaction Stoichiometry (Two-Site Non-Competitive Sandwich Assay):**
   - **Step 1 (Immunocapture):** Exactly **$40\text{ }\mu\text{L}$** of patient serum, calibrator, or control is pipetted into a reaction cuvette and incubated with **$20\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of superparamagnetic microbeads** covalently coated with high-affinity monoclonal anti-human `Tg` capture antibodies targeting epitope domain 1 on the $660\text{ kDa}$ `Tg` homodimer, along with $100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of assay buffer.
   - **Step 2 (Sandwich Formation):** Simultaneously or sequentially, **$100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of *ABEI*-labeled monoclonal anti-human `Tg` antibody** directed against a distinct, non-overlapping epitope domain 2 is introduced. The mixture incubates at $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.2^\circ\text{C}$ for $15\text{ minutes}$. Endogenous `Tg` molecules are firmly sandwiched between the magnetic solid-phase capture antibody and the chemiluminescent *ABEI* tracer antibody.
   - **Step 3 (Magnetic Separation & High-Efficiency Wash):** The cuvette enters the magnetic separation station. A permanent rare-earth neodymium magnetic field holds the magnetic microbead complexes against the cuvette wall while the aspirator removes unbound proteins, heterophile antibodies, and matrix components. Three cycles of buffered wash solution ($400\text{ }\mu\text{L/cycle}$) eliminate unreacted tracer.
   - **Step 4 (Chemiluminescence Flash Triggering & Photon Measurement):** The cuvette moves into the dark-chamber luminometer.
     - **Starter Reagent 1** ($0.12\%\text{ H}_2\text{O}_2$ in dilute $\text{HNO}_3$) and **Starter Reagent 2** ($0.35\text{ mol/L NaOH}$) are injected simultaneously.
     - In the alkaline oxidizing environment, *ABEI* undergoes catalytic oxidation, generating an electronically excited 3-aminophthalate intermediate that emits an intense **3-second flash chemiluminescence photon burst at $\lambda_{\max} = 420\text{ nm}$**.
     - A temperature-regulated photomultiplier tube (`PMT`) captures total emitted photons as Relative Light Units (`RLU`). Total photon emission is **directly proportional** to the concentration of serum `Tg`.
2. **Calibration Architecture:**
   - The analyzer processor calculates `Tg` concentration in `ng/mL` using a stored **10-point Master Calibration Curve** (4-Parameter Logistic `4PL` algorithm) recalibrated every $28\text{ days}$ using a lot-specific 2-point on-board recalibrator set (`Cal 1` and `Cal 2`) traceable to `CRM BCR-457`.

### 3.2 Step-by-Step Bench Operating SOP & Instrument Verification Protocol
1. **Daily Maintenance & Optics Inspection (`ISO 15189:2022` Clause 6.4):**

   - Execute instrument daily automated initialization: probe internal/external fluidic flushes, syringing prime, and dark-count PMT background light test ($\text{RLU} < 100$).

### 3.3 Functional Sensitivity (`LoQ`), Linearity (`AMR/CRR`) & High-Dose Hook Effect Protection
1. **Ultrasensitive Limit of Quantitation (`LoQ` / Functional Sensitivity per `CLSI EP17-A2`):**
   - **Limit of Blank (`LoB`):** **$0.02\text{ ng/mL}$** (determined by $60$ replicates of zero-analyte diluent over $3\text{ days}$).
   - **Limit of Detection (`LoD`):** **$0.04\text{ ng/mL}$** ($\text{LoB} + 1.645 \times \text{SD}_{\text{low}}$).
   - **Functional Sensitivity (`LoQ` at $\text{CV} \le 20\%$):** **$\le 0.10\text{ ng/mL}$** (established via $10$-day precision profiles on low-concentration patient pools).
   - *Clinical Oncology Impact:* A functional sensitivity of $\le 0.10\text{ ng/mL}$ fulfills international requirements for "2nd-Generation Ultrasensitive Tg assays," permitting reliable confirmation of complete biochemical remission on basal Levothyroxine suppression ($\text{Tg} < 0.20\text{ ng/mL}$), thereby avoiding routine recombinant human TSH stimulation in low-risk thyroidectomy patients.
2. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`) & Extended Reportable Range (`CRR` per `CLSI EP06-Ed2`):**
   - **Direct `AMR`:** **$0.10 \text{ to } 500.0\text{ ng/mL}$** (linear polynomial regression verified with $r^2 \ge 0.995$ and non-linearity $< 5\%$).
   - **Automated Dilution (`CRR`):** Onboard automated $1:10$ and $1:100$ dilution extending reportable range to **$50,000\text{ ng/mL}$**.
3. **High-Dose "Hook" Effect Mitigation Protocol:**
   - In patients with bulky metastatic follicular or papillary carcinoma (extensive pulmonary or osteolytic skeletal metastases), circulating `Tg` may exceed $50,000\text{ to }100,000\text{ ng/mL}$. In two-site sandwich assays, extreme antigen excess saturates both solid-phase capture and tracer antibodies simultaneously, preventing cross-linking and causing a paradoxically suppressed false-normal signal (e.g., reporting $15\text{ ng/mL}$ instead of $80,000\text{ ng/mL}$).
   - *Laboratory Defense:* Reagent antibody excess on the `Snibe Maglumi X3` prevents hook effect up to **$100,000\text{ ng/mL}$**.
   - *Mandatory Bench Reflex Protocol:* Any specimen from a patient with imaging-proven metastatic thyroid cancer whose direct measured `Tg` reads discordantly low ($< 50\text{ ng/mL}$) must undergo an immediate **$1:100$ manual dilution verification**. If the diluted concentration (multiplied by $100$) exceeds the neat result by $> 50\%$, hook effect is diagnosed, and the dilution-corrected value is officially reported.

## 4. Metrological Traceability, `ISO/TS 20914` Measurement Uncertainty, `EFLM` Biological Variation & Westgard Six-Sigma Statistical Process Control

### 4.1 Calibration Hierarchy & Unbroken Metrological Chain (`ISO 17511:2020`)
- **Primary Reference Material:** Certified Reference Material **`CRM BCR-457`** (*Human Thyroglobulin*, Institute for Reference Materials and Measurements — `IRMM`, European Commission).
- **Value Assignment:** Assigned certified mass concentration based on gravimetric preparation, amino acid analysis, and international multi-center collaborative quantification.
- **Traceability Cascade:** `CRM BCR-457` $\rightarrow$ Manufacturer Working Calibrator (purified human thyroglobulin in human serum matrix) $\rightarrow$ Product Master Calibrator $\rightarrow$ Commercial 2-Point Master Curve RFID Calibrators $\rightarrow$ Patient Serum Result.

### 4.2 EFLM Biological Variation, Desirable Specifications & Reference Change Value (`RCV`)

| Biological Variation / Performance Parameter | Quantified Value | Clinical Derivation & Regulatory Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Within-Subject Biological Variation ($CV_I$)** | **$14.0\%$** | Determined in healthy euthyroid cohorts; driven by physiologic TSH pulsatility. |

- **Sigma Metric Calculation Formula:**

  - **Rejection Rule $1_{3s}$:** One QC value exceeding $\pm 3\text{ SD}$ $\rightarrow$ Halt testing, invalidate current run, inspect optics/wash fluidics.
  - **Rejection Rule $2_{2s}$:** Two consecutive QC values exceeding $+2\text{ SD}$ or $-2\text{ SD}$ $\rightarrow$ Reject batch; recalibrate analyzer.

### 5.3 Cervical Lymph Node Fine-Needle Aspiration Tg Washout (`FNA-Tg`) Decision Matrix

- **Diagnostic Thresholds:**

## 7. Comprehensive Clinical Pathophysiology, Molecular Oncology, Temporal Kinetics & Subspecialty Differential Diagnosis Matrix

### 7.1 Pathophysiology, Biosynthesis & Molecular Mechanisms of Disease
1. **Normal Thyroid Colloid Physiology:**
   - Thyroglobulin is a massive homodimeric scaffold protein synthesized exclusively by thyroid follicular cells. Its transcription is directly governed by thyroid-specific transcription factors (`NKX2-1` / `TTF-1`, `FOXE1` / `TTF-2`, `PAX8`) and stimulated by TSH receptor activation via the $\text{G}\alpha_s\text{–cAMP–PKA}$ signaling pathway.
   - Nascent `Tg` polypeptide undergoes extensive core glycosylation and folding in the endoplasmic reticulum, quality-checked by calnexin/calreticulin chaperones, before apical exocytosis into the follicular lumen. In the colloid, `TPO` catalyzes the covalent iodination of tyrosine residues and oxidative coupling of iodotyrosines (`MIT` and `DIT`) to synthesize thyroxine ($T_4$) and triiodothyronine ($T_3$). Under basal conditions, small amounts of intact `Tg` ($1\text{–}2\%\text{ of total synthesized pool}$) escape transcellular degradation and enter the lymphatic and capillary circulation.
2. **Molecular Oncology of Differentiated Thyroid Carcinoma (`DTC`):**
   - Papillary Thyroid Carcinoma (`PTC`, $\sim 85\%$ of DTC) is driven by activating mutations in the `MAPK` pathway, primarily `BRAF V600E` ($\sim 60\%$) or `RET/PTC` rearrangements ($\sim 15\%$). Follicular Thyroid Carcinoma (`FTC`, $\sim 10\%$ of DTC) is driven by `RAS` mutations (`NRAS`, `HRAS`, `KRAS`) or `PAX8-PPARG` fusions.
   - Well-differentiated carcinoma cells retain the machinery for `Tg` gene transcription, synthesis, and secretion into the bloodstream. However, neoplastic colloid lumens lack normal follicular polarity; tumor `Tg` is directly secreted into tumor neo-vessels, making circulating `Tg` an ultra-specific tumor marker following total surgical ablation of normal thyroid tissue.
   - *Dedifferentiation & Loss of Marker:* When DTC dedifferentiates into Poorly Differentiated (`PDTC`) or Anaplastic Thyroid Carcinoma (`ATC`), epigenetic silencing and `TERT` promoter mutations downregulate `TTF-1` and `Tg` expression. Patients may develop extensive FDG-avid metastatic disease with paradoxical loss of `Tg` elevation!
   - *Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma (`MTC`):* Derived from neural crest parafollicular C-cells; **`MTC` does NOT produce Thyroglobulin!** MTC surveillance requires **Serum Calcitonin and Carcinoembryonic Antigen (`CEA`)**.

### 7.2 In-Vivo Temporal Elimination Kinetics & Doubling Time (`Tg-DT`) Dynamics
1. **Clearance Kinetics:** Serum `Tg` exhibits two-compartment elimination kinetics: a rapid distribution phase ($t_{1/2\alpha} \approx 6\text{–}10\text{ hours}$) and a prolonged metabolic clearance phase (**$t_{1/2\beta} \approx 65\text{ hours}$**, range $50\text{–}80\text{ hours}$). Complete elimination after total thyroidectomy takes $20\text{ to }30\text{ days}$.
2. **Thyroglobulin Doubling Time (`Tg-DT`) Formulation:**
   - In patients with biochemically persistent or recurrent DTC, the rate of exponential `Tg` increase provides far greater prognostic insight than isolated absolute levels:
     $$\text{Tg-DT} = \frac{(t_2 - t_1) \times \ln(2)}{\ln(\text{Tg}_2) - \ln(\text{Tg}_1)} = \frac{t_2 - t_1}{\log_2(\text{Tg}_2 / \text{Tg}_1)}$$
   - *Prognostic Stratification:*
     - **$\text{Tg-DT} < 1.0\text{ Year}$:** Aggressive tumor progression, high probability of distant metastases, radioiodine refractoriness, high $^{18}\text{F-FDG}$ PET avidity, and 10-year overall survival $< 50\%$.
     - **$\text{Tg-DT } 1.0\text{ – } 3.0\text{ Years}$:** Intermediate progression; warrants close imaging surveillance and reconsideration of radioiodine therapy.
     - **$\text{Tg-DT} > 3.0\text{ Years}$:** Indolent recurrence; excellent 10-year survival ($> 95\%$); conservative surveillance or targeted local therapy is often appropriate.

### 7.4 Special Populations: Pediatric Differentiated Thyroid Carcinoma & Pregnancy Surveillance
1. **Pediatric DTC Surveillance Nuances:**
   - Pediatric patients ($< 18\text{ years}$) with DTC exhibit unique molecular epidemiology, characterized by a higher frequency of gene rearrangements (`RET/PTC` fusions in $45\text{–}60\%$ and `NTRK` fusions in $10\text{–}15\%$) and a lower prevalence of point mutations (`BRAF V600E` in $< 30\%$).
   - *The Pediatric Paradigm:* Children frequently present with extensive multifocal primary tumors, extensive cervical lymphadenopathy ($60\text{–}80\%$), and diffuse miliary pulmonary metastases ($20\text{–}30\%$), yet achieve outstanding overall survival ($> 98\%$ at 20 years).
   - In pediatric athyreotic patients, a stable low-level detectable basal `Tg` ($0.50\text{ to }2.0\text{ ng/mL}$) often persists for years without structural progression. Aggressive re-treatment with cumulative radioiodine doses is avoided to prevent secondary malignancies and pulmonary fibrosis; longitudinal stability of `Tg` trend is the therapeutic compass.
2. **Thyroid Cancer Surveillance During Pregnancy:**
   - Placental human chorionic gonadotropin (`hCG`) peaks at $10\text{–}12\text{ weeks}$ of gestation. Because hCG shares structural homology with TSH, high hCG weakly stimulates the TSH receptor, driving physiological increases in residual thyroid follicular `Tg` secretion.
   - In women with known DTC, serum `Tg` may increase by $20\%\text{ to }40\%$ during the second and third trimesters without representing structural tumor progression.
   - Diagnostic radioactive iodine scans ($^{131}\text{I}$ / $^{123}\text{I}$) are **strictly contraindicated** during pregnancy and lactation due to fetal thyroid destruction. Surveillance relies exclusively on serial basal `Tg`, `Anti-Tg`, and high-resolution neck Doppler ultrasonography.

## 8. `ISO 22367` Failure Mode & Effects Analysis (FMECA) Risk Matrix, `Anti-Tg` Interference Reflexing & `CPA 2019` Medico-Legal Shield

### 8.1 The Catastrophic `Anti-Tg` Autoantibody Interference Mechanism & Reflex Cascade
1. **The Immunometric Sandwich Trap:**
   - In automated two-site sandwich immunometric assays (`CLIA` / `ECLIA`), capture and detection antibodies must bind simultaneously to distinct surface epitopes on the circulating $660\text{ kDa}$ `Tg` dimer.
   - When a patient possesses circulating anti-thyroglobulin autoantibodies (`Anti-Tg`, prevalent in $20\text{–}25\%$ of DTC patients), endogenous `Anti-Tg` binds sterically to these epitopes, completely blocking capture and/or tracer binding.
   - **Lethal Clinical Consequence:** The analyzer reports a **falsely undetectable `Serum Tg` ($< 0.10\text{ ng/mL}$)** in a patient with extensive progressive metastatic thyroid cancer! This false reassurance delays life-saving surgery or radioiodine therapy.
   - *(Note: In legacy competitive radioimmunoassays [`RIA`], endogenous `Anti-Tg` competes with reagent antibodies for $^{125}\text{I-Tg}$ tracer, producing erratic falsely elevated `Tg` results).*
2. **Definitive Reflex Protocol:**
   - **LIMS Hard-Lock Rule:** Every sample with `Anti-Tg` $\ge 4.0\text{ IU/mL}$ triggers an automated lock preventing standalone `Tg` release.
   - **Alternative Pathway 1 (Surrogate Marker Trend):** In `Anti-Tg` positive patients, serial quantitative `Anti-Tg` titers (`SDL-SOP-IMM-3.1.8`) serve as an indirect surrogate tumor marker. Disappearance or $> 50\%$ decline in `Anti-Tg` titer indicates complete cure, whereas rising `Anti-Tg` indicates active cancer recurrence.
   - **Alternative Pathway 2 (Peptide Digestion LC-MS/MS Reflex):** For definitive direct `Tg` measurement in `Anti-Tg` positive patients, reflex to Isotope-Dilution LC-MS/MS. Patient serum proteins undergo immunoaffinity enrichment, dithiothreitol reduction, iodoacetamide alkylation, and complete trypsin digestion. Trypsin cleaves both `Tg` and interfering `Anti-Tg` antibodies into short linear peptides. ### 8.4 Reference LC-MS/MS Tryptic Digestion Protocol (`CLSI C62-A` Technical Workflow)
1. **Sample Preparation & Denaturation:** Exactly $100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of patient serum is enriched with anti-thyroglobulin antibody-conjugated magnetic beads, washed, and denatured using $6.0\text{ mol/L}$ urea in $50\text{ mmol/L}$ ammonium bicarbonate buffer ($\text{pH } 8.0$).
2. **Reduction & Alkylation:** Disulfide bridges are reduced with $10\text{ mmol/L}$ dithiothreitol (DTT) at $60^\circ\text{C}$ for $30\text{ minutes}$, followed by cysteine alkylation with $25\text{ mmol/L}$ iodoacetamide in the dark at room temperature for $30\text{ minutes}$.
3. **Trypsin Digestion:** Sequencing-grade modified porcine trypsin is added at an enzyme-to-substrate ratio of $1:20$ and incubated at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $16\text{ hours}$. Trypsin enzymatically hydrolyzes peptide bonds at the carboxyl side of lysine and arginine residues, dismantling both `Tg` and interfering patient autoantibodies into short linear peptides.
4. **Internal Standard Spiking & Solid Phase Extraction:** The digest is spiked with a known concentration of synthetic stable-isotope-labeled internal standard peptide `VIFDANAPVAVR*` (incorporating $^{13}\text{C}_{6},^{15}\text{N}_{4}\text{-L-Arginine}$, $\Delta m/z = +10\text{ Da}$). Desalting is performed using a C18 micro-SPE cartridge.
5. **LC-MS/MS MRM Acquisition:** Analyzed on a Triple-Quadrupole Mass Spectrometer via electrospray ionization positive mode (`ESI+`). Multiple Reaction Monitoring (`MRM`):

   - Internal Standard `VIFDANAPVAVR*`: Precursor ion $[\text{M}+2\text{H}]^{2+} = m/z\ 627.9 \rightarrow$ Product ion $y_8^+ = m/z\ 809.5$.
   - True thyroglobulin is quantified from the analyte-to-internal standard peak area ratio against a matrix-matched calibration curve traceable to `BCR-457`.
