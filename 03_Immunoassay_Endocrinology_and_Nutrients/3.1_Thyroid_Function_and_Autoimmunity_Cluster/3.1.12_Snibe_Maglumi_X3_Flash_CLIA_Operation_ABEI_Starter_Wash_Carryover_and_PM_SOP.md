## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Immunoassay Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the daily startup, **Flash Chemiluminescence (`ABEI` — $N\text{-(4-Aminobutyl)-}N\text{-ethylisoluminol}$)** Starter $1+2$ light-check verification, **FITC-Anti-FITC Paramagnetic Microbead** homogenization, **10-Point Master Curve + 2-Point RFID Calibrator Adjustment**, **3-Phase Wash Station Unbound-Tracer Separation**, **High-Dose Hook / Carryover Verification (`> 100,000 mIU/mL` $\beta\text{-hCG}$, `AFP`, `Ferritin`, `Tg`, `PSA`, `Prolactin`)**, and Preventive Maintenance (`PM`) for the **Snibe Maglumi X3** ($200\text{ tests/hr}$ continuous random-access `CLIA` analyzer) and any backup/upgrade `CLIA`/`ECLIA` platform.
2. **NABL 112 Compliance (`Clinical Biochemistry / Immunoassay & Endocrinology`):**
   - Mandates documented verification of **Photomultiplier Tube (`PMT`) Dark Count & Light Check (`RLU` — Relative Light Units)**, onboard reagent cooling ($2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$), lot-specific `RFID` 2-point master-curve recalibration, daily multi-level immunoassay `IQC`, and **Mandatory Interference Shields for Biotin (`Vitamin B7`), Heterophile/HAMA Antibodies, Macro-Analytes (`PEG 6000` Macroprolactin / Macro-TSH), and High-Dose Hook Effect (`SOP 0.1.4`)**.

## Section 2: Physical Principle of `ABEI` Flash Chemiluminescence & Paramagnetic Microbead Separation
1. **Non-Enzyme Small-Molecule Label (`ABEI`):**
   - Unlike enzyme-linked chemiluminescence (`ALP` or `HRP` labels that are sensitive to ambient temperature shifts and endogenous serum phosphatase/peroxidase/azide), **ABEI ($N\text{-(4-Aminobutyl)-}N\text{-ethylisoluminol}$)** is a thermally stable, low-molecular-weight non-enzyme organic molecule.
   - **Flash Emission Kinetics:** Inside the light-tight `PMT` measuring chamber at $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$, injection of **Starter 1 (Alkaline $\text{NaOH}$ Buffer)** and **Starter 2 ($\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ + Catalyst)** oxidizes `ABEI` into an excited aminophthalate state, emitting an intense photon flash at **$\lambda = 425\text{ nm}$** integrated over exactly **$3.0\text{ seconds}$** (`RLU`).
2. **Assay Formats on `Maglumi X3` (`Sandwich` vs. `Competitive` `RLU` Relationship):**
   - **Two-Site Immunometric ("Sandwich") Format** (*e.g., `TSH`, `LH`, `FSH`, `PRL`, `PTH`, `ACTH`, `Ferritin`, `hs-cTnI`, `NT-proBNP`, `PCT`, `IL-6`, `β-hCG`, `Tumor Markers`*):
     $$\text{Analyte Concentration} \propto \text{Direct Proportion to Measured } \text{RLU} \quad (\uparrow \text{Analyte} \implies \uparrow \text{RLU})$$
   - **Competitive Binding / Displacement Format** (*Low-Molecular-Weight Haptens: `FT3`, `FT4`, `Total T3`, `Total T4`, `25-OH Vitamin D`, `Vitamin B12`, `Folate`, `Estradiol E2`, `Progesterone`, `Testosterone`, `Cortisol`, `Aldosterone`*):
     $$\text{Analyte Concentration} \propto \text{Inverse Proportion to Measured } \text{RLU} \quad (\uparrow \text{Analyte} \implies \downarrow \text{RLU})$$

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Reaction Chamber & Wash Buffer Pre-Heater Temperature** | **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$** | $< 36.8^\circ\text{C}$ or $> 37.2^\circ\text{C}$ | Wait $15\text{ min}$ after system wake-up; check Peltier incubation block before running temperature-sensitive `FT4` / `FT3` equilibrium assays. |

| **4. ## Section 4: RFID 10-Point Master Curve & 2-Point Recalibration Protocol (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.5`)
1. **Traceability to International WHO / IFCC Reference Preparations:**
   - Every `Maglumi X3` reagent integral contains a built-in **`RFID` Microchip** encoding the factory **10-Point 4-Parameter Logistic (`4PL`) / Cubic Spline Master Calibration Curve** traceable to **WHO International Standards (`NIBSC`)** or **IFCC Reference Materials** (e.g., `TSH WHO 3rd IRP 81/565`, `LH WHO 80/552`, `FSH WHO 92/510`, `PRL WHO 3rd IS 84/500`, `β-hCG WHO 4th IS 75/589`, `25-OH Vitamin D NIST SRM 2972a / VDSP`, `hs-cTnI IFCC`, `PCT Brahms`).
2. **Two-Point Onboard Calibration Adjustment (`Calibrator 1 — Low` & `Calibrator 2 — High`):**
   - Run the integral's internal/external **2-Point Calibrators in duplicate** to mathematically adjust the factory 10-point Master Curve to local instrument `PMT`, temperature, and wash conditions.
   - **Mandatory Calibration Acceptance Criteria (`4 Criteria`):**
     1. Replicate `CV %` of Calibrator 1 and Calibrator 2 `RLU` must be **$\le 5.0\%$**.
     2. Ratio of Measured `RLU` to Target `RFID RLU` must fall within **$0.80\text{ – }1.20$** ($80\%\text{–}120\%$).
     3. Monotonic curve direction verified (`Cal 2 RLU >> Cal 1 RLU` for Sandwich; `Cal 1 RLU >> Cal 2 RLU` for Competitive).
     4. Subsequent **Level-1, Level-2 (and Level-3) Third-Party Immunoassay Controls (`Bio-Rad Lyphochek Immunoassay Plus / Specialty`)** must fall within **$\pm 2\text{ SD}$**.
   - **Mandatory Recalibration Frequency:** (1) Every **New Reagent Kit Lot**, (2) Every **$14\text{–}28\text{ Days}$** (per kit `IFU` onboard calibration interval), (3) After any **`Starter 1 & 2` Lot Change** or **`PMT` / Wash-Station Maintenance**, and (4) Whenever `IQC` exhibits a systematic `2:2s` or `4:1s` shift.

## Section 5: 3-Phase Magnetic Separation Wash Efficiency & High-Titer Carryover Verification
1. **Magnetic Wash Station Physics:**
   - Because immune complexes bound to paramagnetic microbeads represent $< 1\%$ of total added `ABEI` conjugate in low-analyte samples, the **3-Phase Magnetic Separation Wash Station** must achieve a **Wash Efficiency $> 99.999\%$** (residual unbound tracer $< 1\text{ ppm}$).
   - **Daily Wash Needle Verification:** Verify all 3 aspiration/dispense concentric needles in the magnetic wash station dispense evenly and aspirate reaction cuvettes completely dry (`Residual Volume < 2 uL`). Partial blockage of Wash Needle #3 causes **erratic false-positive spikes across Sandwich assays (`hs-cTnI`, `β-hCG`, `HIV/HBsAg/HCV`)!**
2. **Sample Probe Carryover Protection for Extreme Dynamic-Range Analytes (`10^7-Fold Range`):**
   - Analytes such as **$\beta\text{-hCG}$ (`0.5 to 1,000,000 mIU/mL`)**, **AFP (`0.5 to 500,000 ng/mL`)**, **Ferritin (`1 to 100,000 ng/mL`)**, **Thyroglobulin**, and **Total PSA** span $6\text{–}7$ orders of magnitude.
   - **Mandatory Protocol:** Use **Disposable Conductive Pipette Tips** (or verified high-pressure interior/exterior `System Wash Solution` flushing with verified sample carryover **$< 1.0\text{ ppm}$ [$< 10^{-6}$]**).
   - **Automatic Reflex Rule (`LIMS CARRYOVER CHECK`):** Whenever any patient specimen yields **$\beta\text{-hCG} > 50,000\text{ mIU/mL}$**, **$\text{AFP} > 10,000\text{ ng/mL}$**, or **$\text{HBsAg / HCV / HIV COI} > 500$**, automatically **re-verify the immediately following specimen** if it tests borderline/weakly positive for the same analyte!

## Section 6: Mandatory 4-Pillar Immunoassay Interference Shield (`SOP 0.1.4` Bench Execution)
| Interference Type | Susceptible `Maglumi X3` / `CLIA` Assays | Clinical Trigger for Suspicion | Mandatory Bench Verification Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. High-Dose Hook Effect (`Prozone`)** | One-step / Sandwich assays: **$\beta\text{-hCG}$, `PRL`, `Ferritin`, `AFP`, `CA-125`, `PSA`, `Tg`** | Giant pituitary adenoma, molar pregnancy/GTN, massive HCC/Myeloma/HLH with unexpectedly low/moderate result | Perform **$1:10$ and $1:100$ Automated/Manual Dilution** in `CLIA` Diluent; if diluted result jumps $> 5\times$, report the diluted-corrected peak value (`[STAMP-HOOK]`). |
| **2. Exogenous Biotin (`Vitamin B7 > 5 mg/day`)** | Streptavidin-Biotin capture assays (*Note: `Maglumi X3` uses `FITC-Anti-FITC` capture, providing intrinsic Biotin resistance!*) | Discordant biochemical hyperthyroidism (`High FT4/FT3 + Low TSH`) in an asymptomatic patient | Check Biotin history (`SOP 3.2.11`); impose $48\text{–}72\text{h}$ Biotin washout or verify on `FITC-Anti-FITC` capture platform (`[STAMP-BIOTIN]`). |
| **3. Macro-Analyte Complexes (`IgG-Bound`)** | **Prolactin (`Macroprolactin`, $15\text{–}25\%$ of hyperprolactinemia)** & **TSH (`Macro-TSH`)** | Isolated `PRL > 40 ng/mL` or `TSH > 15 uIU/mL` with normal `FT4` and normal menses/fertility | Mix $200\ \mu\text{L}$ Serum $+ 200\ \mu\text{L}$ **$25\%\text{ w/v}$ `PEG 6000`** ($1:1$), vortex, spin at $3,000 \times g \times 15\text{ min}$, and assay supernatant (`Recovery <= 40% = Macroprolactinemia`; `SOP 3.3.4`). |
| **4. Heterophile / `HAMA` / `RF` Antibodies** | `hs-cTnI`, `TSH`, `β-hCG`, `CA-125` | Persistently elevated result without clinical progression or dynamic rise/fall | Perform **Serial Dilution Linearity ($1:2, 1:4, 1:8$)** (non-linear recovery outside $80\%\text{–}120\%$ proves heterophile interference) or treat with **Heterophile Blocking Tube (`HBT`)**. |

## Section 7: Preventive Maintenance (`PM`) Matrix & Print-Ready `Maglumi X3` Logbook (`NABL-IMM-LOG-01`)
- **Daily:** Empty solid Reaction-Cuvette waste bin and liquid biohazard waste; prime `Starter 1 & 2` and `System Wash Buffer`; run `PMT System Light Check`; verify reagent carousel temperature ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$); execute **Daily Probe & Wash-Needle Maintenance Clean**.
- **Weekly:** Clean Sample/Reagent Probe exterior and Magnetic Wash Station needles ($1, 2, 3$) with $70\%$ `IPA` swab; run **Special Protein/Hypochlorite Tubing Clean**; clean barcode scanner window and dust filter.
- **Monthly / Semi-Annual:** Clean `Starter` injector nozzles inside the `PMT` chamber with warm distilled water; verify syringe pump seals, magnetic field strength, $37.0^\circ\text{C}$ incubator calibration, and $< 1\text{ ppm}$ carryover certification.
