## 🚨 4. Mandatory Amber Light-Protection & Cold-Chain Pre-Analytical Shield

> **⚠️ PHOTOLABILE & THERMOLABILE VITAMIN / PEPTIDE ALERT:**  
> Vitamins **$\text{A}$ (Retinol)**, **$\text{E}$ ($\alpha$-Tocopherol)**, **$\text{K}_1$ (Phylloquinone)**, **$\text{B}_1$ (Thiamine)**, **$\text{B}_2$ (Riboflavin)**, **$\text{B}_6$ (PLP)**, **$\text{B}_9$ (Folate)**, and **$\text{C}$ (Ascorbic Acid)** undergo rapid **UV/fluorescent photodegradation** ($20\%\text{–}60\%$ loss within $2\text{ hours}$ of room-light exposure), while **Vitamin $\text{C}$, Vitamin $\text{B}_6$, `iPTH`, `Calcitonin`, and `Osteocalcin`** undergo rapid thermal/proteolytic degradation at room temperature. **Mandatory Protocol:** Wrap tubes in **aluminum foil / amber transport sleeves immediately upon venipuncture**, chill on ice ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$), centrifuge in a refrigerated centrifuge, and freeze aliquots at $-20^\circ\text{C}$ if not analyzed within $2\text{ hours}$. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes under CPA 2019.*
