## 2. Pre-Analytical Chronobiology, Phlebotomy Protocol & Rejection Criteria

### 2.1 Menstrual Cycle Chronobiology & Diurnal Phlebotomy Timing Rules
1. **Female Baseline Ovarian Reserve & PCOS Profile (`FSH`, `LH`, `E2`, `17-OHP`, Androgens):**
   - **Mandatory Sampling Window:** Strictly collect between **Day 2 and Day 4 of the Menstrual Cycle** (Early Follicular Phase; Day 1 defined as the first day of bright red, full menstrual flow).
   - **Chronobiological Rationale:** Baseline suppression of inhibin B and estradiol permits uninhibited pituitary gonadotropin secretion. Drawing past Day 5 risks physiologic rise in Estradiol ($> 80\text{ pg/mL}$) which exerts negative feedback suppression on FSH, masking subtle elevations characteristic of diminished ovarian reserve. Furthermore, luteal corpus luteum secretion of `17-OHP` invalidates screening for non-classical congenital adrenal hyperplasia.
2. **Ovulation Confirmation Protocol (`Progesterone`):**
   - **Mandatory Sampling Window:** Draw on **Day 21 of a standard 28-day cycle** (specifically, **mid-luteal phase; 7 days prior to anticipated next menses**). For patients with irregular cycle lengths ($N\text{ days}$), calculate sampling day as $N - 7\text{ days}$ (e.g., Day 28 for a 35-day cycle). In amenorrheic women, perform random sampling alongside Serum hCG.
3. **Male Hypogonadism & Androgen Protocol (`Testosterone`, `Free T`, `SHBG`, `LH`, `FSH`):**
   - **Diurnal Phlebotomy Window:** Strictly collect in the **fasting state between $07:00\text{ AM and }10:00\text{ AM}$**. Serum testosterone exhibits an acute early morning circadian peak driven by nocturnal pulsatile LH secretion. Afternoon draws demonstrate an unstandardized $20\%\text{–}40\%$ drop, leading to false-positive hypogonadism diagnoses.
   - **Mandatory Confirmation:** Endocrine Society guidelines require confirmation of low morning testosterone on **two separate morning draws at least 1–2 weeks apart**, outside periods of acute intercurrent febrile or systemic illness.
4. **Hyperprolactinemia Screening Protocol (`Prolactin`):**
   - **Rest & Stress Protocol:** Patient must have awakened at least $2\text{ to }3\text{ hours}$ prior to sampling to avoid residual sleep-peak elevations. Prior to venipuncture, enforce **30 minutes of seated, quiet relaxation** in a low-stimulus environment.
   - **Physical Pre-Conditions:** Strict prohibition of breast examination, nipple stimulation, sexual intercourse, and intense exercise for $48\text{ hours}$ pre-test. In needle-phobic patients, insert an indwelling IV catheter with a saline lock, allowing 30 minutes rest before drawing blood without venipuncture stress.

- **Oral Contraceptive Pills (OCPs) & Estrogen Therapies:** Must be discontinued for at least **4 to 6 weeks** prior to basal HPG evaluation. OCPs suppress pituitary gonadotropins, cause multi-cystic ovarian involution, and increase hepatic SHBG by $200\%\text{–}400\%$, yielding falsely depressed Free Androgen Index.
- **Spironolactone & Cyproterone Acetate:** Discontinue for **4 to 6 weeks** due to potent peripheral androgen receptor blockade and alterations in steroidogenesis.
- **Metformin:** Document administration; although safe to continue during fertility evaluation, metformin reduces hyperinsulinemia-mediated ovarian androgen hypersecretion.
- **Dopamine D2 Receptor Antagonists (Gastroprokinetics & Psychotropics):** Domperidone, Levosulpiride, Metoclopramide, Haloperidol, and Risperidone trigger profound iatrogenic hyperprolactinemia ($50\text{–}250\text{ ng/mL}$). Withhold for **72 hours** under prescribing physician consent prior to testing.
- **High-Dose Biotin (Vitamin $\text{B}_7$):** Must be discontinued for **48 to 72 hours** prior to sampling.

Draw Tube #3 (after blood culture and sodium citrate). Allow complete upright clotting for 30 minutes at room temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$). ## 3. Analytical Architecture, Reaction Physics & Interference Elimination

### 3.1 Chemiluminescence Reaction Physics (Snibe Maglumi X3 Flash-CLIA)
1. **Peptide & Glycoprotein Hormones (`LH`, `FSH`, `PRL`, `AMH`, `SHBG`) — 2-Site Non-Competitive Sandwich Assay:**
   - **Solid Phase Capture:** Monoclonal antibody tagged with Fluorescein Isothiocyanate (FITC) binds specifically to the target hormone epitope. Anti-FITC paramagnetic microbeads ($2.8\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ diameter) are added to capture the immunocomplex via high-affinity immune recognition.
   - **Chemiluminescent Tracer:** Monoclonal antibody directed against a distinct non-overlapping epitope is labeled with Amino-Butyl-Ethyl-Isoluminol (`ABEI`).
   - **Signal Generation:** After automated magnetic separation and washing (removing unbound matrix components), Starter Reagents (Starter 1: $\text{NaOH} + \text{H}_2\text{O}_2$; Starter 2: Catalytic activator) are injected. Direct chemical oxidation of ABEI triggers rapid flash chemiluminescence at $425\text{ nm}$ peaking in $< 3\text{ seconds}$. Relative Light Units (`RLU`) measured by the photomultiplier tube are **directly proportional** to analyte concentration.
2. **Steroid Hormones (`E2`, `P4`, `Total Testosterone`, `17-OHP`, `DHEA-S`) — Competitive Displacement Flash-CLIA:**
   - **Displacement Chemistry:** Endogenous steroids tightly bound to transport proteins (SHBG and Corticosteroid-Binding Globulin `CBG`) are released by an acidic/surfactant displacement cocktail containing 8-anilino-1-naphthalenesulfonic acid (`ANS`) and danazol.
   - **Competitive Binding:** Liberated endogenous steroid competes with a fixed quantity of ABEI-conjugated steroid derivative for limited binding sites on specific purified monoclonal anti-steroid antibodies immobilized on magnetic microbeads.
   - **Signal Generation:** Unbound fraction is washed away under magnetic field. Emitted light at $425\text{ nm}$ is **inversely proportional** to endogenous steroid concentration, quantified against a calibrated 6-point logistic master calibration curve.

### 3.2 Glycoprotein Hormone $\alpha$-Subunit Cross-Reactivity Shield
- Human LH, FSH, TSH, and hCG belong to the cystine-knot glycoprotein hormone family, sharing an **identical 92-amino acid $\alpha$-subunit**. Cross-reactivity in single-antibody assays leads to catastrophic clinical misdiagnoses (e.g., cross-reaction between elevated hCG in pregnancy with LH/FSH assays).
- **Assay Specificity Architecture:** The Maglumi X3 dual-monoclonal sandwich architecture utilizes antibodies targeting unique, conformational epitopes on the divergent $\beta$-subunits:
  - $\beta\text{-LH}$ ($121\text{ amino acids}$), $\beta\text{-FSH}$ ($111\text{ amino acids}$), $\beta\text{-TSH}$ ($118\text{ amino acids}$), and $\beta\text{-hCG}$ ($145\text{ amino acids}$).
  - Cross-reactivity testing per **CLSI EP07-Ed3** with supra-physiological concentrations confirms analytical isolation:
    - Pure Recombinant $\text{hCG}$ at $250,000\text{ mIU/mL}$: Cross-reactivity in LH assay is $< 0.03\%$; in FSH assay $< 0.01\%$.
    - Pituitary $\text{TSH}$ at $1,000\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$: Cross-reactivity in LH assay is $< 0.01\%$; in FSH assay $< 0.01\%$.
    - Pituitary $\text{FSH}$ at $1,000\text{ mIU/mL}$: Cross-reactivity in LH assay is $< 0.02\%$.

### 3.3 Free Androgen Index (FAI) & Vermeulen Law-of-Mass-Action Calculated Free Testosterone
1. **Free Androgen Index (`FAI`):**
   $$\text{FAI} = \left[ \frac{\text{Total Testosterone (nmol/L)}}{\text{SHBG (nmol/L)}} \right] \times 100$$
   *(Note: If Total Testosterone is measured in $\text{ng/dL}$, convert to $\text{nmol/L}$ by multiplying by $0.0347$)*.
2. **Vermeulen Iterative Mass-Action Algorithm (`cFT`):**
   Automated inside the Sawariya Diagnostic Lab LIMS. Total circulating testosterone exists in three fractions: SHBG-bound ($60\%\text{–}70\%$, high affinity), Albumin-bound ($30\%\text{–}40\%$, low affinity), and Free Unbound Testosterone ($1\%\text{–}2\%$). Solved via quadratic equilibrium:
   $$[T_{\text{free}}]^2 \cdot K_t \cdot K_a \cdot [Alb] + [T_{\text{free}}] \cdot \left\{ 1 + K_a [Alb] + K_t [SHBG] - K_t [TT] \right\} - [TT] = 0$$
   - Association constant for SHBG ($K_t$): $1.0 \times 10^9\text{ L/mol}$.
   - Association constant for Albumin ($K_a$): $3.6 \times 10^4\text{ L/mol}$.
   - Serum Albumin: Co-measured, or default set to $4.3\text{ g/dL}$ ($6.2 \times 10^{-4}\text{ mol/L}$).

### 3.4 Analytical Interferences & Mitigations (Hook Effect, Biotin, Macroprolactin)
- **High-Dose Hook Effect Mitigation (Giant Prolactinoma):** In giant pituitary prolactinomas ($\text{PRL} > 5,000\text{ ng/mL}$), simultaneous binding of excess antigen to capture and tracer antibodies saturates both independently, yielding falsely low RLU and falsely reporting prolactin as normal or mildly elevated ($40\text{–}100\text{ ng/mL}$).
  - *SOP Hard-Lock:* Any specimen from a patient with a confirmed pituitary macroadenoma ($> 10\text{ mm}$ on MRI) or visual field defect presenting with paradoxical normal/mild PRL must be reflexively re-tested at a **$1:100$ automated saline dilution**.
- **Polyethylene Glycol (PEG 6000) Macroprolactin Screen:** $15\%\text{–}25\%$ of hyperprolactinemic samples represent benign Macroprolactin (monomeric prolactin complexed with IgG autoantibodies, molecular weight $> 150\text{ kDa}$).
  - *Bench Procedure:* Add $200\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ serum to $200\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of $25\%$ aqueous PEG 6000. Vortex 60 seconds, incubate at $4^\circ\text{C}$ for 15 minutes, centrifuge at $10,000 \times g$ for 10 minutes. Measure monomeric prolactin in the clear supernatant and multiply by 2.
  - *Post-PEG Recovery %:* $\text{Recovery} = [(\text{Supernatant PRL} \times 2) \div \text{Native PRL}] \times 100\%$.
    - $\text{Recovery} > 60\%$: Confirms true monomeric hyperprolactinemia.
    - $\text{Recovery} < 40\%$: Macroprolactinemia predominant; suppresses inappropriate pituitary imaging.
    - $\text{Recovery } 40\%\text{–}60\%$: Equivocal; correlate with clinical symptomatology.
- **Biotin Interference Architecture:** Maglumi X3 Flash-CLIA utilizes anti-FITC magnetic beads rather than streptavidin-biotin architecture, demonstrating complete analytical immunity to physiological and therapeutic biotin up to $1,500\text{ ng/mL}$.

### 3.5 Bench Verification Protocols (`CLSI EP15-A3`, `EP06-Ed2`, `EP17-A2`)
1. **Precision & Trueness Verification (`CLSI EP15-A3` $5\times 5$ Design):**
   - Assayed over 5 consecutive operating days, running 5 replicates per run across 3 concentration levels (low, normal, high).
   - Within-run variance $s_r^2 = MS_{\text{within}}$ and between-day variance $s_{\text{day}}^2 = (MS_{\text{between}} - MS_{\text{within}}) / 5$. Total intermediate precision $s_{\text{total}} = \sqrt{s_r^2 + s_{\text{day}}^2}$.
   - Verification Acceptance: $s_{\text{total}} \le \sigma_{\text{claim}} \cdot \sqrt{\chi^2_{0.95, 20} / 20}$, where $\chi^2_{0.95, 20} = 31.41$.
2. **Linearity Verification (`CLSI EP06-Ed2` Polynomial Analysis):**
   - Evaluated using 7 equidistant admixtures prepared by gravimetrically mixing low and high human serum pools.
   - Tested using first-, second-, and third-order orthogonal polynomials. Non-linearity is deemed clinically insignificant if the maximum difference between linear and polynomial regression fits satisfies $\Delta = \lvert Y_{\text{poly}} - Y_{\text{linear}} \rvert \le 0.5 \times \text{TE}_a$.
3. **Detection Limits (`CLSI EP17-A2` LoB, LoD, LoQ):**
   - Limit of Blank (`LoB`) established from 60 zero-analyte matrix blanks: $\text{LoB} = \mu_{\text{blank}} + 1.645 \cdot \sigma_{\text{blank}}$.
   - Limit of Detection (`LoD`) from 60 low-level native serum samples: $\text{LoD} = \text{LoB} + 1.645 \cdot \sigma_{\text{low}}$.
   - Limit of Quantitation (`LoQ`) verified as the lowest concentration achieving total $CV_A \le 15.0\%$.

### 3.6 Calibration Hierarchy, RFID Master Curve & Mathematical Modeling
1. **10-Point Master Calibration Curve via RFID Tag:** Each Snibe Maglumi X3 reagent integral incorporates an integrated Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) microchip encoded with a factory-derived 10-point master calibration curve established against WHO International Standards or NIST certified reference materials.
2. **Active 2-Point Recalibration Protocol:** The bench technologist performs an active 2-point recalibration (running calibrator low and calibrator high in duplicate) under three defined operational conditions:
   - When introducing a new reagent integral or new lot number.
   - At mandatory 28-day calibration expiration intervals.
   - Following preventive maintenance, fluidic replacement, optical realignment, or persistent multi-rule Westgard IQC failure.
3. **Mathematical Logistic Curve Fitting:**
   - **4-Parameter Logistic (4PL) Model for Sandwich Assays (`LH`, `FSH`, `PRL`, `AMH`, `SHBG`):**
     $$y = D + \frac{A - D}{1 + \left(\frac{x}{C}\right)^B}$$
     where $y$ is Relative Light Units (RLU), $x$ is analyte concentration, $A$ is the minimum asymptotic response (background zero-signal), $D$ is the maximum asymptotic response, $C$ is the inflection point (concentration at midpoint response), and $B$ is the Hill slope factor.
   - **Logit-Log Inverse Transformation for Competitive Steroids (`E2`, `P4`, `Total T`, `17-OHP`, `DHEA-S`):**
     $$\text{logit}(y/y_0) = \ln\left[\frac{y/y_0}{1 - (y/y_0)}\right] = \alpha + \beta \ln(x)$$
     where $y_0$ represents maximum tracer binding in the absence of unlabelled competitor. Active recalibration slope factor must fall within $0.90 \le \beta / \beta_{\text{master}} \le 1.10$.

### 5.3 PCOS Diagnostic Matrix (Rotterdam 2023 International Evidence-Based Guidelines)
According to the 2023 International Evidence-Based Guidelines for the Assessment and Management of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), diagnosis in adults requires **at least 2 of the following 3 features** after mandatory exclusion of competing etiologies (thyroid disease via `TSH`, hyperprolactinemia via `PRL`, non-classical CAH via `8 AM Follicular 17-OHP`, and Cushing's syndrome):
1. **Ovulatory Dysfunction:** Oligo-amenorrhea (menstrual cycles $< 21\text{ days}$ or $> 35\text{ days}$, or $< 8\text{ cycles/year}$) or luteal phase defect (Mid-Luteal Progesterone $< 3.0\text{ ng/mL}$).
2. **Clinical and/or Biochemical Hyperandrogenism:**
   - Hirsutism (modified Ferriman-Gallwey score $\ge 4\text{–}6$ depending on ethnicity), female-pattern alopecia, or severe cystic acne.
   - Biochemical hyperandrogenism demonstrated by elevated **Calculated Free Testosterone (`cFT`)**, elevated **Free Androgen Index (`FAI > 4.5%`)**, or elevated Total Testosterone. If normal, assess `DHEA-S` and `Androstenedione`.
3. **Polycystic Ovarian Morphology (`PCOM`) on Ultrasound OR Elevated Serum `AMH`:**
   - High-resolution transvaginal ultrasound showing $\ge 20\text{ follicles}$ per ovary ($2\text{–}9\text{ mm}$) and/or ovarian volume $\ge 10\text{ mL}$ (in the absence of dominant follicles $> 10\text{ mm}$ or corpus luteum).
   - **2023 Guideline Landmark Update:** In adult females ($\ge 8\text{ years}$ post-menarche), elevated Serum **$\text{AMH} > 4.5\text{ ng/mL}$ ($> 32.0\text{ pmol/L}$)** can formally substitute for ultrasound PCOM!
   - *Adolescent Strict Rule:* In adolescents, both hyperandrogenism AND ovulatory dysfunction are required for diagnosis. Ultrasound PCOM and serum AMH must NOT be used due to high physiological follicle density in normal puberty.

#### **5.3.1 The Four Rotterdam 2023 PCOS Phenotypes & Clinical Management**
- **Phenotype A (Classic / Full Syndrome):** Hyperandrogenism + Ovulatory Dysfunction + PCOM/Elevated AMH. Highest metabolic risk, profound insulin resistance, dyslipidemia, and metabolic syndrome.
- **Phenotype B (Non-PCO / Neuroendocrine):** Hyperandrogenism + Ovulatory Dysfunction, normal ovarian morphology and normal AMH. Driven primarily by altered GnRH pulse frequency with elevated LH:FSH ratio $> 2:1$.
- **Phenotype C (Ovulatory PCOS):** Hyperandrogenism + PCOM/Elevated AMH, regular ovulatory menstrual cycles. Milder metabolic risk profile, fertility generally preserved.
- **Phenotype D (Non-Hyperandrogenic PCOS):** Ovulatory Dysfunction + PCOM/Elevated AMH, normal clinical and biochemical androgens. Mildest phenotype; requires rigorous exclusion of hypothalamic amenorrhea.

#### **5.3.2 Ethnic Cutoffs for Hirsutism & Long-Term Metabolic Surveillance**
- **Ethnic Stratification for Modified Ferriman-Gallwey (mFG) Score:**
  - South Asian (including Indian / Haryanvi cohort at Sawariya Diagnostic Lab): Pathological hirsutism defined as $\text{mFG} \ge 6$.
  - Middle Eastern / Mediterranean: Pathological threshold $\text{mFG} \ge 8$.
  - East Asian: Pathological threshold $\text{mFG} \ge 4$.
- **Mandatory Cardiometabolic Surveillance Protocol (Rotterdam 2023):**
  - All diagnosed PCOS patients must undergo baseline and annual fasting lipid profiles (total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides).
  - Baseline 75g 2-hour Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT) regardless of BMI; repeat every 1–2 years if high risk or prediabetic.
  - Blood pressure and BMI monitoring at every clinical consultation.
  - Sleep apnea screening (STOP-Bang questionnaire) in obese/overweight phenotypes.

## 6. Six Comprehensive Clinical Vignettes & Diagnostic Decisional Trees

### Case 1: Lean PCOS in a 24-Year-Old Female
- **Clinical Presentation:** 24-year-old unmarried female presenting with oligomenorrhea (cycle length $45\text{–}65\text{ days}$) and refractory facial acne. BMI is $20.8\text{ kg/m}^2$, blood pressure $114/72\text{ mmHg}$. No clinical acanthosis nigricans.
- **Laboratory Panel (Day 3 Early Follicular, 08:30 AM Draw):**
  - Serum LH: $14.8\text{ mIU/mL}$ (High; reference $2.4\text{–}12.6$)
  - Serum FSH: $4.9\text{ mIU/mL}$ (Reference $3.5\text{–}12.5$) $\rightarrow$ **$\text{LH:FSH Ratio} = 3.02 : 1$**
  - Serum Estradiol: $42.0\text{ pg/mL}$ (Reference $20\text{–}85$)
  - Serum Prolactin: $12.4\text{ ng/mL}$ (Reference $4.8\text{–}23.3$)
  - Serum Total Testosterone: $54.0\text{ ng/dL}$ (Upper normal; reference $10\text{–}55$)
  - Serum SHBG: $36.0\text{ nmol/L}$ (Reference $26\text{–}110$) $\rightarrow$ **$\text{FAI} = 5.2\%$** (Elevated $> 4.5\%$)
  - Serum AMH: $8.4\text{ ng/mL}$ (Markedly elevated; reference $1.5\text{–}4.5$)
  - Serum 8 AM 17-OHP: $0.75\text{ ng/mL}$ (Normal $< 1.5$)
  - Serum TSH: $1.85\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$ (Normal $0.4\text{–}4.2$)
- **Audit & Interpretation:** LH:FSH ratio $> 3:1$ reflects neuroendocrine GnRH hyperpulsatility driving theca cell androgen production. Marked elevation of AMH ($8.4\text{ ng/mL}$) confirms high pre-antral follicle density, fulfilling 2023 Rotterdam criteria without requiring ultrasound. 17-OHP and TSH rule out NCCAH and hypothyroidism.
- **Diagnostic Conclusion:** **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (Phenotype B — Lean Neuroendocrine PCOS)**.
- **Clinical Follow-up & Treatment Monitoring:** Prescribed combination myo-inositol / D-chiro-inositol ($40:1$ ratio, $2,000\text{ mg/day}$) and cyclic micronized progesterone. Repeat Day 3 panel at 6 months demonstrated normalization of the LH:FSH ratio to $1.4:1$, reduction in AMH to $5.2\text{ ng/mL}$, and restoration of regular $32\text{–}34\text{ day}$ ovulatory cycles.

### Case 2: Severe Obese PCOS with Insulin Resistance & Metabolic Syndrome
- **Clinical Presentation:** 29-year-old female presenting with secondary amenorrhea of 8 months duration, severe hirsutism (Ferriman-Gallwey score 16), and inability to conceive for 3 years. BMI is $35.4\text{ kg/m}^2$, waist circumference $104\text{ cm}$. Marked velvety hyperpigmentation (acanthosis nigricans) over the neck and axillae.
- **Laboratory Panel (Random Day Sampling due to Amenorrhea, 08:00 AM Fasting):**
  - Serum Beta-hCG: $< 1.2\text{ mIU/mL}$ (Negative)
  - Serum LH: $7.2\text{ mIU/mL}$ (Normal)
  - Serum FSH: $5.8\text{ mIU/mL}$ (Normal) $\rightarrow$ $\text{LH:FSH Ratio} = 1.24 : 1$ (Normal)
  - Serum Total Testosterone: $52.0\text{ ng/dL}$ (Borderline; reference $10\text{–}55$)
  - Serum SHBG: **$11.2\text{ nmol/L}$** (Markedly suppressed; reference $26\text{–}110$)
  - **Free Androgen Index (`FAI`):** **$16.1\%$** (Critically elevated; reference $< 4.5\%$)
  - **Calculated Free Testosterone (`cFT`):** **$12.8\text{ pg/mL}$** (Markedly elevated; reference $1.0\text{–}6.5$)
  - Serum AMH: $10.6\text{ ng/mL}$ (Markedly elevated; reference $1.5\text{–}4.5$)
  - Fasting Glucose: $118\text{ mg/dL}$; Fasting Insulin: $36.0\text{ }\mu\text{IU/mL}$ $\rightarrow$ **$\text{HOMA-IR} = 10.49$**
  - Serum 8 AM 17-OHP: $0.90\text{ ng/mL}$ (Normal)
- **Audit & Interpretation:** Normal LH:FSH ratio demonstrates that LH elevation is NOT required for PCOS diagnosis. Profound hyperinsulinemia ($10.49\text{ HOMA-IR}$) potently suppresses hepatic SHBG transcription. Relying solely on Total Testosterone would have missed severe biochemical hyperandrogenism, unmasked by FAI ($16.1\%$) and Vermeulen Free Testosterone ($12.8\text{ pg/mL}$).
- **Diagnostic Conclusion:** **Severe Insulin-Resistant PCOS (Phenotype A) with Metabolic Syndrome**.
- **Clinical Follow-up & Treatment Monitoring:** Commenced on intensive lifestyle modification, structured aerobic resistance exercise, and Metformin Extended-Release titrated to $1,500\text{ mg/day}$. ### 10.1 Dual-Language Patient Advisory & Preparation Guide (English & हिन्दी)

#### **English Patient Guidance:**
- **Why Timing Matters:** Reproductive hormones change significantly every single week of your menstrual cycle. Tests for baseline fertility, ovarian egg reserve, and PCOS (`LH`, `FSH`, `Estradiol`, `AMH`, `17-OHP`) must be drawn strictly between **Day 2 and Day 4 of your period** (Day 1 is your first day of full bleeding). Testing later in the month will produce inaccurate results.
- **Progesterone (Ovulation Test):** Must be drawn on **Day 21** of a 28-day cycle, or exactly 7 days before your next expected period.
- **Male Hormone Tests:** Testosterone levels peak in the early morning and drop sharply by the afternoon. Men must have blood drawn **fasting between 07:00 AM and 10:00 AM**.
- **Prolactin Precautions:** Sit quietly and relax for **30 minutes** before blood collection. Do not perform heavy exercise, breast self-examination, or engage in sexual intimacy for 48 hours before testing, as these temporarily cause false-high prolactin results.
- **Acidity & Stomach Medicines:** Common gastric medicines containing **Domperidone or Levosulpiride** artificially raise prolactin levels. Inform your doctor and lab staff about all medications you take.
