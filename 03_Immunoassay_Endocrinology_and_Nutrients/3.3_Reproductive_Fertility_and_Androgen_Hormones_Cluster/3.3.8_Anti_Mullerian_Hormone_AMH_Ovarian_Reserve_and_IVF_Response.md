## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `CLSI` Verification, Calibration & Six Sigma QC

1. **Method Principle (2-Site Non-Competitive Sandwich Flash-CLIA):**
   - Patient serum ($40\text{ }\mu\text{L}$) is incubated in a complement-inhibiting assay buffer with **paramagnetic microbeads coated with monoclonal anti-AMH antibody (pro-region/mature interface)** and **ABEI-labeled monoclonal anti-AMH detection antibody**.
   - **Direct Signal:** Measured `RLU` at $425\text{ nm}$ is **directly proportional** to $\text{AMH}$ concentration.
2. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`), `LoQ` & Auto-Dilution (`CRR`):**
   - **Limit of Detection (`LoD`):** $0.02\text{ ng/mL}$ ($0.14\text{ pmol/L}$) \| **Limit of Quantitation (`LoQ` — Critical for POI / Menopause Prediction):** **$0.06\text{ ng/mL}$** ($0.43\text{ pmol/L}$ at $CV \le 15\%$).
   - **Linear `AMR`:** **$0.06 \text{ – } 24.00\text{ ng/mL}$** ($0.43 \text{ – } 171.4\text{ pmol/L}$).
   - **Clinical Reportable Range (`CRR` with $1:10$ Auto-Dilution — Pediatric Male & Granulosa Cell Tumor):** Up to **$240.0\text{ ng/mL}$** ($1,714\text{ pmol/L}$).
3. **Calibration & Six Sigma IQC:**
   - **2-Point Recalibration** every $28\text{ days}$ or reagent lot change.
   - **Daily 3-Level IQC:** Level 1 ($\sim 0.80\text{ ng/mL}$ — DOR threshold), Level 2 ($\sim 2.50\text{ ng/mL}$ — Normal reserve), Level 3 ($\sim 7.50\text{ ng/mL}$ — PCOS/OHSS range). Westgard rules: **$1_{3s}$ / $2_{2s}$ / $R_{4s}$**.

## 4. EFLM Biological Variation, Reference Change Value (`RCV %`), Six Sigma (`TEa`) & Measurement Uncertainty (`MU`)

| Metric | International Reference Value | Clinical & Quality Governance Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Within-Subject Biological Variation ($CV_I\%$)** | **$12.0\%$** | Lowest intra-cycle variation of all female reproductive hormones (vs $22.5\%$ for `E2` and $23\%$ for `LH`). |
| **Between-Subject Biological Variation ($CV_G\%$)** | **$48.0\%$** | Wide inter-individual spread reflecting genetic primordial follicle endowment and age. |
| **Index of Individuality ($\text{II} = CV_I / CV_G$)** | **$0.25$** | **Marked Individuality ($\text{II} \ll 0.6$):** Serial tracking within the same woman is highly sensitive to chemotherapy/surgical ovarian injury. |
| **Desirable Analytical Imprecision ($CV_A\%$)** | **$\le 5.0\%$** *(Lab Achieved on Maglumi X3: $\sim 3.2\%$)* | Yields **Sigma Metric $\ge 5.2\sigma$** at $\text{TE}_a = 18\%$. |
| **Total Allowable Error ($\text{TE}_a\%$)** | **$\pm 18.0\%$** *(EFLM / RCPA)* | Maximum permissible total analytical error. |
| **Target Expanded Measurement Uncertainty ($U\%$, $k=2$)** | **$\le 10.0\%$** ($95\%$ confidence level) | Essential around the $1.0\text{ ng/mL}$ Bologna POSEIDON and $3.5\text{ ng/mL}$ PCOS cutoffs. |
| **Reference Change Value (`RCV %` at $95\%$ / $99\%$)** | **$\pm 35.1\%$ ($95\%$) / $\pm 46.3\%$ ($99\%$)** | A drop $> 35\%$ after ovarian cystectomy/endometrioma surgery or chemotherapy reflects true loss of follicular reserve. |
| **Minimum Retesting Interval (`MRI`)** | **$6 \text{ to } 12\text{ months}$** *(or $3\text{m}$ post-OCP stop / surgery)* | Do not repeat $\text{AMH}$ monthly during routine fertility workups. |

## 7. In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics, Clinical Decision Thresholds & Pathophysiology

- **Biological Half-Life ($t_{1/2}$):** Approximately **$27 \text{ to } 36\text{ hours}$** in human circulation (confirmed by post-bilateral-oophorectomy kinetic decay studies); reaches complete clearance within $5\text{–}7\text{ days}$ after surgical removal of ovaries or granulosa cell tumor.
- **Why $\text{AMH}$ is Elevated $2\text{ to }3\times$ in `PCOS`:** Women with PCOS have both a **$2\text{–}3\times$ larger pool of arrested $2\text{–}8\text{ mm}$ small antral follicles** AND **$75\times$ higher intrinsic $\text{AMH}$ production per granulosa cell**. High intra-ovarian $\text{AMH}$ inhibits `FSH`-induced **Aromatase (`CYP19A1`)**, directly causing follicular arrest and anovulation.

## 10. Master LIMS Copy-Paste Report Block (Physician + Patient Summary + Medico-Legal Shield)

> **SERUM ANTI-MÜLLERIAN HORMONE (`AMH`) — OVARIAN RESERVE, `PCOS 2023` & `IVF` INTERPRETATION**  
> - **Cycle-Independent Ovarian Reserve Marker:** Secreted by granulosa cells of $2\text{–}8\text{ mm}$ pre-antral/small antral follicles; can be measured on **any day of the menstrual cycle**.  
> - **Adult Female Clinical Decision Tiers (`1.0 ng/mL = 7.143 pmol/L`):**  
>   - **$> 3.5 \text{ – } 4.0\text{ ng/mL}$:** **High Ovarian Reserve / `PCOS` Phenotype** (2023 International PCOS Guideline adult criterion; high **`OHSS` risk** in IVF).  
>   - **$1.5 \text{ – } 3.5\text{ ng/mL}$:** **Optimal Ovarian Reserve** (Good expected IVF response).  
>   - **$1.0 \text{ – } 1.49\text{ ng/mL}$:** **Low-Normal / Borderline Reserve** (Correlate with age and ultrasound `AFC`).  
>   - **$0.5 \text{ – } 0.99\text{ ng/mL}$:** **Diminished Ovarian Reserve (`DOR`)** (Reduced IVF oocyte yield; individualized stimulation protocol advised).  
>   - **$< 0.5\text{ ng/mL}$:** **Severely Diminished Ovarian Reserve** (or perimenopausal transition).  
> - **🛡️ Mandatory Fertility Counseling Shield (`ASRM` / `ESHRE`):** **$\text{AMH}$ measures egg QUANTITY (for IVF retrieval), NOT egg QUALITY or natural fertility!** A young woman with low $\text{AMH}$ who ovulates regularly has the **same monthly chance of natural pregnancy** as an age-matched woman with normal $\text{AMH}$. Reversible $20\%\text{–}30\%$ suppression occurs with **Oral Contraceptive Pills (`OCPs`)**, GnRH agonists, pregnancy, and severe Vitamin D deficiency.  
> - **Plain-Language Patient Summary:** *AMH is a hormone that estimates the size of your remaining ovarian egg pool ('ovarian reserve'). While it helps fertility doctors choose the safest medication dose for IVF, it does NOT measure the health or quality of your eggs—which depends mainly on your age.*  
> - **🛡️ Medico-Legal & NABL ISO 15189:2022 Shield:** *Measured by 2-Site Sandwich Flash-CLIA traceable to WHO 1st IS 16/190. Always correlate with Transvaginal Ultrasound Antral Follicle Count (`AFC`); not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
