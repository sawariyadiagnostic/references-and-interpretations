## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Dimeric $\alpha\text{–}\beta_A / \alpha\text{–}\beta_B$ Specificity & Six Sigma QC

1. **Method Principle (2-Site Dimeric Sandwich Immunoassay with Controlled $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ Methionine Oxidation):**
   - **Molecular Structure:** Biologically active Inhibins are heterodimers consisting of an identical **$18\text{ kDa }\alpha\text{-subunit}$** linked to a distinct **$14\text{ kDa }\beta\text{-subunit}$**:
     - **`Inhibin A` ($\alpha + \beta_A$):** Secreted by the **Dominant Pre-Ovulatory Follicle, Corpus Luteum, and Placental Syncytiotrophoblast**.
     - **`Inhibin B` ($\alpha + \beta_B$):** Secreted by **Small Pre-Antral/Early Antral Follicles in Females** and exclusively by **Testicular Sertoli Cells in Males**.
   - **Assay Reaction:** Capture antibody binds the **$\beta_A$** (for `Inhibin A`) or **$\beta_B$** (for `Inhibin B`) subunit (frequently after controlled $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ oxidation of $\beta$-subunit methionine residues to methionine sulfoxide to expose linear high-affinity epitopes), while the detection antibody binds the **$\alpha$-subunit**.
   - **Cross-Reactivity Shield:** Cross-reactivity between `Inhibin A` and `Inhibin B`, or with **Activin A ($\beta_A\beta_A$)** and **Activin B ($\beta_B\beta_B$)**, is **$< 0.1\%$** because free $\beta$-dimers (Activins) lack the $\alpha$-subunit required by the detection antibody! *(Note: In Ovarian Granulosa Cell Tumors, high concentrations of free monomeric $\alpha$-subunit precursors [`Pro-\alpha C`] are also secreted alongside dimeric `Inhibin B` and `Inhibin A`).*
2. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`), `LoQ` & Auto-Dilution (`CRR`):**
   - **`Inhibin A` Linear `AMR`:** **$2.0 \text{ – } 1,000.0\text{ pg/mL}$** \| **With $1:10$ Auto-Dilution (`CRR`):** Up to **$10,000\text{ pg/mL}$**.
   - **`Inhibin B` Linear `AMR`:** **$5.0 \text{ – } 1,000.0\text{ pg/mL}$** \| **With $1:10$ Auto-Dilution (`CRR`):** Up to **$10,000\text{ pg/mL}$**.

## 7. In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics, Clinical Decision Thresholds & Pathophysiology

- **Biological Half-Life ($t_{1/2}$):** Biphasic clearance — fast phase $t_{1/2} \approx 15\text{–}30\text{ minutes}$ and terminal phase $t_{1/2} \approx 1\text{–}2\text{ hours}$. Following complete surgical resection of an Ovarian Granulosa Cell Tumor (or bilateral oophorectomy), serum **`Inhibin B` and `Inhibin A` fall to undetectable levels ($< 5\text{–}10\text{ pg/mL}$) within 24 to 72 hours**!
- **Why Both `Inhibin B` AND `Inhibin A` (+ `AMH`) Should Be Ordered for Ovarian Sex-Cord Stromal Tumors (NCCN / ESGO Guidelines):**
  - While **`Inhibin B`** is the primary secretome of **$90\%$ of Adult & Juvenile Granulosa Cell Tumors** and **Sertoli-Leydig Cell Tumors**, approximately **$5\%\text{–}10\%$ of Granulosa Cell Tumors and Thecomas secrete predominantly `Inhibin A`**, and **$80\%$ of Mucinous Ovarian Epithelial Carcinomas** (where `CA-125` is often falsely normal!) secrete `Inhibin A/B`. Co-monitoring **`Inhibin B` + `Inhibin A` + `AMH` (`3.3.8`)** achieves **$> 98\%$ surveillance sensitivity**!

## 10. Master LIMS Copy-Paste Report Block (Physician + Patient Summary + Medico-Legal Shield)

> **DIMERIC `INHIBIN A` & `INHIBIN B` — ONCOLOGY, FERTILITY & MALE SERTOLI INTERPRETATION**  
> - **Subunit & Organ Specificity:**  
>   - **`Inhibin B` ($\alpha\beta_B$):** Secreted by **Ovarian Granulosa Cells** (small antral follicles, peaking on **Cycle Day 2–4**) in females and **Testicular Sertoli Cells** in males (*males produce ONLY `Inhibin B`, never `Inhibin A`!*).  
>   - **`Inhibin A` ($\alpha\beta_A$):** Secreted by the **Dominant Follicle, Corpus Luteum (Day 21 peak), and Placenta** (elevated to $\sim 1.9\text{ MoM}$ in 2nd-Trimester **Trisomy 21 Down Syndrome** & Pre-Eclampsia).  
> - **Key Clinical Applications:**  
>   1. **Ovarian Sex-Cord Stromal Tumor Marker (`Granulosa Cell Tumor` & `Mucinous Ovarian Cancer`):** In postmenopausal or post-oophorectomy women, normal levels are undetectable (**`Inhibin B < 10 pg/mL`, `Inhibin A < 5 pg/mL`**). Elevations detect primary or recurrent **Granulosa Cell Tumors** ($90\%$ sensitivity; combine with **`AMH`** for $> 98\%$ sensitivity).  
>   2. **Male Infertility (`Obstructive` vs `Non-Obstructive Azoospermia`):** Normal **`Inhibin B` ($\ge 80\text{–}350\text{ pg/mL}$)** with normal `FSH` confirms preserved Sertoli cell spermatogenesis (**Obstructive Azoospermia**), whereas **`Inhibin B < 40–80 pg/mL`** with high `FSH` indicates **Primary Seminiferous Tubule Failure (`NOA`)**.  
>   3. **Pediatric Delayed Puberty:** Basal **`Inhibin B < 35 pg/mL`** in a peripubertal boy differentiates **Congenital Hypogonadotropic Hypogonadism (`CHH`)** from benign Constitutional Delay (`CDGP`).  
> - **Plain-Language Patient Summary:** *Inhibin A and Inhibin B are specialized proteins made by the ovaries (in women), the placenta (during pregnancy), and the sperm-supporting Sertoli cells of the testes (in men). Doctors use them to monitor ovarian reserve, male sperm production, prenatal Down syndrome risk, or specialized ovarian tumors.*  
> - **🛡️ Medico-Legal & NABL ISO 15189:2022 Shield:** *Measured by 2-Site Dimeric Sandwich Immunoassay. Hemolyzed specimens ($> 150\text{ mg/dL}$ Hb) are rejected due to peroxidase interference. Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
