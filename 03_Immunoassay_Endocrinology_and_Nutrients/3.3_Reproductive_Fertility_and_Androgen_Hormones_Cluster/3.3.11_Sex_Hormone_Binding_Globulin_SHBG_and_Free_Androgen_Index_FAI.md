## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `Free Androgen Index (FAI)` Mathematics & Six Sigma QC

1. **Method Principle (2-Site Non-Competitive Sandwich Flash-CLIA):**
   - Patient serum is pre-diluted on-board and incubated with **paramagnetic microbeads coated with monoclonal anti-SHBG antibody** and **ABEI-labeled monoclonal anti-SHBG detection antibody**. Measured `RLU` at $425\text{ nm}$ is **directly proportional** to `SHBG` concentration ($\text{nmol/L}$).
2. **Exact Mathematical Equation for `Free Androgen Index (FAI)` (LIMS Auto-Calculation):**
   - **Rule #1 (Mandatory Molar Unit Conversion Before Division):** Both `Total Testosterone` and `SHBG` **MUST be in $\text{nmol/L}$** before dividing! Dividing `Total Testosterone` in $\text{ng/dL}$ directly by `SHBG` in $\text{nmol/L}$ causes a **$28.84\text{-fold}$ mathematical error**!
     $$\text{Total Testosterone (nmol/L)} = \text{Total Testosterone (ng/dL)} \times 0.0347 \quad \left[\text{or } \text{Total Testosterone (ng/mL)} \times 3.467\right]$$
     $$\text{Free Androgen Index (FAI, Unitless Ratio)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Testosterone (nmol/L)}}{\text{SHBG (nmol/L)}} \right) \times 100$$
   - **Rule #2 (Why `FAI` Is Valid in Females/Children But Mathematically INVALID in Adult Males):**
     - In **females** ($\text{Total T} \approx 0.5\text{–}2.0\text{ nmol/L}$ vs $\text{SHBG} \approx 30\text{–}100\text{ nmol/L}$), `< 5%` of `SHBG` binding sites are occupied by Testosterone, so `FAI` correlates linearly ($r > 0.90$) with measured Free Testosterone and is **endorsed by the 2023 International PCOS Guideline as a primary first-line test for biochemical hyperandrogenism**.
     - In **adult males** ($\text{Total T} \approx 12\text{–}30\text{ nmol/L}$ vs $\text{SHBG} \approx 15\text{–}50\text{ nmol/L}$), Testosterone saturates $40\%\text{–}80\%$ of `SHBG` binding sites, violating the linear approximation! **Never use `FAI` to diagnose male hypogonadism—always use `Vermeulen Calculated Free Testosterone` (`3.3.10`) in males!**
3. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`), `LoQ` & Auto-Dilution (`CRR`):**
   - **Limit of Detection (`LoD`):** $0.5\text{ nmol/L}$ \| **Limit of Quantitation (`LoQ`):** **$1.5\text{ nmol/L}$** ($CV \le 10\%$).
   - **Linear `AMR`:** **$1.5 \text{ – } 200.0\text{ nmol/L}$** \| **With $1:5$ Auto-Dilution (`CRR` — Pregnancy / OCPs):** Up to **$1,000.0\text{ nmol/L}$**.

## 7. In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics, Clinical Decision Thresholds & Pathophysiology

- **Biological Half-Life ($t_{1/2}$):** Approximately **$7\text{ days}$** in human plasma (synthesized by hepatocytes under opposing transcriptional control: **Estrogens and Thyroid Hormone [$\text{T}_3$] stimulate `HNF-4\alpha` to $\uparrow \text{SHBG}$**, whereas **Insulin, Monosaccharides [Fructose/Glucose]-induced hepatic *de novo* lipogenesis, Androgens, and Growth Hormone suppress `HNF-4\alpha` to $\downarrow \text{SHBG}$**).
- **Relative Steroid Binding Affinity Hierarchy of `SHBG`:**
  $$\text{Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)} \ (K_a = 2.7 \times 10^9) > \text{Testosterone} \ (K_a = 1.0 \times 10^9) > \text{Androstenediol} > 17\beta\text{-Estradiol (E2)} \ (K_a = 0.3 \times 10^9) \gg \text{DHEA / Androstenedione (Negligible)}$$
- **Specialized Clinical Utility in Thyroid Hormone Resistance (`RTH-\beta`) vs `TSH`-Secreting Pituitary Adenoma (`TSHoma`):** Because hepatic `SHBG` synthesis is directly stimulated by intracellular thyroid hormone receptor-$\beta$ ($\text{TR}\beta$), **`SHBG` is markedly ELEVATED ($> 120\text{ nmol/L}$) in true thyrotoxicosis and `TSHoma`**, but remains **NORMAL in Pituitary/Peripheral Resistance to Thyroid Hormone (`RTH-\beta`)**!

## 10. Master LIMS Copy-Paste Report Block (Physician + Patient Summary + Medico-Legal Shield)

> **SEX HORMONE-BINDING GLOBULIN (`SHBG`) & FREE ANDROGEN INDEX (`FAI`) — CLINICAL INTERPRETATION**  
> - **Formula & Unit Rule:** $\text{FAI} = \left[\frac{\text{Total Testosterone (nmol/L)}}{\text{SHBG (nmol/L)}}\right] \times 100$, where $\text{Total Testosterone (nmol/L)} = \text{Total Testosterone (ng/dL)} \times 0.0347$.  
> - **In Females (`PCOS 2023` & Metabolic Marker):**  
>   - **Normal Female `FAI`:** **$0.5 \text{ – } 4.5$** \| **Biochemical Hyperandrogenism (`PCOS`):** **`FAI > 4.5 – 5.0`**.  
>   - **Low `SHBG` ($< 26\text{ nmol/L}$):** Serves as an independent biomarker of **hepatic insulin resistance / hyperinsulinemia, PCOS, NAFLD, and hypothyroidism**, increasing unbound Free Testosterone even when Total Testosterone is normal!  
>   - **High `SHBG` ($> 110\text{ nmol/L}$):** Seen with **Oral Contraceptive Pills (`OCPs`)**, pregnancy, hyperthyroidism, or liver cirrhosis.  
> - **In Males:** Do not use `FAI` in adult men; use **`SHBG` + `Total Testosterone`** to compute **Vermeulen Calculated Free Testosterone (`3.3.10`)**.  
> - **Plain-Language Patient Summary:** *SHBG is a protein made by the liver that carries testosterone and estrogen in the blood. When insulin levels are high (as in PCOS or prediabetes), the liver makes less SHBG, leaving more 'free' active testosterone in the blood to cause symptoms like facial hair or acne even when Total Testosterone looks normal.*  
> - **🛡️ Medico-Legal & NABL ISO 15189:2022 Shield:** *Measured by 2-Site Sandwich Flash-CLIA traceable to WHO 2nd IS 08/266. High-dose Biotin ($> 5\text{ mg/day}$) may cause falsely low results. Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
