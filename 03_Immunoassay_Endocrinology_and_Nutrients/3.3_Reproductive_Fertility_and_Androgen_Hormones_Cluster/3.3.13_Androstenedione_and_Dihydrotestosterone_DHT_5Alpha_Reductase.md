## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `Testosterone : DHT` Ratio Mathematics & Six Sigma QC

1. **Method Principles:**
   - **Androstenedione ($\Delta^4$-Androstenedione, `A4`):** Competitive Flash-CLIA on **Snibe MAGLUMI X3** using ABEI-labeled Androstenedione derivative and high-specificity monoclonal anti-Androstenedione antibody ($< 0.5\%$ cross-reactivity with Testosterone, DHEA, and $17\text{-OHP}$).
   - **$5\alpha$-Dihydrotestosterone (`DHT`):** Because `Testosterone` circulates at **$10\times$ higher concentration** than `DHT` in males and shares $98\%$ structural similarity, direct `DHT` immunoassays use **oxidative cleavage of the $\Delta^4$-double bond of Testosterone (`KMnO4` oxidation) or organic solvent extraction** (or **LC-MS/MS**) to eliminate Testosterone cross-reactivity ($< 0.05\%$).
2. **Calculated `Testosterone : DHT` (`T : DHT`) Ratio for $5\alpha$-Reductase Type 2 Deficiency (`SRD5A2`):**
   - **Mandatory Identical-Unit Rule:** Convert both `Total Testosterone` and `DHT` to **`ng/dL`** ($1\text{ ng/dL} = 10\text{ pg/mL}$) BEFORE dividing:
     $$\text{T : DHT Ratio (Unitless)} = \frac{\text{Total Testosterone (ng/dL)}}{\text{DHT (ng/dL)}} = \frac{\text{Total Testosterone (ng/dL)} \times 10}{\text{DHT (pg/mL)}}$$
   - **Pediatric `hCG` Stimulation Protocol for `46,XY DSD`:** In pre-pubertal infants/children (after mini-puberty), basal Testosterone is low ($< 10\text{ ng/dL}$); administer **intramuscular `hCG` ($1,000\text{–}1,500\text{ IU/day}$ for $3\text{ days}$)** and measure **post-`hCG` `Total Testosterone`, `Androstenedione`, and `DHT`** ($24\text{h}$ after final injection).
     - **Post-`hCG` $\text{T : Androstenedione}$ Ratio $< 0.8$:** Diagnostic of **$17\beta$-Hydroxysteroid Dehydrogenase Type 3 (`17\beta-HSD3`) Deficiency**!
     - **Post-`hCG` $\text{T : DHT}$ Ratio $> 20\text{ : }1$** (typically $> 25\text{–}30\text{ : }1$, vs normal $< 12\text{ : }1$): Diagnostic of **$5\alpha$-Reductase Type 2 (`SRD5A2`) Deficiency**!
3. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`), `LoQ` & Six Sigma IQC:**
   - **Androstenedione `AMR`:** **$15.0 \text{ – } 1,000.0\text{ ng/dL}$** ($0.52 \text{ – } 34.9\text{ nmol/L}$).
   - **`DHT` `AMR`:** **$10.0 \text{ – } 2,500.0\text{ pg/mL}$** ($1.0 \text{ – } 250.0\text{ ng/dL}$ \| $0.034 \text{ – } 8.60\text{ nmol/L}$).
   - **Daily 3-Level IQC:** Verified per Westgard **$1_{3s}$ / $2_{2s}$ / $R_{4s}$** rules.

## 7. In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics, Clinical Decision Thresholds & Pathophysiology

- **Gold-Standard Monitoring Pair in Treated Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (`CAH` — `17-OHP` + `Androstenedione`):**
  - While `17-OHP` (`3.3.7`) fluctuates wildly during the day and should be kept mildly elevated ($400\text{–}1,200\text{ ng/dL}$), **Serum `Androstenedione` has a much smoother profile and SHOULD be maintained within the normal age- and sex-specific reference interval**!
  - In **adult males with Classic `CAH`**, measuring `Total Testosterone` alone is misleading because adrenal Testosterone looks "normal" while suppressing pituitary `LH/FSH` and causing infertility; an **$\text{Androstenedione : Testosterone}$ ratio $> 0.5\text{–}1.0$** proves poor `CAH` control and alerts the clinician to screen for **Testicular Adrenal Rest Tumors (`TARTs`)** via scrotal ultrasound!
- **Peripheral Intracrinology of `DHT` & `3\alpha-Androstanediol Glucuronide`:** `DHT` has **$2.5\text{–}3\times$ higher affinity** and **$5\times$ slower dissociation rate** at the intracellular Androgen Receptor than Testosterone. In **Idiopathic Hirsutism** (hirsutism with regular ovulatory cycles and normal `Total/Free Testosterone`), pilosebaceous hair follicles overexpress **$5\alpha$-Reductase**, converting normal circulating `Androstenedione`/`Testosterone` locally into `DHT`.

## 8. Comprehensive Interference Matrix & `ISO 22367` FMEA Risk Management Table

| FMEA Failure Mode | Pre-/Analytical Root Cause | Clinical / Patient Risk (`S × O × D`) | Mandatory Preventive / Corrective Action (`CAPA`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Calculating `T : DHT` Ratio Without Converting `DHT` from `pg/mL` to `ng/dL` ($10\times$ Error)** | Dividing `Total T` ($500\text{ ng/dL}$) by `DHT` ($500\text{ pg/mL}$) yields a ratio of $1.0$ instead of converting $500\text{ pg/mL} = 50\text{ ng/dL} \rightarrow \text{Ratio} = 10.0$! | Complete diagnostic failure in `46,XY DSD` workups (Risk Priority: **CRITICAL**). | Report `DHT` in **BOTH `pg/mL` and `ng/dL`** and hard-code the unit conversion in the LIMS `T:DHT` calculator. |
| **Non-Extracted `DHT` Immunoassay Cross-Reacting with High Male `Testosterone`** | Untreated competitive `DHT` antibodies have $5\%\text{–}10\%$ cross-reactivity with `Testosterone` (which is $10\times$ more abundant!). | Falsely normal `DHT` in a patient with true $5\alpha$-Reductase Deficiency or on `Finasteride` (Risk Priority: **HIGH**). | Always use **oxidative-cleavage / solvent-extraction or `LC-MS/MS`** for `DHT` measurement in pediatric DSD and `Finasteride` monitoring. |

## 10. Master LIMS Copy-Paste Report Block (Physician + Patient Summary + Medico-Legal Shield)

> **ANDROSTENEDIONE (`A4`) & $5\alpha$-DIHYDROTESTOSTERONE (`DHT`) — CLINICAL INTERPRETATION**  
> - **Androstenedione (`A4` — $50\%$ Ovarian / $50\%$ Adrenal in Females):**  
>   - **Second-Tier `PCOS 2023` Marker:** Detects biochemical hyperandrogenism when `Total/Free Testosterone` are borderline; values **$> 350\text{–}500\text{ ng/dL}$** warrant exclusion of **Virilizing Adrenal/Ovarian Tumors** or **Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (`CAH`)**.  
>   - **`CAH` Therapeutic Monitoring:** Co-monitored with **`17-OHP`**; in males with `CAH`, an **$\text{Androstenedione : Testosterone}$ ratio $> 0.5\text{–}1.0$** indicates suboptimal adrenal control and risk of **Testicular Adrenal Rest Tumors (`TARTs`)**. *(Note: Breast cancer drug **Exemestane [`Aromasin`]** cross-reacts to cause falsely high `Androstenedione` in immunoassays!)*  
> - **$5\alpha$-Dihydrotestosterone (`DHT` — Most Potent Tissue Androgen):**  
>   - Synthesized from Testosterone via **$5\alpha$-Reductase (`SRD5A1/2`)** in prostate, skin, and hair follicles ($1\text{ ng/dL} = 10\text{ pg/mL}$).  
>   - **Normal Adult Male `Total T : DHT` Ratio (in `ng/dL`):** **$8\text{ : }1 \text{ to } 16\text{ : }1$**. A post-`hCG` **`T : DHT` ratio $> 20\text{ : }1$** in `46,XY DSD` indicates **$5\alpha$-Reductase Type 2 Deficiency**; in adults, suppressed `DHT` confirms **Finasteride / Dutasteride** efficacy.  
> - **Plain-Language Patient Summary:** *Androstenedione is a precursor hormone from the ovaries and adrenal glands that converts into Testosterone and Estrogen, while DHT is the ultra-active form of Testosterone that acts on hair follicles and the prostate.*  
> - **🛡️ Medico-Legal & NABL ISO 15189:2022 Shield:** *Correlate with morning follicular sampling, medication history (Finasteride, Dutasteride, Exemestane, Biotin), and LC-MS/MS confirmation in pediatric DSD; not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
