### 2.1 Snibe Maglumi X3 Daily Startup & Fluidics Operating Procedure
1. **Physical & Environmental Checks:** Verify room temperature ($20^\circ\text{C} \text{ – } 25^\circ\text{C}$) and relative humidity ($40\% \text{ – } 65\%$). Record values in `SDL/IMM/LOG-01`.
2. **PMT Dark Count Verification:** The photomultiplier tube (PMT) background dark current must be measured before activating reagents. Standard acceptance: $\text{Dark Count} \le 30\text{ RLU}$. If $> 30\text{ RLU}$, execute optical chamber decontamination wash.
3. **Fluidics System Priming:** Prime Starter 1 (Hydrogen Peroxide, $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$) and Starter 2 (Sodium Hydroxide, $\text{NaOH}$) through 3 automated dispense cycles. Inspect transparent tubing for air micro-bubbles or precipitation.
4. **Wash Buffer & Vacuum Waste Lines:** Ensure Maglumi Concentrated Wash Buffer is diluted $1:10$ with laboratory Type 1 CLRW water (Resistivity $> 10\ \text{M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$). Verify peristaltic pump vacuum reads between $-75\text{ to } -85\text{ kPa}$.

### 2.2 Reagent Integral RFID Tracking & On-Board Stability Management
1. **Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Integration:** Every Maglumi X3 reagent integral contains an embedded RFID tag pre-encoded with the manufacturer's 10-point Master Calibration Curve, kit lot number, and expiration date.
2. **On-Board Carousel Stability:** The refrigerated reagent carousel maintains temperatures at $2^\circ\text{C} \text{ – } 8^\circ\text{C}$. On-board shelf-life is electronically monitored and capped at 28 days. Any reagent pack exceeding 28 days on-board is locked by LIMS and rejected.
3. **Paramagnetic Microbead Resuspension:** Automated mechanical rocking ensures magnetic micro-particles are completely suspended prior to aspiration. Visual inspection confirms absence of settled pellet.

### 2.3 2-Point Active Recalibration Protocol & Reagent Lot-to-Lot Verification (`CLSI EP26-A`)
1. **Recalibration Frequency:** Calibration is mandated:
   - Every 28 days for active on-board reagent integrals.
   - Upon loading a new reagent kit lot.
   - Following preventive maintenance, PMT optical replacement, or pipette probe replacement.
   - Whenever IQC results show persistent systematic bias or fail Westgard multi-rules ($1_{3s}$ or $2_{2s}$).
2. **2-Point Calibrator Acceptance Criteria:**
   - Run Calibrator 1 and Calibrator 2 in duplicate ($n=2$).
   - Precision: Calibration replicate $\%CV \le 5.0\%$.
   - Signal Ratio: Calibrator signal ratio vs RFID Master Curve: $0.80 \le \frac{\text{Measured RLU}}{\text{RFID Target RLU}} \le 1.20$.
3. **Lot-to-Lot Reagent Verification (`CLSI EP26-A`):** Before switching to a new reagent lot, run 5 patient sera spanning clinical decision intervals and 2 levels of QC on both the old and new lot simultaneously. Bias between lots must satisfy:
$$\text{Bias}_{\text{lot}} = \frac{\lvert \bar{X}_{\text{new}} - \bar{X}_{\text{old}} \rvert}{\bar{X}_{\text{old}}} \times 100\% \le 0.33 \times \text{TEa}$$

## Section 3: Internal Quality Control (IQC) Architecture & Statistical Process Control

### 3.1 Daily Multi-Level IQC Design
- **Quality Control Materials:** Independent, third-party assayed liquid/lyophilized human serum matrix controls (**Bio-Rad Lyphochek Immunoassay Plus Control**, 3 Levels; and **Bio-Rad Lyphochek Tumor Marker Control**, 2 Levels). Manufacturer-supplied kit controls are never used as primary IQC.
- **Run Frequency:** Every 24 hours prior to patient sample testing, and immediately following major maintenance or recalibration.
- **Statistical Base Period:** Means and Standard Deviations ($SD$) are established from a minimum of 20 replicates measured over 20 distinct operating days during non-consecutive shifts (`CLSI C24-Ed4`).

### 3.2 Westgard Multi-Rule Evaluation Logic
Daily results are plotted on automated Levey-Jennings (L-J) charts. The system applies the classic Westgard algorithm:

```
[Daily IQC Result Run]
         |
         v
  [Any value > 2 SD?] ------------------ NO ------------------> [ACCEPT RUN: Release Patient Results]
         |
        YES (Warning Rule 1_2s)
         |
         +---> [Check 1_3s Rule: Result > 3 SD?] -------------------> REJECT: Random Error
         |
         +---> [Check 2_2s Rule: 2 consecutive results > 2 SD?] ----> REJECT: Systematic Error
         |
         +---> [Check R_4s Rule: Difference between levels > 4 SD?] -> REJECT: Random Error
         |
         +---> [Check 4_1s Rule: 4 consecutive results > 1 SD?] ----> REJECT: Systematic Error
         |
         +---> [Check 10_x Rule: 10 consecutive results on one side] -> REJECT: Systematic Drift / Shift
```

### 3.3 Bench CAPA & Patient Impact Containment Workflow for Out-of-Control IQC
When an IQC rule rejection occurs, bench technologists execute the immediate containment protocol:
1. **Immediate LIMS Quarantine:** Maglumi X3 bidirectional interface locks the affected analyte. No patient reports can be generated or released.
2. **Quarantine Patient Batch:** All patient specimens processed since the last acceptable IQC run are quarantined for mandatory re-testing.
3. **5-Whys Root Cause Analysis (RCA):**
   - *Why 1:* Did the control fail? (e.g., TSH L2 exceeded $+3.2\ SD$).
   - *Why 2:* Why did L2 exceed $+3.2\ SD$? (Starter 1 delivery volume was deficient).
   - *Why 3:* Why was Starter 1 volume deficient? (Micro-bubble blockage in peristaltic tubing).
   - *Why 4:* Why was there a bubble blockage? (Starter 1 bottle ran low without triggering sensor).
   - *Why 5:* Why did it run low? (Technologist skipped morning liquid volume verification checklist).
4. **Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA):** Replace starter reagents, perform automated 5-cycle prime, re-verify PMT dark count, recalibrate with 2-point Calibrators, re-run IQC Levels 1, 2, and 3.
5. **Patient Impact Containment:** Once IQC passes within $\pm 1.5\ SD$, repeat 10 consecutive patient samples from the quarantined batch. Calculate percentage concordance:
$$\text{Concordance} = 100\% - \frac{\lvert X_{\text{repeat}} - X_{\text{initial}} \rvert}{X_{\text{initial}}} \times 100\%$$
If concordance is $\ge 95\%$, release results. If clinically significant shifts ($> \text{TEa}$) are discovered, amend reports and contact treating clinicians immediately. Record all actions in `SDL/IMM/LOG-01`.

### 4.2 Statistical Performance Evaluation Metrics per `ISO 13528:2022`
Laboratory performance is evaluated using the Standard Deviation Index ($SDI$) / $Z\text{-score}$:

$$Z = \frac{X_{\text{lab}} - X_{\text{peer}}}{\sigma_{\text{peer}}}$$

Where:
- $X_{\text{lab}}$ = Result obtained by Sawariya Diagnostic Lab on Maglumi X3.
- $X_{\text{peer}}$ = Robust Consensus Mean of the Maglumi X3 / ABEI peer group.
- $\sigma_{\text{peer}}$ = Standard Deviation of the peer group.

```
Z-Score Acceptance Bands:
  \lvert Z \rvert <= 2.0  : SATISFACTORY / ACCEPTABLE (No action required; log in SDL/IMM/LOG-09)
  2.0 < \lvert Z \rvert < 3.0 : QUESTIONABLE / WARNING (Requires internal investigation & trend audit)
  \lvert Z \rvert >= 3.0  : UNSATISFACTORY / ACTION REQUIRED (Mandatory formal CAPA within 15 days)
```

### 4.3 EQAS Root Cause Investigation & 8D CAPA Protocol for Discrepant Results ($\lvert Z \rvert \ge 2.5$)
Any PT cycle returning $\lvert Z \rvert \ge 2.5$ triggers a formal investigation logged in `SDL/IMM/LOG-09`:
1. **Pre-Analytical Investigation:** Check reconstitution log, pipette calibration certificate, water purity log, storage condition of lyophilized vial, and reconstitution equilibration time ($30\text{ min}$ in dark).
2. **Clerical & Transcription Check:** Verify reported units ($\mu\text{IU/mL}$ vs $\text{mIU/L}$, $\text{ng/dL}$ vs $\text{nmol/L}$, $\text{pg/mL}$ vs $\text{pmol/L}$), decimal point placement, and analyzer code selection on the PT portal.
3. **Analytical Investigation:** Audit L-J charts for the specific run date. Check reagent lot number, 2-point calibration slope, PMT counts, and lot-to-lot verification data.
4. **Corrective Action & Effectiveness Verification:** Re-analyze archived EQAS frozen aliquot (retained at $-20^\circ\text{C}$). If discrepancy is confirmed, execute recalibration or lot replacement. Monitor next 3 months of EQAS performance to verify Z-score return to $\lvert Z \rvert \le 1.0$.

### 6.2 Pipetting Probe Carryover Verification Protocol
To prevent false-positive elevation in low-concentration samples processed immediately after ultra-high samples (e.g., Total $\beta\text{-hCG}$, Ferritin, Total PSA), probe carryover is verified quarterly using the five-sample sequence ($H_1, H_2, H_3, L_1, L_2, L_3$):

$$\% \text{Carryover} = \frac{L_1 - L_3}{H_3 - L_3} \times 100\%$$

**NABL Acceptance Threshold:** $\% \text{Carryover} < 0.001\%$ ($< 10\text{ ppm}$). If carryover $\ge 0.001\%$, clean probe wash well, inspect probe internal Teflon coating, replace wash pump seals, and re-verify.
