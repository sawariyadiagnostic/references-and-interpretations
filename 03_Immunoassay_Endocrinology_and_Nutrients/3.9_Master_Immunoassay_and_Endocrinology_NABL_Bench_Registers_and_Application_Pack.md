## Section 1: Purpose & NABL Portal (`NABL 100 / NABL 112`) Application Readiness
This Master Departmental Pack serves two critical regulatory functions for **Department 03: Immunoassay, Endocrinology, Vitamins, Acute Cardiac/Sepsis & Oncology Biomarkers**:
1. **Turnkey `NABL 100` Portal Scope Submission Table:** Provides the complete parameter-by-parameter **Scope of Accreditation Matrix** (Analyte, Specimen Matrix, Standardized Flash `CLIA` / `ECLIA` Examination Technique, Analytical Measuring Range [`AMR`], Expanded Measurement Uncertainty [`MU %` at $k=2$, `ISO/TS 20914`], and Controlled SOP Code) for all immunoassays executed on the **Snibe Maglumi X3 (`ABEI` Flash `CLIA`)** and backup immunoassay systems.
2. **Mandatory Endocrinology/Oncology Quality Assurance & 10 Print-Ready Bench Registers (`NABL 112`):** Establishes the **Mandatory `PEG 6000` Monomeric Prolactin/TSH Precipitation Verification**, **High-Dose Hook Dilution Log**, **Pre-Test Counseling & `PC & PNDT Act 1994` Prenatal Screening Compliance (`Form G`)**, and the **10 Mandatory Live Immunoassay Bench Registers**.
