## 🏛️ 2.2a — MASTER PANEL: Complete Liver Function Test (`LFT`), `R-Ratio`, `FIB-4` & `APRI`

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — COMPLETE LIVER FUNCTION TEST (LFT) MASTER PANEL]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
The Liver Function Test (LFT) panel evaluates four physiological domains of the hepatobiliary system:
1. **Excretory & Bilirubin Metabolism:** Total, Direct (Conjugated + $\delta$-Bilirubin), and Indirect (Unconjugated) Bilirubin.
2. **Hepatocellular Membrane & Mitochondrial Integrity:** $\text{SGPT (ALT — Cytoplasmic, } t_{1/2} = 47 \pm 10\text{h)}$ and $\text{SGOT (AST — 80\% Mitochondrial / 20\% Cytoplasmic, } t_{1/2} = 17 \pm 5\text{h)}$.
3. **Biliary Canalicular / Cholestatic Integrity:** Alkaline Phosphatase ($\text{ALP}, t_{1/2} = 7\text{ days}$) and $\gamma$-Glutamyl Transferase ($\text{GGT}, t_{1/2} = 14\text{–}26\text{ days}$).
4. **Hepatic Synthetic Capacity:** Serum Albumin ($t_{1/2} = 19\text{–}21\text{ days}$), Total Protein, Globulins, $\text{A:G Ratio}$, and Cholinesterase ($t_{1/2} = 10\text{–}14\text{ days}$).

**ACG / AASLD Integrated LFT Pattern Recognition Matrix ($R\text{-Ratio} = [\text{ALT/ULN}] \div [\text{ALP/ULN}]$):**
| LFT Pattern | Key Biochemical Signature | Primary Clinical Differential Diagnosis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Hepatocellular Injury Pattern**<br>*($R\text{-Ratio} > 5.0$)* | Predominant $\uparrow \text{ALT (SGPT)}$ & $\uparrow \text{AST (SGOT)}$ with normal or mildly raised $\text{ALP}$ | - **Mild–Moderate ($< 5\times \text{ULN}$):** Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (**MASLD/NAFLD**), Alcoholic Liver Disease, Chronic Hepatitis B/C, Drug/Herbal DILI (Statins, ATT, NSAIDs, Ayurveda/CAM), Celiac/Thyroid disease.<br>- **Severe ($> 15\times \text{ULN}$ / $> 1,000\text{ U/L}$):** Acute Viral Hepatitis (A, E, B), Paracetamol overdose, Ischemic Hepatitis ("Shock Liver" — LDH:ALT $> 1.5$), Autoimmune Hepatitis, or Acute Common Bile Duct Stone passage. |
| **2. Mixed Hepatitic-Cholestatic**<br>*($R\text{-Ratio } 2.0 \text{ – } 5.0$)* | Parallel elevation of both $\text{ALT/AST}$ and $\text{ALP/GGT}$ | Drug-Induced Liver Injury (DILI — Augmentin, Phenytoin, Anti-TB drugs), Early/Resolving Biliary Obstruction, Acute Alcoholic Hepatitis, or Granulomatous Hepatitis. |
| **3. Cholestatic Pattern**<br>*($R\text{-Ratio} < 2.0$)* | Predominant $\uparrow \text{ALP}$ + $\uparrow \text{GGT}$ ($\pm \uparrow$ Direct Bilirubin) | **Extrahepatic Biliary Obstruction** (Choledocholithiasis, Pancreatic/Biliary Stricture or Malignancy) or **Intrahepatic Cholestasis** (DILI, Primary Biliary Cholangitis, Sepsis/TPN, Intrahepatic Cholestasis of Pregnancy, Infiltrative TB/Sarcoidosis/Lymphoma). |
| **4. Isolated Indirect Hyperbilirubinemia** | $\uparrow$ Total & Indirect Bilirubin ($> 80\%$ Indirect); **Normal $\text{ALT, AST, ALP, GGT}$** | **Gilbert Syndrome** (benign *UGT1A1* promoter polymorphism in $5\text{–}8\%$ of population, triggered by fasting/stress/illness) or **Hemolysis** (correlate with Retic count, LDH, Haptoglobin, Peripheral Smear). |
| **5. Synthetic Dysfunction & Fibrosis** | $\downarrow$ Serum Albumin ($< 3.5\text{ g/dL}$), $\downarrow \text{A:G Ratio}$ ($< 1.0$), $\uparrow \text{PT/INR}$ | Chronic Liver Disease / Cirrhosis, Severe Malnutrition, Nephrotic Syndrome, or Protein-Losing Enteropathy. Calculate **$\text{FIB-4} = (\text{Age} \times \text{AST}) \div (\text{PLT}[10^9/\text{L}] \times \sqrt{\text{ALT}})$** ($< 1.30$ Low Fibrosis Risk; $> 2.67$ High Advanced Fibrosis/Cirrhosis Risk). |

**🛡️ Pre-Analytical & Medico-Legal Shield (LFT):**  
Normal LFT values do not exclude compensated chronic liver disease or advanced hepatic fibrosis. Conversely, mild transient elevations ($< 2\times \text{ULN}$) occur after strenuous exercise, intramuscular injections, fatty meals, or intercurrent viral illness. **In-vitro hemolysis** falsely elevates $\text{AST (SGOT)}$ and interferes with Diazo bilirubin methods. *Correlate clinically; not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🟡 2.2b — STANDALONE: Serum Bilirubin (Adult & AAP 2022 Neonatal Bhutani Hour-Specific Risk Stratification)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — SERUM BILIRUBIN & NEONATAL BHUTANI INTERPRETATION]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
Bilirubin is the breakdown product of heme catabolism from senescent erythrocytes ($80\text{–}85\%$) and hemoproteins. **Indirect (Unconjugated) Bilirubin** is water-insoluble and albumin-bound; the liver conjugates it via *UGT1A1* into water-soluble **Direct (Conjugated) Bilirubin** for biliary excretion. Clinical scleral icterus becomes visible at Total Bilirubin $> 2.5\text{–}3.0\text{ mg/dL}$.

**Neonatal Hour-Specific Total Serum Bilirubin (`TSB`) Risk Thresholds ($\ge 35\text{ Weeks}$ Gestation — AAP 2022 / Bhutani Guidelines):**
| Postnatal Age (Hours) | Low-Intermediate Risk ($40^{\text{th}}\text{–}75^{\text{th}}$ Percentile) | High-Intermediate Risk ($75^{\text{th}}\text{–}95^{\text{th}}$ Percentile) | High-Risk Zone ($\ge 95^{\text{th}}$ Percentile / Phototherapy Check) | 🚨 Critical Panic / Exchange Transfusion Alert |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$0 \text{ – } 24\text{ Hours}$** | $> 5.0\text{ mg/dL}$ | $> 6.0\text{ mg/dL}$ | **$\ge 8.0\text{ mg/dL}$** *(Pathological <24h Jaundice — Rule out ABO/Rh HDN)* | **$\ge 10.0 \text{ – } 12.0\text{ mg/dL}$** |
| **$25 \text{ – } 48\text{ Hours}$** | $8.0 \text{ – } 10.9\text{ mg/dL}$ | $11.0 \text{ – } 12.9\text{ mg/dL}$ | **$\ge 13.0 \text{ – } 15.0\text{ mg/dL}$** | **$\ge 18.0\text{ mg/dL}$** |
| **$49 \text{ – } 72\text{ Hours}$** | $11.0 \text{ – } 13.4\text{ mg/dL}$ | $13.5 \text{ – } 15.4\text{ mg/dL}$ | **$\ge 15.5 \text{ – } 17.5\text{ mg/dL}$** | **$\ge 20.0\text{ mg/dL}$** |
| **$> 72\text{ Hours}$ ($4\text{–}7\text{ Days}$)** | $13.0 \text{ – } 14.9\text{ mg/dL}$ | $15.0 \text{ – } 16.9\text{ mg/dL}$ | **$\ge 17.0 \text{ – } 19.0\text{ mg/dL}$** | **$\ge 20.0 \text{ – } 25.0\text{ mg/dL}$** |
| **Conjugated / Direct Bilirubin (Neonate)** | Normal $< 1.0\text{ mg/dL}$ | — | **Direct $> 1.0\text{ mg/dL}$ (or $> 20\%$ of TSB) = Neonatal Cholestasis** | Urgent evaluation for **Biliary Atresia** (Kasai window $< 60\text{ days}$) |

**🛡️ Pre-Analytical & Legal Shield:** Direct sunlight or fluorescent phototherapy light degrades bilirubin via photo-isomerization to lumirubin by **$30\text{–}50\%\text{ per hour}$** (wrap tubes in amber foil). In prolonged cholestasis, covalently albumin-bound **Delta-Bilirubin ($\delta$-bilirubin, $t_{1/2} = 14\text{–}21\text{ days}$)** causes Direct Bilirubin to remain elevated for weeks after biliary decompression. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🧪 2.2c — STANDALONE: Transaminases (`ALT/SGPT`, `AST/SGOT`), `ALP`, `GGT`, Proteins, Ammonia, Bile Acids & Wilson Disease

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — SPECIALIZED HEPATOBILIARY INTERPRETATION]**

- **De Ritis Ratio ($\text{AST:ALT}$):** $< 1.0$ in MASLD/NAFLD and acute viral hepatitis; **$> 2.0$** in **Alcoholic Hepatitis** (mitochondrial AST release + Vitamin $\text{B}_6$ P5P depletion), Wilson Disease (ratio often $> 2.2$ with disproportionately **low/normal $\text{ALP}$**; $\text{ALP:Total Bilirubin} < 4.0$), or non-hepatic rhabdomyolysis.
- **Combined $\text{ALP}$ + $\text{GGT}$ Rule:** $\uparrow \text{ALP}$ + $\uparrow \text{GGT}$ confirms **hepatobiliary/cholestatic origin**; $\uparrow \text{ALP}$ + **Normal $\text{GGT}$** indicates **Bone** (pediatric growth spurt, Vitamin D deficiency/osteomalacia, fracture, Paget's) or **Placental** (2nd/3rd trimester pregnancy) origin.
- **Serum Cholinesterase (`2.2.9`):** Sensitive marker of **Organophosphate / Carbamate Pesticide Poisoning** (inhibition $> 50\%$ from baseline indicates acute cholinergic toxicity), **Suxamethonium (Succinylcholine) Apnea** (atypical dibucaine-resistant pseudocholinesterase), and hepatic synthetic reserve.
