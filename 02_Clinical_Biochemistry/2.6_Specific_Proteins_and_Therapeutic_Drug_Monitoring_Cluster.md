## 🧬 2.6a — STANDALONE: Complement `C3` & `C4` and Quantitative Immunoglobulins (`IgG`, `IgA`, `IgM`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — COMPLEMENT C3/C4 & QUANTITATIVE IMMUNOGLOBULINS]**

**1. Complement `C3` ($90 \text{ – } 180\text{ mg/dL}$) & Complement `C4` ($10 \text{ – } 40\text{ mg/dL}$):**
- **Pathophysiological Pathway Differential (Consumption vs Acute-Phase Synthesis):**
  - **Classical Pathway Activation (Both `C3` $\downarrow$ AND `C4` $\downarrow$):** Immune-complex consumption seen in **Active Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE / Lupus Nephritis Flare)**, Type II/III Mixed Cryoglobulinemia, Serum Sickness, and Subacute Bacterial Endocarditis.
  - **Alternative Pathway Activation (Isolated `C3` $\downarrow$ with Normal `C4`):** Characteristic of **Acute Post-Streptococcal Glomerulonephritis (`PSGN` — $\text{C3}$ normalizes within $6\text{–}8\text{ weeks}$)**, **$\text{C3}$ Glomerulopathy / Dense Deposit Disease (persistent $\text{C3} \downarrow > 8\text{ weeks}$)**, Post-Infectious GN, and Gram-negative septic shock.
  - **Isolated Profound `C4` Reduction (`C4` $< 5\text{ mg/dL}$ with Normal `C3`):** Classic screening hallmark of **Hereditary or Acquired Angioedema (`HAE` — $\text{C1}$-Esterase Inhibitor Deficiency)** or congenital $\text{C4A/C4B}$ null alleles.
  - **Elevated `C3` & `C4`:** Both are positive **acute-phase reactants** synthesized by the liver; a concomitant systemic inflammatory state may mask mild complement consumption.

**2. Quantitative Immunoglobulins (`IgG`: $700\text{–}1,600\text{ mg/dL}$, `IgA`: $70\text{–}400\text{ mg/dL}$, `IgM`: $40\text{–}230\text{ mg/dL}$):**
- **Polyclonal Hypergammaglobulinemia (Broad Diffuse Increase):**
  - *Predominant $\uparrow\text{IgG}$:* **Autoimmune Hepatitis (`AIH`)**, Systemic Lupus Erythematosus, Sjogren's Syndrome, chronic infections (HIV, Tuberculosis, Kala-Azar).
  - *Predominant $\uparrow\text{IgA}$ (Often with $\beta\text{-}\gamma$ Bridging on SPEP):* **Alcoholic / MASLD Cirrhosis**, IgA Nephropathy (Berger's disease), Inflammatory Bowel Disease, Mucosal Infections.
  - *Predominant $\uparrow\text{IgM}$:* **Primary Biliary Cholangitis (`PBC`)**, acute viral/parasitic infections (Malaria, Brucellosis, Hyperreactive Malarial Splenomegaly).
- **Monoclonal Elevation (Single Class Markedly $\uparrow$ with Reciprocal Immunoparesis of Uninvolved Classes):** Suspect **Multiple Myeloma ($\text{IgG}$ or $\text{IgA}$)**, **Waldenström Macroglobulinemia ($\text{IgM}$)**, or **MGUS** — must confirm clonality via **Serum Protein Electrophoresis (`SPEP`), Immunofixation (`IFE`), and Serum Free Light Chains (`sFLC` $\kappa/\lambda$)**.
- **Hypogammaglobulinemia:** Primary immunodeficiencies (**Selective $\text{IgA}$ Deficiency** [$< 7\text{ mg/dL}$ — risk of anaphylaxis to $\text{IgA}$-containing blood products!], Common Variable Immunodeficiency [`CVID`], X-linked Agammaglobulinemia) or secondary loss/suppression (Nephrotic Syndrome, Protein-Losing Enteropathy, CLL, Multiple Myeloma Light-Chain Disease, Rituximab/Corticosteroid therapy). *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 📊 2.6b — STANDALONE: `SPEP`, Immunofixation (`IFE`) & Serum Free Light Chains (`sFLC` $\kappa/\lambda$)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — SPEP, IFE & SERUM FREE LIGHT CHAINS (IMWG CRITERIA)]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
Serum Protein Electrophoresis (`SPEP`) separates serum proteins into 6 fractions (**Albumin, $\alpha_1$, $\alpha_2$, $\beta_1$, $\beta_2$, and $\gamma$-Globulin**) to detect **monoclonal paraproteins (`M-Spike` / M-band)** and classic disease patterns:
- **$\alpha_1$-Globulin Flatline:** Severe **$\alpha_1$-Antitrypsin (`AAT`) Deficiency** ($Pi^*ZZ$).
- **$\alpha_2$-Globulin Spike + Marked Hypoalbuminemia:** **Nephrotic Syndrome** (selective retention of high-molecular-weight $\alpha_2$-macroglobulin [$725\text{ kDa}$] with urinary loss of albumin).
- **$\beta\text{-}\gamma$ Bridging (Fusion of $\beta$ and $\gamma$ zones):** **Hepatic Cirrhosis** (polyclonal $\text{IgA}$ increase).
- **Sharp Narrow Spike in $\gamma$ or $\beta$ Region (`M-Spike`):** **Monoclonal Gammopathy** (MGUS, Smoldering Myeloma, Multiple Myeloma, Waldenström Macroglobulinemia, AL Amyloidosis).

**Serum Free Light Chains (`sFLC` $\kappa$ [$3.3\text{–}19.4\text{ mg/L}$], $\lambda$ [$5.7\text{–}26.3\text{ mg/L}$], Normal $\kappa/\lambda$ Ratio: $0.26 \text{ – } 1.65$):**
- **Renal Reference Adjustment (`eGFR < 60 mL/min`):** Because free light chains are cleared by the kidneys, polyclonal retention in Chronic Kidney Disease shifts the **Renal Reference $\kappa/\lambda$ Ratio to $0.37 \text{ – } 3.10$**.
- **IMWG SLiM-CRAB Myeloma-Defining Biomarker:** An **Involved : Uninvolved $\text{sFLC}$ Ratio $\ge 100$** (provided the involved free light chain is $\ge 100\text{ mg/L}$) is diagnostic of active **Multiple Myeloma requiring therapy** even in the absence of end-organ CRAB features.

**🛡️ Mandatory Analytical Shield:** Therapeutic monoclonal antibodies (**Daratumumab, Isatuximab, Elotuzumab**) are humanized $\text{IgG}-\kappa$ molecules that can produce an iatrogenic small $\text{IgG}-\kappa$ band ($0.05\text{–}0.15\text{ g/dL}$) on `SPEP/IFE`; clinical history of anti-CD38 therapy must be communicated to the laboratory. High-dose **Antigen Excess (Prozone / Hook Effect)** in extreme light-chain myeloma can falsely lower nephelometric/turbidimetric $\text{sFLC}$ unless kinetic reaction slope monitoring and auto-dilution (`1:10` to `1:100`) are enforced. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🩸 2.6c — STANDALONE: $\alpha_1\text{-Antitrypsin}$ (`AAT`), Prealbumin (`Transthyretin`) & `Haptoglobin`

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — AAT, PREALBUMIN & HAPTOGLOBIN INTERPRETATION]**

- **$\alpha_1\text{-Antitrypsin}$ (`AAT` — Normal: $90 \text{ – } 200\text{ mg/dL}$):** Primary serine protease inhibitor protecting pulmonary alveoli from neutrophil elastase. **Severe Hereditary Deficiency ($< 57\text{ mg/dL}$ or $< 11\text{ }\mu\text{mol/L}$, typically $Pi^*ZZ$ genotype)** predisposes to early-onset **panacinar basilar emphysema** and **neonatal cholestasis / adult cryptogenic cirrhosis**. *(Note: $\text{AAT}$ is a positive acute-phase reactant; check concomitantly with $\text{CRP}$).*
- **Prealbumin / Transthyretin (`PAB` — Normal: $20 \text{ – } 40\text{ mg/dL}$):** Hepatic transport protein with a **short biological half-life ($t_{1/2} \approx 2\text{ days}$)** compared to Albumin ($t_{1/2} \approx 20\text{ days}$). Serves as a sensitive marker of **acute protein-calorie malnutrition** and **response to enteral/parenteral nutritional refeeding** (expected rise $\ge 4\text{ mg/dL/week}$ with adequate nutrition, provided $\text{CRP}$ is stable, as $\text{PAB}$ is a negative acute-phase reactant).
- **Serum Haptoglobin (`Hp` — Normal: $30 \text{ – } 200\text{ mg/dL}$):** Irreversibly binds free hemoglobin released during red cell destruction; the $\text{Hp-Hb}$ complex is cleared within minutes by hepatic $\text{CD163}$ macrophages. **Markedly Decreased / Undetectable Haptoglobin ($< 10\text{–}25\text{ mg/dL}$)** accompanied by elevated **$\text{LDH}$, Indirect Bilirubin, and Reticulocytosis** is the gold-standard biochemical signature of **Intravascular (and severe Extravascular) Hemolysis** (Sensitivity $\sim 83\%$, Specificity $\sim 96\%$). Also reduced in advanced hepatic cirrhosis and congenital ahaptoglobinemia ($\sim 1\text{–}4\%$ of populations). *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🔄 2.6e — Smart LIMS Auto-Reflex & Utilization Cascades (Cluster 2.6)

1. **Monoclonal Gammopathy Diagnostic Reflex (`IMWG`):**
   - `IF SPEP Shows M-Spike OR Hypogammaglobulinemia OR Beta-2/Alpha-2 Asymmetry` $\rightarrow$ **Auto-Reflex Serum Immunofixation (`IFE`) + Serum Free Light Chains (`sFLC` $\kappa/\lambda$, `2.6.3`) + Quantitative `IgG/IgA/IgM` (`2.6.2`)** + suggest **Serum Calcium (`2.5.1`)**, **Creatinine (`2.3.2`)**, and **Hemoglobin (`1.1.2`)**.
2. **Phenytoin Hypoalbuminemia / Uremia Sheiner-Tozer Auto-Calculation:**
   - `IF Total Phenytoin Ordered AND Serum Albumin < 3.5 g/dL` $\rightarrow$ **Auto-Calculate & Report Sheiner-Tozer Adjusted Phenytoin (`2.6.6`)** ($K = 0.2$ if $\text{eGFR} \ge 15$; $K = 0.1$ if $\text{eGFR} < 15\text{ mL/min}$) so clinicians do not miss Phenytoin toxicity masked by low albumin!
3. **Digoxin Electrolyte Safety Check:**
   - `IF Serum Digoxin >= 1.2 ng/mL` $\rightarrow$ **Auto-Check/Suggest Serum Potassium (`2.3.6`), Magnesium (`2.5.4`), and Creatinine (`2.3.2`)**.
4. **Lithium Tube & Renal-Thyroid Safety Check:**
   - `IF Serum Lithium >= 1.5 mmol/L` $\rightarrow$ Verify specimen was **NOT** collected in a Green-Top Lithium Heparin tube $\rightarrow$ Trigger **30-Minute Critical Read-Back** + suggest **Serum Creatinine (`2.3.2`), Sodium (`2.3.5`), and TSH (`3.1.2`)**.
