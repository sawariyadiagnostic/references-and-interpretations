## 🏛️ 2.3a — MASTER PANEL: Complete Kidney / Renal Function Test (`KFT` / `RFT`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — COMPLETE KIDNEY FUNCTION TEST (KFT / RFT) MASTER PANEL]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
The Renal Function Test (KFT/RFT) evaluates **glomerular filtration capacity** (Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea/BUN, Cystatin C, $\text{eGFR}$), **purine catabolism & tubular handling** (Uric Acid), and **fluid/acid-base electrolyte homeostasis** ($\text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+, \text{Cl}^-, \text{HCO}_3^-$, Anion Gap).

**Integrated Azotemia Differential Matrix:**
| Diagnostic Category | `BUN : Creatinine` Ratio | `Urea : Creatinine` Ratio | Biochemical & Clinical Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pre-Renal Azotemia**<br>*(Renal Hypoperfusion)* | **$> 20 : 1$** | **$> 40 : 1$** | **Disproportionate rise in Urea/BUN** relative to Creatinine due to enhanced ADH-mediated passive proximal/medullary tubular urea reabsorption ($\text{FE}_{\text{Na}} < 1\%$, $\text{FE}_{\text{Urea}} < 35\%$, Urine $\text{Na}^+ < 20\text{ mmol/L}$, Urine Osmolality $> 500\text{ mOsm/kg}$). Causes: **Dehydration/hypovolemia**, vomiting/diarrhea, heart failure, sepsis/shock, **upper GI hemorrhage**, high-protein diet, or catabolic states/corticosteroids. |
| **2. Intrinsic Renal Disease**<br>*(Parenchymal Damage)* | **$10 : 1 \text{ to } 15 : 1$** | **$20 : 1 \text{ to } 35 : 1$** | **Parallel elevation of both Creatinine and Urea/BUN** with reduced $\text{eGFR}$ ($\text{FE}_{\text{Na}} > 2\%$, Urine $\text{Na}^+ > 40\text{ mmol/L}$, muddy brown granular casts). Causes: Acute Tubular Necrosis (ATN), Glomerulonephritis, Diabetic/Hypertensive Nephropathy, NSAID/Aminoglycoside/Contrast nephrotoxicity, or Acute Interstitial Nephritis. |
| **3. Post-Renal Azotemia**<br>*(Urinary Tract Obstruction)* | Initially $> 15:1$, later $10\text{–}15:1$ | Initially $> 35:1$, later $20\text{–}30:1$ | Bilateral ureteric/renal calculi, Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH), neurogenic bladder, urethral stricture, or pelvic malignancy (correlate with USG KUB). |
| **4. Low Ratio ($< 10 : 1$)** | **$< 10 : 1$** | **$< 20 : 1$** | Severe liver disease (impaired ornithine/urea cycle synthesis), severe protein malnutrition, SIADH, pregnancy (physiological hemodilution + $50\%$ GFR surge), or **rhabdomyolysis** (massive skeletal muscle creatine/creatinine release). |

**🛡️ Mandatory Pre-Analytical & Medico-Legal Shield (KFT/RFT):**  
*Serum Creatinine is a relatively late marker of renal injury ("creatinine-blind window"): up to **$40\%\text{–}50\%$ of functional nephron mass may be lost** before Serum Creatinine rises above the upper reference limit. Conversely, isolated mild variations in Creatinine, Urea, or Electrolytes may reflect hydration status, skeletal muscle mass, dietary protein/creatine intake, or pre-analytical in-vitro hemolysis (affecting Potassium). Correlate with Urine Routine/UACR and clinical examination. Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🧮 2.3b — STANDALONE: Serum Creatinine, Cystatin C & KDIGO 2024 `eGFR` Staging

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — SERUM CREATININE, CYSTATIN C & eGFR INTERPRETATION]**

**KDIGO 2024 GFR & Albuminuria Matrix for Chronic Kidney Disease:**
| GFR Category | `eGFR` ($\text{mL/min/1.73 m}^2$) | Descriptive Term & Clinical Management Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G1** | **$\ge 90$** | Normal or High GFR *(Not CKD unless structural/histological kidney damage or albuminuria $\text{UACR} \ge 30\text{ mg/g}$ is present for $> 3\text{ months}$)*. |
| **G2** | **$60 \text{ – } 89$** | Mildly Decreased GFR *(Normal in older adults unless accompanied by markers of kidney damage/albuminuria)*. |
| **G3a** | **$45 \text{ – } 59$** | Mildly to Moderately Decreased GFR *(Confirm with **2021 CKD-EPI Combined Creatinine-Cystatin C eGFR (`2.3.9`)** & $\text{UACR}$)*. |
| **G3b** | **$30 \text{ – } 44$** | Moderately to Severely Decreased GFR *(Monitor Calcium, Phosphorus, iPTH, 25-OH Vit D, Hb, Bicarbonate; adjust renally cleared drug doses)*. |
| **G4** | **$15 \text{ – } 29$** | Severely Decreased GFR *(Nephrology co-management required; avoid NSAIDs, iodinated contrast, and gadolinium)*. |
| **G5** | **$< 15$** | Kidney Failure / Uremia *(Assess need for renal replacement therapy / dialysis / transplantation)*. |

**KDIGO 2012/2024 Acute Kidney Injury (`AKI`) Staging Criteria (Rapid Rise over 48 Hours to 7 Days):**
- **Stage 1 AKI:** Serum Creatinine rise $\ge 0.3\text{ mg/dL}$ ($\ge 26.5\text{ }\mu\text{mol/L}$) within $48\text{ hours}$ OR $1.5\text{–}1.9\times$ baseline within $7\text{ days}$ (or Urine Output $< 0.5\text{ mL/kg/h}$ for $6\text{–}12\text{h}$). **Urine/Plasma `NGAL` (`2.3.13`)** rises within **2 to 6 hours** of tubular injury (24–48 hours before Creatinine).
- **Stage 2 AKI:** Serum Creatinine $2.0\text{–}2.9\times$ baseline.
- **Stage 3 AKI:** Serum Creatinine $\ge 3.0\times$ baseline OR increase to $\ge 4.0\text{ mg/dL}$ ($\ge 353.6\text{ }\mu\text{mol/L}$) OR initiation of renal replacement therapy.

## ⚡ 2.3c — STANDALONE: Serum Electrolytes, Anion Gap & Pseudohyperkalemia / Pseudohyponatremia Shields

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — SERUM ELECTROLYTES & ACID-BASE ANION GAP]**

**Anion Gap (`AG`) & Albumin-Corrected Anion Gap (`Figge Formula`):**
$$\text{Anion Gap} = \text{Na}^+ - (\text{Cl}^- + \text{HCO}_3^-) \quad [\text{Normal: } 8 \text{ – } 16\text{ mmol/L}]$$
$$\text{Corrected Anion Gap} = \text{Measured AG} + 2.5 \times (4.0 - \text{Serum Albumin [g/dL]})$$
- **High Anion Gap Metabolic Acidosis (`HAGMA`, $\text{AG} > 16\text{ mmol/L}$ — `GOLD MARK`):** **G**lycols (Ethylene/Propylene glycol), **O**xoproline (chronic Paracetamol), **L**-Lactate (Sepsis/Hypoxia), **D**-Lactate, **M**ethanol, **A**spirin (Salicylates), **R**enal Failure (Uremia), **K**etoacidosis (Diabetic/Alcoholic/Starvation).
- **Normal Anion Gap (Hyperchloremic) Metabolic Acidosis (`NAGMA`, $\text{AG } 8\text{–}12\text{ mmol/L}$ with $\uparrow \text{Cl}^-$):** Acute/chronic diarrhea, Renal Tubular Acidosis (Type 1, 2, 4), Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitors (Acetazolamide), or excess $0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$ resuscitation.

**🛡️ MANDATORY PRE-ANALYTICAL & MEDICO-LEGAL SHIELD (ELECTROLYTE ARTIFACTS):**
1. **Pseudohyperkalemia ($\text{K}^+$):** Intracellular $\text{K}^+$ ($\sim 140\text{ mmol/L}$) is $35\times$ plasma $\text{K}^+$. Fist clenching during venipuncture, narrow-gauge needle aspiration, **in-vitro hemolysis** ($+0.3\text{ mmol/L K}^+$ per $100\text{ mg/dL}$ `H-Index`), refrigeration of whole blood before centrifugation, delayed separation ($> 2\text{h}$), or **marked Thrombocytosis ($> 500 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$) / Leukocytosis ($> 50 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$)** causes artifactual serum $\text{K}^+$ elevation. **Repeat on Lithium Heparin Plasma + 12-lead ECG before treating.**
2. **Pseudohyponatremia ($\text{Na}^+$):** Correct for hyperglycemia ($\text{Corrected Na}^+ = \text{Na}^+ + 0.016 \times [\text{Glucose} - 100]$). Severe hypertriglyceridemia ($> 1,500\text{ mg/dL}$) or paraproteinemia ($> 10\text{ g/dL}$) causes falsely low $\text{Na}^+$ on Indirect ISE analyzers; verify on Direct ISE / ABG analyzer. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
