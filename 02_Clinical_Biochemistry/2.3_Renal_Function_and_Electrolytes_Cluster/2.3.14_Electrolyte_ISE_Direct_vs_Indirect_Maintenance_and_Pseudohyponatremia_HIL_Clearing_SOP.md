## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Electrolyte Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the operation, two-point millivolt ($\text{mV}$) slope calibration, daily enzymatic/hypochlorite deproteinization, and troubleshooting of **Ion-Selective Electrode (`ISE`) Modules** measuring **Sodium ($\text{Na}^+$), Potassium ($\text{K}^+$), Chloride ($\text{Cl}^-$), Ionized Calcium ($\text{iCa}^{2+}$), and Lithium ($\text{Li}^+$)**, and to enforce the **Mandatory NABL 112 Protocol for Direct vs. Indirect `ISE` Electrolyte Exclusion Effect (Pseudohyponatremia)** and **Lipemic/Hemolyzed Specimen Clearing**.
2. **NABL 112 Specific Requirement:** NABL assessors specifically audit whether the laboratory knows whether its electrolyte module uses **Indirect `ISE` (pre-diluted $1:20$ to $1:34$ with diluent)** or **Direct `ISE` (undiluted whole blood/serum)**, and how the laboratory prevents reporting lethal **spurious pseudohyponatremia** in patients with severe hypertriglyceridemia ($\text{TG} > 800\text{ mg/dL}$ / Lipemia Index `L > 300`) or paraproteinemia ($\text{Total Protein} > 10.0\text{ g/dL}$).

## Section 2: Physical Principle of Ion-Selective Electrodes (`Nernst Equation`) & Membrane Architecture
At $37.0^\circ\text{C}$, the electrical potential ($E$, in $\text{mV}$) developed across an ion-selective membrane relative to a constant $\text{Ag/AgCl}$ Reference Electrode ($3.0\text{ M KCl}$ bridge) obeys the **Nernst Equation**:
$$E = E_0 + \frac{2.303 \cdot R \cdot T}{z \cdot F} \log_{10}(a_i) \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{Theoretical Nernst Slope at } 37^\circ\text{C} = \frac{61.5\text{ mV}}{z}$$

| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |

Sodium ($\text{Na}^+$)** | Crown Ether / $\text{Na}^+$-Selective Aluminosilicate Glass Capillary | $+1$ | **$52.0 \text{ – } 64.0\text{ mV/dec}$** | $6\text{–}12\text{ Months}$ ($10,000\text{ tests}$) | Protein/lipid coating ($\downarrow$ slope); Benzalkonium heparin skin antiseptics ($\uparrow \text{Na}^+$). |

## Section 3: Direct `ISE` vs. Indirect `ISE` — Mandatory Pseudohyponatremia & Lipemia Clearing Protocol
### 3.1 Biophysical Mechanism of the Electrolyte Exclusion Effect
- Normal plasma consists of **$93\%$ Aqueous Phase ($\text{Plasma Water}$)** and **$7\%$ Solid Phase (Proteins + Lipids)**. Dissolved electrolytes ($\text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+, \text{Cl}^-$) reside **exclusively in the $93\%$ aqueous phase**.
- **Direct `ISE` (Standalone Electrolyte Analyzer / ABG Analyzer):** Aspirates **undiluted** serum/whole blood and measures $\text{Na}^+$ activity directly in plasma water $\to$ **100% IMMUNE to hyperlipidemia and hyperproteinemia**.
- **Indirect `ISE` (Integrated Chemistry Analyzer `ISE` Module with $1:31$ Pre-Dilution):** Dilutes a fixed total volume of serum ($15\ \mu\text{L}$) assuming $93\%$ water. When severe hypertriglyceridemia ($\text{TG} = 2,500\text{ mg/dL}$) or myeloma paraproteinemia ($\text{Total Protein} = 13\text{ g/dL}$) expands the non-aqueous solid phase to **$20\%$** (reducing plasma water to **$80\%$**), the fixed $15\ \mu\text{L}$ pipette aspirates $14\%$ less water, causing a **spurious pseudohyponatremia** ($\text{Reported Na}^+ = 122\text{ mmol/L}$ when true plasma-water $\text{Na}^+ = 140\text{ mmol/L}$!).

### 3.2 Mandatory 3-Tier Laboratory Reflex Protocol for Indirect `ISE` Systems
Whenever any specimen exhibits **$\text{Na}^+ < 130\text{ mmol/L}$**, **Lipemia Index `L-Index > 200`** (milky/turbid serum), or **$\text{Total Protein} > 9.5\text{ g/dL}$** on an Indirect `ISE` module:
1. **Option A (Gold Standard — Direct `ISE` Re-Analysis):** Immediately re-analyze the undiluted specimen on the **Direct `ISE` Electrolyte / Blood Gas Analyzer (`SOP 2.7.1`)** and release the Direct `ISE` Sodium, Potassium, and Chloride values.
2. **Option B (High-Speed Micro-Centrifugation / Lipemia Clearing for Turbid Sera):** Spin $500\ \mu\text{L}$ serum at **$15,000 \times g$ for $15\text{ minutes}$** (or use non-ionic cyclodextrin/polyethylene glycol lipid-clearing reagent `LipoClear`), aspirate the clear infranatant below the chylomicron cream layer, and re-run on the analyzer.
3. **Option C (Validated Waugh /Dimeski Plasma Water Correction Formula — If Direct `ISE` Unavailable):**
   $$\text{Plasma Water Fraction } (\text{PW\%}) = 99.1 - (0.73 \times \text{Total Protein}_{\text{g/dL}}) - (0.00103 \times \text{Total Lipids}_{\text{mg/dL}})$$
   $$\text{Corrected } [\text{Na}^+] = \text{Indirect } [\text{Na}^+] \times \frac{93.0}{\text{PW\%}}$$

## Section 4: Mandatory 4-Point Pseudohyperkalemia & $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ Cross-Contamination Shield
Before releasing or telephoning any critical **$\text{K}^+ \ge 6.0\text{ mmol/L}$** (or unexpected $\text{K}^+ > 5.3\text{ mmol/L}$ in an outpatient with normal `eGFR`):
1. **Check `H-Index` (Hemolysis):** Since intracellular erythrocyte $[\text{K}^+] \approx 105\text{ mmol/L}$ ($25\times$ plasma), $100\text{ mg/dL}$ free $\text{Hb}$ (`H-Index = 100`) falsely elevates $\text{K}^+$ by **$+0.35\text{ to } +0.50\text{ mmol/L}$**. **NEVER apply a mathematical correction formula to release a hemolyzed Potassium** — reject (`REJ-HEM`) and request a fresh non-hemolyzed draw!
2. **Check Concurrent Serum Calcium (`2.5.1`) & Alkaline Phosphatase (`2.2.5`) for $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ Contamination:**
   - If $\text{K}^+ > 6.5\text{ mmol/L}$ is accompanied by **Total Calcium $< 6.0\text{ mg/dL}$** and **$\text{ALP} < 20\text{ U/L}$**, the phlebotomist poured blood from a Lavender-Top $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ tube or contaminated the syringe needle tip (`CLSI GP41` Order-of-Draw violation) $\to$ **Suppress all results and recollect (`REJ-EDTA-CONTAM`)!**
3. **Check Concurrent `CBC` (`1.1.1`) for Severe Thrombocytosis or Leukocytosis:**
   - If $\text{Platelets} > 600 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$ or $\text{WBC} > 50 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$, in-vitro clotting releases platelet/leukocyte $\text{K}^+$ (`Serum K+ > Plasma K+` by $0.5\text{–}3.0\text{ mmol/L}$). Request a **Green-Top Lithium Heparin Plasma tube** walked immediately to the lab (`[BAND-K-PSEUDO]`).
4. **Verify Refrigerated Whole-Blood Storage Delay:** Whole blood stored at $4^\circ\text{C}$ before centrifugation inhibits erythrocyte $\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+\text{-ATPase}$, leaking $\text{K}^+$ at $+0.2\text{ mmol/L per hour}$.

## Section 5: Daily `ISE` Maintenance, Deproteinization & Calibration Logbook
1. **Every Morning (`08:00 AM`):** Perform `ISE` Fluidic Prime, Two-Point Calibration (`Standard A` & `Standard B`), verify Nernst slopes ($\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+ = 52\text{–}64\text{ mV/dec}$; $\text{Cl}^- = -45\text{ to } -62\text{ mV/dec}$), and run **2-Level `ISE` Controls**.
2. **Every Evening (`End of Shift` — Mandatory after $\ge 50\text{ samples/day}$):**
   - Run **Enzymatic Pepsin/Subtilisin Deproteinizer (`ISE Cleaning Solution`)** for $5\text{–}10\text{ minutes}$ to strip fibrin/lipoprotein film from the Valinomycin and Chloride PVC membranes.
   - Run **Sodium Conditioning Solution ($0.2\text{ M NH}_4\text{HF}_2$ / Etching Conditioner)** weekly to rejuvenate the $\text{Na}^+$ glass capillary surface.
