### 2.1 Part A: Parameters Applied for NABL `ISO 15189:2022` Accredited Scope (`Discipline: Biological — Clinical Biochemistry`)
> **Metrological & Six-Sigma Calculation Key (`ISO/TS 20914:2019` & `CLSI C24-Ed4`):**
> - **Expanded Measurement Uncertainty ($U\%$ at $k=2$, $95\%$ confidence):** $U\% = 2 \times \sqrt{\text{CV}_A^2 + u_{\text{cal/bias}}^2}$, verified from 6-month cumulative `IQC` (`CV_A`) and `CMC Vellore / Bio-Rad EQAS` root-mean-square bias.
> - **Westgard Six-Sigma Metric ($\sigma$):** $\sigma = (\text{TE}_a\% - \lvert\text{Bias}\%\rvert) / \text{CV}_A\%$. Control rules assigned strictly by $\sigma$ tier: **$\ge 6.0\sigma \to \text{1}_{3s}$** ($N=2, R=1$); **$5.0\text{–}5.99\sigma \to \text{1}_{3s}/\text{2}_{2s}/\text{R}_{4s}$** ($N=2, R=1$); **$4.0\text{–}4.99\sigma \to \text{1}_{3s}/\text{2}_{2s}/\text{R}_{4s}/\text{4}_{1s}$** ($N=4, R=1$).

### 4.2 Refrigerated ($2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$) Capped Specimen Retention & Bio-Medical Waste Disposal Log (`NABL 112` & `BMW Rules 2016/2019`)

## Section 5: Out-of-Control (`OOC`) IQC & Unsatisfactory `EQAS` ($\lvert Z \rvert \ge 3.0$) Decision Tree & Patient Look-Back Protocol (`CLSI C24-Ed4`)

### 5.1 Step-by-Step `OOC` Containment & Moving-Bracket Patient Look-Back Workflow
```
[STEP 1: VIOLATION DETECTED ON BONAVERA CHEM 400 / 200 OR EASYLYTE]
   ├── Daily IQC Violates Assigned Westgard Sigma Rule (1_3s, 2_2s, R_4s, or 4_1s)
   └── OR Monthly CMC Vellore / Bio-Rad EQAS Returns Action Signal (|Z| >= 3.0) or Repeat Warning (2x |Z| > 2.0)
                          │
                          ▼
[STEP 2: IMMEDIATE AUTOMATED CONTAINMENT ("STOP THE LINE")]
   ├── Middleware asserts hard verification lock (`LIMS-QC-LOCK`) for the affected analyte(s).
   ├── Zero patient results for the affected parameter can be released or auto-verified.
   └── Inspect Levey-Jennings chart & Reaction Monitor Curve (`A0`, lag phase, linearity) to classify error:
        ├── RANDOM ERROR (`1_3s` or `R_4s`): Check probe clot, micro-bubble in QC cup, fibrin strand, or cuvette scratch.
        └── SYSTEMATIC ERROR (`2_2s` or `4_1s` or `EQAS |Z| >= 3.0`): Check reagent stability, open-vial contamination,
            calibrator lot drift, Peltier 37.0°C drift, lamp 340 nm ADC drop, or ISE electrode protein coating.
                          │
                          ▼
[STEP 3: SEQUENTIAL TROUBLESHOOTING & CORRECTIVE ACTION (`CAPA`)]
   ├── 3A. Pour fresh aliquot of IQC from a newly reconstituted/unopened vial (never re-aspirate the same dead-volume cup).
   ├── 3B. If still OOC: Check onboard reagent cassette volume/bubbles; load fresh cassette & verify Cuvette Water Blank.
   ├── 3C. Perform 2-Point / Full Calibration (`REG-BIO-05`), verifying K-Factor drift <= +/-5.0% of Master Factor.
   └── 3D. Re-run both Level 1 and Level 2 IQC (plus retained frozen EQAS aliquot if investigating an EQAS failure).
        └── Release analytical hold ONLY when both L1 and L2 achieve |Z| <= 1.5 SD.
                          │
                          ▼
[STEP 4: MANDATORY RETROSPECTIVE PATIENT LOOK-BACK (`CLSI C24-Ed4 MOVING-BRACKET PROTOCOL`)]
   ├── Identify all N patient specimens analyzed since the LAST DOCUMENTED PASSING IQC RUN (or last valid EQAS cycle).
   ├── Retrieve capped 2–8°C retained serum/plasma tubes (`Section 4.2`) and re-test in reverse chronological brackets of 5:
   │    ├── Re-run the most recent 5 patient specimens on the restored/calibrated system (or verified backup `Chem 200`).
   │    ├── Calculate percentage difference: |Result_new - Result_initial| / Result_initial * 100.
   │    ├── IF difference > 0.5 * TE_a (or > RCV_95% across a clinical decision cutoff):
   │    │    ├── Immediately place an electronic recall hold (`AMEND-HOLD`) on those reports.
   │    │    └── Step backward another bracket of 5 samples until 5 consecutive samples show difference <= 0.5 * TE_a.
   │    └── Issue Amended Reports (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.4.1.8`) clearly marked "AMENDED REPORT — SUPERSEDES PREVIOUS
   │         RESULT ISSUED ON [DATE/TIME] DUE TO ANALYTICAL RECALIBRATION" and notify the treating clinician (`REG-BIO-10`).
   └── Document complete root cause, bracketed patient IDs, and preventive action in `REG-BIO-06` / `REG-BIO-07`.
```
