## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, `CLSI GP41` Order-of-Draw, Stability & Quantitative `HIL` Rejection Matrix

### 2.1 Dual-Protocol Antenatal Preparation & Phlebotomy (`IADPSG` vs `DIPSI`)
Because obstetricians across Haryana and India utilize either the **International IADPSG / ADA / WHO / FIGO 3-Sample Fasting Protocol** or the **Government of India MoHFW / FOGSI / DIPSI Single-Sample Non-Fasting Protocol**, the laboratory supports both with explicit protocol tagging on the report:
1. **Protocol A — `IADPSG / ADA / WHO` One-Step 3-Sample $75\text{g}$ OGTT ($24\text{–}28\text{ Weeks}$ or First Antenatal Visit):**
   - **Preparation:** Strict **8 to 12 hours overnight fasting** after 3 days of normal diet ($\ge 150\text{g}$ carbohydrate/day).
   - **Draws:** Collect **`0-Hr` (Fasting)** venous Grey-Top tube $\rightarrow$ Administer **$75.0\text{g}$ anhydrous glucose ($82.5\text{g}$ glucose monohydrate)** in $300\text{ mL}$ water over 5–10 minutes $\rightarrow$ Collect **`1-Hr` ($60\text{ min}$)** and **`2-Hr` ($120\text{ min}$)** venous Grey-Top samples while patient remains seated at rest.
2. **Protocol B — `DIPSI / MoHFW Govt. of India / FOGSI` Single-Step $75\text{g}$ Test (First Antenatal Visit & Repeat at $24\text{–}28\text{ Weeks}$):**
   - **Preparation:** Performed **irrespective of the last meal (fasting or non-fasting)** so pregnant women travelling from rural/peripheral areas are never turned away.
   - **Draws:** Administer **$75.0\text{g}$ anhydrous glucose ($82.5\text{g}$ glucose monohydrate)** dissolved in $300\text{ mL}$ water over 5–10 minutes $\rightarrow$ Collect a **SINGLE venous plasma sample at exactly `2 Hours` ($120\text{ min}$)**.
3. **Critical Pre-Analytical Glycolysis Control at the $92\text{ mg/dL}$ IADPSG Cutoff:**
   - A loss of just $5\text{ mg/dL}$ in an uncentrifuged tube lowers a true GDM fasting value of $95\text{ mg/dL}$ to $90\text{ mg/dL}$ (false negative in $> 40\%$ of cases per HAPO/EFLM studies). Therefore, **every antenatal OGTT tube must be centrifuged at $1,500 \times g$ for $10\text{ minutes}$ within 15–20 minutes of draw** (or collected in **Citrate-Buffered $\text{NaF/EDTA}$ tubes**).

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `CLSI` Verification, Calibration & Six Sigma QC

### 3.1 Analytical Principle (UV Enzymatic Hexokinase / G-6-PDH)
$$\text{D-Glucose} + \text{ATP} \xrightarrow[\text{pH } 7.8, 37^\circ\text{C}]{\text{Hexokinase, Mg}^{2+}} \text{G-6-P} + \text{ADP} \quad \longrightarrow \quad \text{G-6-P} + \text{NADP}^+ \xrightarrow{\text{G-6-PDH}} \text{6-Phosphogluconate} + \text{NADPH} + \text{H}^+$$
- **Mandatory Venous Plasma Requirement:** Both IADPSG (HAPO study) and DIPSI guidelines are calibrated to **laboratory venous plasma enzymatic assays**. Point-of-care capillary glucometers must NOT be used to diagnose GDM due to physiological pregnancy hemodilution ($\text{Hct } 30\text{–}34\%$) and $\pm 15\%$ strip imprecision across the tight $92\text{ mg/dL}$ threshold.

### 3.2 Calibration, Six Sigma IQC & Primary-Backup Comparability (`CLSI EP31-A`)
- **Calibration & IQC:** NIST SRM 965b-traceable calibration; Level 1 IQC ($\sim 85\text{ mg/dL}$) closely guards the **$92\text{ mg/dL}$** IADPSG fasting threshold, and Level 2 ($\sim 250\text{ mg/dL}$) guards the **$140\text{ / }153\text{ / }180\text{ mg/dL}$** post-load thresholds. Six Sigma **$\ge 5.5\sigma$**.
- **Bonavera Chem 400 $\leftrightarrow$ Chem 200 Comparability (`CLSI EP31-A`):** Slope $0.98\text{–}1.02$; bias $\le \pm 2.0\%$ ($\le \pm 1.8\text{ mg/dL}$ at $92\text{ mg/dL}$).
- **AMR & `CRR`:** **AMR:** **$2.0 \text{ – } 750.0\text{ mg/dL}$**; $1:5$ auto-dilution **CRR to $3,750\text{ mg/dL}$**.

## 7. In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics, Placental Diabetogenic Pathophysiology & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Why Gestational Diabetes Peaks at $24\text{–}28\text{ Weeks}$ (Pedersen Maternal-Fetal Hyperglycemia-Hyperinsulinemia Hypothesis):**
  - **1st Trimester ($0\text{–}13\text{ Weeks}$):** Estrogen/progesterone enhance maternal insulin sensitivity and fetal siphoning lowers maternal fasting glucose by $10\text{–}15\text{ mg/dL}$. Hyperglycemia detected in the 1st trimester usually represents **pre-existing undiagnosed Type 2 Diabetes (Overt DM)**.
  - **2nd & 3rd Trimester ($24\text{–}28\text{ Weeks}$ onward):** Exponential placental secretion of **Human Placental Lactogen (`hPL`)**, **Placental Growth Hormone**, **Progesterone**, **Cortisol**, and **TNF-$\alpha$** induces a **$50\%\text{–}60\%$ physiological reduction in maternal peripheral insulin sensitivity**. If maternal pancreatic $\beta$-cells cannot mount a $2\text{ to }3\text{-fold}$ compensatory insulin surge, maternal hyperglycemia ensues.
  - **Fetal Consequences:** Maternal glucose crosses the placenta freely via facilitated diffusion (*GLUT1*), whereas **maternal insulin does NOT cross the placenta**. After $12\text{ weeks}$ gestation, the fetal pancreas responds to maternal hyperglycemia with **fetal hyperinsulinemia**, acting as a potent anabolic growth factor causing **fetal macrosomia**, shoulder dystocia, polyhydramnios, surfactant suppression (neonatal RDS), and **severe neonatal hypoglycemia (`< 40 mg/dL`) immediately after umbilical cord clamping**.
- **Smart LIMS Auto-Reflex Cascades:**
  - `IF` Antenatal OGTT meets GDM or Overt DM cutoffs $\rightarrow$ Recommend **$\text{HbA1c}$ (`2.1.7`)** (to distinguish Overt DM $\ge 6.5\%$ from true GDM) + **TSH (`3.1.2`)** + **Urine Ketones / Routine (`5.1.1`)**.
  - `IF` Patient received antenatal **Betamethasone / Dexamethasone** for fetal lung maturity within the past **5 to 7 days** $\rightarrow$ Flag result as **Steroid-Induced Transient Hyperglycemia** and advise repeating OGTT $\ge 7\text{ days}$ post-steroid dose.

- **`[BAND-LOW]` (Any timepoint $< 60\text{ mg/dL}$):** *"ANTENATAL HYPOGLYCEMIA / REACTIVE DIP: Plasma glucose $< 60\text{ mg/dL}$ in pregnancy may reflect prolonged maternal fasting (accelerated starvation of pregnancy) or reactive post-load hyperinsulinemia. Advise frequent small complex-carbohydrate meals."*
- **`[BAND-NORMAL]` (`0-Hr` $< 92\text{ mg/dL}$, `1-Hr` $< 180\text{ mg/dL}$, AND `2-Hr` $< 120\text{–}139\text{ mg/dL}$):** *"NORMAL ANTENATAL GLUCOSE TOLERANCE: Values are below both IADPSG ($92 / 180 / 153\text{ mg/dL}$) and DIPSI ($< 140\text{ mg/dL}$) diagnostic thresholds for Gestational Diabetes Mellitus. - **`[BAND-BORDERLINE]` (DIPSI `2-Hr` $120 \text{ – } 139\text{ mg/dL}$ OR IADPSG-DIPSI Discordant `2-Hr` $140 \text{ – } 152\text{ mg/dL}$):** *"GESTATIONAL GLUCOSE INTOLERANCE (GGI) / DIPSI-POSITIVE GDM: A 2-hour post-$75\text{g}$ value of $120\text{–}139\text{ mg/dL}$ indicates Gestational Glucose Intolerance (GGI per DIPSI), while **$140\text{–}152\text{ mg/dL}$ is diagnostic of GDM under DIPSI / MoHFW Govt. of India criteria** (though below the $153\text{ mg/dL}$ IADPSG threshold). Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) and close obstetric follow-up are recommended."*
- **`[BAND-HIGH]` (IADPSG `0-Hr` $\ge 92$, `1-Hr` $\ge 180$, OR `2-Hr` $\ge 153\text{ mg/dL}$; OR DIPSI `2-Hr` $\ge 140\text{ mg/dL}$):** *"GESTATIONAL DIABETES MELLITUS (GDM — POSITIVE): One or more plasma glucose values meet or exceed international IADPSG/ADA/WHO ($0\text{h} \ge 92$, $1\text{h} \ge 180$, $2\text{h} \ge 153\text{ mg/dL}$) and/or DIPSI ($2\text{h} \ge 140\text{ mg/dL}$) diagnostic criteria for GDM. Initiate Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) and monitor for antenatal targets: Fasting $< 95\text{ mg/dL}$ and 2-Hour Post-Meal $< 120\text{ mg/dL}$. If Fasting $\ge 126\text{ mg/dL}$ or 2-Hr $\ge 200\text{ mg/dL}$, classify as **Overt Diabetes in Pregnancy**."*
- **`[BAND-CRITICAL]` (Antenatal Glucose $< 55\text{ mg/dL}$ or $> 250\text{ mg/dL}$):** *"🚨 CRITICAL OBSTETRIC GLYCEMIC ALERT: Severe maternal hyperglycemia ($> 250\text{ mg/dL}$) or hypoglycemia ($< 55\text{ mg/dL}$) carries immediate risk of maternal ketoacidosis and fetal compromise. Screen Blood $\beta\text{-Hydroxybutyrate}$ / Urine Ketones immediately. Critical read-back to Obstetrician completed within 30 minutes."*
