## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To define the standardized operational protocol, daily startup photometer/cuvette blank verification, Type-I/Type-II `CLRW` water conductivity verification, reagent/calibrator traceability (`IFCC / NIST SRM / CDC`), sample-to-sample and reagent-to-reagent carryover verification (`CLSI EP10`), bi-annual Primary (`Bonavera Chem-400`) vs. Backup (`Bonavera Chem-200`) split-sample comparability (`CLSI EP09 / ISO 15189:2022 Cl. 7.3.7.4`), and scheduled Preventive Maintenance (`PM`) for the Department of Clinical Biochemistry.
2. **NABL 112 & ISO 15189:2022 Compliance:**
   - **NABL 112 (`Specific Criteria for Clinical Biochemistry`):** Requires documented daily verification of reaction bath temperature ($37.0 \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$), `CLRW` water resistivity/conductivity ($\le 1.0\ \mu\text{S/cm}$ or $\ge 1.0\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$), optical cuvette cell-blank absorbance limits across all $12$ wavelengths ($340\text{–}800\text{ nm}$), lot-specific calibrator traceability logs, and bidirectional `Westgard Sigma Rules` (`1:3s`, `2:2s`, `R:4s`, `4:1s`) before releasing patient results.
   - **Hardware-Agnostic Design:** Applicable to the **Bonavera Chem-400** ($400\text{ photometric tests/hr}$ primary analyzer), **Bonavera Chem-200** ($200\text{ photometric tests/hr}$ backup analyzer), and any upgrade to high-throughput modular spectrophotometric clinical chemistry platforms without altering clinical SOP identifiers.

## Section 2: Daily Pre-Start Environmental, Water Purification & Analyzer Readiness Protocol (`07:30 AM`)
1. **Environmental & Power Stability Verification:**
   - Verify Biochemistry room ambient temperature is **$20.0^\circ\text{C}\text{–}25.0^\circ\text{C}$** and relative humidity is **$30\%\text{–}60\%$** (prevents condensation on optical filters and reagent carousel).
   - Verify Online Double-Conversion Sine-Wave `UPS` battery health and output voltage ($230\text{ V} \pm 5\%$, $50\text{ Hz}$); verify dedicated Earth-to-Neutral potential is **$< 1.0\text{ V AC}$** (critical to prevent stray voltage noise in the $340\text{ nm}$ UV photometer and `ISE` reference electrode).
2. **Type-I / Type-II Clinical Laboratory Reagent Water (`CLRW — CLSI GP40`) Daily Verification:**
   - Check in-line conductivity meter on the RO + Deionization + $0.22\ \mu\text{m}$ UV water purification plant:
     - **Target Conductivity:** **$\le 1.0\ \mu\text{S/cm}$** at $25^\circ\text{C}$ (Resistivity **$\ge 1.0\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$**; Optimal Type-I $> 10.0\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$).
     - **Microbial Colony Count (Monthly):** $< 10\text{ CFU/mL}$; **Silicate:** $< 0.05\text{ mg/L}$.
     - *Mandatory Lock:* If water conductivity exceeds $> 2.0\ \mu\text{S/cm}$, **DO NOT run Calcium (`2.5.1`), Magnesium (`2.5.4`), Iron (`2.5.5`), Phosphorus (`2.5.3`), or Alkaline Phosphatase (`2.2.5`)** until resin cartridges are regenerated/replaced.
3. **Reagent Carousel ($2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$) & Probe Inspection:**
   - Confirm onboard Peltier reagent cooling compartment temperature is **$4.0^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8.0^\circ\text{C}$** ($24/7$ continuous power).
   - Inspect Sample Probe ($S$), Reagent Probe 1 ($R_1$), Reagent Probe 2 ($R_2$), and Teflon/piezoelectric Mixing Paddles for straightness, absence of salt crystals or fibrin threads, and crisp vertical wash-well jet streams.

## Section 3: Daily Startup Photometer, Cuvette Water-Blank & Thermal Bath Verification
| Verification Parameter | Target Acceptance Specification (`Bonavera Chem-400 / Chem-200`) | Automated Flag / Failure Threshold | Mandatory Corrective Action Before Loading Controls |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Reaction Rotor Incubation Temperature** | **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$**<br>*(Range: $36.9^\circ\text{C}\text{–}37.1^\circ\text{C}$)* | $< 36.8^\circ\text{C}$ or $> 37.2^\circ\text{C}$<br>*(Causes $\pm 7\%$ error per $1^\circ\text{C}$ in `ALT/AST/ALP/LDH/CK`!)* | Allow $15\text{ min}$ thermal stabilization; check Peltier dry-bath / water-bath circulation sensor; abort enzyme assays until $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$ is restored. |
| **2. Halogen Tungsten Lamp ($12\text{V}/20\text{W}$) ADC Intensity ($340\text{ nm}$)** | **Within Manufacturer `ADC` Reference Window**<br>*(Zero drift $< 0.0020\text{ Abs/min}$)* | `340 nm Lamp Low / Unstable` Flag or `ADC` outside limits | Replace Halogen Lamp (mandatory if $> 1,500\text{ operating hours}$ or $340\text{ nm}$ noise $> 0.0005\text{ Abs}$), align optical focal path, and re-run Dark Current + Water Blank. |
| **3. Hard-Glass / Quartz Reaction Cuvette Water Blank (`Cell Blank`)** | **$340\text{ nm Abs} \le 0.0800\text{ Abs}$**<br>**SD across $80\text{–}90$ cuvettes $< 0.0015\text{ Abs}$** | Any individual cuvette Blank $\Delta\text{Abs} > \pm 0.0300$ from baseline or $> 0.1200\text{ Abs}$ | Execute **Acid/Alkaline Detergent Cuvette Wash**; if individual cuvette still fails, software-mask the scratched/stained cuvette and replace the cuvette segment. |
| **4. Automated `HIL` (Hemolysis, Icterus, Lipemia) Bichromatic Check** | `H` ($570/600\text{ nm}$), `I` ($480/505\text{ nm}$), `L` ($660/800\text{ nm}$) Blank $= 0$ | Baseline `HIL` offset $> \pm 5\text{ Index Units}$ | Clean sample probe and `Saline 0.9%` diluent line; re-zero `HIL` saline blank. |

## Section 4: Metrological Traceability, Calibration Triggers & 2-Level Third-Party IQC (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.5 & 7.3.7`)
1. **Higher-Order Metrological Traceability (`ISO 17511`):**
   - **Substrates / Metabolites:** Creatinine (`NIST SRM 967` — `IDMS` Enzymatic/Compensated Jaffe), Glucose (`NIST SRM 965`), Urea (`NIST SRM 909`), Uric Acid (`NIST SRM 909b`), Total Calcium (`NIST SRM 956`), Cholesterol/Triglycerides/HDL (`CDC CRMLN / Abell-Kendall`), HbA1c (`IFCC mmol/mol` & `NGSP %`), Total Protein/Albumin (`ERM-DA470k/IFCC`).
   - **Enzymes (`37.0°C IFCC Primary Reference Procedures`):** `ALT`, `AST` (with/without P-5-P), `ALP` (`AMP` buffer), `GGT` (`Szasz/IFCC`), `LDH` (`L-to-P`), `CK-NAC`, `Amylase` (`EPS-G7`), `Lipase`.
2. **Mandatory Re-Calibration Triggers (Never Rely on Fixed Factor $K$ Without Verification!):**
   - (1) Every **Reagent Lot Change**, (2) Every **Lamp or Cuvette Replacement**, (3) Whenever **Level-1 or Level-2 `IQC` violates Westgard `1:3s` or `2:2s`** after fresh control reconstitution, and (4) At scheduled intervals (**Every $14\text{–}28\text{ days}$** depending on onboard reagent open-vial stability).
   - **Enzyme K-Factor Verification:** Even for zero-order kinetic enzymes (`340 nm NADH` molar absorptivity $\varepsilon = 6,300\text{ L}\cdot\text{mol}^{-1}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$), calibrate against an **IFCC-traceable Multi-Enzyme Calibrator** on every reagent lot change and verify optical pathlength ($10.0\text{ mm}$).
3. **Daily Multi-Level Internal Quality Control (`IQC`) Gate:**
   - Run **2 Levels (Normal Level-1 & Pathological Level-2)** of **Third-Party Commutable Human-Serum-Matrix Control** every morning before patient samples, and repeat at **$8\text{-hour}$ shift handover** for high-volume STAT parameters (`Glucose`, `Creatinine`, `Bilirubin`, `Calcium`, `Troponin`).

## Section 5: Sample-to-Sample & Reagent-to-Reagent Carryover Prevention Protocol (`CLSI EP10`)
### 5.1 High-Risk Reagent-to-Reagent Chemical Carryover Pairs (Special Probe Wash Programming)
Because discrete random-access analyzers share reagent and sample probes across tests, specific reagent pairs cause severe chemical contamination unless programmed with **Dedicated Acid/Alkaline Probe Wash Cycles (`Special Wash`)** in the `Bonavera Chem-400 / Chem-200` software:

| Contaminating "Donor" Assay | Vulnerable "Recipient" Assay | Mechanism of Chemical Interference | Mandatory Analyzer Software `Special Wash` Programming |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Triglycerides / Lipase / Cholesterol** *(Contain Microbial Lipase & Esterase + Free Glycerol)* | **Next Lipase (`2.5.9`) or Triglycerides (`2.4.3`)** | Residual esterase/lipase on $R_1/R_2$ probe hydrolyzes substrate prematurely $\to$ **Falsely elevated Lipase/TG** | Program **Alkaline Detergent Wash ($200\ \mu\text{L}$)** between Lipase/TG and subsequent lipid/pancreatic assays. |
| **2. Direct/Total Bilirubin (`DPD / Diazo`) or Jaffe Creatinine (`Picric Acid / NaOH`)** | **Total Calcium (`Arsenazo III` / `o-CPC`), Magnesium (`Xylidyl Blue`), or Iron (`Ferrozine`)** | Strong alkali/chromogen alters dye-metal chelation $\text{pH}$ $\to$ **Spurious Calcium/Mg/Iron drift** | Program **Acid Wash ($0.1\text{ M HCl}$) + Deionized Water Flush** immediately after `Jaffe Creatinine` and `Bilirubin`. |
| **3. Inorganic Phosphorus (`Phosphomolybdate`) or Phosphate-Buffered Reagents (`AST/ALT/ALP`)** | **Inorganic Phosphorus (`2.5.3`)** | Trace $\text{PO}_4^{3-}$ ions in phosphate buffers contaminate the phosphorus cuvette $\to$ **False Hyperphosphatemia** | Program **Acid Wash + Double `CLRW` Water Rinse** before every `Inorganic Phosphorus` pipetting cycle. |
| **4. Immunoturbidimetric Assays (`HbA1c`, `CRP`, `ASO`, `RF`, `Cystatin C`, `Microalbumin`)** | **Subsequent Turbidimetric or Trace Assays** | Latex-antibody complexes coat cuvette walls $\to$ **Optical scatter carryover** | Dedicate specific cuvette Wash Station cycle with **Alkaline Hypochlorite / Surfactant** after latex assays. |

### 5.2 Semi-Annual Sample Carryover Verification (`H1-H2-H3-L1-L2-L3` Protocol)
Every **6 months** (and after any sample probe replacement), run a high-concentration patient specimen ($H_1, H_2, H_3$: e.g., Glucose $\approx 500\text{ mg/dL}$, ALT $\approx 1,000\text{ U/L}$, Bilirubin $\approx 20\text{ mg/dL}$, $\beta\text{-hCG}$ / CRP high) followed immediately by a low-normal specimen ($L_1, L_2, L_3$):
$$\text{Carryover (\%)} = \frac{L_1 - L_3}{H_3 - L_3} \times 100 \le \mathbf{0.5\%} \quad (\text{or } |L_1 - L_3| < 1\text{ SD of Low Control})$$

## Section 6: Bi-Annual Primary (`Chem-400`) vs. Backup (`Chem-200`) Split-Sample Comparability (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7.4`)
1. **Weekly Split-Sample Check (`Every Wednesday`):** Run **2 fresh patient serum samples** (spanning normal and abnormal ranges) on both **Bonavera Chem-400 (Primary)** and **Bonavera Chem-200 (Backup)**.
2. **Semi-Annual 20-Sample `CLSI EP09` Comparability Verification:**
   - Run $20$ patient specimens across the analytical measuring range on both analyzers.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** Inter-instrument `% Bias` at medical decision levels must be **$\le \frac{1}{2} \times \text{CLIA '2024 } TE_a$** (e.g., Glucose $\le \pm 4.0\%$, Creatinine $\le \pm 5.0\%$, Calcium $\le \pm 0.5\text{ mg/dL}$, ALT/AST $\le \pm 7.5\%$, Passing-Bablok slope $0.95\text{–}1.05$, $r \ge 0.990$).

## Section 7: Scheduled Preventive Maintenance (`PM`) Matrix & Print-Ready Biochemistry Analyzer Logbook
### 7.1 Preventive Maintenance Schedule (`Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Semi-Annual`)
- **Daily (`Start & End of Shift`):** Check `CLRW` conductivity ($\le 1.0\ \mu\text{S/cm}$), reaction bath temperature ($37.0^\circ\text{C}$), reagent cooler ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$), empty concentrated waste & biohazard container, execute **Daily Alkaline Probe & Cuvette Wash**, and inspect probe needles.
- **Weekly (`Every Saturday`):** Clean Sample/$R_1$/$R_2$ probes externally with $70\%$ Isopropyl Alcohol (`IPA`), clean mixing paddles, flush wash-station peristaltic tubing with $0.5\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite (followed by double `CLRW` rinse), and clean dust filters on Peltier cooling fans.
- **Monthly (`1st Working Day`):** Replace/inspect Hamilton syringe Teflon plunger seals if micro-bubbles appear, deep-soak reaction cuvettes in $2\%$ enzymatic/acid wash solution, clean optical filter wheel window, and verify sample probe liquid-level capacitance (`LLD`) and clot-detection pressure sensor.
- **Semi-Annual (`Every 6 Months — Authorized Biomedical Engineer`):** NIST-traceable thermistor verification of $37.0^\circ\text{C}$ reaction rotor, photometer wavelength accuracy ($340, 405, 505, 546, 578, 630, 700\text{ nm}$) and linearity verification, syringe volumetric gravimetric calibration, and carryover (`%k <= 0.5%`) certification.
