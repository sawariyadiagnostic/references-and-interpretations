## 🏛️ 2.1a — MASTER PANEL: Complete Diabetic Profile (`FBS`, `PPBS`, `HbA1c`, `Urine Glucose`, `UACR`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — MASTER DIABETIC PROFILE]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
The Complete Diabetic Profile integrates **acute glycemic status** (Fasting and 2-Hour Post-Prandial Plasma Glucose) with **chronic 8–12 week glycemic exposure** ($\text{HbA1c}$ / Estimated Average Glucose $\text{eAG}$) and **renal tubular/glomerular microvascular assessment** (Urine Glucose & Spot $\text{UACR}$).

**ADA 2025 / ICMR Unified Diagnostic Criteria for Adults (Non-Pregnant):**
| Diagnostic Category | Fasting Plasma Glucose (`FBS`) | 2-Hr Post-Prandial / OGTT (`PPBS`) | `HbA1c` ($\%$ / $\text{mmol/mol}$) | Random Glucose (`RBS`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Normal / Normoglycemia** | $70 \text{ – } 99\text{ mg/dL}$ ($3.9\text{–}5.5\text{ mmol/L}$) | $< 140\text{ mg/dL}$ ($< 7.8\text{ mmol/L}$) | $< 5.7\%$ ($< 39\text{ mmol/mol}$) | $< 140\text{ mg/dL}$ ($< 7.8\text{ mmol/L}$) |
| **Prediabetes (IFG / IGT)** | $100 \text{ – } 125\text{ mg/dL}$ *(IFG)* | $140 \text{ – } 199\text{ mg/dL}$ *(IGT)* | $5.7\% \text{ – } 6.4\%$ ($39\text{–}47\text{ mmol/mol}$) | — |
| **Diabetes Mellitus** | $\ge 126\text{ mg/dL}$ ($\ge 7.0\text{ mmol/L}$) | $\ge 200\text{ mg/dL}$ ($\ge 11.1\text{ mmol/L}$) | $\ge 6.5\%$ ($\ge 48\text{ mmol/mol}$) | $\ge 200\text{ mg/dL}$ *(+ classic hyperglycemic symptoms)* |
| **ADA Adult Treatment Target** | $80 \text{ – } 130\text{ mg/dL}$ | $< 180\text{ mg/dL}$ *(peak)* | $< 7.0\%$ ($< 53\text{ mmol/mol}$) | — |

**Discordance Between $\text{FBS/PPBS}$ and $\text{HbA1c}$ (Glycation Gap Analysis):**
- **High $\text{FBS/PPBS}$ + Normal $\text{HbA1c}$:** Recent-onset acute hyperglycemia ($< 3\text{ weeks}$: stress hyperglycemia, corticosteroid therapy, acute pancreatitis) or shortened RBC lifespan (hemolytic anemia, G6PD deficiency, blood loss/transfusion, pregnancy, hemoglobinopathy). Reflex to **Serum Fructosamine (`2.1.8`)**.
- **Normal $\text{FBS}$ + High $\text{HbA1c}$:** Predominant post-prandial hyperglycemic excursions, or falsely elevated $\text{HbA1c}$ due to Iron/Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$/Folate deficiency anemia (prolonged RBC survival), splenectomy, or uremic carbamylated Hb.

**🛡️ Mandatory NABL & Medico-Legal Note:**  
*Per ADA and ICMR guidelines, in the absence of unequivocal symptomatic hyperglycemia (polyuria, polydipsia, unexplained weight loss, or hyperglycemic crisis), a diagnosis of Diabetes Mellitus requires **two abnormal test results** from either the same sample (e.g., both $\text{FBS}$ and $\text{HbA1c}$ above diagnostic cutoffs) or two separate test samples on different days. Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 📊 2.1b — STANDALONE: Glycosylated Hemoglobin (`HbA1c`) & Estimated Average Glucose (`eAG`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — HbA1c & eAG INTERPRETATION]**

**Clinical Overview:**  
$\text{HbA1c}$ reflects average plasma glucose concentration over the preceding **8 to 12 weeks** (weighted $\sim 50\%$ toward the most recent 30 days, $25\%$ from days 30–60, and $25\%$ from days 60–120) via non-enzymatic ketoamine glycation of the N-terminal valine of the hemoglobin $\beta$-chain. Estimated Average Glucose ($\text{eAG}$) is derived via the Nathan ADAG regression formula: $\text{eAG (mg/dL)} = (28.7 \times \text{HbA1c } \%) - 46.7$.

**Clinical Decision Thresholds (ADA 2025 / ICMR / NGSP-IFCC):**
| Category | $\text{HbA1c}$ ($\%$) | IFCC ($\text{mmol/mol}$) | $\text{eAG}$ ($\text{mg/dL}$) | $\text{eAG}$ ($\text{mmol/L}$) | Clinical Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Normal / Non-Diabetic** | $< 5.7\%$ | $< 39$ | $< 117$ | $< 6.5$ | Normal glycemic homeostasis. |
| **Prediabetes** | $5.7\% \text{ – } 6.4\%$ | $39 \text{ – } 47$ | $117 \text{ – } 137$ | $6.5 \text{ – } 7.6$ | Increased risk for Diabetes & CVD; lifestyle modification and annual monitoring advised. |
| **Diabetes Mellitus** | $\ge 6.5\%$ | $\ge 48$ | $\ge 140$ | $\ge 7.8$ | Consistent with Diabetes *(confirm with repeat testing if asymptomatic)*. |
| **Tight / Standard Glycemic Control** | $< 6.5\% \text{ – } 7.0\%$ | $< 48 \text{ – } 53$ | $< 140 \text{ – } 154$ | $< 7.8 \text{ – } 8.6$ | Recommended for most non-pregnant adults to prevent microvascular complications. |
| **Less Stringent Target** | $< 7.5\% \text{ – } 8.0\%$ | $< 58 \text{ – } 64$ | $< 169 \text{ – } 183$ | $< 9.4 \text{ – } 10.2$ | Appropriate in frail elderly patients, history of severe hypoglycemia, or advanced comorbidities. |

**🛡️ MANDATORY ANALYTICAL & BIOLOGICAL LIMITATION SHIELD ($\text{HbA1c}$):**
1. **Falsely Lowered $\text{HbA1c}$ (Shortened Erythrocyte Survival):** Hemolytic anemia, G6PD deficiency, acute or chronic blood loss, recent blood transfusion (within 90 days), **pregnancy** (increased RBC turnover + hemodilution in 2nd/3rd trimesters), erythropoietin/iron therapy recovery phase, Dapsone/Ribavirin therapy, or chronic liver disease.
2. **Falsely Elevated $\text{HbA1c}$ (Prolonged Erythrocyte Survival / Assay Interference):** Untreated **Iron Deficiency Anemia**, **Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$ or Folate deficiency**, asplenia/splenectomy, chronic renal failure (carbamylated hemoglobin in uremia), chronic alcohol/salicylate use, or severe hypertriglyceridemia ($> 1,500\text{ mg/dL}$) / hyperbilirubinemia ($> 20\text{ mg/dL}$).
3. **Hemoglobinopathies ($\text{HbS, HbD-Punjab, HbE, HbC}$, High $\text{HbF} > 10\%$):** Structural hemoglobin variants prevalent in North India ($\text{HbD-Punjab}, \beta\text{-Thalassemia trait}$) may interfere depending on assay method. Where RBC turnover is abnormal, **Serum Fructosamine (`2.1.8`)** (reflecting 2–3 week glycated albumin) or **OGTT / SMBG** must be used instead of $\text{HbA1c}$. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🍽️ 2.1c — STANDALONE: Fasting (`FBS`), Post-Prandial (`PPBS`) & Random Blood Glucose (`RBS`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — FBS, PPBS & RBS INTERPRETATION]**

**Clinical Overview:**
- **Fasting Blood Sugar ($\text{FBS}$):** Measured after **8 to 12 hours** of overnight caloric fast; reflects basal hepatic glucose output (gluconeogenesis/glycogenolysis) and hepatic insulin sensitivity.
- **2-Hour Post-Prandial Blood Sugar ($\text{PPBS}$):** Measured exactly **2 hours from the first bite** of a meal; reflects first- and second-phase pancreatic $\beta$-cell insulin secretion and peripheral skeletal muscle glucose uptake.

**Key Clinical & Pre-Analytical Scenarios:**
- **Post-Prandial Glucose Lower Than Fasting ($\text{PPBS} < \text{FBS}$):** Commonly observed in **early insulin resistance / reactive hyperinsulinemia** (exaggerated delayed second-phase insulin surge), diabetic gastroparesis (delayed gastric emptying), low-carbohydrate meals, vigorous post-meal physical activity, or rapid-acting secretagogues/insulin.
- **Renal Glycosuria Note:** Urine glucose typically appears only when plasma glucose exceeds the **renal proximal tubular $\text{SGLT2}$ threshold ($\sim 180\text{ mg/dL}$)**. However, in **pregnancy** (lowered threshold), **SGLT2 inhibitor therapy** (*Dapagliflozin, Empagliflozin, Canagliflozin*), or Fanconi syndrome, marked glycosuria ($3+/4+$) is an **expected therapeutic/physiological finding** even with normal blood glucose; conversely, elderly/CKD patients have elevated thresholds ($> 220\text{ mg/dL}$) with negative urine sugar despite severe hyperglycemia.
- **🛡️ Pre-Analytical Glycolysis Shield:** In unseparated whole blood without Sodium Fluoride ($\text{NaF}$) / Citrate-EDTA buffer, cellular enolase/hexokinase glycolysis consumes glucose at **$5\%\text{–}7\%\text{ per hour}$ ($7\text{–}10\text{ mg/dL/hr}$)** at room temperature, and up to $25\text{ mg/dL/hr}$ in leukocytosis, erythrocytosis, or bacterial contamination. Capillary glucometer whole-blood readings differ by **$10\%\text{–}15\%$** from laboratory venous plasma Hexokinase/GOD-POD assays and are not interchangeable for primary diagnosis. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*

## 🤰 2.1d — STANDALONE: Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (`OGTT` — Adult & Gestational Diabetes `DIPSI` / `IADPSG`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Haryana OB-GYN Dual-Guideline Standard:** Both **IADPSG (Fasting 3-sample $75\text{g}$ OGTT)** and **DIPSI / MoHFW Govt. of India (Non-fasting single-step 2-hour $75\text{g}$ OGTT)** are practiced across Haryana hospitals. Providing both criteria side-by-side prevents clinical ambiguity between obstetricians and endocrinologists.

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — OGTT & GESTATIONAL DIABETES (GDM) INTERPRETATION]**

**1. Non-Pregnant Adult $75\text{g}$ Anhydrous Glucose OGTT (ADA / WHO):**
- **Fasting ($0\text{ Hr}$):** Normal $< 100\text{ mg/dL}$ | Impaired Fasting Glucose (IFG): $100\text{–}125\text{ mg/dL}$ | Diabetes: $\ge 126\text{ mg/dL}$
- **2-Hour Post-$75\text{g}$ Load:** Normal $< 140\text{ mg/dL}$ | Impaired Glucose Tolerance (IGT): $140\text{–}199\text{ mg/dL}$ | Diabetes: $\ge 200\text{ mg/dL}$

**2. Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM) Screening in Pregnancy ($24\text{–}28\text{ Weeks}$ or First Antenatal Visit):**
| Guideline Protocol | Fasting State | Fasting Cutoff | 1-Hour Cutoff | 2-Hour Cutoff | Diagnostic Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IADPSG / ADA / WHO One-Step ($75\text{g}$ OGTT)** | **8–12h Overnight Fast Required** | $\ge 92\text{ mg/dL}$ ($5.1\text{ mmol/L}$) | $\ge 180\text{ mg/dL}$ ($10.0\text{ mmol/L}$) | $\ge 153\text{ mg/dL}$ ($8.5\text{ mmol/L}$) | **Any SINGLE value** meeting or exceeding threshold is diagnostic of GDM. |
| **DIPSI / MoHFW India ($75\text{g}$ Single-Sample)** | **Irrespective of Last Meal (Fasting or Non-Fasting)** | — | — | **$\ge 140\text{ mg/dL}$** ($7.8\text{ mmol/L}$) | 2-Hr Venous Plasma Glucose $\ge 140\text{ mg/dL}$ is diagnostic of GDM *($120\text{–}139\text{ mg/dL}$ = Gestational Glucose Intolerance [GGI]; $\ge 200\text{ mg/dL}$ = Overt Diabetes in Pregnancy)*. |

**🛡️ Pre-Analytical & Legal Shield:** Patient must ingest $75\text{g}$ anhydrous glucose ($82.5\text{g}$ glucose monohydrate) dissolved in $300\text{ mL}$ water over 5–10 minutes, remain seated, and abstain from smoking, tea/coffee, food, or walking during the 2-hour test window. Vomiting of the glucose load or acute intercurrent illness invalidates the test. *Correlate clinically; not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
