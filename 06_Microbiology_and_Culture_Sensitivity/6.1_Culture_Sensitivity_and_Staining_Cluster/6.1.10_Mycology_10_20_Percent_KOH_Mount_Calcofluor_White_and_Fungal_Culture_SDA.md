## ⚙️ Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `CLSI EP15/EP06` Verification, Calibration, Six Sigma IQC & Comparability

1. **International Method Principle:**
   - **Assay Methodology:** Direct Keratinolytic Clearing Microscopy (**10% KOH with 40% Dimethyl Sulfoxide [DMSO] & Glycerol** for Skin/Corneal/Sputum/Tissue; **20–40% KOH** for Nail Clippings) + **Calcofluor White (0.1% CFW + 10% KOH) UV/Violet Fluorescence (355–410 nm)** + Dual-Temperature **Sabouraud Dextrose Agar (SDA with Chloramphenicol ± Cycloheximide) & Brain-Heart Infusion (BHI) Agar incubated at BOTH 25–28°C (Molds/Saprophytes) AND 37°C (Yeasts/Thermal Dimorphic Fungi) for 4 Weeks (28 Days)** + **Lactophenol Cotton Blue (LPCB) Scotch-Tape / Slide-Culture Tease Mount** + **HiCrome / CHROMagar Candida Plus & VITEK-2 YST v8.01+ / MALDI-TOF MS (for *Candida auris*)** (Potassium Hydroxide (10% / 20% KOH + 40% DMSO) Direct Fungal Mount, Calcofluor White Chitin Fluorescence, Mucorales (Broad Aseptate 90° Ribbon Hyphae) vs. Aspergillus (Narrow Septate 45° Dichotomous Hyphae) Emergency Triage, Dermatophyte (Trichophyton / Microsporum / Epidermophyton) Culture, Candida auris Identification)
   - **Chemical / Immunological / Analytical Reaction Principle:**
     Primary Analytical Principle: Direct 10–20% KOH / Calcofluor White Chitin-Binding Microscopy + Dual-Tube SDA Culture (Tube 1: SDA with Chloramphenicol/Gentamicin [Cycloheximide-FREE!]; Tube 2: SDA with Chloramphenicol + Cycloheximide [Mycosel/Dermatophyte Test Medium for skin/hair/nails]) + 2-Hour Human Serum Germ-Tube Test at 37°C + CHROMagar + LPCB Conidial Ontogeny. Calibration: Calcofluor White Fluorescence Filter Check (365 nm Excitation / 435 nm Emission) + Dual Incubator Temperature Logs (25°C & 37°C) + VITEK-2 YST v8.01+ / CHROMagar Control Strains (Daily temperature check + Every batch of KOH/CFW/CHROMagar). Reference/Confirmatory Standard: Direct KOH/Calcofluor Microscopy + Histopathology (GMS/PAS) + Dual-Temperature SDA Culture + MALDI-TOF MS / ITS Sequencing + CLSI M27/M38 Broth Microdilution Antifungal MIC.
2. **Universal Step-by-Step Operational Protocol (Hardware-Agnostic):**
   - **Step 1 (Pre-Run Environmental, Biosafety & Media Sterility Verification):** Verify Class II Biosafety Cabinet (`BSC-II`) airflow velocity, incubator temperatures ($35.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ aerobic / $5\%\text{ CO}_2$; $28^\circ\text{C}$ & $37^\circ\text{C}$ fungal), uninoculated media batch sterility ($48\text{h}$ check), pH ($7.2\text{–}7.4$ for Mueller-Hinton Agar), and Gram/ZN/Auramine stain daily control slides.
   - **Step 2 (Calibration, McFarland Turbidity & Traceability):** Verify calibrated nichrome/sterile loops ($1.0\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ & $10.0\text{ }\mu\text{L}$), densitometer **`0.5 McFarland` ($1.5 \times 10^8\text{ CFU/mL}$) barium sulfate/latex standard** for `AST` inoculum standardization, automated `VITEK-2`/`BACTEC`/`GeneXpert` optical calibration, and ocular micrometer calibration.

(5.5 Sigma). External Proficiency Testing (EQAS): CAP Surveys (Mycology F) / PGIMER Chandigarh (WHO Collaborating Centre for Reference & Research on Fungi of Medical Importance — National Mycology EQAS India) / UK NEQAS Mycology.

3. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`), `LoD`/`LoQ` & Clinical Reportable Range (`CRR`):**
   - Analytical Measuring Range (AMR): Direct KOH/CFW Microscopy + 28-Day Culture to Definitive Species & CLSI M27/M38 Antifungal MIC
