### 5.2 Physiological, Obstetric, Acute Trauma/ICU & Environmental Modifiers
- **The `3-Day Rule` for Hospitalized Stool Cultures (`ASM / IDSA`):** In patients hospitalized for **$> 72\text{ hours}$ (`> 3 days`)** who develop nosocomial diarrhea, routine enteric culture for *Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter*, and *Vibrio* has a $< 0.5\%$ yield and should NOT be performed—instead test for ***Clostridioides difficile* Toxin A/B + `GDH`**!
- **Contraindication to Antibiotics in `STEC` (*E. coli* `O157:H7`) vs Mandatory Treatment in *Shigella/Cholera*:** In bloody diarrhea caused by **Shiga-toxin-producing *E. coli* (`STEC`)**, administering fluoroquinolones, $\beta$-lactams, or TMP-SMX induces bacteriophage lysis and massive Shiga-toxin release, **precipitating Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (`HUS`)**!
- **Estrogen & Reproductive Age Modifiers on High Vaginal Swab (`HVS` `Nugent Score`):** During reproductive years, estrogen drives glycogen production and *Lactobacillus crispatus* dominance ($\text{pH } 3.8\text{–}4.5$; `Nugent Score 0–3`), whereas prepubertal girls and postmenopausal women have physiological low-*Lactobacillus* flora and higher vaginal $\text{pH}$ without Bacterial Vaginosis.
