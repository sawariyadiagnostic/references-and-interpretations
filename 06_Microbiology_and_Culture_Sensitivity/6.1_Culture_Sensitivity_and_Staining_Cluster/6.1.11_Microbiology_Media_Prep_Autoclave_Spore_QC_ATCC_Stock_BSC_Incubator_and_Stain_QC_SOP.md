## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Medical Microbiology Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To establish the comprehensive quality assurance, sterility, growth-promotion, and equipment verification protocols required by **NABL 112 (`Specific Criteria for Medical Microbiology`)** and **CLSI M02 / M07 / M100 (34th Edition)** across:
   - **Culture Media Preparation & Acceptance (`ISO/TS 11133` / `CLSI M22`):** Batch $\text{pH}$ verification ($25^\circ\text{C}$), **$4.0\text{ mm} \pm 0.5\text{ mm}$ Mueller-Hinton Agar (`MHA`) Depth & Divalent Cation ($\text{Ca}^{2+}/\text{Mg}^{2+}$) Verification**, $5\%$ Sterility Incubation ($35^\circ\text{C} \times 48\text{h}$), and Growth-Promotion / Inhibition (`GPT`) using **`ATCC` Reference Control Strains**.
   - **ATCC Reference Strain Master Bank (`-70°C / -80°C` Cryo-Beads — Passage $\le 5$ Rule)** & Weekly Working Subculture Protocol.
   - **Autoclave Physical, Chemical & Weekly Biological Spore Indicator (`Geobacillus stearothermophilus ATCC 7953` — $121^\circ\text{C} \times 15\text{ min}$) Verification** for Media Sterilization vs. Dedicated Biohazard Decontamination Autoclaves (`BMW Rules 2016`).
   - **Class II Type A2 Biosafety Cabinet (`BSC` — Inflow Velocity, HEPA Integrity, Magnehelic Gauge & UV-C Lamp Hour Log)**, **$35^\circ\text{C} \pm 1^\circ\text{C}$ Aerobic / $5\%\text{ CO}_2$ / $28^\circ\text{C}$ BOD Fungal Incubators**, and **Automated Blood Culture System (`BACT/ALERT` / `BACTEC`) Daily Calibration**.
   - **Daily Microbiological Stain QC (`Gram`, `Ziehl-Neelsen AFB`, `Auramine-O`, `Albert's`, `India Ink`, `10% KOH / LPCB`)** and **Weekly `CLSI M100` Antimicrobial Susceptibility Disk (`AST`) Zone-Diameter QC**.

### 2.2 The 4-Tier `ATCC` Passage Hierarchy (`Maximum 5 Passages from Reference Stock — CLSI M02`)
$$\text{Lyophilized Reference Pellet (P0)} \longrightarrow \text{Master Cryo-Bead Bank at } -80^\circ\text{C } (\text{P1}) \longrightarrow \text{Monthly Stock Slant } (\text{P2}) \longrightarrow \text{Weekly Working Plate } (\text{P3–P4})$$
- **Never subculture a working `ATCC` strain beyond Passage 5 (`P5`)**, and **NEVER use clinical patient isolates as routine `AST` quality control strains** (clinical isolates undergo spontaneous plasmid loss and MIC drift!).

## Section 4: Two-Autoclave Segregation (`Clean Media` vs `Biohazard Decontamination`) & Weekly Spore QC
1. **Strict Physical Separation (`NABL 112 & BMW Rules 2016`):**
   - **Autoclave #1 (`Clean Media & Glassware Sterilization Autoclave`):** Located in the Clean Media Preparation Room; operated at **$121^\circ\text{C}$ ($15\text{ psi}$) for $15\text{ minutes}$** (or $115^\circ\text{C}$ for $10\text{ min}$ for heat-labile sugar broths).
   - **Autoclave #2 (`Dedicated Biohazard Waste & Used Culture Decontamination Autoclave`):** Located in the Washing/Decontamination Zone; operated at **$121^\circ\text{C}$ ($15\text{ psi}$) for $30\text{–}45\text{ minutes}$** (because agar plates and blood culture bottles require longer thermal penetration!). **NEVER sterilize clean culture media inside a biohazard waste autoclave!**
2. **3-Tier Autoclave Sterilization Verification:**
   - **Every Load (Physical + Class-1/Class-4/5 Chemical Integrator):** Record cycle temperature ($121^\circ\text{C}$), pressure ($15\text{ psi}$), holding time ($15\text{ min}$ / $30\text{ min}$), Bowie-Dick / Autoclave Indicator Tape color change, and internal **Class-5 Chemical Integrator Strip** placed in the geometric center of the load.
   - **Weekly Biological Spore Indicator (`Geobacillus stearothermophilus ATCC 7953`, $10^6\text{ spores/ampoule}$):**
     - Place a self-contained *Geobacillus stearothermophilus* spore ampoule inside the center of the largest media flask/waste bag during the Monday cycle.
     - Incubate the autoclaved test ampoule alongside an **un-autoclaved Positive Control ampoule** at **$56^\circ\text{C}\text{–}60^\circ\text{C}$ for $24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$**:
       - **Test Ampoule:** Must remain **Purple (`No Growth / No Acid — PASS`)**.
       - **Un-Autoclaved Control Ampoule:** Must turn **Yellow (`Turbid Growth / Acid — Viability Confirmed`)**.

## Section 5: Biosafety Cabinet (`Class II A2 BSC`), Incubator ($35^\circ\text{C} / \text{CO}_2 / 28^\circ\text{C}$) & Daily Stain / Disk QC
1. **Class II Type A2 Biosafety Cabinet (`BSC` — Mandatory for All Sputum `AFB`/`CBNAAT`, Fungal & Aerosol Procedures):**
   - **Daily Before Work:** Turn on blower for $15\text{ min}$ to purge air; verify **Magnehelic Differential Pressure Gauge** across the `HEPA` filter is within the calibrated green zone ($\text{typically } 10\text{–}25\text{ mm WG}$ / $0.4\text{–}1.0\text{ in WG}$); wipe work deck with **$70\%$ `IPA`** (or $1\%$ NaOCl followed by $70\%$ `IPA` to prevent stainless-steel pitting; **5% Phenol / Lysol** for Mycobacteriology); log cumulative **UV-C Germicidal Lamp ($254\text{ nm}$) hours**.
   - **Annual Third-Party NABL-Traceable Certification (`NSF/ANSI 49`):** Downflow velocity ($0.30\text{–}0.50\text{ m/s}$), Inflow face velocity (**$\ge 0.50\text{ m/s}$ [$100\text{ fpm}$]**), `PAO/DOP` `HEPA` Filter Leak Test ($99.99\%$ retention at $0.3\ \mu\text{m}$), and Smoke-Pattern Airflow Visualization.
2. **Daily Microbiological Stain & Reagent Quality Control (`Every Morning Before Reading Patient Slides`):**
   - **Gram Stain (`6.1.9`):** Stain 1 control slide carrying mixed ***S. aureus* `ATCC 25923` (Deep Violet `GPC`) + *E. coli* `ATCC 25922` (Crisp Pink `GNR`)** every morning and on every new bottle of Crystal Violet, Gram's Iodine, Decolorizer, or Safranin.
   - **Ziehl-Neelsen (`ZN`) & Auramine-O `AFB` Stain (`6.1.7`):** Stain 1 Known Positive `AFB` Control Slide + 1 Known Negative (*E. coli*) Slide daily.
   - **Biochemical Identification Reagents (Catalase $3\%\text{ H}_2\text{O}_2$, Oxidase $1\%$ Tetramethyl-$p$-phenylenediamine, Coagulase Plasma, Indole Kovac's):** Verify daily/per-run with Positive & Negative `ATCC` controls; verify **$0.5\text{ McFarland}$ BaSO4 Nephelometer (`Densichek`)** ($0.08\text{–}0.10\text{ OD}$ at $625\text{ nm} = 1.5 \times 10^8\text{ CFU/mL}$) before setting up any `AST`!
