### 5.2 Physiological, Obstetric, Acute Trauma/ICU & Environmental Modifiers
- **Ultra-Sensitivity Limit of Detection (`15.6 CFU/mL` via Multi-Copy `IS6110`/`IS1081`):** `GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra` improves sensitivity over first-generation `Xpert` from $112.6\text{ CFU/mL}$ down to **$15.6\text{ CFU/mL}$**, raising sensitivity in **Smear-Negative Pulmonary TB to $78\%$**, **HIV-TB to $90\%$**, and **Tuberculous Meningitis (`CSF`) to $70\%\text{–}95\%$**.
- **Interpretation of `'MTB Detected — TRACE'` (`rpoB` Indeterminate):** Because `'Trace'` detects multi-copy insertion sequences (`IS6110`/`IS1081`) below the single-copy `rpoB` threshold, Rifampicin resistance is always Indeterminate: in **CSF, Lymph Node/Tissue, Pediatric, or HIV+ patients**, `'Trace'` is treated as **True Positive Active TB**; in **non-HIV adults with prior treated TB within the last $5\text{ years}$**, `'Trace'` may reflect residual dead DNA from old healed TB—correlate with **`MGIT-960` Liquid Culture**!
- **Dead DNA Persistence (Why `CBNAAT` is Contraindicated for Treatment Monitoring):** `PCR` amplifies DNA from dead *M. tuberculosis* for **$1\text{ to }4\text{ years}$ after successful cure**; follow-up monitoring at Month 2 and Month 6 must use **`AFB Smear` (`6.1.7`) & `MGIT Culture`**.

## 🔬 Section 7: In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics ($t_{1/2}$), Pathophysiological Severity Grading, Differential Diagnosis & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

### 7.1 In-Vivo Kinetics & Turnover Dynamics
Mycobacterial DNA from non-viable dead bacilli persists in lung cavities and macrophages for **1 to 5+ years** after complete microbiological cure.

### 7.2 Pathophysiological Differential Diagnosis
- **Causes of Elevated / Positive / Abnormal-High Values:**
  MTB DETECTED (High / Medium / Low / Very Low) — RIFAMPICIN RESISTANCE NOT DETECTED: CRITICAL POSITIVE: *MYCOBACTERIUM TUBERCULOSIS* COMPLEX DNA DETECTED — **RIFAMPICIN RESISTANCE NOT DETECTED** (Wild-Type *rpoB* 81-bp RRDR Confirmed by Melting-Curve Analysis).
  • **Recommended Action (NTEP India Guidelines)**: Initiate standard Weight-Band First-Line Anti-Tubercular Therapy (**2HRZE / 4HRE**), AND reflex specimen to **First-Line Line-Probe Assay (FL-LPA — *katG* & *inhA*) or Xpert MTB/XDR** per NTEP Universal DST policy to rule out **Isoniazid-Mono-Resistant TB (Hr-TB)**.
  • **Treatment Monitoring Rule**: Do NOT repeat CBNAAT/Truenat during or after therapy to assess treatment response (dead bacillary DNA persists for years); monitor pulmonary TB response with **AFB Smear (6.1.7) and MGIT Culture at Month 2 and Month 6**. | MTB DETECTED — TRACE (IS6110/IS1081 Positive; rpoB Negative) — RIFAMPICIN RESISTANCE INDETERMINATE: CRITICAL PAUCIBACILLARY POSITIVE: *MYCOBACTERIUM TUBERCULOSIS* COMPLEX DNA DETECTED (**TRACE LOAD** — ~15 to 50 CFU/mL) | **RIFAMPICIN RESISTANCE: INDETERMINATE**.
