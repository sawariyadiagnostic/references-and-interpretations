## Section 3: Semi-Annual Cumulative Hospital Antibiogram (`CLSI M39`) & Intrinsic Resistance Suppression Rules
1. **`CLSI M39` Cumulative Antibiogram Construction (Published Semi-Annually to Hospital `HICC` & Clinicians):**
   - Extract **only the FIRST diagnostic isolate per patient per species per analysis period** (exclude duplicate surveillance/repeat isolates to prevent MDR bias!).
   - Stratify `% Susceptible (%S — excluding Intermediate %I)` by **Clinical Setting (`OPD` vs `IPD General Wards` vs `ICU`)** and **Specimen Source (`Urine` vs `Non-Urine / Blood / Respiratory`)** for any species with **$\ge 30\text{ non-duplicate isolates}$**.
2. **Mandatory `CLSI M100` Intrinsic Resistance & Cascade Reporting Rules Verified in LIMS:**
   - **Never report** Cephalosporins, Aminoglycosides, Clindamycin, or Trimethoprim-Sulfamethoxazole as `"Susceptible"` for ***Enterococcus* spp.** (causes lethal treatment failure in vivo!).
   - **Never report** 1st/2nd-generation Cephalosporins, Cephamycins, or Aminoglycosides as `"Susceptible"` for ***Salmonella* and *Shigella* spp.**
   - **Never report** **Daptomycin** on Respiratory (`Sputum / ET / BAL`) isolates (inactivated by pulmonary surfactant!), and **never report Nitrofurantoin or Fosfomycin** on non-urinary (`Blood / Pus / CSF`) isolates!
