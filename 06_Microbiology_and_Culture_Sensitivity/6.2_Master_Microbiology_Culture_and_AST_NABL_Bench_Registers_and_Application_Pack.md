### Cumulative Hospital Antibiogram (`CLSI M39`) & Intrinsic Resistance Rules
1. **Antibiogram Compilation:** The Hospital Infection Control Committee (HICC) compiles and publishes a cumulative antibiogram semi-annually per `CLSI M39-Ed5`. Data includes only the **first diagnostic isolate per patient per species** during the analysis period to prevent duplicate multidrug-resistant isolate bias. Minimum threshold for reporting: $\ge 30\ \text{non-duplicate isolates}$.
2. **Intrinsic Resistance Suppression Rules (Enforced by LIMS Hard-Locks):**
   - *Enterococcus spp.:* Automatically suppress Cephalosporins, Aminoglycosides (routine therapy), Clindamycin, and SXT (causes lethal clinical failure in vivo).
   - *Salmonella / Shigella spp.:* Automatically suppress 1st/2nd-generation Cephalosporins, Cephamycins, and Aminoglycosides.
   - *Pseudomonas aeruginosa:* Automatically suppress Ampicillin, Amoxicillin-Clavulanate, Cefotaxime, Ceftriaxone, Ertapenem, Chloramphenicol, and Tetracyclines.
   - *Stenotrophomonas maltophilia:* Automatically suppress all beta-lactams and carbapenems except Ceftazidime (due to intrinsic L1 metallo-beta-lactamase and L2 serine-beta-lactamase). Drug of choice: Trimethoprim-Sulfamethoxazole (SXT).

### Mathematical Calibration & Metrological Equations
1. **Gravimetric Inoculation Loop Calibration Equation (`ISO 8655-7 / CLSI M22-A3`):**
   $$V = \frac{W_1 - W_0}{\rho_{\text{water}} \times Z_T}$$
   Where $W_0$ is tare weight of sterile boat, $W_1$ is weight after 10 loop deliveries of deionized water, $\rho_{\text{water}} = 0.9982\ \text{g/cm}^3$ at $20^\circ\text{C}$, and $Z_T$ is atmospheric temperature-pressure correction factor. For a $1\ \mu\text{L}$ loop, 10 deliveries must weigh $10.0 \pm 1.0\ \text{mg}$.
2. **Spectrophotometric Nephelometric Verification Formula (`CLSI M02`):**
   $$A_{625} = -\log_{10}\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right)$$
   Absorbance of the working 0.5 McFarland $\text{BaSO}_4$ standard is measured in a matched $10\text{-mm}$ optical cuvette at $\lambda = 625\ \text{nm}$. Acceptable optical density: $0.080 \le A_{625} \le 0.130$.
3. **Sterilization Lethality Integral ($F_0$ Value per `ISO 11134`):**
   $$F_0 = \Delta t \sum 10^{\frac{T - 121.1}{z}}$$
   Where $T$ is temperature measured by thermocouple inside the liquid core, $\Delta t$ is measurement time interval ($1\ \text{minute}$), and $z = 10^\circ\text{C}$ for *Geobacillus stearothermophilus*. An autoclave cycle is verified as sterile only if total accumulated $F_0 \ge 15.0\ \text{minutes}$.
4. **Microscopic Micrometry Reticle Calibration Formula:**
   $$\text{Calibration Factor}\ (\mu\text{m/ocular unit}) = \frac{\text{Stage Micrometer Distance}\ (\mu\text{m})}{\text{Number of Ocular Divisions Overlapping}}$$
   Calibrated annually for each objective ($10\times, 40\times, 100\times$) using NABL-certified stage micrometer ($0.01\ \text{mm}$ division).

## Section 4: Internal Quality Control (IQC) Protocol per `NABL 112` & `CLSI M100-Ed34`
Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing (`AST`) quality control at Sawariya Diagnostic Lab is structured on the rigorous statistical validation framework mandated by **CLSI M100 34th Edition (2024)** and **NABL 112**:

### Daily vs Weekly AST IQC Conversion Protocol (`CLSI M100 Table 4`)
1. **Daily IQC Requirement:** Daily testing of all standard reference strains (*E. coli* ATCC 25922, *S. aureus* ATCC 25923, *P. aeruginosa* ATCC 27853, *E. faecalis* ATCC 29212) must be performed for **20 or 30 consecutive calendar testing days** whenever:
   - A new antimicrobial disk or automated testing panel is introduced.
   - A new lot of Mueller-Hinton Agar or AST disk cartridges is received.
   - Significant equipment maintenance, incubator replacement, or major personnel changes occur.
2. **Weekly Testing Conversion Criteria:** The laboratory is permitted to switch from Daily to Weekly AST IQC only if:
   - **For 20 consecutive days:** No more than $1$ out of $20$ zone diameters falls outside the CLSI acceptable range for each individual drug-organism combination.
   - **For 30 consecutive days:** No more than $3$ out of $30$ zone diameters fall outside the CLSI acceptable range for each individual drug-organism combination.
3. **Out-of-Control Action on Weekly Testing:** If during weekly testing any single zone diameter falls outside the CLSI acceptable range:
   - Perform immediate root-cause check (inoculum turbidity, disk expiry, agar depth, incubator temp).
   - If an obvious error (wrong disk, uncalibrated caliper) is identified and corrected, retest immediately on that day.
   - If no obvious cause is identified, **immediately revert to Daily IQC for 5 consecutive days**. All 5 consecutive days must fall within acceptable limits before weekly testing can be reinstated.

### Technologist Monthly Concordance Protocol (Cohen's Kappa $\kappa \ge 0.85$)
Every month, 5 bench technologists and duty pathologists independently examine a panel of 20 pre-characterized blinded microscopic slides (5 Gram stains, 5 AFB ZN smears, 5 Albert stains, 5 KOH fungal mounts). Inter-observer agreement is statistically assessed using Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$):
$$\kappa = \frac{P_o - P_e}{1 - P_e}$$
Where $P_o$ is the observed proportional agreement and $P_e$ is the expected agreement by chance. The minimum NABL-accredited performance standard is $\kappa \ge 0.85$. Any technologist scoring $\kappa < 0.85$ is immediately scheduled for 10 hours of supervised bench retraining and must achieve $\kappa \ge 0.90$ on a re-test panel before releasing patient results independently.

### Statistical Evaluation & Out-of-Control CAPA Algorithm
EQAS performance is evaluated via the Standard Deviation Index ($SDI$ / $Z$-score):
$$Z\text{-score} = \frac{x_{\text{lab}} - X_{\text{assigned}}}{\sigma_{\text{PT}}}$$
- **$\lvert Z \rvert \le 2.0$:** Satisfactory Performance.
- **$2.0 < \lvert Z \rvert < 3.0$:** Questionable / Warning. Requires internal review and re-examination of working strain handling.
- **$\lvert Z \rvert \ge 3.0$ OR Any Categorical AST Error:** Unsatisfactory Performance.
  - **Very Major Error (VME):** Lab reports *Susceptible* when Reference is *Resistant* (False Susceptible). Critical hazard to patient life; zero tolerance permitted.
  - **Major Error (ME):** Lab reports *Resistant* when Reference is *Susceptible* (False Resistant). Unnecessary escalation of antimicrobial therapy.
  - **Mandatory CAPA:** Upon notification of any $\lvert Z \rvert \ge 3.0$, VME, or ME, the Quality Manager triggers a formal Root Cause Analysis within 72 hours using the 5-Why and Ishikawa (Fishbone) model across Materials, Machine, Milieu, Measurement, and Manpower. Corrective and preventive actions are verified through a blinded internal PT challenge within 30 days.

### Five-Percent Batch Sterility Verification Protocol
From every media preparation autoclave run, **$5\%$ of the total prepared plates (minimum 2 plates, maximum 10 plates)** are randomly sampled from the beginning, middle, and end of the pouring run. Samples are incubated at $35 \pm 2^\circ\text{C}$ for $48\text{ hours}$ (and an additional $5\text{ days}$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$ for fungal media). The entire batch is released for clinical use only if **zero microbial growth ($0\ \text{CFU}$)** is observed across all sterility check plates. If even a single colony is detected, the entire batch is quarantined, autoclaved, and discarded.
