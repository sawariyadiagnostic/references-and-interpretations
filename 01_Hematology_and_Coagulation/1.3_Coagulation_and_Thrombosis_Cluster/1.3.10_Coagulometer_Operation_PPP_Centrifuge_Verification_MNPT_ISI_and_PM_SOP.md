## Section 1: Purpose, Regulatory Scope & NABL 112 Compliance Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To define the complete standard operating procedure for **Automated / Semi-Automated Coagulometer Operation**, daily **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.2^\circ\text{C}$ reaction-well thermal verification**, **Lyophilized Thromboplastin/Cephalin Reagent Reconstitution & Stability Tracking**, **Geometric Mean Normal Prothrombin Time (`MNPT`) & `ISI` Verification**, and **Semi-Annual Centrifuge Platelet-Poor Plasma (`PPP < 10 x 10^3/uL`) Qualification**.
2. **NABL 112 & International Regulatory Standards:**
   - **NABL 112 (`Specific Criteria for Hematology & Immunohaematology`):** Explicitly mandates:
     1. Daily verification of the coagulometer incubation and reaction block at **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$** (target $\pm 0.2^\circ\text{C}$).
     2. Local calculation/verification of **Geometric Mean Normal Prothrombin Time (`MNPT`)** from **$n \ge 20$ normal healthy donors** for every new lot of `PT` Thromboplastin reagent.
     3. Documented **Semi-Annual Centrifuge Verification** proving that harvested Citrate plasma achieves a residual platelet count **$< 10 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$ (`< 10 \times 10^9/\text{L}`)**.
     4. Two levels of Coagulation Internal Quality Control (**`Level 1: Normal Control Plasma`** and **`Level 2: Abnormal Therapeutic/Pathological Control Plasma`**) run every shift / every $8\text{ hours}$.
   - **CLSI H21-A5 (`Collection, Transport, and Processing of Blood Specimens for Testing Plasma-Based Coagulation Assays`).**
   - **CLSI H47-A2 (`One-Stage Prothrombin Time (PT) Test and Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (APTT) Test`) & CLSI H54-A (`Procedures for Validation of INR and Local Calibration of PT/INR Systems`).**

## Section 3: Daily Coagulometer Startup, $37.0^\circ\text{C}$ Thermal Block QC & Optical Self-Test
Enzymatic coagulation cascades (`Factor VIIa/TF`, `Factor Xa/Va Prothrombinase`, and `Thrombin`) are exponentially temperature-dependent: a deviation of just **$\pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ from $37.0^\circ\text{C}$ alters `PT` by $0.8\text{–}1.5\text{ seconds}$ and `aPTT` by $2.5\text{–}4.5\text{ seconds}$**!
1. **Daily Thermal Equilibration & Verification (`07:30 AM`):**
   - Switch ON the coagulometer and allow a mandatory **$15\text{–}20\text{ minute}$ thermal stabilization period**.
   - Verify the digital incubation-block and reagent-well temperature display (**Mandatory Target: $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.2^\circ\text{C}$; NABL Absolute Permissible Range: $36.5^\circ\text{C} \text{ to } 37.5^\circ\text{C}$**).
   - **Weekly External NABL-Traceable Thermometer Cross-Check:** Every Monday morning, place $500\ \mu\text{L}$ of distilled water into a reaction cuvette inside the $37^\circ\text{C}$ incubation block, insert a **NABL-Calibrated Digital RTD Pt100 Probe Thermometer**, and verify after $5\text{ minutes}$ that the true liquid temperature matches the display within **$\pm 0.2^\circ\text{C}$**.
2. **Optical LED Intensity & Cuvette Blank Check:**
   - Verify that all optical channels ($405\text{ nm}$, $575\text{ nm}$, $660\text{ nm}$) pass the startup self-test with `0` stray dust/light alarms.
3. **Micropipette & Starter-Pipette Verification (`ISO 8655`):**
   - Inspect the automatic reagent-trigger pipette (or dedicated $50\ \mu\text{L}$, $100\ \mu\text{L}$, $200\ \mu\text{L}$ coagulation micropipettes) for smooth plunger action and clean silicone O-ring seal.

## Section 4: Lyophilized Coagulation Reagent Reconstitution, Maturation & Stability Protocol
Improper reconstitution of lyophilized thromboplastin or control plasma is the **#1 cause of analytical `PT`/`aPTT` shifts**. All bench staff must strictly follow the **5-Step Gentle Reconstitution Rule**:
1. **Temperature Equilibration Before Opening:** Remove the lyophilized reagent/control vial from $2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ storage and let it stand at room temperature ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for **$10\text{ minutes}$** before popping the vacuum seal.
2. **Volumetric Precision with Type-I `CLRW` Water (`>= 10 MOhm.cm`):**
   - Using a **NABL-calibrated Class A volumetric pipette or ISO 8655 verified micropipette**, dispense the exact manufacturer-specified volume ($1.00\text{ mL}$, $2.00\text{ mL}$, or $5.00\text{ mL}$) of **fresh Type-I `CLRW` Reagent Grade Water ($\text{pH } 6.5\text{–}7.0$, preservative-free)** down the inner glass wall of the vial.
   - *Never use normal saline ($0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$) or tap/RO water to reconstitute lyophilized plasma or thromboplastin (alters ionic strength and calcium concentration!).*
3. **Gentle Swirl & Mandatory $15\text{–}30\text{ Minute}$ Hydration Rest (`ZERO Vortexing`):**
   - Replace the rubber stopper, gently swirl in a figure-of-eight motion for $10\text{ seconds}$ to wet all cake powder, and **allow the vial to stand undisturbed at room temperature ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for $15\text{–}30\text{ minutes}$** (`30 min` for Thromboplastin / `Lupus dRVVT`; `15 min` for Control Plasma).
   - **STRICT PROHIBITION:** **NEVER shake vigorously or vortex coagulation reagents or control plasmas!** Vigorous shaking denatures labile **Factor V and Factor VIII** at the air-liquid interface and disrupts liposomal phospholipid micelles!
4. **Pre-Warming Prior to Assay Execution:**
   - Pre-warm working `PT` Thromboplastin and $0.025\text{ M}$ Calcium Chloride ($\text{CaCl}_2$) in the **$37.0^\circ\text{C}$ reagent well for a minimum of $5\text{–}10\text{ minutes}$** (and maximum $2\text{ hours}$ per aliquot) before initiating clotting reactions.
5. **Mandatory Reconstitution Labeling & On-Board / Refrigerated Stability Limits:**
   - Every reconstituted vial must be immediately labeled with: **`[Date & Time of Reconstitution] + [Expiry Date/Time] + [Tech Initials]`**.

| Coagulation Reagent / Control | Post-Reconstitution Stability at $2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ | Maximum Stability at $37.0^\circ\text{C}$ (In-Use Well) | Critical Degradation Sign |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **`PT` Recombinant / Rabbit Brain Thromboplastin (`ISI 0.95–1.15`)** | **$5\text{ – }7\text{ Days}$** (tightly capped) | **$2\text{ – }4\text{ Hours}$** (aliquot only daily need!) | Upward drift in Normal Control `PT` $> +0.8\text{ sec}$. |
| **`aPTT` Cephalin + Ellagic Acid / Micronized Silica** | **$14\text{ – }30\text{ Days}$** (ready-to-use liquid) | **$4\text{ Hours}$** (Resuspend silica before pipetting!) | Silica settling causes falsely prolonged `aPTT` if not mixed. |
| **$0.025\text{ M}$ Calcium Chloride ($\text{CaCl}_2$)** | **$30\text{ Days}$** after opening | **$8\text{ Hours}$** at $37^\circ\text{C}$ | Microbial growth or evaporation concentrates $\text{Ca}^{2+}$. |
| **Normal (`Level 1`) & Abnormal (`Level 2`) Control Plasma** | **$24\text{ Hours}$** at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ (**$4\text{–}8\text{ Hours}$** at $18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) | **DO NOT store at $37^\circ\text{C}$!** (Pre-warm cuvette for $180\text{ sec}$ only) | Factor V/VIII decay causes progressive `aPTT`/`PT` prolongation after $8\text{h}$ at room temp! |

## Section 5: Geometric Mean Normal Prothrombin Time (`MNPT`) & Local `ISI` Verification (`CLSI H54-A`)
### 5.1 Why Arithmetic Mean Is Forbidden for `MNPT`
Prothrombin Time values in a healthy population follow a **right-skewed log-normal distribution**. Both the **World Health Organization (`WHO`)** and **`CLSI H54-A` / `NABL 112`** strictly mandate that the denominator of the `INR` equation must be the **Geometric Mean Normal Prothrombin Time (`MNPT`)** established locally on the laboratory's specific coagulometer and reagent lot.

### 5.2 Step-by-Step Protocol for Establishing & Verifying `MNPT` (Every New Thromboplastin Lot)
1. **Donor Cohort Selection ($n \ge 20$ Healthy Ambulatory Adults):**
   - Collect fresh $3.2\%$ Buffered Sodium Citrate ($9:1$) plasmas from **at least $20$ healthy, non-medicated adult subjects** ($10$ adult males and $10$ non-pregnant, non-OCP adult females aged $18\text{–}55\text{ years}$ with normal liver function).
   - Process to `PPP` within $< 1\text{ hour}$ and analyze across **at least $3$ separate working days** (to incorporate day-to-day analytical variation) using the new Thromboplastin reagent lot.
2. **Exact Mathematical Calculation of Geometric `MNPT`:**
   - Convert each of the $n=20$ individual normal `PT` results ($\text{PT}_1, \text{PT}_2, \dots, \text{PT}_{20}$) to its natural logarithm $\ln(\text{PT}_i)$, compute the mean of the natural logarithms, and take the antilog ($\exp$):
     $$\text{MNPT (seconds)} = \exp\left( \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{20} \ln(\text{PT}_i)}{20} \right) = \left( \text{PT}_1 \times \text{PT}_2 \times \dots \times \text{PT}_{20} \right)^{\frac{1}{20}}$$
   - **Rolling Lot Verification Shortcut (`CLSI H54-A`):** Once a full $20$-donor `MNPT` is established for a reagent/instrument platform, subsequent lots of the same thromboplastin with identical `ISI` may be verified using **$n = 5\text{–}10$ normal plasmas + 2 Certified INR Verification Plasmas** provided the new geometric mean lies within **$\pm 3.0\%$ ($\le 0.4\text{ seconds}$)** of the established `MNPT`.
3. **Programming `MNPT` and Instrument-Specific `ISI` into Coagulometer & LIMS:**
   - Enter the verified `MNPT` (e.g., $12.2\text{ sec}$) and the manufacturer's **Instrument/Method-Specific `ISI`** (mandatory target **`ISI` $\le 1.20$**, ideally **$0.95\text{–}1.08$**) into both the Coagulometer and the LIMS master formula:
     $$\text{INR} = \left( \frac{\text{Patient PT (sec)}}{\text{MNPT (sec)}} \right)^{\text{ISI}}$$
   - **Independent Manual Calculator Audit:** Upon every `MNPT`/`ISI` entry, the Senior Hematopathologist manually calculates the `INR` for `Level 1` (`PT = 12.2 sec -> INR = 1.00`) and `Level 2` (`PT = 28.5 sec -> INR = 2.38`) using a scientific calculator and signs off the **LIMS Calculation Verification Certificate (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7`)**.

## Section 6: Semi-Annual Coagulation Centrifuge Platelet-Poor Plasma (`PPP`) Verification SOP (`CLSI H21-A5`)
### 6.1 Clinical & Analytical Rationale
Residual platelets ($> 10 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$) in inadequately centrifuged Citrate plasma release **Platelet Factor 4 (`PF4`)** (which neutralizes Unfractionated Heparin in vitro, falsely shortening `aPTT`) and expose anionic **Phosphatidylserine phospholipids** upon freezing/thawing (which quench Lupus Anticoagulant antibodies, causing **false-negative `dRVVT`/`SCT` results**!).

### 6.2 Mandatory 6-Monthly Empirical Verification Procedure (`NABL 112` Audit Requirement)
Every **6 months** (January & July) and after any centrifuge motor/rotor repair:
1. **Step 1 (Tachometer & Timer Verification):** Verify that the Coagulation Centrifuge (`1,500 x g` program) achieves the exact required Rotational Speed ($\text{RPM}$) computed from its rotor radius $r_{\text{mm}}$ (`SOP 0.1.14`):
   $$\text{RPM} = 1,000 \times \sqrt{\frac{1,500}{1.118 \times r_{\text{mm}}}}$$
   *(Example: For rotor radius $r = 140\text{ mm}$, $1,500 \times g = \mathbf{3,096\text{ RPM}}$ for **$15\text{ minutes}$ with brake OFF**).*
2. **Step 2 (5-Sample `PPP` Residual Platelet Count Audit on `MEK-1503`):**
   - Select **5 random routine $3.2\%$ Buffered Sodium Citrate tubes** with normal baseline EDTA platelet counts ($150\text{–}400 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$).
   - Centrifuge all 5 Citrate tubes at **$1,500 \times g$ for $15\text{ minutes}$ (brake OFF)**.
   - Aspirate the top $2/3$ supernatant plasma (`PPP`) into secondary polypropylene tubes using a plastic transfer pipette (staying $\ge 5\text{ mm}$ above the buffy coat).
   - **Aspirate each of the 5 harvested `PPP` aliquots on the `Nihon Kohden MEK-1503` Hematology Analyzer (`1.1.15`)** and print the platelet counts.
3. **Mandatory NABL Pass Criterion:**
   - **ALL 5 harvested `PPP` samples MUST yield a residual Platelet Count $< 10 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$ (`< 10 \times 10^9/\text{L}`)**!
   - If any sample shows $\ge 10 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$, increase centrifugation time from $15\text{ min}$ to $20\text{ min}$ or speed to $1,800 \times g$ and re-verify until $5/5$ pass.
