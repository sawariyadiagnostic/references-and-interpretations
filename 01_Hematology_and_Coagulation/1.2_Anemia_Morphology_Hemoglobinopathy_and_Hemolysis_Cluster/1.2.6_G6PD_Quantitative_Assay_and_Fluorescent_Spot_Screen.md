## Section 2: Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Enzyme Stability, Buffy-Coat Removal & Rejection Matrix
1. **Optimal Specimen:** Venous whole blood in $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ (Lavender Top), Acid-Citrate-Dextrose (`ACD` Solution A/B — highest stability), or Lithium/Sodium Heparin. **NEVER use Sodium Fluoride (`NaF`) or Oxalate tubes** ($\text{F}^-$ and oxalate strongly inhibit glycolytic/pentose-phosphate enzymes).
2. **Enzyme Thermal Stability Profile (CLSI H09-A2):**
   - **Ambient ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$):** $\le 24\text{ hours}$ ($\text{G6PD Mediterranean}$ and Class I unstable variants lose $> 15\%$ activity after $24\text{ h}$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$).
   - **Refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$):** **Up to $7\text{ days}$ in $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$** (up to $14\text{–}21\text{ days}$ in `ACD`).
   - **Frozen Whole Blood ($-20^\circ\text{C}$):** **REJECT (`REJ-FROZEN-WB`)** — uncontrolled freeze-thaw of unwashed whole blood without stabilizing $\text{NADP}^+ / \beta\text{-mercaptoethanol}$ denatures $\text{G6PD}$.
3. **Mandatory Pre-Analytical Leukocyte & Platelet Depletion (When $\text{WBC} > 15,000/\mu\text{L}$ or $\text{PLT} > 500,000/\mu\text{L}$):**
   - Leukocytes and platelets contain **$50\text{ to } 100\text{ times}$ more $\text{G6PD}$ activity per cell** than mature erythrocytes and do NOT lose $\text{G6PD}$ activity in $\text{G6PD}$-deficient variants (`G6PD Mediterranean` / `Orissa` / `Kerala-Kalyan`).
   - **SOP Rule:** Centrifuge $\text{EDTA}$ blood at $1,200\times g$ for $10\text{ min}$, **aspirate and discard plasma and the entire buffy coat (plus top $10\%$ of RBC column)**, and wash the remaining packed RBCs twice in cold $0.9\% \text{ NaCl}$ before preparing the digitonin/saponin hemolysate! *(Note: Removing the top $10\%$ of the spun RBC column also partially depletes buoyant young reticulocytes).*

## Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Calibration & Quality Control
### 1. Standardized Quantitative UV-Kinetic Spectrophotometric SOP ($340\text{ nm}, 37^\circ\text{C}$)
1. **Hemolysate Preparation & Lysate Hemoglobin Measurement:**
   - Lyse $50\ \mu\text{L}$ of buffy-coat-depleted washed packed RBCs in $1.0\text{ mL}$ of **Digitonin/Saponin Stabilizing Hemolysis Reagent** (containing $10\ \mu\text{mol/L } \text{NADP}^+$ and $0.7\text{ mmol/L}$ $2\text{-mercaptoethanol}$ to prevent $\text{G6PD}$ tetramer dissociation). Stand at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ for $10\text{ minutes}$ and centrifuge to remove stroma.
   - Measure the exact **Hemolysate Hemoglobin concentration ($\text{Hb}_{\text{lysate}}$ in $\text{g/dL}$)** on `MEK-1305/1503` or via Drabkin's Cyanmethemoglobin / SLS method at $540\text{ nm}$.
2. **Enzymatic Kinetic Reaction ($37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$):**
   - Pipette $1,000\ \mu\text{L}$ of Tris-HCl Buffer ($\text{pH } 8.0$, $10\text{ mmol/L } \text{MgCl}_2$) + $30\ \mu\text{L}$ $\text{NADP}^+$ ($0.2\text{ mmol/L}$) + $20\ \mu\text{L}$ Hemolysate into a $1.0\text{ cm}$ pathlength quartz/UV cuvette at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $5\text{ minutes}$.
   - Initiate reaction by adding $30\ \mu\text{L}$ **Substrate ($\text{Glucose-6-Phosphate}$, $0.6\text{ mmol/L}$)**. Mix and record $\Delta A_{340}/\text{min}$ over $3\text{ minutes}$ in the linear phase.
3. **Molar Extinction Coefficient Calculation ($\varepsilon_{\text{NADPH at } 340\text{ nm}} = 6.22\text{ L}\cdot\text{mmol}^{-1}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$):**
   $$\text{G6PD Activity (U/g Hb at } 37^\circ\text{C)} = \frac{\Delta A_{340}/\text{min} \times \text{Total Assay Volume (mL)} \times 100}{6.22 \times \text{Hemolysate Sample Volume (mL)} \times \text{Hb}_{\text{lysate}}\text{ (g/dL)}}$$
   *(Note: Never report $\text{G6PD}$ in `U/L of whole blood` in anemic patients, because low RBC mass causes falsely low `U/L` results; always normalize to **`U/g Hb`** or `U/10^12 RBCs`).*

### 2. Three-Level Internal Quality Control (IQC) & Normal Male Median (`AMM`) Verification
- **Adjusted Male Median (`AMM`) Calibration:** Per WHO 2022 guidelines, establish the local **$100\%$ Adjusted Male Median (`AMM`)** using $30\text{–}50$ non-anemic healthy adult males (excluding the lowest $10\%$ deficient outliers). Standard $37^\circ\text{C}$ ICSH `AMM` $= \mathbf{10.0 \text{ – } 11.5\text{ U/g Hb}}$ (Reference interval: $8.5 \text{ – } 15.5\text{ U/g Hb}$).
- **Daily IQC:** Run **Normal Control ($\sim 10.5\text{ U/g Hb}$)**, **Intermediate Control ($\sim 4.5\text{ U/g Hb}$ — critical $30\text{–}70\%$ Tafenoquine decision threshold)**, and **Deficient Control ($\sim 1.2\text{ U/g Hb}$)**.

### 2. Neonatal & Pediatric Age-Stratified Reference Intervals (CALIPER / ICSH)
Because neonates possess physiological reticulocytosis ($3\text{–}7\%$) and a younger mean erythrocyte age, **normal neonatal $\text{G6PD}$ activity is $40\text{–}60\%$ higher than adult levels**:
- **Term Neonate ($0\text{–}30\text{ Days}$):** **$12.5 \text{ – } 22.5\text{ U/g Hb}$** *(A value of $7.5\text{–}9.5\text{ U/g Hb}$ in a jaundiced neonate with reticulocytosis is **subnormal/suspect** and must be repeated at $3\text{ months}$ of age!)*.
- **Infant ($1\text{–}6\text{ Months}$):** **$10.0 \text{ – } 18.0\text{ U/g Hb}$**.
- **Child ($> 6\text{ Months}$) & Adult:** **$8.5 \text{ – } 15.5\text{ U/g Hb}$**.

## Section 7: Intra-Erythrocytic Enzyme Decay Kinetics & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades
### 1. Exponential Decay of `G6PD` Across the $120$-Day Erythrocyte Lifespan (The Acute Hemolysis Trap!)
- Mature anucleate erythrocytes lack ribosomes and **cannot synthesize new $\text{G6PD}$ protein**.
- In wild-type `G6PD B`, the enzymatic half-life inside circulating RBCs is **$\sim 62\text{ days}$**, so young reticulocytes possess **$4\text{ to } 5\text{ times}$ higher $\text{G6PD}$ activity** than senescent $120$-day-old RBCs.
- In **`G6PD Mediterranean` ($563\text{ C}\to\text{T}$)**, the mutant enzyme is catastrophically unstable ($t_{1/2} \approx \text{hours to days}$); mature RBCs have $< 1\%$ activity, whereas newly released **reticulocytes still retain substantial $\text{G6PD}$ activity**!
- **Pathophysiological Consequence:** During an **acute hemolytic crisis** (triggered by primaquine, infection, or fava beans), the oldest, severely deficient RBCs are already lysed and cleared, leaving behind a peripheral blood population enriched in **reticulocytes ($8\text{–}25\%$) and leukocytosis**. Performing a $\text{G6PD}$ test during or within $2\text{–}4\text{ weeks}$ of acute hemolysis frequently yields a **FALSELY NORMAL (`8.5–11.0 U/g Hb` or Fluorescent Spot Normal) result**!

### 2. Smart LIMS Automated Reflex Cascade
1. **`G6PD` ordered in patient with $\text{Hb} < 9.0\text{ g/dL}$ or `PBS` showing Bite Cells / Polychromasia:** $\to$ Auto-reflex **Reticulocyte Count (`1.2.2`)** and **Supravital Heinz Body Stain**. If `Retic > 3.0%` and `G6PD` is $7.0\text{–}11.5\text{ U/g Hb}$, trigger **`[BAND-BORDERLINE-ACUTE-HEMOLYSIS-CAVEAT]`** and mandate repeat testing in **$8\text{–}12\text{ weeks}$** (or compute the **Pyruvate Kinase-to-G6PD Ratio** / `G6PD` gene PCR).

## Section 9: Conditional Band-Specific Pathologist Sign-Out Library
- **`[BAND-DEFICIENT-CRITICAL]` (`G6PD < 3.0 U/g Hb` [`< 30% AMM`] or `Fluorescent Spot: No Fluorescence`):**  
  ***CRITICAL: SEVERE G6PD DEFICIENCY DETECTED (`X.X U/g Hb`; `< 30%` of Normal Median).*** *The patient has severe erythrocytic Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase deficiency and is at high risk of **acute life-threatening intravascular/extravascular hemolysis** upon exposure to oxidative drugs, fava beans, naphthalene, or severe infections. **STRICTLY CONTRAINDICATED DRUGS:** Primaquine (daily regimen), Tafenoquine, Dapsone, Rasburicase, **Methylene Blue** (use Ascorbic Acid if methemoglobinemia occurs), Nitrofurantoin, Sulfonamides, and Phenazopyridine. Issue patient a permanent G6PD Deficiency Medical Alert Card.*
- **`[BAND-INTERMEDIATE-HETEROZYGOTE]` (`G6PD 3.0 – 8.0 U/g Hb` [`30% – 80% AMM`]):**  
  ***INTERMEDIATE G6PD ACTIVITY (`X.X U/g Hb`; `XX%` of Normal Median):*** *In a **female patient**, this intermediate activity is characteristic of **Heterozygous G6PD Deficiency** (mosaicism due to X-chromosome inactivation / Lyonization). In a **male patient** (or anemic patient with reticulocytosis), this may represent **true G6PD deficiency partially masked by young reticulocytes during/after an acute hemolytic crisis** or recent transfusion. **Clinical Guidance:** **Single-dose Tafenoquine is CONTRAINDICATED ($< 70\%$ threshold)**; avoid high-risk oxidative drugs (Dapsone, Rasburicase, Methylene Blue). If tested near a hemolytic episode, **repeat quantitative G6PD in 8 to 12 weeks**.*
- **`[BAND-NORMAL-STEADY]` (`G6PD 8.5 – 15.5 U/g Hb` [`> 80% AMM`] with Normal Hb & Retic `< 2.5%`):**  
  *Quantitative erythrocyte G6PD activity is within normal reference limits, indicating adequate protection against oxidative hemolysis (provided the patient has not experienced an acute hemolytic episode within the past $4\text{ weeks}$ or blood transfusion within $90\text{–}120\text{ days}$).*
- **`[BAND-NORMAL-WITH-HEMOLYSIS-WARNING]` (`G6PD 8.5 – 15.5 U/g Hb` in Patient with Anemia / Jaundice / Retic `> 3.0%`):**  
  *Quantitative G6PD activity falls within the numerical reference range; **HOWEVER, because this specimen was collected during an active anemia/hemolytic state (or elevated reticulocyte count), a FALSE-NORMAL result cannot be excluded** (young reticulocytes contain up to $5\times$ higher G6PD activity than senescent RBCs). **Mandatory Recommendation:** Withhold suspect oxidative drugs and **repeat quantitative G6PD assay 8 to 12 weeks after complete resolution of hemolysis**, or perform molecular `G6PD` gene mutation testing.*
