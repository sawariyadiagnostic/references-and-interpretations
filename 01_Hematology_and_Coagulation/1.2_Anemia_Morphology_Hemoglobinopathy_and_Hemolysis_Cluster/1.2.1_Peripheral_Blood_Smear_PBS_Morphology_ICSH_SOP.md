## Section 2: Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, EDTA Kinetics, Stability & Rejection Matrix
1. **Optimal Specimen:** Venous whole blood collected in dipotassium ($\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$, $1.5\text{–}2.2\text{ mg/mL}$ blood, spray-dried, Lavender Top) per CLSI H04-A6 / H20-A2. Fingerstick capillary wedge smears made directly without anticoagulant avoid $\text{EDTA}$-induced platelet clumping (`pseudothrombocytopenia`), though feather-edge platelet aggregation can occur.
2. **Fill-Volume Mandate:** Fill $\text{EDTA}$ tube to $\ge 75\%$ of stated volume. Under-filling ($< 50\%$ fill, hypertonic $\text{K}_2\text{/K}_3\text{-EDTA} > 2.5\text{ mg/mL}$) causes osmotic shrinkage of erythrocytes, artifactual echinocytosis/crenation, reduced $\text{PCV}$, and falsely elevated $\text{MCHC}$.
3. **Time-to-Smear Window (Critical Pre-Analytical Variable):**
   - **Ideal Window:** Prepare wedge film within **$1\text{–}2\text{ hours}$** of venipuncture (maximum **$< 3\text{–}4\text{ hours}$** at $18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$).
   - **Refrigerated $\text{EDTA}$ ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$):** Acceptable up to $12\text{–}24\text{ hours}$ for CBC counts, **NOT acceptable for fresh de-novo WBC morphology** (induces cold agglutinin RBC clumping and neutrophil nuclear degeneration).

### Quantitative Pre-Analytical Storage Artifact & Rejection Matrix
| Storage Duration / Condition | Morphological Artifact Induced | Mandatory Laboratory Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\text{EDTA}$ Storage $> 4\text{ Hours}$ ($25^\circ\text{C}$)** | Neutrophil cytoplasmic vacuolation (mimics sepsis), nuclear swelling, clover-leaf lobulation, karyorrhexis/pyknosis (mimics apoptosis/dysplasia), monocyte pseudopodia, and RBC crenation (`echinocytes`). | **Append Mandatory NABL Storage Artifact Disclaimer** or request fresh sample for dysplastic/sepsis morphology. |
| **Macro-Clot or Micro-Fibrin Strands** | Depletes platelets, traps granulocytes at feather edge, distorts RBCs. | **REJECT IMMEDIATELY (`REJ-CLOT`).** Request fresh atraumatic draw. |
| **Cold Agglutinins (Winter / *Mycoplasma*)** | Spontaneous massive RBC agglutination on slide; spuriously low RBC count, $\text{MCV} > 115\text{ fL}$, $\text{MCHC} > 37\text{ g/dL}$ on `MEK-1305`/`1503`. | Warm $\text{EDTA}$ tube and glass slide to **$37^\circ\text{C}$ for $15\text{–}30\text{ min}$**, re-run analyzer, and prepare pre-warmed smear. |
| **$\text{EDTA}$-Dependent Platelet Satellitism / Clumping** | Analyzer flags `PLT Clumps?` with spurious thrombocytopenia ($< 100\times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$); $10\times/100\times$ smear shows platelet aggregates or rosettes around neutrophils. | Suppress automated PLT; report **Manual Platelet Estimate** and request **$3.2\%$ Trisodium Citrate tube** ($\text{Corrected PLT} = \text{Citrate PLT} \times 1.1$). |
| **Severe Lipemia / Hyperproteinemia** | Hazy bluish background staining (`azurophilic haze`), pseudo-rouleaux, blurred RBC margins. | Note background proteinaceous/lipemic staining; correlate with Serum Protein Electrophoresis (`SPEP`) / Triglycerides. |

## Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Calibration & Quality Control
### 1. Standardized Wedge Film Preparation & Buffered Leishman-Giemsa SOP
1. **Wedge Technique:** Place a $5\text{–}10\ \mu\text{L}$ drop of well-mixed $\text{EDTA}$ blood ($8\text{–}10$ gentle inversions) $1\text{ cm}$ from the frosted end of a clean, grease-free $75\times 25\text{ mm}$ glass slide. Using a smooth spreader slide at a **$30^\circ\text{–}35^\circ$ angle** (increase to $40^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$ if $\text{PCV} < 25\%$ to shorten film; decrease to $20^\circ\text{–}25^\circ$ if $\text{PCV} > 55\%$ to lengthen film), back into the drop and push forward smoothly to produce a $2.5\text{–}3.0\text{ cm}$ bullet-shaped film with a smooth **monolayer counting zone** just proximal to the feather edge.
2. **Air Drying & Fixation:** Air-dry rapidly at ambient temperature (use cold air fan during high-humidity monsoon to prevent water-artifact vacuolation/punched-out RBCs).
3. **Leishman Staining Protocol:**
   - Flood slide with undiluted **Leishman stain** ($0.15\%\text{ w/v}$ in anhydrous methanol) for **$2.0\text{ minutes}$** (alcoholic fixation).
   - Add **double the volume ($1:2$) of Sorensen's Phosphate Buffer ($\text{pH } 6.80 \pm 0.05$)** directly onto the slide, mix gently by blowing with a Pasteur pipette until a metallic green sheen forms on the surface, and incubate for **$8.0\text{–}10.0\text{ minutes}$**.
   - Wash off horizontally with a gentle stream of $\text{pH } 6.8$ buffer for $30\text{ seconds}$ (preventing metallic scum precipitation), wipe the back of the slide with methanol-soaked gauze, and air-dry vertically.
4. **Microscopic Inspection Zone ("Sweet Spot"):** Examine the **monolayer zone** where erythrocytes barely touch without overlapping and retain central pallor ($\sim 200\text{–}250\text{ RBCs}$ per $100\times$ Oil Immersion Field [`OIF`, $\text{FN } 20\text{–}22$]).

### 2. Internal Quality Control (IQC), Verification & Cross-Checking
- **Buffer pH Verification:** Check Sorensen's buffer daily with calibrated pH meter ($\text{Target pH } 6.80 \pm 0.05$). If $\text{pH} < 6.4$ (too acidic), RBCs stain excessively bright pink/red and WBC nuclei/granules look pale; if $\text{pH} > 7.2$ (too alkaline), RBCs appear grey-blue and eosinophil granules turn deep blue.
- **Batch Stain QC:** Every new lot of Leishman/Giemsa stain and daily staining batch is verified using a normal control smear: RBCs salmon-pink, neutrophil nuclei deep purple with lilac specific granules, eosinophil granules brick-red/orange, basophil granules dark violet-black, platelets violet-purple chromomere with pale blue hyalomere.
- **Analyzer-to-Smear Cross-Check (`MEK-1305` / `MEK-1503` Verification):**
  - **Rule of Three Check:** $\text{Hb} \times 3 \approx \text{PCV } \% \pm 3\%$; $\text{RBC } (\times 10^6/\mu\text{L}) \times 3 \approx \text{Hb } (\text{g/dL})$. Violation flags cold agglutinins, spherocytosis ($\text{MCHC} > 36\text{ g/dL}$), or lipemia/hemolysis.
  - **Manual Platelet Estimate Multiplier:** Average platelets across $10\text{ OIFs}$ ($100\times$ objective) $\times \mathbf{15,000/\mu\text{L}}$ (or $\times 20,000/\mu\text{L}$ for wide-field $\text{FN } 22$ oculars) must agree within $\pm 20\%$ of automated platelet count.
  - **Nucleated RBC (`nRBC`) WBC Correction Formula:** When $\ge 5\text{ nRBCs} / 100\text{ WBCs}$ are counted on smear, correct automated Total Leukocyte Count (`TLC`):
    $$\text{Corrected WBC } (/\mu\text{L}) = \frac{\text{Uncorrected Automated WBC} \times 100}{100 + \text{Number of nRBCs per 100 WBCs}}$$

## Section 4: EFLM Biological Variation, Counting Imprecision (`CLSI H20-A2`) & Measurement Uncertainty
Manual differential counts follow a binomial statistical distribution (`Rümke Binomial Confidence Limits`, CLSI H20-A2), where sampling error depends heavily on the total number of cells counted ($100$ vs $200$ cells).

| Parameter | EFLM Within-Subject ($CV_I \%$) | EFLM Between-Subject ($CV_G \%$) | Analytical $CV_A \%$ ($200$-Cell Manual vs Automated) | RCV % ($95\%$ Bidirectional) | Rümke $95\%$ Confidence Interval ($200$-Cell Count) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neutrophils (`%` / Abs)** | $11.4\%$ | $21.3\%$ | Manual: $4.5\%$ / Auto: $1.8\%$ | $34.0\%$ | At $50\%$ true value: $43\% \text{ – } 57\%$ |
| **Lymphocytes (`%` / Abs)** | $10.2\%$ | $27.8\%$ | Manual: $5.8\%$ / Auto: $2.2\%$ | $32.6\%$ | At $30\%$ true value: $24\% \text{ – } 37\%$ |
| **Monocytes (`%` / Abs)** | $14.3\%$ | $31.5\%$ | Manual: $16.5\%$ / Auto: $5.5\%$ | $60.6\%$ | At $6\%$ true value: $3\% \text{ – } 10\%$ |
| **Eosinophils (`%` / Abs)** | $19.5\%$ | $58.2\%$ | Manual: $22.0\%$ / Auto: $7.2\%$ | $81.5\%$ | At $3\%$ true value: $1\% \text{ – } 6\%$ |
| **Schistocyte `%` ($1,000\text{ RBCs}$)** | $12.0\%$ | $25.0\%$ | $14.0\%$ ($1,000$-cell count) | $51.0\%$ | At $1.0\%$ cutoff: $0.5\% \text{ – } 1.8\%$ |

## Section 5: ICSH 2015 Standardized Morphological Grading Table & Age-Stratified Cutoffs
Per the **International Council for Standardization in Haematology (ICSH 2015)**, terms such as *"mild"*, *"moderate"*, or *"occasional"* are replaced by standardized **2+ (Moderate)** and **3+ (Marked)** quantitative thresholds (1+ is reserved strictly for high-significance cells like schistocytes, teardrops, spherocytes, or bite cells).

### 1. ICSH 2015 Quantitative Erythrocyte Morphology Grading Matrix ($100\times$ OIF, $\sim 200\text{ RBCs/field}$)
| Morphological Poikilocyte / Inclusion | `1+` (Rare / Mild — Report Only for High-Impact Cells) | `2+` (Moderate — Clinically Significant) | `3+` (Marked — Severe Abnormality) | Primary Pathophysiological Mechanism & Disease Association |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anisocytosis / Microcytes / Macrocytes** | *Do not report 1+* ($< 11\%$) | **$11\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Iron deficiency, thalassemia, $\text{B}_{12}$/folate deficiency, MDS, liver disease. |
| **Hypochromia** (Central pallor $> \frac{1}{3}$ diameter) | *Do not report 1+* ($< 11\%$) | **$11\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Decreased hemoglobin synthesis (`MCH < 27 pg`): IDA, $\beta/\alpha$-Thalassemia, Sideroblastic anemia. |
| **Polychromasia** (Bluish-grey young RBCs) | $< 5\%$ | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Reticulocytosis / stress erythropoiesis (hemolysis, acute blood loss, post-hematinic therapy). |
| **Schistocytes** (Helmet / triangular fragments) | **$< 1.0\%$** (Normal $< 0.5\%$) | **$1.0\% \text{ – } 2.0\%$** | **$> 2.0\%$** | **CRITICAL MAHA:** TTP, HUS, DIC, HELLP syndrome, malignant HTN, prosthetic valve shear. |
| **Spherocytes** (Dense micro-spheres lacking pallor) | $1\% \text{ – } 4\%$ | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Spectrin/ankyrin loss: **Warm AIHA (`DAT+`)**, **Hereditary Spherocytosis (`DAT-`)**, ABO HDFN, *Clostridium* sepsis. |
| **Target Cells (`Codocytes`)** | *Do not report 1+* ($< 5\%$) | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Excess membrane cholesterol/phospholipid vs Hb: **Thalassemia**, **HbC/D/E/S**, Obstructive Jaundice/Liver disease, Post-splenectomy. |
| **Pencil Cells / Elliptocytes (`Ovalocytes`)** | *Do not report 1+* ($< 5\%$) | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | **Iron Deficiency Anemia** (elongated hypochromic pencil cells); **Hereditary Elliptocytosis** ($> 25\text{–}75\%$ cigar-shaped normochromic elliptocytes). |
| **Macro-Ovalocytes** ($\text{MCV} > 100\text{ fL}$, egg-shaped) | $2\% \text{ – } 4\%$ | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Impaired nuclear DNA synthesis: **Megaloblastic Anemia ($\text{B}_{12}$/Folate)**, Myelodysplastic Syndrome (`MDS`). |
| **Teardrop Cells (`Dacryocytes`)** | $1\% \text{ – } 4\%$ | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Splenic pitting of bone marrow fibrosis/infiltration: **Primary Myelofibrosis**, **Myelophthisis** (metastatic carcinoma/TB), $\beta$-Thalassemia Major. |
| **Sickle Cells (`Drepanocytes`)** | **Any ($\ge 1\%$)** | **$1\% \text{ – } 2\%$** | **$> 2\%$** | $\text{HbS}$ ($\beta6\text{ Glu}\to\text{Val}$) polymerization: **Sickle Cell Anemia (`HbSS`)**, $\text{HbS}-\beta\text{ Thal}$, $\text{HbSC}$. |
| **Bite Cells (`Degmacytes`) & Blister Cells** | **$1\% \text{ – } 2\%$** | **$2\% \text{ – } 5\%$** | **$> 5\%$** | Splenic removal of denatured globin (**Heinz bodies**): **G6PD Deficiency crisis**, Dapsone/Nitrofurantoin toxicity. |
| **Acanthocytes (`Spur Cells`)** | $< 5\%$ | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | Irregular $3\text{–}12$ thorny projections: Spur-cell cirrhosis, abetalipoproteinemia, McLeod/neuroacanthocytosis, hypothyroidism, post-splenectomy. |
| **Echinocytes (`Burr Cells`)** | *Check EDTA storage first* | **$5\% \text{ – } 20\%$** | **$> 20\%$** | $10\text{–}30$ uniform short projections: **Uremia / Renal Failure**, pyruvate kinase deficiency, or **$> 4\text{ h}$ EDTA storage artifact**. |
| **Basophilic Stippling** (Coarse ribosomal aggregates) | $1\% \text{ – } 2\%$ | **$2\% \text{ – } 5\%$** | **$> 5\%$** | Pyrimidine 5'-nucleotidase inhibition: **$\beta$-Thalassemia Trait**, **Lead ($\text{Pb}$) Poisoning** (Ayurvedic *Bhasma* / battery exposure), Megaloblastic/Sideroblastic anemia. |
| **Howell-Jolly Bodies / Pappenheimer Bodies** | **$\ge 1\%$** | **$2\% \text{ – } 5\%$** | **$> 5\%$** | Nuclear DNA remnant (`HJ`) or iron-laden mitochondrial granules (`Pappenheimer`): **Hyposplenism / Post-splenectomy**, Sickle Cell autosplenectomy, Sideroblastic anemia. |

### 2. Age-Stratified Physiological Morphological Variations
- **Term & Preterm Neonates ($0\text{–}28\text{ Days}$):** Physiological macrocytosis ($\text{MCV } 100\text{–}125\text{ fL}$), polychromasia, $3\text{–}10\text{ nRBCs}/100\text{ WBCs}$ (up to $20\text{–}30$ in preterm/hypoxia; disappears by Day $5\text{–}7$), and mild hyposplenic changes ($1\text{–}2\%$ target cells, acanthocytes, or Howell-Jolly bodies). Schistocytes up to $1.0\%$ (term) or $2.0\text{–}5.0\%$ (preterm) may occur physiologically.
- **Pregnancy (2nd & 3rd Trimester):** Mild physiological neutrophilia ($8,000\text{–}15,000/\mu\text{L}$) with occasional myelocytes/metamyelocytes ($1\text{–}3\%$), Döhle bodies, and mild gestational thrombocytopenia ($100,000\text{–}149,000/\mu\text{L}$). **Warning:** Any schistocytes $> 1.0\%$ with thrombocytopenia requires immediate exclusion of **HELLP Syndrome / Preeclampsia / TTP**.

## Section 6: NABL & CLSI GP47 Critical ("Panic") Morphological Findings & 30-Minute Read-Back
Any of the following **Life-Threatening Morphological Panic Findings** discovered on Peripheral Blood Smear must be verified by a Consultant Pathologist and communicated verbally to the treating physician within **$\le 30\text{ minutes}$** with mandatory read-back documentation:

| Critical Morphological Finding | Quantitative / Qualitative Trigger | Immediate Life-Threatening Clinical Condition | Mandatory Reflex & Physician Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Circulating Leukemic Blasts (New Diagnosis)** | **$\ge 1\%$ Blasts** (especially with **Auer Rods** or Faggot cells) | **Acute Myeloid Leukemia (`AML`), Acute Promyelocytic Leukemia (`APL-M3` — extreme DIC risk), or Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (`ALL`)**. | Immediate Hematology referral; initiate DIC screen (`PT, aPTT, Fibrinogen, D-Dimer`) if `APL`/faggot cells suspected; order Flow Cytometry. |
| **2. Microangiopathic Hemolysis (`Schistocytes`)** | **Schistocytes $> 1.0\%$** of $1,000\text{ RBCs}$ ($> 2\text{ per } 100\times\text{ OIF}$) + Thrombocytopenia | **Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (`TTP`), HUS, Overt DIC, or Obstetric HELLP Syndrome**. | Urgent LDH, Indirect Bilirubin, Reticulocyte Count, Coagulation Profile, `ADAMTS13` level; avoid platelet transfusion in suspected TTP; evaluate for therapeutic plasma exchange (`TPE`). |
| **3. Hemoparasites (*Plasmodium falciparum* / Hyperparasitemia)** | **Any *P. falciparum* rings/gametocytes, schizonts in peripheral blood, or Parasitic Index $> 2\text{–}5\%$** | **Severe / Cerebral Malaria** or *Babesia* / *Trypanosoma* / *Leishmania donovani* (`LD` bodies) / Microfilariae. | Immediate antimalarial therapy & statutory public health notification (`NVBDCP`). |
| **4. Sickle Crisis + Acute Chest / Sequestration** | **Drepanocytes ($2+/3+$)** + marked `nRBCs` ($> 10/100\text{ WBCs}$) + severe anemia | **Acute Sickle Vaso-Occlusive / Hemolytic / Splenic Sequestration Crisis**. | Urgent hydration, oxygenation, cross-match for exchange/simple transfusion, Sickling & HPLC confirmation. |
| **5. Neutrophil Intracellular Bacteria / Fungi** | Phagocytosed diplococci, bacilli, or budding yeast (*Candida*/*Histoplasma*) inside neutrophils/monocytes | **Fulminant Overwhelming Bacteremia / Fungemia / Meningococcemia**. | Immediate blood cultures and empiric broad-spectrum IV antimicrobials. |
| **6. Severe True Thrombocytopenia on Smear** | Confirmed **$< 20,000/\mu\text{L}$** ($< 1\text{ PLT/OIF}$) with **zero platelet clumps** | **Spontaneous Intracranial / Mucosal Hemorrhage Risk** (Dengue Shock, Severe ITP, Aplastic Anemia). | Bleeding precautions, avoid IM injections/NSAIDs, urgent clinical evaluation. |

## Section 7: Erythrocyte/Leukocyte Lifespan Kinetics, Mentzer Index & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades
### 1. Cellular Lifespan & Morphological Turnaround Kinetics
- **Normal Erythrocyte (`RBC`):** $110\text{–}120\text{ days}$ ($t_{1/2}$ by $^{51}\text{Cr} \approx 28\text{–}35\text{ days}$). Microcytic/macrocytic morphological replacement takes $60\text{–}90\text{ days}$, whereas **polychromatophilic stress reticulocytes** appear within **$48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$** of acute hemolysis or hemorrhage and peak at **$5\text{–}10\text{ days}$** following iron/$\text{B}_{12}$/folate therapy.
- **Neutrophils:** Bone marrow transit time $6\text{–}10\text{ days}$; intravascular half-life **$6\text{–}8\text{ hours}$** before tissue margination. Toxic granulations, Döhle bodies, and left-shift bandemia appear within **$4\text{–}12\text{ hours}$** of endotoxemia/sepsis.
- **Platelets:** Lifespan **$8\text{–}10\text{ days}$**; large/giant reticulated platelets (`MPV > 12 fL`) appear within $24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$ of peripheral destruction (`ITP`, `Dengue`).

### 2. Discriminant Indices for Microcytic Hypochromic Smears ($\text{MCV} < 80\text{ fL}$)
| Discriminant Formula | Mathematical Equation | Suggests $\beta$-Thalassemia Trait | Suggests Iron Deficiency Anemia (`IDA`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mentzer Index (1973)** | $\text{MCV (fL)} \div \text{RBC Count } (\times 10^6/\mu\text{L})$ | **$< 13.0$** (High RBC count $> 5.0\times 10^6/\mu\text{L}$) | **$> 13.0$** (Low RBC count $< 4.5\times 10^6/\mu\text{L}$) |
| **Green & King Index (1989)** | $(\text{MCV}^2 \times \text{RDW-CV}) \div (100 \times \text{Hb})$ | **$< 65$** ($\text{RDW-CV}$ typically normal $12\text{–}15\%$) | **$> 65$** ($\text{RDW-CV}$ typically elevated $> 16\%$) |
| **Srivastava Index** | $\text{MCH (pg)} \div \text{RBC Count } (\times 10^6/\mu\text{L})$ | **$< 3.8$** | **$> 3.8$** |

### 3. Smart LIMS Automated Reflex Cascade
1. **`PBS` shows Microcytic Hypochromic + Pencil Cells + $\text{RDW} > 16\%$:** $\to$ Reflex **Serum Ferritin, Iron, TIBC & Transferrin Saturation**.
2. **`PBS` shows Microcytic Hypochromic + Target Cells + Basophilic Stippling + Mentzer $< 13$:** $\to$ Reflex **$\text{Hb}$ HPLC (`1.2.3`)**.
3. **`PBS` shows Spherocytes ($2+/3+$) + Polychromasia + `nRBCs`:** $\to$ Reflex **Reticulocyte Count (`1.2.2`), Direct Coombs Test (`1.2.7`), LDH & Indirect Bilirubin**. If `DAT` is Negative $\to$ Reflex **Osmotic Fragility (`1.2.9`) / EMA Binding Test**.
4. **`PBS` shows Bite Cells / Blister Cells:** $\to$ Reflex **Supravital Heinz Body Stain & Quantitative G6PD Assay (`1.2.6`)**.
5. **`PBS` shows Schistocytes $> 1.0\%$ + Thrombocytopenia:** $\to$ Reflex **Coagulation & DIC Panel (`1.3.1`), LDH, Urine/Plasma Free Hb (`1.2.9`)**.

## Section 8: Analytical, Pre-Analytical, Endogenous & Drug Interference + ISO 22367 FMEA Table
| Interference / Drug Category | Specific Agent / Clinical Condition | Morphological Effect on Peripheral Smear | Mitigation Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oxidative Drugs** | Dapsone, Primaquine, Nitrofurantoin, Sulfasalazine, Phenazopyridine | Induces **Bite cells (`degmacytes`), Blister cells (`eccentrocytes`), spherocytes, and polychromasia** due to Heinz body pitting. | Correlate with medication list; perform Heinz body supravital stain and G6PD assay. |
| **DNA Synthesis Inhibitors** | Hydroxyurea, Methotrexate, Azathioprine, Pemetrexed, Zidovudine (`AZT`), Phenytoin | Induces non-nutritional **macro-ovalocytosis ($\text{MCV } 105\text{–}125\text{ fL}$)** and neutrophil hypersegmentation mimicking $\text{B}_{12}$/Folate deficiency. | Check drug history (especially Hydroxyurea in Sickle Cell / MPN patients) before diagnosing nutritional megaloblastosis. |
| **Cytokine / Steroid Therapy** | G-CSF (`Filgrastim`), Systemic Corticosteroids | G-CSF induces marked **leukocytosis, left shift (myelocytes/promyelocytes), pronounced toxic granulations, Döhle bodies, and giant platelets** mimicking leukemoid sepsis. | Verify G-CSF administration within past $7\text{ days}$. |
| **Monoclonal Proteins / IV Contrast / Dextran** | Multiple Myeloma (`IgG`/`IgA`), Waldenström (`IgM`), IVIG | Induces pronounced **Rouleaux formation** ("stack of coins") and bluish background stain. | Differentiate true autoagglutination (3D irregular clumps, persists after $1:1$ normal saline dilution) from rouleaux (disperses with normal saline). |
