## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the preparation, ripening, filtration, buffer $\text{pH}$ verification, and daily microscopic Internal Quality Control (`IQC`) of all hematological stains (**Leishman Stain, Giemsa Stain, Supravital New Methylene Blue / Brilliant Cresyl Blue, and Kleihauer-Betke Acid-Elution Reagents**), alongside **Binocular Microscope Preventive Maintenance (`Köhler Illumination`)** and the **Mandatory NABL 112 Hematology Specimen & Slide Retention Schedule**.
2. **NABL 112 & ICSH Compliance:**
   - **NABL 112 (`Specific Criteria for Hematology & Immunohaematology`):** Requires every batch and daily working bottle of Romanowsky and supravital stain to be checked for **buffer $\text{pH}$ ($6.80 \pm 0.05$ for routine morphology; $7.20 \pm 0.05$ for malaria parasites)**, filtered daily to prevent metallic scum artifact, verified with a documented **Daily Stain QC Log**, and supported by a formal **Slide & Specimen Retention Archive**.
   - **ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 6.4 (`Microscope Maintenance`), 6.6 (`Reagents and Consumables`), 7.3.7 (`Internal Quality Control`), & 7.4.4 (`Storage, Retention and Disposal of Clinical Samples`).**

## Section 2: Standardized Preparation, Ripening & Daily Filtration of Hematology Stains
### 2.1 Leishman Stain ($0.15\%\text{ w/v}$ in $100\%$ Acetone-Free Anhydrous Methanol — `Dacie & Lewis` Standard)
1. **Reagent Specifications:**
   - **Leishman Stain Powder (Polychromed Eosin-Methylene Blue Azure Salt):** $1.50\text{ g}$ (AR/Microscopy Certified Grade).
   - **Solvent:** **$1,000\text{ mL}$ of $100\%$ Acetone-Free Anhydrous Methyl Alcohol (`Methanol`, $\text{H}_2\text{O} < 0.05\%$)**.
   - *Critical Warning:* Presence of even $> 0.1\%$ acetone or $> 0.5\%$ water in the methanol solvent causes incomplete cellular fixation, "water-artifact" refractile vacuolation inside erythrocytes (mimicking ring-stage malaria parasites!), and rapid stain deterioration.
2. **Preparation & Ripening Procedure (if prepared in-house) or Lot Acceptance (if commercial ready-to-use):**
   - Weigh $1.50\text{ g}$ Leishman powder into a clean, dry mortar (or amber borosilicate bottle containing $20\text{–}30$ sterile $5\text{ mm}$ glass beads).
   - Add $100\text{ mL}$ increments of acetone-free methanol, grinding/mixing thoroughly until dissolved, and make up the volume to $1,000\text{ mL}$ in a tightly stoppered amber borosilicate stock bottle.
   - **Mandatory Oxidative Ripening (Polychroming):** Incubate the tightly stoppered stock bottle at **$37.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $48\text{ hours}$** (with gentle shaking $3\times$ daily) OR mature at room temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) in the dark for **$7\text{ days}$** before bench use (allows controlled oxidation of methylene blue into **Azure B**, which is essential for purple chromatin and lilac neutrophil granule staining).
3. **Daily Working Dropper-Bottle Filtration & Moisture Protection:**
   - Every morning at $08:00\text{ AM}$, filter $50\text{–}100\text{ mL}$ of ripened stock Leishman stain through **Whatman No. 1 filter paper** into a dry amber working dropper bottle.
   - Keep the dropper bottle tightly capped between smears (methanol is highly hygroscopic and absorbs atmospheric humidity during Haryana monsoon months!).

### 2.2 Giemsa Stain (Stock & Working $10\%\text{ v/v}$ Dilution for Thick/Thin Malaria & Blood Parasite Smears)
1. **Stock Giemsa Formula:** $3.80\text{ g}$ Giemsa Azure B/Eosin Y powder dissolved in $250\text{ mL}$ pure Glycerol at $60^\circ\text{C}$ for $2\text{ hours}$, followed by $250\text{ mL}$ acetone-free Methanol.
2. **Working Giemsa Stain (Freshly Prepared — Stable for $< 6\text{ Hours}$ Only):**
   - Dilute **$1\text{ part}$ filtered Stock Giemsa + $9\text{ parts}$ Sorensen's Phosphate Buffer ($\text{pH } 7.20$ for Malaria; $\text{pH } 6.80$ for routine morphology)** immediately prior to staining ($15\text{–}20\text{ minutes}$ staining time).

### 2.3 Supravital Reticulocyte Stain ($1.0\%\text{ w/v}$ New Methylene Blue in Citrate-Saline — `ICSH` Reference)
1. **Formula:** Dissolve **$1.0\text{ g}$ New Methylene Blue (`CI 52030`)** (or Brilliant Cresyl Blue) in $100\text{ mL}$ Citrate-Saline solution ($1\text{ part } 3.0\text{ g/dL}$ Trisodium Citrate + $4\text{ parts } 0.9\%\text{ w/v}$ $\text{NaCl}$).
2. **Filtration & Storage:** Store in an amber bottle at $2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$; **filter a $5\text{ mL}$ working aliquot through a $0.45\ \mu\text{m}$ syringe filter or Whatman No. 1 paper every morning** prior to mixing $1:1$ with $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ blood at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $15\text{–}20\text{ min}$ (prevents precipitated dye granules from mimicking `HbH` golf-ball inclusions or reticulofilamentous RNA!).

## Section 3: Sorensen's Phosphate Buffer Preparation & Daily pH Verification ($\text{pH } 6.80$ vs $\text{pH } 7.20$)
The electrostatic binding of basic **Azure B** (positive charge $\to$ binds acidic DNA/RNA chromatin and basophil granules) and acidic **Eosin Y** (negative charge $\to$ binds basic hemoglobin and eosinophil major basic protein) is strictly governed by the **$\text{pH}$ of the dilution buffer**.

### 3.1 Stock Sorensen's $0.067\text{ M}$ ($M/15$) Phosphate Solutions
- **Stock Solution A (Acidic — $0.067\text{ M}$ Potassium Dihydrogen Phosphate, $\text{KH}_2\text{PO}_4$):** Dissolve **$9.08\text{ g}$ anhydrous $\text{KH}_2\text{PO}_4$** in $1,000\text{ mL}$ Type-I `CLRW` Distilled Water.
- **Stock Solution B (Alkaline — $0.067\text{ M}$ Disodium Hydrogen Phosphate, $\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4$):** Dissolve **$9.47\text{ g}$ anhydrous $\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4$** (or $11.88\text{ g}$ $\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$) in $1,000\text{ mL}$ Type-I `CLRW` Distilled Water.

### 3.2 Working Buffer Mixing Ratios & Mandatory pH Acceptance Windows
| Application | Target Buffer $\text{pH}$ Window | Volume of Stock A ($\text{KH}_2\text{PO}_4$) | Volume of Stock B ($\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4$) | Made Up To ($1\text{ L}$ Working Buffer) | Biological Purpose of Specific $\text{pH}$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Routine `PBS` & Bone Marrow Leishman / Giemsa Staining (`1.2.1`)** | **$6.80 \pm 0.05$**<br>*(Range: $6.75\text{–}6.85$)* | **$50.8\text{ mL}$** | **$49.2\text{ mL}$** | Dilute $100\text{ mL}$ mixed stock to **$1,000\text{ mL}$** with distilled water | Optimal polychromatic differentiation of salmon-pink RBCs, lilac neutrophil granules, and crisp purple nuclear chromatin. |
| **2. Malaria & Blood Parasite Giemsa Staining (`1.2.1` / `4.1.5`)** | **$7.20 \pm 0.05$**<br>*(Range: $7.15\text{–}7.25$)* | **$28.0\text{ mL}$** | **$72.0\text{ mL}$** | Dilute $100\text{ mL}$ mixed stock to **$1,000\text{ mL}$** with distilled water | **Slightly alkaline $\text{pH } 7.20$ is mandatory to accentuate *P. vivax / P. ovale* Schüffner's dots and *P. falciparum* Maurer's clefts!** |

## Section 4: Daily Microscopic Stain Quality Control (`IQC`) Grading Matrix & Troubleshooting
Every morning before staining patient smears, the Bench Technologist must stain **one normal control smear**, evaluate it under $100\times$ oil immersion, and obtain Pathologist sign-off if any abnormality is noted:

| Cellular Element | Acceptable (`PASS`) Staining Appearance ($\text{pH } 6.80$) | If Buffer Too Acidic ($\text{pH } < 6.50$) or Under-Stained | If Buffer Too Alkaline ($\text{pH } > 7.10$) or Over-Stained | If Methanol Contaminated with Water / Unfiltered Scum |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Erythrocytes (`RBCs`)** | **Salmon-pink to terracotta-red** with smooth central pallor ($1/3$ diameter). | Bright fierybrick-red / orange; nuclear chromatin overly pale. | **Muddy slate-blue or grey-purple** (`basophilic tint` mimicking polychromasia!). | Refractile "punched-out" target/ring vacuoles (water artifact) or black coccoid dye precipitates over RBCs. |
| **Neutrophils** | **Deep purple chromatin**; pale pink cytoplasm with **fine lilac/mauve granules**. | Pale blue nuclei; eosinophilic granules overly prominent. | Dark blue-black hyper-condensed nuclei; coarse pseudo-toxic dark granules. | Crenated / lysed cell margins due to delayed fixation. |
| **Eosinophils** | **Bright refractile copper/orange-red uniform spherical granules**. | Blazing red granules; nuclear lobes indistinct. | Dull maroon/greyish granules. | Degranulated / ruptured cell membrane. |
| **Lymphocytes & Monocytes** | Crisp clumped violet-purple chromatin (`Lymph`) with sky-blue cytoplasm; lacy grey-blue `Mono` cytoplasm. | Washed-out pale blue nuclei; nucleoli falsely prominent. | Uniformly dark ink-blue nuclei obscuring chromatin clumping vs blasts! | Smudged / ragged cytoplasmic borders. |
| **Platelets & Slide Background** | **Violet-purple chromomere granules** with pale blue hyalomere; **crystal-clear background**. | Pale invisible platelets (risk of missing platelet clumps!). | Blue-grey proteinaceous background haze between RBCs. | **Metallic refractile purple-black precipitate scum** mimicking bacteria or *Plasmodium* chromatin dots! |

> **Corrective Action for Failed Stain QC:**
> 1. **Too Pink/Red ($\text{pH } < 6.5$):** Verify buffer $\text{pH}$ with calibrated pH meter; add Stock B ($\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4$) dropwise to restore $\text{pH } 6.80$ or increase buffered staining time from $8\text{ min}$ to $10\text{ min}$.
> 2. **Too Blue/Grey ($\text{pH } > 7.1$):** Adjust buffer down to $\text{pH } 6.80$ using Stock A ($\text{KH}_2\text{PO}_4$) or rinse slide in $\text{pH } 6.80$ buffer for an extra $30\text{–}60\text{ seconds}$ until salmon-pink color appears.
> 3. **Refractile Vacuoles / Precipitate Scum:** Immediately discard the working Leishman dropper bottle, dry the bottle in a $60^\circ\text{C}$ hot-air oven, and refill by freshly filtering from the sealed anhydrous stock bottle through Whatman No. 1 filter paper.

## Section 5: Binocular Brightfield Microscope Operation, Köhler Illumination & Maintenance SOP
To guarantee diagnostic morphological accuracy at $400\times$ (`High Dry`) and $1,000\times$ (`100x Plan Achromat Oil Immersion`), all Hematology & Clinical Pathology microscopes must undergo structured daily/weekly care (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.4`):
1. **Daily Köhler Illumination Alignment (Start of Shift):**
   - Place a stained control slide on the mechanical stage and focus using the **$10\times$ objective**.
   - Close the **Field Diaphragm** at the base completely; rack the **Abbe Condenser ($N.A. = 1.25$)** vertically until the octagonal edges of the field diaphragm are in razor-sharp focus on the specimen plane.
   - Center the condenser image using the two condenser centering screws, then open the Field Diaphragm just until it fills the field of view.
   - Adjust the **Condenser Aperture Iris Diaphragm** to match the Numerical Aperture ($N.A.$) of the active objective:
     - For **$10\times / 40\times$ unstained wet mounts** (Sickling, Urine, Parasites): Lower condenser slightly and close iris to **$30\text{–}40\%$** for high phase contrast.
     - For **$100\times$ Oil Immersion Stained `PBS` (`N.A. 1.25`)**: Elevate condenser to its **topmost stop** and open condenser iris to **$80\text{–}90\%$** for maximum optical resolution ($0.2\ \mu\text{m}$ resolving power required to see Auer rods, Howell-Jolly bodies, and malaria chromatin dots!).
2. **Strict $40\times$ Objective Protection Rule (`Zero Oil on 40x`):**
   - The $40\times$ High-Dry objective has a very short working distance ($0.6\text{ mm}$). Once a drop of synthetic immersion oil ($n_D = 1.515$) is placed on the smear for the $100\times$ lens, **NEVER rotate the $40\times$ objective back across the oiled field!** Rotate the nosepiece **the opposite way through the $4\times / 10\times$ lens** to prevent oil from wicking inside the $40\times$ spring-loaded barrel.
3. **End-of-Day Optical Cleaning Protocol:**
   - Wipe immersion oil from the **$100\times$ Oil Objective** immediately after each session using **Optical Lens Tissue Only** (moistened with a single drop of $70\%$ Isopropanol or Commercial Optical Cleaner — **NEVER use Xylene, Acetone, or coarse facial tissue**, which dissolves lens cement and scratches anti-reflective coatings!).
   - Turn brightness rheostat to minimum before switching OFF power, and cover microscope with its dust-proof vinyl hood containing a regenerated silica-gel desiccant pouch (prevents fungal etching of optics during monsoon humidity).
4. **Annual Ocular Micrometer & Stage Calibration:**
   - Calibrated annually by NABL-traceable optical engineer using a **$1\text{ mm}$ Stage Micrometer ($0.01\text{ mm} = 10\ \mu\text{m}$ divisions)** to verify cell sizing ($1\text{ ocular division at } 100\times = 1.0\ \mu\text{m}$).

## Section 6: Mandatory NABL 112 Hematology Specimen, Slide & Record Retention Schedule
In strict compliance with **NABL 112 (`Specific Criteria for Hematology & Immunohaematology`)** and **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.4.4**, the laboratory enforces the following minimum retention times before biomedical waste disposal (`SOP 0.1.13`):

| Specimen / Slide / Record Category | Storage Condition & Temperature | Minimum Mandatory NABL 112 Retention Period | Post-Retention Disposal or Archival Action |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Routine $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ Whole Blood Tubes (`CBC`, `ESR`, `Retic`, `Sickling`, `G6PD`)** | Tightly capped in chronological dated racks at **$2^\circ\text{C} \text{ to } 8^\circ\text{C}$** | **Minimum $48\text{ Hours}$** from report release | Autoclave / pre-treat and dispose in **Red Bag / Yellow Bag** (`HSPCB BMWM 2016`). |
| **2. Hemoglobin `HPLC` (`1.2.3`) & Immunohematology (`DAT`/`IAT` `1.2.7`–`1.2.8`) EDTA/Serum Tubes** | Tightly capped at **$2^\circ\text{C} \text{ to } 8^\circ\text{C}$** | **Minimum $7\text{ Days}$** | Allows reflex family cascade screening or antibody elution verification. |
| **3. Citrate Coagulation Plasma (`PPP` — `PT/INR`, `aPTT`, `Fibrinogen`, `D-Dimer`)** | Capped primary tube or aliquot at **$2^\circ\text{C} \text{ to } 8^\circ\text{C}$** (or **$-20^\circ\text{C} / -70^\circ\text{C}$** for `LA`/Thrombophilia) | **Minimum $24\text{ Hours}$** ($7\text{ Days}$ if frozen at $\le -20^\circ\text{C}$ for special coagulation) | Dispose in Yellow/Red BMWM stream after $24\text{h}$ ($7\text{d}$ for frozen special coagulation). |
| **4. | **5. Abnormal Non-Malignant `PBS` (Severe Anemia, Megaloblastic, MAHA/Schistocytes, Sickle, Thalassemia) & Positive Parasite Smears (`Malaria`, `Microfilaria`, `LD Bodies`)** | Mounted with DPX + coverslip (or immersion oil thoroughly removed with isopropanol tissue), labeled with Barcode/Lab ID, filed in **Pathologist Positive Slide Box** | **Minimum $1\text{ Year}$ (`NABL 112`)** | Retained for NABL Surveillance/Re-assessment vertical audits and external slide proficiency reviews. |
| **6. | **7. Analyzer Raw Histograms/Scattergrams, `IQC` Levey-Jennings Charts, `EQAS` Reports & Maintenance Logs** | Encrypted LIMS Database (`BSA 2023 Sec. 63`) + Signed Physical Binders | **Minimum $2\text{ Years}$ (`NABL 112`)**<br>*(Final Patient Reports: $3\text{–}5\text{ Years}$)* | Archived in tamper-evident QMS store room. |
