## Section 2: Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Prophylactic `RhIG` History & Rejection Matrix
1. **Optimal Specimen:**
   - **Plain Serum (`Clot Activator`, Red Top, $3.0\text{–}4.0\text{ mL}$)** PLUS **$\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ Whole Blood ($2.0\text{ mL}$, Lavender Top)** for Autocontrol / `DAT` and ABO/RhD confirmation.
   - *(Why Serum + EDTA: Plain serum preserves complement for detecting rare complement-dependent Kidd [$\text{Anti-Jk}^a/\text{Jk}^b$] antibodies in tube testing, while $\text{EDTA}$ plasma prevents in-vitro complement interference in gel cards).*
2. **AABB Pre-Transfusion Specimen Age Mandate ($72\text{-Hour}$ Rule):**
   - If the patient has been **transfused or pregnant within the preceding $90\text{ days}$** (or history is uncertain), the `IAT` / cross-match specimen **MUST be collected within $\le 72\text{ hours}$ ($3\text{ days}$)** prior to scheduled transfusion to detect rapidly emerging secondary anamnestic alloantibodies.
3. **Mandatory Antenatal `RhIG` (Anti-D Injection) History on TRF:**
   - Record exact **Date and Dose ($150\ \mu\text{g}$ vs $300\ \mu\text{g}$) of any prophylactic Anti-D (`Rhogam`/`KamRho`) injection** administered during the current pregnancy (e.g., at $28\text{ weeks}$ or after bleeding/amniocentesis). Passive prophylactic `RhIG` causes a **weakly positive `IAT` (Titre $\le 1:2 \text{ to } 1:4$) for $8\text{ to } 12\text{ weeks}$ post-injection**!
4. **Frozen Archival Mandate for Positive Antenatal Sera:**
   - Whenever an antenatal antibody titration is performed, **freeze an aliquot of the current maternal serum at $-20^\circ\text{C}$ to $-80^\circ\text{C}$** so it can be thawed and run **in parallel on the exact same batch of reagent RBCs** alongside the next follow-up sample $2\text{–}4\text{ weeks}$ later!

## Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Calibration & Quality Control
### 1. Standardized $3$-Cell `IAT` Antibody Screen & $11$-Cell Identification SOP
1. **Reagent Red Cell Phenotype Requirements (`Cell I, II, III`):**
   - **Homogeneity (`Dosage Effect`) Mandate:** Clinically dangerous antibodies of the **Kidd ($\text{Jk}^a, \text{Jk}^b$), Duffy ($\text{Fy}^a, \text{Fy}^b$), Rh ($\text{c, E}$), and MNS** systems exhibit strong **dosage**—reacting significantly stronger (or exclusively) with **homozygous (`double-dose`, e.g., $Jk^{a+}Jk^{b-}$)** RBCs than heterozygous (`single-dose`, $Jk^{a+}Jk^{b+}$) RBCs. A 3-cell panel (`R1R1`, `R2R2`, `rr`) ensures homozygous expression of critical antigens.
2. **Gel Card (`CAT`) $37^\circ\text{C}$ `IAT` Procedure:**
   - Pipette $50\ \mu\text{L}$ of $0.8\%$ `Cell I`, `Cell II`, `Cell III`, and `Patient Autocontrol RBCs` into four labelled `LISS/Coombs` microtube wells.
   - Add $25\ \mu\text{L}$ of patient serum/plasma to each well.
   - Incubate card at **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $15.0\text{ minutes}$** in the dry-block ID-Incubator.
   - Centrifuge at $85\times g$ for $10.0\text{ minutes}$ and grade reactions (`0` to `4+`).
   - **If Screen is Positive ($\ge w+/1+$ in any cell):** Proceed to an **$11$-Cell Antibody Identification Panel** (plus Papain/Ficin enzyme-treated panel, which **enhances Rh and Kidd** antibodies while **destroying Duffy [$\text{Fy}^a, \text{Fy}^b$] and $\text{M, N, S}$** antigens!).

### 2. Standardized Serial Doubling-Dilution Antenatal Antibody Titration SOP (`AABB`)
1. **Master Dilution Tube Technique (Change Pipette Tip at Every Step!):**
   - Set up $12$ glass tubes labelled `Neat (1:1)`, `1:2`, `1:4`, `1:8`, `1:16`, `1:32`, `1:64`, `1:128`, `1:256`, `1:512`, `1:1024`, `1:2048`.
   - Pipette $200\ \mu\text{L}$ of $0.9\% \text{ NaCl}$ into tubes `1:2` through `1:2048`. Add $200\ \mu\text{L}$ of patient serum to `Neat` and `1:2`.
   - Mix `1:2` thoroughly and **discard the pipette tip**. Using a **brand-new pipette tip**, transfer $200\ \mu\text{L}$ from `1:2` to `1:4` *(carryover from reusing a single tip creates false titres of $1:512$ instead of $1:16$!)*. Repeat tip change at every dilution.
   - **(2) Parallel Testing of Previous Frozen Serum:** Thaw the patient's previous archived serum specimen and dilute in parallel against the **same donor Group O $R_1R_1 / R_2R_2$ RBC suspension**.
3. **Endpoint Definition:** The **Titre** is reported as the **reciprocal of the highest dilution demonstrating $\ge 1+$ macroscopic agglutination** (do not use `w+` microscopic trace as the endpoint). A clinically significant rising titre requires a **$\ge 4\text{-fold}$ ($\ge 2\text{-tube}$) increase** when tested in parallel with the previous sample.

## Section 7: Passive `RhIG` vs Active Immune Alloimmunization Kinetics & LIMS Reflexes
- **Pharmacokinetics of Prophylactic Anti-D (`RhIG` $300\ \mu\text{g}$ IM/IV):**
  - Peak serum concentration occurs at $2\text{–}7\text{ days}$ post-IM injection; biological half-life ($t_{1/2}$) is **$21\text{–}24\text{ days}$**.
  - **Differentiation Table (Passive `RhIG` vs Active Immune $\text{Anti-D}$):**
    - **Passive `RhIG`:** Documented injection within $\le 12\text{ weeks}$; Saline-Tube `IAT` titre is strictly **$\le 1:4$** ($1+$ to $2+$ in Gel); strictly pure $\text{IgG}$ (never agglutinates in direct saline at room temperature); **titre decreases** on repeat testing $2\text{–}4\text{ weeks}$ later.
    - **Active Immune $\text{Anti-D}$:** No `RhIG` within $12\text{ weeks}$ OR Saline-Tube `IAT` titre **$\ge 1:8\text{–}1:16$**; often shows a **$\ge 4\text{-fold}$ rise** over $2\text{–}4\text{ weeks}$ (or contains an $\text{IgM}$ saline-reactive component in primary sensitization). **Once active immune $\text{Anti-D}$ is confirmed, prophylactic `RhIG` is ineffective and contraindicated.**

## Section 9: Conditional Band-Specific Pathologist Sign-Out Library
- **`[BAND-IAT-NEGATIVE]` (`Cell I: 0 | Cell II: 0 | Cell III: 0 | Autocontrol: 0`):**  
  *Indirect Antiglobulin Test (`IAT` / Indirect Coombs Antibody Screen) using a 3-cell Group O reagent panel at $37^\circ\text{C}$ is **NEGATIVE** for clinically significant irregular red cell alloantibodies. **Antenatal Guidance (if $\text{RhD}$-Negative Mother):** No evidence of maternal $\text{RhD}$ alloimmunization; patient is eligible for routine antenatal Anti-D (`RhIG` $300\ \mu\text{g}$) prophylaxis at $28\text{ weeks}$ gestation and within $72\text{ hours}$ postpartum (if infant is $\text{RhD}$-positive).*
- **`[BAND-IAT-POS-PASSIVE-RHIG]` (`Weak Positive 1+, Titre <= 1:4, Documented RhIG within 12 Weeks`):**  
  ***WEAKLY POSITIVE INDIRECT COOMBS TEST (Titre `1:X`, Grade `1+`):*** *In view of the documented administration of prophylactic Anti-D Immunoglobulin (`RhIG`) within the preceding $12\text{ weeks}$, this low-titre reactivity ($\le 1:4$) is consistent with **passive circulating prophylactic Anti-D** rather than active maternal immunization. **Recommendation:** Continue scheduled postpartum `RhIG` prophylaxis; if clinically uncertain, repeat `IAT` titration in $4\text{ weeks}$ (passive `RhIG` titres remain stable or decline, whereas true immune antibodies rise $\ge 4\text{-fold}$).*
- **`[BAND-IAT-POS-TRUE-ALLOIMMUNIZATION-LOW]` (`Positive IAT, Titre 1:2 to 1:8, No Recent RhIG`):**  
  ***POSITIVE INDIRECT COOMBS TEST — MATERNAL ALLOIMMUNIZATION DETECTED (`[Anti-D / Specify]`, Titre `1:X`):*** *Clinically significant irregular red cell IgG antibody detected below the critical threshold ($< 1:16$). **Clinical Guidance:** Prophylactic `RhIG` is no longer indicated once true $\text{Anti-D}$ alloimmunization is established. **Repeat serial `IAT` antibody titration every 4 weeks until 28 weeks gestation, and every 2 weeks thereafter** (archive frozen serum for parallel testing).*
- **`[BAND-IAT-CRITICAL-TITRE]` (`Positive IAT, Titre >= 1:16 [or >= 1:8 for Anti-Kell] or >= 4-Fold Rise`):**  
  ***CRITICAL ANTENATAL ANTIBODY TITRE ALERT (`[Anti-D / Anti-c / Anti-Kell]`, Titre `1:XX`):*** *The maternal irregular IgG antibody titre has reached/exceeded the **critical threshold ($\ge 1:16$; or $\ge 1:8$ for Anti-Kell)** associated with significant risk of **Hemolytic Disease of the Fetus and Newborn (`HDFN`) and fetal hydrops**. **Mandatory Action:** Immediate referral to **Maternal-Fetal Medicine (`MFM`)** for non-invasive fetal anemia monitoring via **Middle Cerebral Artery Peak Systolic Velocity (`MCA-PSV`) Doppler Ultrasonography**.*
