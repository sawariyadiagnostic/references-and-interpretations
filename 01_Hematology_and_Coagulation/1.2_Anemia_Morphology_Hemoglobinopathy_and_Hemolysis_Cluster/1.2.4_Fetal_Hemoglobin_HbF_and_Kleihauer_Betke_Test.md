## Section 2: Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Timing Post-Delivery/Trauma & Rejection Matrix
1. **Optimal Specimen:** Venous whole blood in $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ ($2.0\text{–}4.0\text{ mL}$, Lavender Top).
2. **Critical Obstetric Collection Window for `FMH` Assessment:**
   - Collect maternal $\text{EDTA}$ blood **$\ge 30\text{–}60\text{ minutes}$ (ideally within $2\text{ hours}$) after delivery, abdominal trauma, placental abruption, external cephalic version (`ECV`), or amniocentesis**, allowing fetal cells entering the maternal uterine venous sinuses to equilibrate throughout the maternal systemic circulation, **AND strictly BEFORE therapeutic Anti-D (`RhIG`) administration** (though standard $300\ \mu\text{g}$ RhIG should never be delayed past $72\text{ hours}$).
3. **Stability:**
   - **Kleihauer-Betke Smears:** Prepare thin smears within **$\le 6\text{ hours}$** at $18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$, or within **$\le 24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$** from refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$) $\text{EDTA}$ blood.
   - **HPLC $\text{HbF}$:** Stable $48\text{ hours}$ at $18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$ and **$7\text{ days}$ at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$**.
4. **Rejection Criteria:** Clotted specimen (`REJ-CLOT`), grossly hemolyzed specimen (`REJ-HEM`), or unfixed smears exposed to heat/water vapor prior to $80\%$ ethanol fixation.

## Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Calibration & Quality Control
### 1. Standardized Kleihauer-Betke (`KB`) Acid Elution SOP (`CLSI H52-A2`)
1. **Thin Monolayer Smear & Fixation:** Prepare $2$ ultra-thin wedge smears of maternal $\text{EDTA}$ blood; air-dry for $10\text{–}20\text{ minutes}$. Fix in **$80\%\text{ v/v}$ Ethyl Alcohol for exactly $5.0\text{ minutes}$** at $20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$; rinse gently with distilled water and air-dry.
2. **Critical Acid Elution Step:**
   - Pre-warm **McIlvaine's Citrate-Phosphate Elution Buffer** ($0.1\text{ M}$ Citric Acid + $0.2\text{ M}$ Disodium Hydrogen Phosphate) in a covered Coplin jar in a **$37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$ water bath**. Verify buffer pH with a digital pH meter immediately prior to use: **strictly $\text{pH } 3.20 \pm 0.02$**.
   - Immerse fixed slides into the $37^\circ\text{C}$ buffer for **exactly $5.0\text{ minutes}$** (lifting slides up and down gently at $1$ and $3\text{ minutes}$).
3. **Counterstaining:**
   - Rinse gently in distilled water.
   - Stain in **Mayer's Acid Hematoxylin (or Ehrlich's Hematoxylin) for $3.0\text{ minutes}$** to stain leukocyte nuclei dark violet (confirms slide orientation and excludes nucleated RBCs/lymphocytes).
   - Rinse in water and counterstain in **$0.1\% \text{ – } 0.5\%\text{ w/v}$ Erythrosin-B (or Rose Bengal / Eosin-Y) for $3.0\text{ minutes}$**.
   - Rinse thoroughly in distilled water, air-dry vertically, and examine under $40\times$ and $100\times$ oil immersion.

### 2. Mandatory Positive & Negative Batch Controls (Every Run)
- **Positive Control Slide ($1\%$ Fetal-in-Adult Mix):** Prepare a $1:100$ mixture of fresh ABO-compatible umbilical cord blood ($\text{HbF} > 75\%$) into normal adult male $\text{EDTA}$ blood ($\text{HbF} < 1\%$). Must show $\sim 10$ crisp, bright cherry-red refractile fetal RBCs per $1,000$ pale hollow adult ghost cells.
- **Negative Control Slide ($100\%$ Normal Adult Male Blood):** Must show $100\%$ uniformly eluted pale ghost shadows with **$< 0.05\%$** faint pink maternal `F-cells`.

### 3. Fetomaternal Hemorrhage (`FMH`) Mathematical Equations & RhIG Vial Calculation
Count a minimum of **$2,000\text{ total Maternal Ghost RBCs}$** using a Miller Ocular Disc across multiple monolayer fields and record the number of darkly stained, homogenous **Fetal RBCs ($F$)** vs Adult Ghost RBCs ($M$):
1. **Percentage of Fetal RBCs (`% Fetal Cells`):**
   $$\%\text{ Fetal RBCs} = \left(\frac{\text{Number of Fetal RBCs Counted }(F)}{2,000\text{ Maternal Ghost RBCs }(M)}\right) \times 100$$
   - **(2) Mollison / AABB Fetomaternal Hemorrhage Volume Formula (Assuming Maternal Blood Volume $= 5,000\text{ mL}$ / Red Cell Volume $= 1,800\text{ mL}$ + $22\%$ Fetal MCV & Incomplete Elution Correction Factor $= \mathbf{2,400}$ for Packed Fetal RBCs or $\mathbf{5,000}$ for Fetal Whole Blood):**
   $$\text{Fetal Packed RBC Volume (mL)} = \frac{F}{M} \times 2,400\text{ mL} = (\%\text{ Fetal RBCs}) \times 24$$
   $$\text{Fetal Whole Blood Bleed Volume (mL)} = \frac{F}{M} \times 5,000\text{ mL} = (\%\text{ Fetal RBCs}) \times 50$$
3. **Anti-D Immunoglobulin (`RhIG`) Vial Dosing Calculation ($1\text{ Standard Vial} = \mathbf{300\ \mu\text{g}} \text{ [}1,500\text{ IU]}$ neutralizes $\mathbf{30\text{ mL}}$ of Fetal Whole Blood or $\mathbf{15\text{ mL}}$ of Fetal Packed RBCs):**
   $$\text{Calculated Vials} = \frac{\text{Fetal Whole Blood Volume (mL)}}{30\text{ mL}}$$
   - **AABB / CAP Rounding & Safety Rule:** Because manual `KB` has a $CV_A$ of $20\text{–}35\%$:
     - If the fractional decimal is **$< 0.5$**, round **DOWN** to nearest integer and **add $+1\text{ extra vial}$** (e.g., $1.3 \to 1 + 1 = \mathbf{2\text{ vials}}$).
     - If the fractional decimal is **$\ge 0.5$**, round **UP** to next integer and **add $+1\text{ extra vial}$** (e.g., $1.6 \to 2 + 1 = \mathbf{3\text{ vials}}$).

| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |

| **Infant ($1\text{–}6\text{ Months}$)** | **$5.0\% \text{ – } 60.0\%$** | Progressive decline in pink RBCs | $\gamma \to \beta$ globin gene switching. |

| **Adult Male & Non-Pregnant Female ($> 2\text{ y}$)** | **$< 1.0\% \text{ – } 1.5\%$** | **$< 0.05\%$ Fetal RBCs** ($< 1\text{ per } 2,000$ ghost cells; $0.5\text{–}4.5\%$ faint `F-cells`) | Normal baseline. |

## Section 7: In-Vivo Fetal Cell Clearance Kinetics & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades
- **In-Vivo Lifespan of Transfused Fetal RBCs in Maternal Circulation:**
  - **ABO-Compatible Fetal RBCs:** Survive **$60\text{–}100\text{ days}$** in maternal blood unless cleared by endogenous antibodies or therapeutic `RhIG`.
  - **ABO-Incompatible Fetal RBCs (e.g., Group A/B fetus in Group O mother):** Rapidly hemolyzed by maternal anti-A/anti-B isoagglutinins within **$24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$**, which can yield a **falsely negative `KB` test** if blood is drawn $> 24\text{ hours}$ post-bleed.
  - **Post-RhIG Clearance Monitoring:** After administering calculated `RhIG` for a large `FMH` ($> 30\text{ mL}$), repeat `KB` and `IAT` (`1.2.8`) at **$48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$**; disappearance of fetal cells ($0\%$) and a weakly positive `IAT` ($1+$, passive free Anti-D) proves adequate neutralization.
- **Smart LIMS Reflex Cascade:**
  1. If `KB` shows **$> 5.0\%$ intermediate pink cells** (corresponding to an implausible $> 250\text{ mL}$ fetal bleed in a clinically stable infant) $\to$ **Suspect Maternal Hereditary Persistence of $\text{HbF}$ (`F-cells`), $\beta$-Thalassemia Trait, or Sickle Trait** $\to$ Reflex **Maternal $\text{Hb}$ HPLC (`1.2.3`)** and **Anti-HbF/Anti-CA Flow Cytometry** to avoid massive `RhIG` overdosing!

## Section 9: Conditional Band-Specific Pathologist Sign-Out Library
- **`[BAND-KB-NEGATIVE-STANDARD-RHIG]` (`Fetal RBCs <= 0.1%`, `< 5 mL Fetal Whole Blood`):**  
  *Kleihauer-Betke acid elution screen shows $\le 0.1\%$ fetal erythrocytes (calculated fetomaternal hemorrhage $< 5.0\text{ mL}$ fetal whole blood). **Therapeutic Recommendation:** A **single standard dose of $300\ \mu\text{g}$ ($1,500\text{ IU}$) Anti-D Immunoglobulin (`RhIG`)** administered within $72\text{ hours}$ of delivery/sensitizing event is sufficient to neutralize up to $30\text{ mL}$ of fetal whole blood ($15\text{ mL}$ fetal RBCs).*
- **`[BAND-KB-POSITIVE-LARGE-FMH]` (`Fetal RBCs > 0.3%`, `>= 15 mL Fetal Whole Blood`):**  
  ***CRITICAL FETOMATERNAL HEMORRHAGE ALERT:* Kleihauer-Betke acid elution identifies `X.XX%` fetal erythrocytes (`XX` fetal cells per $2,000$ maternal ghost cells), corresponding to an estimated fetomaternal bleed of **`XX.X mL` Fetal Whole Blood (`XX.X mL` Fetal Packed RBCs)**. **Anti-D (`RhIG`) Dosing Recommendation:** Per AABB/BCSH guidelines ($1\text{ vial } [300\ \mu\text{g} / 1,500\text{ IU}]$ per $30\text{ mL}$ fetal whole blood plus $+1\text{ safety vial}$), a total of **`[X] Vials` ($300\ \mu\text{g}$ each)** is recommended. Advise repeat `KB` and `IAT` $48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$ post-administration to confirm complete clearance of fetal cells.*
- **`[BAND-HBF-HETEROCELLULAR-THAL]` (`HbF 5.0–18.0%`, Heterocellular `KB`):**  
  *Moderately elevated Fetal Hemoglobin ($\text{HbF} = \text{XX.X}\%$) with a **heterocellular (uneven mosaic) distribution** of `F-cells` on Kleihauer-Betke stain. In the presence of microcytic indices and normal $\text{HbA}_2$, this pattern favors **$\delta\beta$-Thalassemia Trait** (or physiological/pharmacological `F-cell` reactivation) over pancellular HPFH.*
- **`[BAND-HBF-PANCELLULAR-HPFH]` (`HbF 15.0–35.0%`, Pancellular `KB`):**  
  *Significantly elevated Fetal Hemoglobin ($\text{HbF} = \text{XX.X}\%$) with a **uniform pancellular (homocellular) distribution** across virtually all erythrocytes on Kleihauer-Betke stain and normocytic indices, characteristic of **Hereditary Persistence of Fetal Hemoglobin (`HPFH` Heterozygote)**.*
