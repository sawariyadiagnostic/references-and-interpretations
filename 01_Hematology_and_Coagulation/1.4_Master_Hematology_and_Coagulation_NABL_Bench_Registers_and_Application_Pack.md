## Section 3: Blinded Inter-Observer Morphology Concordance SOP & Rümke Binomial Confidence Limits (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7.3`)
### 3.1 Monthly 5-Slide Internal Morphology Ring-Trial Protocol
To satisfy **NABL 112** and **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.3.7.3** (comparability of manual microscopic examinations across multiple observers), the Senior Consultant Hematopathologist conducts a **Monthly Blinded 5-Slide Morphology Audit** involving all Authorized Signatory Pathologists and Senior Hematology Bench Technologists:
1. **Slide Selection ($5\text{ Masked Slides / Month}$):**
   - `Slide 1`: Normal Adult `PBS`
   - `Slide 2`: Microcytic Hypochromic vs. Megaloblastic Anemia `PBS`
   - `Slide 3`: Leukocytosis with Left Shift / Toxic Granulation OR Lymphocytosis / Blast Differential `PBS`
   - `Slide 4`: Thrombocytopenia vs. EDTA Platelet Clumping `PBS`
   - `Slide 5`: Supravital Reticulocyte Smear OR Malaria Parasite Smear (`Thick + Thin`).
2. **Statistical Acceptance Criteria (`Rümke 95% Binomial Confidence Envelope` for $200\text{-Cell}$ Manual `DLC`):**
   - Because manual $100\text{-cell}$ or $200\text{-cell}$ differential counts follow a **Binomial Sampling Distribution** ($\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{p(100-p)}{N}}$), an observer's manual `DLC %` is **Concordant (`PASS`)** if it falls within the **Rümke $95\%$ Confidence Envelope** around the Senior Hematopathologist's Reference Count:

| Reference True Cell Percentage ($p\%$) | Rümke $95\%$ Acceptance Range ($100\text{-Cell}$ Count) | Rümke $95\%$ Acceptance Range ($200\text{-Cell}$ Count — **NABL Standard**) | Mandatory Action if Observer Falls Outside Rümke Envelope |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **$1\%$** (e.g., Basophils / Blasts) | $0\% \text{ – } 5\%$ | **$0\% \text{ – } 4\%$** | Mandatory side-by-side multi-head / digital camera microscope retraining on cell identification criteria (`ICSH 2015`). |
| **$5\%$** (e.g., Eosinophils / Monocytes) | $1\% \text{ – } 11\%$ | **$2\% \text{ – } 9\%$** | Re-examine 5 archive training smears (`SOP 0.1.12`). |
| **$10\%$** | $4\% \text{ – } 18\%$ | **$6\% \text{ – } 15\%$** | Verify battlement track technique (`SOP 1.2.1`) to avoid edge-bias overcounting of monocytes/neutrophils. |
| **$30\%$** (e.g., Lymphocytes) | $21\% \text{ – } 40\%$ | **$24\% \text{ – } 37\%$** | Retrain on reactive lymphocyte vs monocyte nuclear/cytoplasmic distinction. |
| **$60\%$** (e.g., Neutrophils) | $50\% \text{ – } 70\%$ | **$53\% \text{ – } 67\%$** | Recount 200 cells and document CAPA. |
| **$75\%$** | $65\% \text{ – } 83\%$ | **$68\% \text{ – } 81\%$** | Recount 200 cells and document CAPA. |

   - **Qualitative Morphology & Parasite Identification Concordance:** Must achieve **$100\%$ concordance** on critical alerts (**Blasts, Promyelocytes/Auer rods, Schistocytes $\ge 1\%$, Sickled cells, Platelet clumps, and Malaria species identification**) and **$\ge 85\%$ ($\kappa \ge 0.85$) concordance** on semi-quantitative `1+ / 2+ / 3+` RBC/WBC grading (`ICSH`).

## Section 4: Weekly Analyzer-to-Backup / Manual Split-Sample Comparability SOP (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7.4`)
Every **Wednesday**, **2 routine patient $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ samples** are tested in parallel across the Primary Analyzer (`Nihon Kohden MEK-1503`) and the Backup Analyzer / Validated Manual Reference Methods:
1. **`Hgb` & `Hct`:** `MEK-1503` vs. Backup Analyzer / Spun Micro-Hematocrit (`PCV` at $12,000 \times g$ for $5\text{ min}$) $\to$ Maximum allowable bias **$\le \pm 3.0\%$**.
2. **`WBC` & `PLT`:** `MEK-1503` vs. Backup Analyzer + Manual Neubauer Chamber / Fonio Smear Estimate $\to$ Maximum allowable bias **$\le \pm 5.0\%$ (`WBC`)** and **$\le \pm 10.0\%$ (`PLT`)**.
3. **`ESR`:** `MEK-1503` Automated Near-IR `ESR` vs. Manual $200\text{ mm}$ ICSH Westergren Column at $60\text{ min}$ $\to$ Maximum allowable difference **$\le \pm 3\text{ mm/hr}$** (for $\text{ESR} < 20\text{ mm/hr}$) or **$\le \pm 15\%$** (for $\text{ESR} \ge 20\text{ mm/hr}$).
