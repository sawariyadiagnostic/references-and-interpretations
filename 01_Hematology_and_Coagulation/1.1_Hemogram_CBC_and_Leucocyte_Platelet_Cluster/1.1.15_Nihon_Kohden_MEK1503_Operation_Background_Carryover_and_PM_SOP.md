## Section 1: Purpose, Regulatory Scope & NABL 112 Compliance Mandate
1. **Purpose:** This Standard Operating Procedure (`SOP`) establishes the complete operational, quality-assurance, background-check, carryover-verification, calibration, troubleshooting, and preventive-maintenance (`PM`) architecture for the **Primary Automated Hematology + Integrated Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (`ESR`) Analyzer (`Nihon Kohden MEK-1503 Celltac α+`)** and secondary/backup automated hematology systems operating in the Department of Hematology & Coagulation.
2. **NABL Accreditation & International Regulatory Alignment:**
   - **ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 6.4 (`Equipment`), 6.5 (`Equipment Calibration & Metrological Traceability`), & 7.3.7 (`Ensuring the Validity of Examination Results`).**
   - **NABL 112 (`Specific Criteria for Accreditation of Medical Laboratories — Hematology & Immunohaematology`):** Mandates documented daily startup background checks, multi-level whole-blood internal quality control (`IQC`), carryover verification (`ICSH`), comparability between Whole-Blood (`WB`) and Pre-Dilute (`PD`) capillary aspiration modes, and daily/weekly/monthly enzymatic and hypochlorite fluidic maintenance logs.
   - **ICSH / CLSI H26-A2 (`Validation, Verification, and Quality Assurance of Automated Hematology Analyzers; Approved Standard — Second Edition`).**
   - **ICSH Reference Methodologies:** Cyanmethemoglobin (`HiCN`) for Hemoglobin metrological traceability, Coulter Principle electrical impedance with hydrodynamic/DynaHelix flow for cell counting/sizing, and **Ciuffetti / Optical Near-Infrared (`NIR` $800\text{–}950\text{ nm}$) Rouleaux Aggregation Kinetics** correlated to the 60-minute International Council for Standardization in Haematology (`ICSH`) Westergren reference method for `ESR`.

## Section 3: Pre-Startup Environmental, Reagent & Fluidic Verification Checklist
Before powering ON or waking the `MEK-1503` from Standby at the start of the morning shift ($07:30\text{ AM}$), the Bench Technologist must verify and log:
1. **Ambient Environment & Power Stability (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.3`):**
   - Room temperature strictly between **$18.0^\circ\text{C} \text{ and } 25.0^\circ\text{C}$** (temperatures $> 30^\circ\text{C}$ alter isotonic diluent conductivity, erythrocyte volume `MCV`, and `ESR` aggregation kinetics; temperatures $< 15^\circ\text{C}$ precipitate cold agglutinins and falsely elevate `MCV`/`MCHC`).
   - Online Sine-Wave `UPS` input/output voltage verified ($220\text{–}240\text{ V AC}$, $50\text{ Hz}$); Dedicated **Earth-to-Neutral Voltage $< 1.0\text{ V AC}$** (stray electrical ground noise creates spurious baseline pulses in the `< 30 fL` platelet impedance channel, causing false thrombocytosis!).
2. **Reagent Inventory & Lot/Open-Vial Expiry Verification (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.6`):**
   - Check remaining volume of `Isotonac` Diluent cubitainer, `Hemolynac` Lyse bottle, `Cleanac` Enzymatic Detergent, and `Cleanac-3` Hypochlorite cleaner.
   - Verify that **Open-Container Stability ($60\text{ days}$ from opening date written on label)** has not expired.
   - Ensure the Diluent cubitainer rests **at the same bench level or $\le 50\text{ cm}$ below the analyzer** (never above the analyzer, which causes siphon flooding) and away from direct air-conditioning drafts or sunlight.
3. **Waste Container Disposal (`HSPCB BMWM Rules 2016`):**
   - Empty the liquid effluent container if $> 75\%$ full after pre-treating with **$1\text{ part } 10\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite per $9\text{ parts}$ effluent** ($1\%$ final active chlorine, contact time $\ge 30\text{ minutes}$) before discharging into the Effluent Treatment Plant (`ETP`) drain (Cross-Ref SOP `0.1.13`).

## Section 4: Daily Startup, Background Count Verification & Pass/Fail Gate
Upon pressing `[ON / STARTUP]`, the analyzer automatically primes all reagent lines, flushes the `CBC` baths and `ESR` optical cell, and executes a **Background Blank Measurement** on pure `Isotonac` diluent.

### 4.1 Mandatory NABL Daily Background Count Acceptance Limits (`MEK-1503` & Equivalent)
| Parameter | Maximum Permissible Background Reading | Root Cause if Exceeded | Mandatory Corrective Action Before Running `IQC` or Patient Samples |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`WBC` (`TLC`)** | **$\le 0.1 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$** | Lyse/diluent microbial contamination, fibrin debris in WBC bath, or electronic ground noise. | Execute `[MAINTENANCE] -> [CLEAN] (Cleanac)` followed by `Background Check`. |
| **`RBC`** | **$\le 0.02 \times 10^6/\mu\text{L}$** | Residual erythrocytes in RBC bath or micro-bubbles in diluent line. | Prime diluent line (`[PRIME ALL]`) to expel air bubbles; inspect cubitainer pickup straw. |
| **`Hgb` (Hemoglobin)** | **$\le 0.1\text{ g/dL}$** | Dirty WBC/Hgb optical photometer window or protein film on cuvette wall. | Run `[STRONG CLEAN] (Cleanac-3 Hypochlorite)` for $5\text{ min}$; re-verify Hgb blank voltage. |
| **`Hct` (`PCV`)** | **$\le 0.5\%$** | Derived from spurious `RBC` background pulses. | Clear RBC aperture via `[REMOVE CLOG]` high-voltage burn + backflush. |
| **`PLT` (Platelet Count)** | **$\le 5 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$**<br>*(Target: $0\text{–}3 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$)* | **Most sensitive indicator!** Caused by poor electrical earthing ($> 1.5\text{ V}$), dust/bacteria in open diluent, or micro-bubbles. | 1. Check `Earth-to-Neutral` voltage ($< 1.0\text{ V}$).<br>2. Execute `Cleanac-3` soak.<br>3. Replace diluent cubitainer if contaminated. |
| **`ESR` Optical Cell Blank** | **$0\text{ mm/hr}$** (`Optical Transmittance OK`) | Protein/fibrin coating inside the quartz/polymer `ESR` capillary flow cell. | Execute `[ESR CELL CLEAN]` with `Cleanac-3`; verify NIR LED reference intensity ($100 \pm 5\%$). |

> **[!CAUTION] Hard NABL Stop Gate:**
> The Bench Technologist is **strictly prohibited** from running Internal Quality Control (`IQC`) or patient specimens until **ALL 6 Background Parameters pass within the limits above** on a printed/LIMS-archived Background Ticket. If Background fails **3 consecutive attempts**, place an `"OUT OF SERVICE — UNDER MAINTENANCE"` tag on the analyzer, route STAT samples to the **Backup Hematology Analyzer / Manual Validated Method**, and log a Non-Conformance (`CAPA`).

## Section 5: Multi-Level Internal Quality Control (`IQC`) & ICSH Retained-Sample Bull's Moving Average
### 5.1 Daily 3-Level Commercial Whole-Blood Control Protocol (`NABL 112`)
1. **Control Material:** Tri-level NABL/CE-IVD Traceable Stabilized Whole-Blood Hematology Control (**`Level 1: Low / Cytopenic`**, **`Level 2: Normal`**, **`Level 3: High / Cytotic`**) + **`2-Level ESR Whole-Blood Control` (`Normal ESR < 15 mm/hr` & `Abnormal Elevated ESR 35–75 mm/hr`)**.
2. **Pre-Warming & Gentle Resuspension Protocol (Critical to Prevent Control Hemolysis):**
   - Remove the control vials from the $2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ refrigerator and hold at **room temperature ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for exactly $15\text{ minutes}$** (cold controls alter `MCV` and `ESR` viscosity!).
   - **NEVER place commercial hematology control vials on a mechanical roller mixer for prolonged periods or vortex them!** Instead, mix strictly by the **3-Step Manual Palm-Roll & Inversion Protocol**:
     1. Hold the vial vertically between both palms and roll back-and-forth for $20\text{ seconds}$.
     2. Invert the vial $180^\circ$ and roll between palms for another $20\text{ seconds}$.
     3. Gently invert the vial $8\text{–}10\text{ times}$ until the red cell button at the bottom of the vial is completely homogenized with zero residual film.
   - Immediately aspirate in `[QC MODE]` (`Level 1`, `Level 2`, `Level 3`), wipe the vial rim and cap threads with a lint-free gauze moistened with isotonic saline, and return to $2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ within $< 30\text{ minutes}$.
3. **Westgard Sigma-Metric Acceptance Rules (Cross-Ref SOP `0.1.7`):**
   - High-Sigma Parameters ($\sigma \ge 5.0$: `Hgb`, `RBC`, `WBC`, `Hct`, `PLT`): Single rejection rule **$1_{3s}$** ($99.7\%$ confidence).
   - Moderate-Sigma Parameters ($\sigma = 3.5\text{–}4.9$: `MCV`, `MCHC`, `RDW`, `MPV`, `ESR`, Differential `%`): Multi-rule **$1_{3s} / 2_{2s} / R_{4s} / 4_{1s}$**.

### 5.2 ICSH Retained Patient Specimen Reproducibility (`24-Hour Delta Check`) & Bull's Moving Average ($X_B$)
Because commercial stabilized hematology controls have a short **open-vial stability ($14\text{ days}$)** and expensive lot changes, `NABL 112` and `ICSH` require supplementary **Retained Patient Specimen Verification**:
1. **Daily Retained Patient Sample Check:**
   - Each afternoon, select **2 routine $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ patient specimens** (one normal `CBC`, one mildly abnormal) analyzed within $< 4\text{ hours}$ of collection, seal tightly, and store at **$2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ overnight**.
   - The next morning (after warming for $15\text{ min}$ and roller-mixing), re-aspirate both specimens and verify that the $24\text{-hour}$ drift stays within **$\pm 2.0\%$ for `Hgb`/`RBC`**, **$\pm 3.0\%$ for `WBC`/`MCV`**, and **$\pm 5.0\%$ for `PLT`** (Note: `ESR` is NOT valid at $24\text{h}$ storage and must be verified within $< 4\text{h}$ at room temperature or $< 12\text{h}$ at $4^\circ\text{C}$).
2. **Bull's Moving Average (`X-Bar-B` Algorithm for Red Cell Indices):**
   - The analyzer continuously calculates the smoothed moving average of **`MCV`, `MCH`, and `MCHC`** across consecutive batches of **$n = 20$ non-oncology patient samples**. Since population mean `MCHC` is biologically constant ($33.5 \pm 1.0\text{ g/dL}$), any shift in batch `MCHC` $> \pm 3\%$ immediately alerts the technologist to **reagent osmolality drift or `Hgb`/`RBC` calibration offset** during the middle of a run!

## Section 6: Carryover Verification (`ICSH / CLSI H26-A2`) & Mode Comparability (`WB` vs `Pre-Dilute`)
### 6.1 Semi-Annual & Post-Maintenance Carryover Verification Protocol
Carryover must be verified upon installation, every **6 months**, and after any sample-probe or rotary-valve replacement:
1. **Procedure:**
   - Select a patient specimen with **high target counts (`Sample H`)**: `WBC` $> 30\text{–}50 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$, `RBC` $> 5.5\text{–}6.5 \times 10^6/\mu\text{L}$, `Hgb` $> 16.5\text{–}19.0\text{ g/dL}$, `PLT` $> 500\text{–}800 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$.
   - Select a cytopenic specimen or isotonic diluent blank (**`Sample L`**).
   - Aspirate **`Sample H` three consecutive times** ($H_1, H_2, H_3$), followed **immediately** by **`Sample L` three consecutive times** ($L_1, L_2, L_3$).
2. **Mathematical Formula (`ICSH H26-A2`):**
   $$\text{Carryover (\%)} = \left( \frac{L_1 - L_3}{H_3 - L_3} \right) \times 100$$
3. **Mandatory NABL Pass Criteria:**
   - **`WBC` Carryover:** **$\le 0.5\%$**
   - **`RBC` Carryover:** **$\le 0.5\%$**
   - **`Hgb` Carryover:** **$\le 0.5\%$**
   - **`PLT` Carryover:** **$\le 1.0\%$**

### 6.2 Whole-Blood (`WB` Open/Closed) vs. Pre-Dilute (`PD` Capillary $20\ \mu\text{L}$) Mode Comparability (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7.4`)
Because neonatal heel-prick and pediatric difficult-draw specimens are occasionally run in **Pre-Dilute (`PD`) Mode** ($20\ \mu\text{L}$ whole blood dispensed into $4\text{ mL}$ pre-dispensed `Isotonac` diluent by the analyzer probe = $1:200$ dilution), quarterly comparability between `WB` mode and `PD` mode is mandatory using **5 EDTA specimens**:
- **Maximum Permissible Bias (`PD` vs `WB`):** `Hgb` $\le \pm 2.5\%$, `RBC` $\le \pm 3.0\%$, `WBC` $\le \pm 5.0\%$, `PLT` $\le \pm 7.0\%$. *(Note: Automated `ESR` is NOT available in Pre-Dilute mode; pediatric `ESR` requires undiluted whole blood or micro-ESR capillary tube).*

## Section 8: Instrument Flag & Histogram Troubleshooting Matrix (`ISO 22367` Risk Mitigation)
| Instrument Flag / Alarm | Underlying Fluidic or Biological Cause | Immediate Bench Troubleshooting & Mandatory Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`CLOG` / `APERTURE BLOCK` (`RBC` or `WBC` Chamber)** | Micro-clot from poorly mixed EDTA tube, fibrin strand, or dust blocking the $100\ \mu\text{m}$ ruby aperture (lengthens aperture transit time and distorts pulse height). | 1. Press `[REMOVE CLOG]` (applies high-frequency electrical cautery across the ruby aperture + backflush).<br>2. If persistent, run `[STRONG CLEAN]` with `Cleanac-3`.<br>3. **Inspect the patient's EDTA tube with two wooden applicator sticks** for micro-clots; if clotted, **REJECT sample (`CLOTTED-EDTA`)** and recollect! |
| **`BUBBLE` / `SHORT SAMPLE` (`ESR` or `CBC` Channel)** | Insufficient blood volume in EDTA tube ($< 1.0\text{ mL}$ for closed/ESR mode), foam from vigorous shaking, or air leak in diluent fitting. | 1. Ensure EDTA tube is filled to the $2.0\text{–}3.0\text{ mL}$ mark and free of surface foam.<br>2. If pediatric short sample ($0.5\text{ mL}$), run `CBC` in open-vial manual aspiration mode and perform `PBS` (`1.2.1`). |
| **`MCHC > 36.5 g/dL` + `RBC/Hgb` Discrepancy Flag** | **1. Cold Agglutinins** (spurious RBC doublets/triplets counted as single giant cells $\to$ false $\downarrow \text{RBC}$, false $\uparrow \text{MCV } 115\text{–}140\text{ fL}$, impossible $\text{MCHC } 38\text{–}55\text{ g/dL}$).<br>**2. Severe Lipemia or Icterus** (turbidity elevates spectrophotometric `Hgb`). | **Step 1:** Pre-warm EDTA tube in **$37.0^\circ\text{C}$ water bath for $15\text{–}20\text{ minutes}$** and re-aspirate immediately (`RBC`, `MCV`, and `MCHC` will normalize completely in Cold Agglutinin disease!).<br>**Step 2:** If unchanged after warming, perform **Saline Plasma Replacement** (spin EDTA, replace turbid plasma with equal volume of `Isotonac` diluent, re-mix, and re-run) or report spun PCV-derived `Hgb` (Cross-Ref SOP `1.1.2`). |
