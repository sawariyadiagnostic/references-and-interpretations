1. **Twin-Aperture DC Electrical Impedance with Patented `DynaHelix Flow™` Coaxial Hydrodynamic Focusing** (`WBC` $100\ \mu\text{m}$ aperture and `RBC/PLT` $75\ \mu\text{m}$ aperture),

4. **Thermostated ($37.0^\circ\text{C}$) Near-Infrared (`NIR`, $\lambda = 870\text{ nm}$) Stop-Flow Optical Syllectometry** for simultaneous `ESR`.

   - **Step 1 (Thermal Equilibration — $15\text{ Minutes}$):** Remove `MEK-6D` vials from the $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ refrigerator and stand upright on the bench at **$18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$ for exactly $15\text{ minutes}$**. *(Aspirating cold control blood increases fluid viscosity, alters `DynaScatter` lyse kinetics, elevates `MCV`, and severely suppresses `ESR` aggregation!)*
   - **Step 2 (Strict Prohibition of Mechanical Mixing):** **NEVER place commercial `MEK-6D` control vials on a mechanical blood roller, shaker, or vortex mixer!** Stabilized aldehyde-fixed control red cells and pseudo-leucocytes undergo mechanical shear hemolysis (`false ↓ RBC, ↓ PLT, ↑ background debris`) if rolled mechanically.
   - **Step 3 (3-Phase Manual Palm-Roll & Inversion — $60\text{ Seconds}$ Total):**
     1. **Phase A ($20\text{ sec}$):** Hold the vial upright vertically between flat palms and roll gently back-and-forth for $20\text{ seconds}$.
     2. **Phase B ($20\text{ sec}$):** Invert the vial $180^\circ$ (cap facing down) and roll between palms for $20\text{ seconds}$ to dislodge the settled cell button from the cap/shoulder.
     3. **Phase C ($20\text{ sec}$):** Gently invert the vial end-over-end $8\text{–}10\text{ times}$ (without creating air foam) and visually inspect the bottom meniscus of the vial against overhead light to confirm **zero residual red cell film**.
   - **Step 4 (Aspiration & Post-Run Hygiene):** Select `[QC] -> [LOT NO.] -> [LEVEL L / N / H]`, aspirate immediately, wipe the probe and vial cap threads with a lint-free tissue dampened with `Isotonac` or $70\%\text{ IPA}$ (preventing dried blood crusts on the cap that fall into the vial next day and clog the aperture!), and return vials to $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ within $< 30\text{ minutes}$.

### 4.4 Bull's Moving Average ($\bar{X}_B$) & Patient-Based Real-Time Quality Control (`PBRTQC`) (`Persona 8` Deep-Dive)

Commercial stabilized controls (`MEK-6D`) are tested only 1–2 times per shift and contain aldehyde-stabilized cells whose osmotic behavior differs from fresh human erythrocytes. To detect **intra-run analytical drift in real time between commercial QC runs**, the `MEK-1503` and Sawariya Diagnostic Lab LIMS execute **Brian Bull's Moving Average Algorithm ($\bar{X}_B$, `Bull 1974 / ICSH / IFCC PBRTQC`)** on red blood cell indices (`MCV`, `MCH`, and `MCHC`) across every batch of **`N = 20` consecutive qualified patient samples**.

#### 4.4.1 Mathematical Formulation of Bull's Square-Root Damped Moving Average ($\bar{X}_B$)
Unlike a simple arithmetic mean (which is easily skewed by a single patient with severe iron-deficiency microcytosis or $\beta$-thalassemia trait), Bull's algorithm applies a **signed square-root transformation to the residuals** from the prior batch mean $\bar{X}_{B, k-1}$, mathematically compressing outliers while preserving high sensitivity to systematic analyzer drift:

1. **Step 1 (Signed Square-Root Residual Sum $S_k$ for Batch $k$ of $N = 20$ Patients):**
   $$S_k = \sum_{i=1}^{20} \text{sign}\left(x_i - \bar{X}_{B, k-1}\right) \cdot \sqrt{\left\lvert x_i - \bar{X}_{B, k-1} \right\rvert}$$
   where $\text{sign}(u) = +1$ if $u > 0$, $-1$ if $u < 0$, and $0$ if $u = 0$.
2. **Step 2 (Squaring Back the Mean Square-Root Residual $F_k$):**
   $$F_k = \text{sign}(S_k) \cdot \left( \frac{S_k}{20} \right)^2$$
3. **Step 3 (Updating the Smoothed Bull's Batch Mean $\bar{X}_{B, k}$):**
   $$\bar{X}_{B, k} = \bar{X}_{B, k-1} + F_k$$

#### 4.4.2 Patient Inclusion / Truncation Filters & Sawariya Diagnostic Lab Target Limits
To prevent non-random clinic batching (e.g., a consecutive batch of antenatal anemia or pediatric thalassemia samples) from triggering false `X-bar_B` alarms, the `MEK-1503` / LIMS applies the following **pre-inclusion truncation gates** before entering a sample into the $N = 20$ batch:
- **Truncation Gate (`Included in N = 20 Batch Only If`):** `RBC` $= 3.20\text{–}6.00 \times 10^6/\mu\text{L}$, `Hb` $= 9.0\text{–}17.5\text{ g/dL}$, `MCV` $= 72.0\text{–}102.0\text{ fL}$, and `MCHC` $= 29.0\text{–}37.5\text{ g/dL}$.
- **Established Sawariya Diagnostic Lab Population Targets (`Charkhi Dadri Adult/Outpatient Cohort`):**
  - **$\bar{X}_B$ `MCV` Target:** **$86.5\text{ fL}$** ; **$\pm 2\%$ Warning:** $84.8\text{–}88.2\text{ fL}$ ; **$\pm 3\%$ Action Limit:** **`< 83.9 fL` or `> 89.1 fL`**
  - **$\bar{X}_B$ `MCH` Target:** **$29.0\text{ pg}$** ; **$\pm 2\%$ Warning:** $28.4\text{–}29.6\text{ pg}$ ; **$\pm 3\%$ Action Limit:** **`< 28.1 pg` or `> 29.9 pg`**
  - **$\bar{X}_B$ `MCHC` Target:** **$33.5\text{ g/dL}$** ; **$\pm 2\%$ Warning:** $32.8\text{–}34.2\text{ g/dL}$ ; **$\pm 3\%$ Action Limit:** **`< 32.5 g/dL` or `> 34.5 g/dL`** *(Most critical physiological constant!)*

### 4.5 ICSH 24-Hour Retained Patient Specimen Reproducibility (`Daily Inter-Day Delta Check`)
To comply with `NABL 112 Issue 05` and `ICSH H26-A2` (bridging consecutive days and verifying patient matrix stability independent of stabilized `MEK-6D` controls):
1. **Selection & Storage ($04:00\text{ PM}$ Daily):** Select **2 fresh routine $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ patient specimens** (`Sample R1`: normal `CBC`; `Sample R2`: abnormal `CBC`, e.g., mild anemia or leukocytosis) tested within $< 4\text{ hours}$ of venipuncture with adequate residual volume ($\ge 1.5\text{ mL}$). Record their Day-1 Baseline results ($X_{\text{Day 1}}$) and store tightly capped at **$2.0^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8.0^\circ\text{C}$ overnight**.
2. **Re-Analysis ($08:00\text{ AM}$ Next Morning):** Equilibrate `Sample R1` and `Sample R2` at room temperature ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for $15\text{ minutes}$, mix on the blood roller for $5\text{ minutes}$ (plus 8 manual inversions), aspirate on the `MEK-1503` ($X_{\text{Day 2}}$), and compute the percentage drift:
   $$\Delta\%_{\text{24h}} = \left( \frac{X_{\text{Day 2}} - X_{\text{Day 1}}}{X_{\text{Day 1}}} \right) \times 100$$
3. **Mandatory NABL 24-Hour Acceptance Criteria:**
   - **`Hb` & `RBC`:** **$\lvert \Delta\%_{\text{24h}} \rvert \le 2.0\%$**
   - **`WBC` & `MCV`:** **$\lvert \Delta\%_{\text{24h}} \rvert \le 3.0\%$** *(Note: If stored outside $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$, `MCV` swells $> +4\%$ due to $\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+\text{-ATPase}$ pump slowing)*
   - **`PLT`:** **$\lvert \Delta\%_{\text{24h}} \rvert \le 5.0\%$**
   - *(Note: Automated `ESR` is excluded from $24\text{-hour}$ retained check because erythrocyte sialic acid and plasma fibrinogen degrade after $4\text{ hours}$ at room temperature / $12\text{ hours}$ at $4^\circ\text{C}$; `ESR` reproducibility is verified within a $< 4\text{-hour}$ window).*

### 5.2 Metrological Calibration Verification (`MEK-CAL`) & Fresh-Blood Cross-Calibration SOP (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.5` & `CAP HEM.25850`)

#### 5.2.1 Metrological Traceability Hierarchy (`ISO 17511:2020`)
All parameters calibrated on the `MEK-1503` trace in an unbroken metrological chain through **Nihon Kohden `MEK-CAL` Assayed Calibrator** to primary international reference procedures:
- **Hemoglobin (`Hb`):** Traceable to the **ICSH / WHO International Hemiglobincyanide (`HiCN`) Reference Standard (`CRM BCR-522`)** measured by reference spectrophotometry at $\lambda = 540.0\text{ nm}$ ($\varepsilon = 11.0\text{ L}\cdot\text{mmol}^{-1}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$).
- **`RBC` & `WBC` Counts:** Traceable to the **ICSH Reference Single-Channel Impedance / Laser Flow Cytometric Counter** using gravimetrically calibrated class-A glass volumetric dilutions.
- **Hematocrit (`Hct`) & `MCV`:** Traceable to the **ICSH Reference Spun Microhematocrit Method** ($12,000 \times g$ for $5\text{ minutes}$ in precision borosilicate capillaries, corrected for $< 1.5\%$ trapped intercellular plasma).
- **Platelet Count (`PLT`):** Traceable to the **ICSH / ISLH International Reference Flow Cytometric `RBC/PLT` Ratio Method** using fluorescently labeled monoclonal **`CD41 / CD61` (anti-GPIIb/IIIa)** antibodies.
- **`ESR`:** Traceable to the **ICSH $200\text{ mm}$ Undiluted/Citrated Westergren Reference Procedure** at $18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$ ($60\text{-minute}$ sedimentation).

#### 5.2.2 Strict Regulatory Rule Separating Calibration from Daily QC (`CAP HEM.25850`)
1. **Commercial Daily IQC Material (`MEK-6D`) MUST NEVER be used to calibrate or adjust calibration factors on the `MEK-1503`!** Using daily QC to adjust calibration masks calibrator/reagent bias and destroys independent trueness verification.
2. Calibration factors (`WBC, RBC, Hb, MCV, PLT, ESR`) are verified **every 6 months** (or after major optical/fluidic repair) using either:
   - **Method A (Primary):** Dedicated commercial **Nihon Kohden `MEK-CAL` Whole-Blood Calibrator** (11-replicate protocol), OR
   - **Method B (ICSH Reference Fresh Whole-Blood Cross-Calibration):** $n = 20$ fresh $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ normal human whole-blood specimens ($< 4\text{ hours}$ from venipuncture) assayed in duplicate against an ICSH reference method / NABL-accredited reference analyzer.

#### 5.2.3 Step-by-Step 11-Replicate `MEK-CAL` Calibration Verification & Factor Adjustment Procedure
1. **Pre-Calibration Preparation:**
   - Perform a Weekly `[STRONG CLEAN]` with `Cleanac-3`, verify `Hgb Blank Voltage` ($4.50 \pm 0.25\text{ V}$), and confirm a clean **Background Check** (`WBC = 0.0`, `RBC = 0.00`, `Hb = 0.0`, `PLT <= 2`).
   - Equilibrate **2 vials of `MEK-CAL`** at room temperature ($18\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for exactly $15\text{ minutes}$ and mix using the **3-Step Manual Palm-Roll Protocol**.
2. **11-Replicate Precision & Bias Run (`[CALIBRATION] -> [AUTO CAL]`):**
   - Aspirate `MEK-CAL` **11 consecutive times** ($R_1, R_2, \dots, R_{11}$), gently inverting the vial 3 times between each aspiration to prevent cell settling.
   - **Discard Run 1 ($R_1$)** (fluidic priming run) and compute the **Arithmetic Mean ($\bar{x}_{10}$), Standard Deviation ($s_{10}$), Repeatability `%CV`, and `% Bias`** from **Runs 2 through 11 ($n = 10$)**:
     $$\%\text{CV} = \left(\frac{s_{10}}{\bar{x}_{10}}\right) \times 100 \qquad \text{and} \qquad \%\text{Bias} = \left(\frac{\bar{x}_{10} - X_{\text{target}}}{X_{\text{target}}}\right) \times 100$$
3. **3-Tier Calibration Decision Gate (`Do Not Adjust` vs `Adjust` vs `Hardware Fault Lockout`):**

### 5.3 Reagent Lot-to-Lot Verification SOP (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.6.3` & `CAP COM.30600`)

Whenever a new manufacturing lot of `Isotonac-3/4`, `Hemolynac-310`, or `Hemolynac-510` is received or opened, it **must be verified before reporting patient results**:
1. **Baseline Run on Old Reagent Lot:** Before disconnecting the expiring reagent lot, run **3-Level `MEK-6D` IQC** and **5 fresh patient $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ specimens** (`Samples P1 to P5`, spanning low, normal, and high counts). Record values as $X_{\text{Old Lot}}$.
2. **Reagent Replacement & Priming:** Connect the new reagent bottle/cubitainer, write the **Date Opened** and **60-Day On-Board Expiry Date** on the container label, and execute `[MAINTENANCE] -> [REAGENT REPLACE / PRIME ALL]` for **3 complete priming cycles** to flush the old lot out of the internal delay coils and chambers.
3. **Verification Run on New Reagent Lot:**
   - Run **Background Check** (must pass all 6 parameters).
   - Run **3-Level `MEK-6D` IQC** (all 3 levels must pass within $\pm 2\text{ SD}$ without `2_2s` shift).
   - Re-aspirate the **same 5 fresh patient specimens (`Samples P1 to P5`)** on the New Reagent Lot ($X_{\text{New Lot}}$) within $< 30\text{ minutes}$ of the baseline run.
4. **Quantitative Acceptance Criteria (`Mean % Bias Across 5 Patient Samples`):**
   $$\text{Mean Lot-to-Lot Bias (\%)} = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=1}^{5} \left( \frac{X_{\text{New Lot}, i} - X_{\text{Old Lot}, i}}{X_{\text{Old Lot}, i}} \right) \times 100$$
   - **`Hb` & `RBC`:** Mean $\lvert \text{Bias} \rvert \le \pm 2.0\%$
   - **`MCV`:** Mean $\lvert \text{Bias} \rvert \le \pm 1.5\%$
   - **`WBC`:** Mean $\lvert \text{Bias} \rvert \le \pm 3.5\%$
   - **`PLT` & `5-Part Diff (ANC/ALC)`:** Mean $\lvert \text{Bias} \rvert \le \pm 5.0\%$

### Scenario 1: Daily Startup, Background Failure (`PLT = 9 x 10^3/uL`), and Electrical Earthing Audit

### Scenario 2: Bull's Moving Average (`X-bar_B`) Drift Triangulation vs Commercial IQC
- **NABL Lead Assessor:** *"Your morning 3-level `MEK-6D` IQC passed at 08:00 AM.
