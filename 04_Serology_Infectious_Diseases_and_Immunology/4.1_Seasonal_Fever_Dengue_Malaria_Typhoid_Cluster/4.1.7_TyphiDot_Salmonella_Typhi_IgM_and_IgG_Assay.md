## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Window-Period Timing, Stability & HIL Rejection Matrix

### Optimal Sampling Window & Species Limitation
- **Optimal Timing:** **Day 5 of Fever Onward** (Sensitivity in Days 1–3 of fever is $< 40\%\text{–}50\%$; rises to $75\%\text{–}88\%$ from Day 5–7 onward).
- **Critical Species Blind Spot (*Salmonella Paratyphi A*):** Because TyphiDot exclusively utilizes the $50\text{ kDa}$ Outer Membrane Protein specific to ***Salmonella Typhi***, it is **frequently NEGATIVE in Enteric Fever caused by *Salmonella Paratyphi A* or *B*** (which accounts for **$20\%\text{ to }35\%$ of all enteric fever cases in North India/Haryana**!).
- **Specimen Type:** Serum or Plasma ($1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ mL}$ from SST / Red-top or $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ tube; verify whole-blood compatibility per manufacturer insert).

### Specimen Stability & Quantitative HIL Rejection Matrix
| Storage Condition | Maximum Stability | Interference Factor | Quantitative Rejection Threshold & Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ambient ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$)** | $\le 8\text{ hours}$ | **Hemolysis Index ($H$)** | Reject if **Free $\text{Hb} > 200\text{ mg/dL}$** (red hemoglobin background obscures faint pink/purple `IgM` test lines). |
| **Refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$)** | **Up to $7\text{ days}$** | **Lipemia Index ($L$)** | Reject gross lipemia (**$\text{TG} > 500\text{ mg/dL}$**) unless high-speed centrifuged (viscous lipids retard capillary migration on nitrocellulose). |
| **Frozen ($-20^\circ\text{C}$)** | $3\text{ months}$ | **Rheumatoid Factor (`RF`)** | High-titer $\text{IgM-RF}$ ($> 80\text{ IU/mL}$) binds to persistent background *S. Typhi* $\text{IgG}$, creating a **false-positive `TyphiDot IgM` band**! |

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Read-Window Discipline & Six Sigma IQC

1. **Step-by-Step Analytical Procedure (Lateral-Flow ICT / Dot-EIA):**
   - Bring cassette/strips and sample diluent buffer to room temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for $20\text{ minutes}$ prior to opening the desiccated pouch.
   - Pipette exact volume ($30\text{–}50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ serum/plasma per kit insert) into the sample well, followed by $1\text{–}2\text{ drops}$ of running buffer held vertically.
   - Read strictly at the manufacturer-validated time (**$15\text{ minutes}$; never read after $20\text{–}25\text{ minutes}$** to prevent evaporative false-positive lines).
2. **Grey-Zone / Trace Line Verification Rule:**
   - Any faint/trace `IgM` shadow line must be correlated with **Quantitative Tube Widal (`SOP 4.1.6`)**, **Complete Blood Count** (looking for normal/low WBC with characteristic **absolute eosinopenia**), **Quantitative `CRP`**, and **Automated Blood Culture**.
3. **Internal Quality Control (`IQC`) & EQAS:**
   - Verify internal Control (`C`) band migration on every cassette. Validate each new lot and weekly performance using characterized *S. Typhi* $\text{IgM}$-positive, $\text{IgG}$-positive, and negative control sera.

## 6. NABL & CLSI GP47 Critical ("Panic") Alerts & Statutory Notification

- **Clinical-Microbiological Escalation:** Because Fluoroquinolone resistance ($>85\%$) and Azithromycin/Ceftriaxone resistance (`XDR` *S. Typhi*) are emerging across South Asia, a positive `TyphiDot IgM` cannot guide antimicrobial susceptibility—clinicians must be advised to obtain **Automated Blood Culture & Sensitivity (`BACTEC`)** prior to escalating to Carbapenems/Azithromycin.
- **IDSP Notification:** Log probable enteric fever cases per state IDSP protocol.

## 7. Kinetic Day-by-Day Rise/Peak/Persistence & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Antibody Persistence ("2-Month Residual `IgM`" Pitfall):** Anti-$50\text{ kDa}$ OMP `IgM` remains detectable for **$60\text{ to }90\text{ days}$** after clinical cure. Therefore, `TyphiDot IgM` **cannot be used as a Test-of-Cure** after completing antibiotic therapy, nor can it prove that a new fever 4 weeks later is a typhoid relapse!
- **Smart LIMS Reflex Cascade:**
  - If `TyphiDot` is ordered on a patient with **Thrombocytopenia ($< 100,000/\mu\text{L}$) and Leukopenia**, append an automatic LIMS alert recommending **`Dengue NS1 / IgM` and `Malaria Smear / RDT`** to prevent missing severe Dengue or Malaria masquerading with a false-positive `TyphiDot IgM`.

## 8. Analytical Cross-Reactivity & ISO 22367 Risk FMEA

1. **False-Positive `TyphiDot IgM` in Dengue, Malaria & Scrub Typhus:** Multiple Indian validation studies (ICMR / CMC Vellore / PGIMER) demonstrate **$15\%\text{ to }25\%$ false-positive `TyphiDot IgM` reactivity** in patients with culture/PCR-proven **Dengue, *P. vivax / P. falciparum* Malaria, Scrub Typhus, Bacteremia, and Autoimmune Rheumatoid Factor (`RF`) positivity** due to polyclonal B-cell activation and heterophile bridging.
2. **Competitive `IgG` Masking of `IgM`:** In standard non-IgG-depleted rapid cards, extremely high endemic `IgG` titers saturate the $50\text{ kDa}$ OMP conjugate, yielding an **isolated `IgG (+)` / false-negative `IgM (-)`** result during acute re-infection.

| Failure Mode (ISO 22367) | Root Cause | Clinical Risk | Mandatory Mitigation Control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dengue / Malaria Misdiagnosed as "Typhoid + Dengue Co-Infection"** | Polyclonal false-positive `TyphiDot IgM` during acute Dengue/Malaria | Unnecessary IV Ceftriaxone; fluid overload in Dengue | Mandatory Cross-Reactivity & Co-Infection Caution Shield in Section 10 |
| ***S. Paratyphi A* Missed by Negative `TyphiDot`** | $50\text{ kDa}$ OMP is specific to *S. Typhi* only | Delayed enteric fever diagnosis | Explicitly state *S. Paratyphi* non-detection; pair with Blood Culture / Tube Widal `AH` |
