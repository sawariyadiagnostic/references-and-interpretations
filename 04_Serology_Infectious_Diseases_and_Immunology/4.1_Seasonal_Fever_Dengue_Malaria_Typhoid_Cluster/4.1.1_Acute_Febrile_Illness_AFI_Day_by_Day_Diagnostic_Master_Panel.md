## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Window-Period Timing, Stability & HIL Rejection Matrix

###Mandatory Fever-Day Clinical History Capture (`Day 1` Anchor)
- **Critical Pre-Analytical Rule:** Phlebotomy staff **must record the exact number of completed days of fever (`Day 1` = first day of temperature $\ge 38.0^\circ\text{C} / 100.4^\circ\text{F}$)** and **prior antibiotic/antimalarial intake** on the LIMS requisition.
- **Specimen Requirements:**
  1. **SST / Red-Top Clot Activator ($4.0\text{ mL}$):** Serum separated within $2\text{ hours}$ at $1500 \times g$ for $10\text{ min}$ for `Dengue NS1/IgM/IgG`, `Widal`, `TyphiDot`, `Scrub Typhus IgM`, `Chikungunya IgM`, `Leptospira IgM`, and `CRP`.
  2. **$\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ Purple-Top ($2.0\text{–}3.0\text{ mL}$):** Whole blood for `Thick & Thin Peripheral Smear for MP` (prepare slides within **$< 1\text{–}2\text{ hours}$** of draw to prevent *P. vivax* exflagellation and storage-induced RBC crenation), `Malaria HRP-2/pLDH RDT`, and `CBC (Platelets / Hematocrit)`.
  3. **Aerobic Blood Culture Bottle ($8\text{–}10\text{ mL}$ Adult / $2\text{–}5\text{ mL}$ Pediatric):** Drawn via strict chlorhexidine-$70\%$ alcohol skin antisepsis **prior to first dose of empirical Ceftriaxone/Azithromycin/Doxycycline**.

### Specimen Stability & Quantitative HIL Rejection Matrix
| Specimen Type | Ambient ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) | Refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$) | Frozen ($-20^\circ\text{C} / -70^\circ\text{C}$) | Quantitative HIL & Pre-Analytical Rejection Thresholds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Serum (Serology)** | $\le 8\text{ hours}$ | **Up to $5\text{–}7\text{ days}$** | $6\text{ months}$ ($\le 2$ freeze-thaw cycles) | **Hemolysis Index ($H$):** Reject if $\text{Hb} > 200\text{ mg/dL}$ ($2+$ hemolysis elevates ELISA background OD and ruins Widal reading).<br>**Lipemia Index ($L$):** Reject if $\text{TG} > 500\text{ mg/dL}$ unless high-speed ultracentrifuged.<br>**Icterus Index ($I$):** Stable up to $\text{Bilirubin } 25\text{ mg/dL}$ on CLIA/ELISA. |
| **EDTA Whole Blood (MP)** | **Smear $< 2\text{ hours}$** | RDT up to $48\text{ hours}$ | **NEVER FREEZE** (Lyses RBCs) | Reject clotted EDTA, micro-clots (causes pseudothrombocytopenia), or EDTA stored $>4\text{ hours}$ at room temperature for morphology. |

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Grey-Zone Protocol, Prozone Dilution & IQC/EQAS

1. **Automated CLIA / Capture ELISA Execution:**
   - Bring serum, calibrators, negative/positive controls, and coated microwells/reagent cartridges to $20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$ for $30\text{ minutes}$ prior to loading.
   - For $\mu$-capture IgM assays (`Dengue MAC-ELISA`, `Chikungunya IgM`), solid-phase anti-human $\mu$-chain captures patient $\text{IgM}$ selectively, preventing false-negative competition from high-titer $\text{IgG}$ and minimizing Rheumatoid Factor (`RF`) interference.
2. **Cutoff Index (`COI` / `S/CO` / `Panbio Units`) & Mandatory Grey-Zone Duplicate Verification:**
   - **Non-Reactive:** $< 0.90\text{ COI}$ (or $< 9.0\text{ Panbio/ELISA Units}$).
   - **Equivocal / Grey-Zone:** **$0.90\text{ to }1.10\text{ COI}$** (or $9.0\text{–}11.0\text{ Units}$) $\rightarrow$ Automatically centrifuge sample at $3,000 \times g$ for $10\text{ min}$ and re-assay in duplicate. If still equivocal, issue a **Window-Period Grey-Zone Alert** requesting a convalescent sample in $48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$.
   - **Reactive:** $> 1.10\text{ COI}$ (or $> 11.0\text{ Units}$).
3. **Mandatory Prozone Dilution Protocol (Agglutination Assays):**
   - Slide Widal and Brucella screening tests suffer from false-negative **Prozone Phenomenon** when hyper-immune antibodies saturate antigen binding sites. Any clinically suspected Week 2+ enteric/zoonotic fever must undergo serial tube dilutions from **$1:20, 1:40, 1:80, 1:160, 1:320\text{ to }1:640$**.
4. **Six Sigma IQC & EQAS:**
   - Run 2 levels of third-party controls (Negative Control $< 0.4\text{ COI}$ and Weak-Positive Control $1.5\text{–}2.5\text{ COI}$) with every batch/24h shift. Enforce Westgard $1_{3s} / 2_{2s} / R_{4s}$ rejection rules and participate in CMC Vellore / RML / IAMM-EQAS Infectious Serology cycles.

## 5. Multi-Stratified Reference Intervals & Endemic North Indian Cutoffs

| Patient Stratum | Normal / Non-Reactive Reference Baseline | Positive / Actionable Diagnostic Cutoff |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adult & Pediatric Viral / Zoonotic Serology (`NS1, IgM`)** | **Non-Reactive** ($< 0.90\text{ COI}$ / $< 9.0\text{ Units}$) | **Reactive** ($> 1.10\text{ COI}$ / $> 11.0\text{ Units}$); Equivocal: $0.90\text{–}1.10$ |
| **North Indian / Haryana Endemic `Widal` Baseline** | `TO` $\le 1:80$; `TH` $\le 1:80\text{–}1:160$; `AH/BH` $\le 1:40$ | **`TO` $\ge 1:160$ AND/OR `TH` $\ge 1:160\text{–}1:320$** (or $\ge 4\text{-fold}$ paired rise) |
| **Malaria Thick/Thin Smear & Bivalent RDT** | **No Malarial Parasites Seen (`MPS`)** / RDT Negative | **Any *Plasmodium* stage detected** (Report Species, Stage & $\%$ Parasitemia) |
| **Pregnancy AFI Stratum** | Same cutoffs; watch for biological false-positive $\text{IgM}$ | **Urgent Triage:** Any Dengue/Malaria/Scrub Typhus in pregnancy carries high risk of preterm labor, PPH, or fetal loss |
| **Immunocompromised / Elderly** | Blunted $\text{IgM}$ response; prolonged viremia/bacteremia | Prioritize **Antigen (`NS1`, `HRP2/pLDH`), Microscopy, Blood Culture & RT-PCR** |

## 6. NABL & CLSI GP47 Critical ("Panic") Alerts & Statutory Notification

> [!WARNING]
> **IMMEDIATE (<30 MINUTE) CRITICAL TELEPHONE NOTIFICATION TRIGGERS IN AFI:**
> 1. ***Plasmodium falciparum* Detection (Any Parasitemia)**, mixed *P.f. + P.v.* infection, presence of *P.f.* schizonts in peripheral blood, or **Parasitic Index $\ge 2.0\%$ in non-immune patients ($\ge 5.0\%$ WHO hyperparasitemia)**.
> 2. **Reactive `Dengue NS1 / IgM` accompanied by Critical Hemogram Triggers:** **Platelet Count $< 20,000\text{–}30,000/\mu\text{L}$** OR **$\ge 20\%$ acute rise in Hematocrit (`PCV` $> 50\%\text{–}52\%$)** indicating impending Dengue Shock Syndrome (`DSS`).
> 3. **Positive Automated Blood Culture Gram-Negative Bacilli (*Salmonella Typhi* / *Enterobacterales*)** $\rightarrow$ Immediate alert within $15\text{ minutes}$ of Gram stain read.
> 4. **Statutory IDSP Notification:** All ELISA/CLIA-confirmed Dengue, Chikungunya, Scrub Typhus, Leptospirosis, and slide/RDT-confirmed Malaria cases must be logged in the daily **Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (`IDSP` Form L)** portal for the Chief Medical Officer (CMO), Haryana.

### Smart LIMS Reflex Cascade Rules
- **Rule AFI-1 (Early Fever Days 1–4):** Prioritize `Dengue NS1`, `Malaria Smear + RDT`, and `Blood Culture`. Suppress standalone `Widal` or append an automatic **Early-Window False-Negative Warning**.
- **Rule AFI-2 (Transition Days 5–7):** Reflexively pair `Dengue NS1` with `Dengue IgM/IgG Capture ELISA` and add `Scrub Typhus IgM` if thrombocytopenia persists with negative Dengue/Malaria.
- **Rule AFI-3 (Dual IgM Positivity Check):** If both `Dengue IgM` and `Leptospira/Scrub/Chikungunya IgM` or `Widal` are reactive, flag for **Polyclonal B-Cell Activation / Flavivirus Cross-Reactivity vs True Co-Infection** and recommend confirmatory RT-PCR or paired convalescent titers.

## 8. Analytical Cross-Reactivity, Tropical Co-Infections & ISO 22367 Risk FMEA

1. **Polyclonal $\text{IgM}$ Cross-Reactivity & Anamnestic Widal Recall:** Acute Malaria, Dengue, or Scrub Typhus frequently induces polyclonal B-cell stimulation, causing false-positive `Widal TH/TO`, `TyphiDot IgM`, or low-level `Dengue IgM` cross-reactivity.
2. **Rheumatoid Factor (`RF`) & Heterophile Interference:** High-titer $\text{IgM-RF}$ binds to the $\text{Fc}$ region of antigen-specific $\text{IgG}$, generating false-positive lines on rapid lateral-flow ICT cards for Dengue, Malaria, and Chikungunya.

| Failure Mode (ISO 22367) | Root Cause | Clinical Risk | Mandatory Mitigation Control | Severity $\times$ Prob |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **False-Negative `Dengue IgM` / `Widal` on Day 2 of Fever** | Premature antibody ordering during viremic/bacteremic window | Missed Dengue/Typhoid; delayed warning-sign monitoring | LIMS Day-of-Fever gate + mandatory Window-Period educational shield on report | $\text{High} \times \text{High}$ (`Mitigated`) |
| **False-Negative `Dengue NS1` on Day 6 of Fever** | Host $\text{IgM/IgG}$ immune complexes sequester $\text{NS1}$ antigen | False reassurance during critical defervescence shock window | Reflex `Dengue IgM/IgG Capture` on all fevers $\ge \text{Day 4–5}$ | $\text{High} \times \text{Med}$ (`Mitigated`) |
| **Rapid ICT Card Reported as Confirmed Diagnosis** | Operator skips ELISA confirmation on weak rapid line | False-positive Dengue/Typhoid therapy; missed bacterial sepsis | NCVBDC/ICMR Rapid ICT Screening Disclaimer hardcoded in Section 10 | $\text{Med} \times \text{Med}$ (`Mitigated`) |

## 9. Conditional Band-Specific Pathologist Sign-Out Library

- **`[BAND-NON-REACTIVE-EARLY-DAY-1-4]`:** *"All tested AFI markers are currently non-reactive. Because specimen collection occurred within the early febrile window (Days 1–4), antibody assays (Dengue IgM, Widal, Scrub Typhus IgM, Chikungunya IgM, Leptospira IgM) have not yet reached seroconversion thresholds. If fever persists beyond Day 5, repeat Dengue IgM Capture ELISA, Quantitative Tube Widal, and serial CBC."*
- **`[BAND-EQUIVOCAL/GREY-ZONE]`:** *"Borderline / Grey-Zone reactivity detected ($0.90\text{–}1.10\text{ COI}$). Verified by repeat duplicate centrifugation testing. This pattern commonly represents early seroconversion during the Day 4–6 immunological transition window or non-specific cross-reactivity. Repeat testing on a fresh serum specimen after 48 to 72 hours is strongly recommended."*
- **`[BAND-REACTIVE]`:** *"Reactive acute febrile marker identified. Correlate with exact day of fever onset, serial Complete Blood Count (specifically Hematocrit/PCV and Platelet trajectory), hepatic/renal biochemistry, and exclude cross-reactive tropical co-infections."*
- **`[BAND-CRITICAL]`:** *"CRITICAL TROPICAL FEVER ALERT: High-risk pathogen/complication identified (*P. falciparum* / hyperparasitemia / severe dengue hemoconcentration or profound thrombocytopenia). Immediate clinical correlation and inpatient hemodynamic monitoring advised."*

   • Rapid Immunochromatographic (ICT) Card tests are rapid screening assays

     rapid tests must be confirmed by Capture ELISA / CLIA / RT-PCR.
   • A single negative Malaria smear/RDT does NOT rule out Malaria; repeat 
     every 6-12 hours for 36-48 hours during febrile paroxysms if suspected.

     reactivity during Malaria, Dengue, or Viral fevers.

     have had a fever. Early tests check for the germ directly, while tests 
     after Day 5 check for your body's immune antibodies. Always monitor daily
