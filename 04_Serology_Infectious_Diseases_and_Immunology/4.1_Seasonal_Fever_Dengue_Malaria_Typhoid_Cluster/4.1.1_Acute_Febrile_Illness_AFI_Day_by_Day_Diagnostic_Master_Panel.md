## 1. Document Control, Standardized Coding & Master Test Panel Bundling

### 1.1 Master Test Panel Scope & Analytical Bundling
The Acute Febrile Illness (AFI) Master Panel at Sawariya Diagnostic Lab is designed as a time-sensitive, day-anchored diagnostic cluster for patients presenting with acute undifferentiated fever ($\ge 38.0^\circ\text{C} / 100.4^\circ\text{F}$) in Haryana. The panel integrates serological, hematological, microbiological, and biochemical markers into a unified diagnostic algorithm:
1. **Dengue Evaluation:** Dengue NS1 Antigen (CLIA / ELISA) + Dengue IgM / IgG Capture Immunoassay.
2. **Malarial Evaluation:** Rapid Dual Antigen RDT (*Pf* HRP-2 / *Pv* pLDH) + Leishman/Giemsa-Stained Peripheral Blood Smear (Thick and Thin) for species identification, quantification, and gametocyte/schizont staging.
3. **Enteric Fever Evaluation:** BACTEC Automated Aerobic Blood Culture (pre-antimicrobial) + Quantitative Tube Widal Agglutination (serial tube dilutions $1:20$ to $1:640$) + TyphiDot Rapid IgM/IgG.
4. **Zoonotic Vector Evaluation:** Scrub Typhus IgM ELISA (recombinant 56-kDa major outer membrane protein antigen) + Leptospira IgM ELISA / Screen.
5. **Systemic End-Organ & Hemodynamic Monitoring:** Complete Blood Count (CBC) with automated/manual Platelet Count and Hematocrit (`PCV`), Liver Function Tests (SGPT/ALT, SGOT/AST, Total and Conjugated Bilirubin), and Renal Function Test (Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea).

### 1.3 Hardware-Agnostic Analytical Platforms & Traceability Hierarchy
- **Chemiluminescence Immunoassay (CLIA):** Snibe MAGLUMI X3 (Flash CLIA using `ABEI` label, magnetic microbead solid phase). Calibration traceable to WHO International Standards / Internal Master Calibrators.
- **Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay (ELISA):** Bio-Rad PR 4100 / Mindray MR-96A Microplate Reader with automated washer. Wavelengths $450\text{ nm} / 630\text{ nm}$. Traceable to National Institute of Virology (NIV) Pune Dengue reference panels and InBios Scrub Typhus international standards.
- **Automated Hematology Analyzer:** 5-Part Differential Analyzer (Mindray BC-5150 / Sysmex XN-350) calibrated using commercial whole blood calibrators traceable to `ICSH` reference method.
- **Clinical Chemistry Analyzer:** Semi-automated / Automated Chemistry (Erba Chem 7 / EM-200) calibrated with multi-parameter calibrators traceable to `IFCC` primary reference materials (`ERM-DA474/IFCC`).
- **Microscopy & Automated Culture:** Olympus CX23 binocular microscope with calibrated ocular micrometer for parasite density; BD BACTEC FX40 automated blood culture instrument monitoring continuous $\text{CO}_2$ production.

### 1.4 Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Window-Period Gating & HIL Interference Management
1. **Mandatory Fever-Day Clinical Anchor:**
   - Phlebotomists and accessioning technicians must explicitly interrogate and document the **Exact Number of Completed Days of Fever** (`Day 1` defined as the initial 24-hour period of documented temperature $\ge 38.0^\circ\text{C} / 100.4^\circ\text{F}$) and prior antibiotic/antimalarial usage.
2. **Specimen Collection Protocol:**
   - **Serum Separator Tube (SST / Gold Top, 4.0 mL):** Centrifuged at $1,500\times g$ for 10 minutes within 2 hours of collection for Dengue serology, Scrub Typhus ELISA, Leptospira ELISA, Widal, and Biochemistry.
   - **$\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ Tube (Lavender Top, 3.0 mL):** Inverted 8–10 times immediately. Used for CBC, Platelet count, Hematocrit, Malaria Smear (thick and thin slides prepared within 1 hour to prevent erythrocyte crenation and parasite exflagellation), and Malaria Dual Ag RDT.
   - **Aerobic Blood Culture Bottle (BD BACTEC Plus Aerobic/F, 8.0–10.0 mL adult; 1.0–3.0 mL pediatric):** Inoculated under sterile technique prior to initiation of empirical antimicrobials.
3. **Quantitative HIL Rejection & Interferences (`CLSI EP07 / EP37`):**
   - **Hemolysis:** Gross hemolysis ($\text{Free Hb} > 200\text{ mg/dL}$) causes non-specific absorbance elevation on ELISA ($450\text{ nm}$) and falsely obscures tube agglutination. Specimen must be rejected and redrawn.
   - **Lipemia:** Triglycerides $> 500\text{ mg/dL}$ produce light scatter in turbidimetric assays; requires high-speed clarification ($10,000\times g$ for 10 min) before pipetting.
   - **Icterus:** Bilirubin up to $25\text{ mg/dL}$ tolerated on Maglumi X3 CLIA; visual agglutination checks for Widal require experienced reading under indirect white light.

### 2.1 Cellular & Molecular Pathophysiology of Fever Dynamics
1. **Pyrogenic Cytokine Cascade:** Microbial invasion triggers pattern-recognition receptors (TLR-4 on Gram-negative endotoxin, TLR-2 on spirochetes, TLR-3/7/8 on viral RNA). Monocytes and macrophages release endogenous pyrogens ($\text{IL-1}\beta$, $\text{TNF-}\alpha$, $\text{IL-6}$, $\text{IFN-}\gamma$), which cross the blood-brain barrier at the organum vasculosum laminae terminalis (OVLT). Local vascular endothelial cells upregulate Cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) and microsomal Prostaglandin E Synthase-1 (mPGES-1), synthesizing Prostaglandin $\text{E}_2$ ($\text{PGE}_2$). $\text{PGE}_2$ binds to EP3 receptors in the preoptic area of the anterior hypothalamus, elevating the thermoregulatory set-point and initiating cutaneous vasoconstriction (chills) and shivering thermogenesis (rigors).
2. **Dengue NS1 Kinetics & Endothelial Disruption:** DENV NS1 is synthesized as an intracellular monomer, dimerizes on membranes, and is secreted as a high-density lipoprotein-like hexamer ($t_{1/2} \approx 48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$). Circulating soluble NS1 directly binds toll-like receptor 4 (TLR-4) on human vascular endothelial cells and induces secretion of Matrix Metalloproteinase-9 (MMP-9) and Heparanase, degrading the endothelial glycocalyx layer (shedding syndecan-1 and heparan sulfate). This glycocalyx breakdown triggers hyper-permeability and massive plasma leakage.
3. **Malarial Intra-Erythrocytic Schizogony:** Merozoites invade erythrocytes. *P. vivax* preferentially parasitizes young reticulocytes via Duffy Antigen Receptor for Chemokines (DARC); *P. falciparum* invades erythrocytes of all ages via Glycophorin A/B/C, generating exponential parasitemia. As mature schizonts rupture every 48 hours, they release hemozoin, parasite toxins, and merozoites, driving synchronous paroxysmal rigors. Sequestration is mediated by *P. falciparum* erythrocyte membrane protein 1 (*Pf*EMP-1) adhering to host receptors (CD36, ICAM-1, EPCR) on vascular endothelium, concealing mature parasites from splenic clearance.
4. **Enteric & Zoonotic Pathodynamics:** *Salmonella enterica* invades M-cells of Peyer's patches, translocates to mesenteric lymph nodes, and undergoes intracellular survival within reticuloendothelial macrophages. Primary bacteremia occurs during Week 1, followed by secondary bacteremia and intestinal reinfection via bile in Week 2. *Orientia tsutsugamushi* targets vascular endothelial cells of arterioles, capillaries, and venules, inducing systemic rickettsial lymphohistiocytic vasculitis, microvascular leakage, and eschar formation. *Leptospira interrogans* disseminates hematogenously, binding fibronectin and collagen, causing extensive capillary endothelial injury, subconjunctival hemorrhage, and acute tubular injury.

### 2.2 Day 1 to Day 3 of Fever (Early Acute Phase: Viremic, Bacteremic & Parasitic Window)
1. **Mandatory Diagnostic Workup:**
   - **Dengue NS1 Antigen Assay:** Highest clinical sensitivity ($> 90\text{–}95\%$) on automated CLIA or microplate ELISA. Establishes confirmed diagnosis within 24–48 hours of fever onset.
   - **Malaria Evaluation:** Thick peripheral blood smear ($20\times$ more sensitive than thin smear; Limit of Detection $\sim 10\text{–}20\text{ parasites}/\mu\text{L}$) + Thin blood smear for species differentiation and parasitic index calculation + Dual Antigen RDT (*Pf* HRP-2 / *Pv* pLDH).
   - **Pre-Antimicrobial Blood Culture:** Inoculation of $8\text{–}10\text{ mL}$ whole blood into BD BACTEC aerobic culture bottles prior to empiric administration of Ceftriaxone, Azithromycin, or Doxycycline. Yield exceeds $80\text{–}85\%$ during the first 72 hours.
   - **Baseline Complete Blood Count:** Absolute baseline documentation of Hematocrit (`PCV`), Total Leukocyte Count (TLC), and Platelet count.
2. **Tests Strictly Contraindicated / Inappropriate:**
   - **Widal Tube/Slide Agglutination:** Antibodies to *S. enterica* O and H antigens are completely absent or reflect uninformative endemic background titers. Reporting positive Widal on Day 1–3 creates false confidence and inappropriate antibiotic therapy.
   - **Dengue IgM / TyphiDot IgM:** Seroconversion has not yet occurred (sensitivity $< 20\%$).

### 2.3 Day 4 to Day 6 of Fever (Critical Transition Window: Immunological Crossover & Shock Phase)
1. **Mandatory Diagnostic Workup:**
   - **Dengue NS1 + IgM Combo Testing:** Essential crossover window. Circulating free NS1 levels decline due to host anti-NS1 IgM binding; standalone NS1 testing yields false negatives in up to $30\text{–}40\%$ of secondary infections. Standalone IgM testing may still be borderline. Testing both antigen and antibody achieves diagnostic sensitivity $> 95\%$.
   - **Hematocrit (`PCV`) Kinetic Surveillance:** Hematocrit must be monitored every 12 to 24 hours. A sudden rise of $\ge 20\%$ above baseline (e.g., jump from $35\%$ to $42\%$, or absolute `PCV` $> 48\text{–}50\%$) is the cardinal objective biomarker of **plasma leakage**, signaling impending Dengue Hemorrhagic Fever (`DHF`) and hypovolemic Dengue Shock Syndrome (`DSS`).
   - **Platelet Nadir Monitoring:** Thrombocytopenia typically reaches its nadir during Days 4–6 due to immune-mediated platelet clearance and megakaryocyte suppression.
   - **Scrub Typhus IgM ELISA:** *Orientia tsutsugamushi* IgM antibodies reach detectable levels ($\text{OD} > 0.50$ or $\text{Index} > 1.10$) by Day 5–6 post-chigger bite.

### 2.4 Day 7+ of Fever (Late Acute / Convalescent Phase & Delayed Complications)
1. **Mandatory Diagnostic Workup:**
   - **Quantitative Tube Widal Agglutination:** Becomes clinically meaningful only after Day 7. Diagnostic criterion requires $\ge 4$-fold rise in paired titers taken 7–10 days apart, or single cut-off titers exceeding local endemic thresholds ($\text{TO} \ge 1:160$, $\text{TH} \ge 1:160$).
   - **TyphiDot IgM/IgG:** Outer membrane protein (OMP) IgM antibodies detectable.
   - **Zoonotic Serology:** Leptospira IgM ELISA / MAT and Scrub Typhus IgM ELISA demonstrate maximum clinical sensitivity ($> 95\%$).
   - **Complication Screening:** LFT/RFT for hepatorenal dysfunction, secondary hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (HLH: Ferritin $> 10,000\text{ ng/mL}$, severe cytopenias), and bacterial superinfections.

### 4.2 Automated Method Precision & Linearity Verification (`CLSI EP15-A3` & `EP06-Ed2`)
1. **Precision Protocol (`CLSI EP15-A3`):** Evaluated over 5 consecutive operating days, 5 replicates per run ($5\times 5$ model) on the Snibe MAGLUMI X3 analyzer using low-positive and high-positive control pools:
   - *Dengue NS1 Antigen:* Repeatability (within-run) $\text{CV}_r = 3.82\%$; Intermediate precision (within-lab) $\text{CV}_{wl} = 5.14\%$.
   - *Dengue IgM Capture:* Within-run $\text{CV}_r = 4.12\%$; Within-lab $\text{CV}_{wl} = 5.86\%$. Both assays meet the biological variation goal ($\text{CV}_A \le 0.5 \times \text{CV}_I$).
2. **Analytical Linearity & Carryover Protocol (`CLSI EP06-Ed2`):**
   - Linearity assessed across 7 serial dilutions of high-titer NS1 serum ($0.10$ to $50.0\text{ COI}$). Polynomial regression verified linear fit with $R^2 = 0.994$, showing non-significant non-linearity ($p > 0.05$).
   - Reagent probe carryover evaluated by running alternating High-Positive ($H_1, H_2, H_3$) and Low-Negative ($L_1, L_2, L_3$) samples:
     $$\text{Carryover \%} = \frac{L_1 - L_3}{H_3 - L_3} \times 100$$
     Calculated carryover was $0.018\%$ (well below the permissible threshold of $< 0.10\%$), validating the high-pressure wash protocol between samples.

### 4.3 Lot-to-Lot Kit Verification Protocol (`CLSI EP12-A2`)
Prior to releasing any newly received batch or lot of rapid ICT cards, ELISA microplates, or CLIA cartridges into routine clinical service, the Serology Quality Supervisor must complete the following protocol:
1. **Specimen Selection:** Minimum of 20 characterized serum/whole blood specimens (10 known verified positives covering low, medium, and high titers; 10 confirmed non-reactive negatives including potential cross-reactors like rheumatoid factor, lipemic, and icteric samples).
2. **Concordance Threshold:** New lot must demonstrate $\ge 95\%$ overall agreement with the current in-use validated lot and reference method. Cohen's Kappa coefficient ($\kappa$) must be $\ge 0.85$.
3. **Internal Quality Control (IQC):** Every test run must include manufacturer-provided or third-party negative control and weak-positive control ($1.5\text{–}2.5\times$ cut-off).
4. **Corrective Action:** If kit verification fails concordant thresholds, the entire lot is quarantined, the vendor is served an NABL-compliant non-conformance notice (`Form NC-LOG`), and verification records are archived for 3 years.

### 5.1 Critical Panic Alert Triggers
1. **Severe Thrombocytopenia:** Absolute Platelet Count $< 20,000/\mu\text{L}$ (Profound risk of spontaneous intracranial, pulmonary, or gastrointestinal hemorrhage in Dengue/Malaria).
2. **Cardinal Plasma Leakage Shock Warning:** Hematocrit (`PCV`) $> 50.0\%$ (Adult Male) or $> 44.0\%$ (Adult Female), or an **acute rise of $\ge 20\%$ above baseline measurement** (Direct indication of impending Dengue Shock Syndrome requiring immediate intravenous crystalloid resuscitation).
3. **Severe / Cerebral Malaria Risk:**
   - Detection of *Plasmodium falciparum* with **Parasitemia Index $> 2.0\%$** ($> 100,000\text{ parasites}/\mu\text{L}$) in non-immune individuals (or $> 5.0\%$ in semi-immune individuals).
   - Presence of *P. falciparum* **schizonts or mature trophozoites** in peripheral blood smear (indicates deep vascular sequestration and high risk of cerebral malaria).
   - Any mixed *P. falciparum* + *P. vivax* infection with severe anemia ($\text{Hb} < 5.0\text{ g/dL}$).
4. **Hepatorenal Failure (Weil's Syndrome / Sepsis):** Total Serum Bilirubin $> 3.0\text{ mg/dL}$ concurrent with Serum Creatinine $> 2.0\text{ mg/dL}$ in any acutely febrile patient.
5. **Critical Microbiological Alert:** Blood culture bottle flagging positive with Gram-negative bacilli on preliminary smear (indicates acute bacteremia / *Salmonella* septicemia).

### 5.3 Transfusion Triage Guidelines & Hemotherapy Protocol in Acute Febrile Illness
In compliance with `WHO`, `NVBDCP`, and `Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS)` national clinical practice guidelines, Sawariya Diagnostic Lab provides evidence-based transfusion support guidance to prevent inappropriate blood product utilization:
1. **Platelet Transfusion Rationalization in Dengue & Malaria:**
   - **Prophylactic Transfusion Restriction:** Prophylactic platelet transfusion is **NOT indicated** in stable patients with platelet count $> 10,000\text{–}20,000/\mu\text{L}$ in the absence of active clinical bleeding. Randomized controlled trials demonstrate that prophylactic transfusions do not prevent severe hemorrhage, do not accelerate platelet recovery, and increase the risk of Transfusion-Related Acute Lung Injury (`TRALI`), fluid overload, and febrile non-hemolytic transfusion reactions (`FNHTR`).
   - **Actionable Transfusion Criteria:**
     - *Emergency Therapeutic Transfusion:* Platelets $< 50,000/\mu\text{L}$ in the presence of active, significant clinical bleeding (hematemesis, melena, profuse epistaxis, abnormal uterine bleeding).
     - *Prophylactic High-Risk Threshold:* Platelets $< 10,000/\mu\text{L}$ even without overt bleeding, due to exponentially increased risk of spontaneous intracranial hemorrhage.
     - *Surgical Intervention:* Platelets $< 50,000/\mu\text{L}$ prior to emergency invasive procedures.
2. **Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP) & Cryoprecipitate Indications:**
   - In Dengue Shock Syndrome (`DSS`) or complicated falciparum malaria, persistent hemorrhage despite volume resuscitation is often driven by Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (`DIC`) or severe consumption coagulopathy.
   - FFP ($10\text{–}15\text{ mL/kg}$) is indicated when $\text{PT-INR} > 1.5$ or $\text{aPTT} > 1.5\times$ normal control in bleeding patients.
   - Cryoprecipitate ($1\text{ unit}/10\text{ kg}$) is indicated when Plasma Fibrinogen falls below $100\text{ mg/dL}$.
3. **Packed Red Blood Cell (PRBC) Resuscitation:**
   - Indicated in *Plasmodium falciparum* acute intravascular hemolysis when $\text{Hb} < 5.0\text{ g/dL}$ (or $< 7.0\text{ g/dL}$ in cardiovascularly unstable patients) or when internal concealed hemorrhage is unmasked by a sudden, steep drop in hematocrit in refractory Dengue shock.
