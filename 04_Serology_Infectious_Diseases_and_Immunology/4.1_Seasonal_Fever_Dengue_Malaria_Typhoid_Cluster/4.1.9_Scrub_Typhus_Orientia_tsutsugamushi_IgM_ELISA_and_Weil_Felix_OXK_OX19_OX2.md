## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Window-Period Timing, Stability & HIL Rejection Matrix

### Optimal Sampling Window & Post-Monsoon Epidemiology
- **Epidemiological Peak:** Post-monsoon / autumn-winter months (**August to January** in Haryana, Himachal, Punjab, Rajasthan, and across rural/peri-urban India) transmitted by the bite of larval trombiculid mites (**"chiggers"**, *Leptotrombidium deliense*) in scrub vegetation/agricultural fields.
- **Kinetic Window:**
  - **Days 1 to 4 of Fever:** Whole-Blood / Buff-Coat or Eschar-Swab **Nested / Real-Time PCR (`47 kDa` or `56 kDa` gene)** is positive, whereas **`Scrub Typhus IgM` is negative in $50\%\text{–}70\%$ of patients**!
  - **Day 5 to Day 7+ of Fever:** `Scrub Typhus IgM ELISA` turns strongly positive ($> 92\%$ sensitivity). **Weil-Felix `OX-K` only turns positive at Day 10–14!**
- **Specimen Type:** Serum ($2.0\text{ mL}$ from SST / Red-top tube).

### Specimen Stability & Quantitative HIL Rejection Matrix
| Storage Condition | Maximum Stability | Interference Factor | Quantitative Rejection Threshold & Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ambient ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$)** | $\le 8\text{ hours}$ | **Hemolysis Index ($H$)** | Reject if **Free $\text{Hb} > 150\text{ mg/dL}$** (interferes with ELISA TMB OD and masks Weil-Felix tube reading). |
| **Refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$)** | **Up to $7\text{ days}$** | **Lipemia Index ($L$)** | Clarify by high-speed centrifugation if **$\text{TG} > 400\text{ mg/dL}$**. |
| **Frozen ($-20^\circ\text{C}$)** | $6\text{ months}$ | **Rheumatoid Factor (`RF`)** | Use $\text{IgG/RF}$-absorbent diluent in IgM ELISA to eliminate false-positive $\text{IgM-RF}$ bridging. |

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Indian Optical Density (`OD`) Cutoff & Tube Protocol

1. **Scrub Typhus `IgM` Microplate ELISA SOP (InBios / ICMR North Indian Cutoff Calibration):**
   - Dilute patient serum $1:100$ in sample diluent; incubate $100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ per well in recombinant $56\text{ kDa}$ antigen-coated strips at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $30\text{ minutes}$.
   - Wash $6\times$ with $300\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ wash buffer, add $100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ HRP-conjugated anti-human $\text{IgM}$ ($30\text{ min}$ at $37^\circ\text{C}$), wash $6\times$, incubate TMB substrate $10\text{ min}$ in dark, stop with $1\text{N }\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, and read optical density (`OD`) at $450\text{ nm}$ ($620\text{ nm}$ reference).
   - **ICMR / Indian Endemic Optical Density (`OD`) Cutoff Calibration:**
     - **Non-Reactive:** $\text{OD}_{450} < 0.45\text{–}0.50$ (or $\text{COI} < 0.90$).
     - **Equivocal / Grey-Zone:** **$\text{OD}_{450}\text{ } 0.50 \text{ to } 0.79$** (or $\text{COI } 0.90\text{–}1.10$) $\rightarrow$ Repeat in duplicate; if persistent, request convalescent serum in $5\text{–}7\text{ days}$.
     - **Reactive (Positive for Acute Scrub Typhus):** **$\text{OD}_{450} \ge 0.80\text{–}1.00$** (or $\text{COI} > 1.10$ per regional ROC validation in North India).
2. **Weil-Felix Quantitative Tube Agglutination SOP (`OX-K, OX-19, OX-2`):**
   - **NEVER rely on Slide Weil-Felix alone!** Perform serial two-fold tube dilutions ($1:20, 1:40, 1:80, 1:160, 1:320, 1:640$) in $0.85\%\text{ NaCl}$ across 3 rows (`Proteus OX-K`, `OX-19`, `OX-2`) plus Saline Control.
   - Incubate at **$37^\circ\text{C}$ (or $50^\circ\text{C}$ for $4\text{h}$) followed by overnight ($16\text{–}18\text{h}$) at $37^\circ\text{C}$** and read granular somatic (`O`) agglutination at bottom of tubes.

## 6. NABL & CLSI GP47 Critical ("Panic") Alerts & Empirical Doxycycline Rule

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL ICMR THERAPEUTIC DIRECTIVE — NEVER DELAY DOXYCYCLINE / AZITHROMYCIN FOR SEROLOGY:**
> Untreated Scrub Typhus progresses rapidly in Week 2 to **Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (`ARDS` / interstitial pneumonitis), Meningoencephalitis, Myocarditis, Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (`DIC`), Acute Kidney Injury, and Multiorgan Failure (case fatality up to $15\%\text{–}30\%$ if delayed)**—yet responds dramatically within **$24\text{ to }48\text{ hours}$** to oral/IV **Doxycycline ($100\text{ mg}$ BD) or Azithromycin ($500\text{ mg}$ OD in pregnancy/young children)**!
> - **Immediate Alert (<30 min):** Notify the treating physician immediately of any reactive `Scrub Typhus IgM` in a patient with thrombocytopenia, transaminitis, dyspnea, or altered sensorium.

## 7. Kinetic Day-by-Day Rise/Peak/Persistence & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Pathognomonic Clinical Triad & Lab Clues:**
  1. **Painless Necrotic Eschar** with black crust and erythematous halo ($30\%\text{–}60\%$ of cases; hidden in axilla, groin, perineum, waist, or inframammary fold) + regional lymphadenopathy.
  2. **Dengue-Mimicking Thrombocytopenia + Elevated Hepatic Transaminases ($\text{AST/ALT } 100\text{–}400\text{ U/L}$) + Markedly Elevated `CRP` ($> 60\text{–}150\text{ mg/L}$)**. *(Note: Unlike Dengue where `CRP` is usually $< 30\text{ mg/L}$ and WBC is leukopenic, Scrub Typhus typically shows **high `CRP` ($> 60\text{ mg/L}$)**, normal-to-elevated WBC, and hypoalbuminemia!).*
- **Smart LIMS Reflex Cascade:** Any post-monsoon AFI patient with **Thrombocytopenia ($< 100,000/\mu\text{L}$) + Negative `Dengue NS1/IgM` + Negative `Malaria Smear/RDT` + Elevated `AST/ALT` or `CRP` $> 50\text{ mg/L}$** automatically triggers a LIMS recommendation for **`Scrub Typhus IgM ELISA`**.

## 8. Analytical Cross-Reactivity & ISO 22367 Risk FMEA

1. **Causes of False-Positive Weil-Felix (`OX-K / OX-19 / OX-2`):**
   - ***Proteus mirabilis* or *Proteus vulgaris* Urinary Tract Infection / Sepsis** (causes massive `OX-K` / `OX-19` titers $> 1:640$!).
   - **Leptospirosis, Relapsing Fever (*Borrelia*), Malaria, Typhoid Fever, Pregnancy, and Chronic Liver Disease**.
2. **Causes of False-Positive / Persistent `Scrub Typhus IgM ELISA`:**
   - Residual $\text{IgM}$ persisting for **$3\text{ to }6\text{ months}$** after a prior post-monsoon Scrub Typhus episode in agricultural workers; polyclonal cross-reactivity during acute Malaria, Leptospirosis, Dengue, or Rheumatoid Factor positivity.

| Failure Mode (ISO 22367) | Root Cause | Clinical Risk | Mandatory Mitigation Control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scrub Typhus Excluded Because Weil-Felix `OX-K` is Negative** | Weil-Felix has $<50\%$ sensitivity and is negative until Day 10–14 | Fatal Scrub Typhus ARDS / Meningoencephalitis due to withheld Doxycycline | Explicitly state Weil-Felix $<50\%$ sensitivity; mandate `Scrub Typhus 56-kDa IgM ELISA` |
| **False-Negative `Scrub Typhus IgM` on Day 3 of Fever** | Sampled before Day 5–7 IgM seroconversion | Delayed Doxycycline therapy | Include mandatory ICMR Empirical Doxycycline Clause in Section 10 |
