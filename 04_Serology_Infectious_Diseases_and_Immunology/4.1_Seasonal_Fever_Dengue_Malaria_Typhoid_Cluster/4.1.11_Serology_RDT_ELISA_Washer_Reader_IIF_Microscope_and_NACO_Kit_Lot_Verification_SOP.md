## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 112 Serology/Immunology Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the pre-use new-lot verification of all **Lateral-Flow Immunochromatographic Rapid Diagnostic Tests (`RDTs`)** (`Dengue NS1/IgM/IgG`, `Malaria HRP2/pLDH`, `HIV 1&2`, `HBsAg`, `HCV`, `Syphilis TP`, `Typhidot`, `Chikungunya`, `Leptospira`, `Scrub Typhus`), **8/12-Channel Automated `ELISA` Microplate Washer Residual-Volume Verification ($\le 2\ \mu\text{L/well}$)**, **450/630 nm `ELISA` Reader Optical Verification**, **Mechanical `RPR/VDRL` Orbital Rotator Verification ($100 \pm 2\text{ RPM}$, $19\text{ mm}$ circumscribing circle, $20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$)**, **LED/Mercury Epifluorescence Microscope (`ANA HEp-2` / `ANCA` `IIF`) Illumination Tracking**, and **In-House Pooled/Low-Positive Grey-Zone Control (`COI 1.2–2.0`) Preparation**.
2. **NABL 112 Specific Requirement (`Medical Microbiology & Immunology — Serology`):**
   - **Mandatory New-Lot / New-Shipment `RDT` & `ELISA` Verification (`ISO 15189:2022 Cl. 6.6.3`):** Every new lot or new shipment of qualitative/semi-quantitative `RDT` cassettes or `ELISA`/`CLIA` kits MUST undergo **documented pre-use verification using characterized Known Positive (including a Weak/Low-Positive near cutoff) and Known Negative specimens** BEFORE putting the lot into clinical patient testing.
   - **Mandatory Internal Procedural Control (`C-Line`) & External Run-Control Rule:** The internal procedural control line (`C-Line`) on an `RDT` cassette verifies **only capillary liquid migration and conjugate hydration** — it does **NOT** replace daily external Positive and Negative Quality Control sera (`NABL 112`)!

## Section 2: Pre-Use New-Lot & New-Shipment `RDT` / `ELISA` Verification Protocol (`5 Pos + 5 Neg Rule`)
1. **Regulatory & Technical Requirement (`CDSCO / ICMR / NACO / NABL 112`):**
   - Upon receipt of any new lot (or new consignment of the same lot) of `RDT` cassettes (`HIV`, `HBsAg`, `HCV`, `Syphilis`, `Dengue NS1/IgM`, `Malaria HRP2/pLDH`, `Typhoid`, `Scrub Typhus`), inspect cold-chain/ambient temperature logger ($2^\circ\text{C}\text{–}30^\circ\text{C}$; never frozen), intact foil pouch desiccant (`Silica Gel Blue/Orange`, not pink/saturated), and `CDSCO`/`NACO` batch release certificate.
2. **Pre-Use Verification Panel (`Minimum 5 Characterized Positive + 5 Characterized Negative Sera` for New Lot; `2 Pos + 2 Neg` for New Shipment of Same Lot):**
   - **5 Characterized Positive Sera:** Must include at least **2 Weak/Low-Positive Sera ($1.5\times\text{–}3.0\times$ Cutoff / `1+` Band Intensity)** from archived $-20^\circ\text{C}$ EQA/confirmed patient serum bank.
   - **5 Characterized Negative Sera:** Must include at least **1 potentially cross-reactive serum** (e.g., Rheumatoid Factor `RF`-positive, pregnant multiparous, or hypergammaglobulinemic serum) to rule out non-specific false positivity.
   - **Acceptance Criteria:** **$100\%$ Diagnostic Concordance ($5/5$ Positive reactive; $5/5$ Negative non-reactive)** with crisp `C-Line` appearance within the exact manufacturer read window ($15\text{–}20\text{ minutes}$). Affix a green **`"LOT VERIFIED — APPROVED FOR BENCH USE"`** sticker signed by the Consultant Microbiologist.

## Section 3: Preparation & Daily Use of In-House Weak-Positive ("Grey-Zone") Serology Controls (`NABL 112`)
1. **Why Kit-Supplied Controls Are Insufficient Alone (`NABL 112 Mandate`):**
   - Manufacturer kit positive controls typically have very high optical density (`OD > 2.5`) or `CLIA COI > 20`, which fail to detect subtle loss of sensitivity near the diagnostic cutoff (`COI = 1.0`).
2. **Standardized Preparation of In-House Weak-Positive Control (`S/CO` or `COI = 1.5 – 2.5`):**
   - Select a confirmed reactive serum specimen (`HBsAg`, `Anti-HCV`, `HIV-1`, `Syphilis`, `Dengue IgM`), heat-inactivate at $56^\circ\text{C}$ for $30\text{ min}$ (where validated by assay `IFU`), and perform a pilot serial dilution ($1:2, 1:4, 1:8, 1:16, 1:32$) in confirmed pooled negative human serum (containing $0.05\%$ ProClin-300 preservative).
   - Select the dilution that yields a reproducible **`S/CO` (`OD / Cutoff` or `COI`) between $1.50$ and $2.50$** (or `1+` distinct line on `RDT`).
   - Filter through a $0.22\ \mu\text{m}$ sterile membrane, dispense **$250\ \mu\text{L}$ single-use aliquots** into sterile screw-cap cryovials, label with Lot #, Analyte, Target `S/CO` ($\pm 20\%$), and store at **$-20^\circ\text{C}$ (stable $6\text{ months}$)** or **$-80^\circ\text{C}$ ($1\text{ year}$)**. Thaw one vial weekly ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$; never refreeze $> 2\times$).

## Section 4: `ELISA` Microplate Reader & Automated Washer Verification & Maintenance SOP
| Equipment & Parameter | Verification Frequency | NABL 112 / ISO 15189 Acceptance Specification | Mandatory Action if Outside Tolerance |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. `ELISA` Reader Filter Wavelength Accuracy ($405, 450, 492, 620/630\text{ nm}$)** | Semi-Annual | **$\pm 2.0\text{ nm}$** of nominal wavelength (`NIST`/Calibrated Verification Plate) | Replace interference filter (`450 nm` TMB / `630 nm` bichromatic reference) and recalibrate optical carriage. |
| **2. `ELISA` Reader Photometric Linearity, Accuracy & Channel Repeatability** | Monthly (Check Plate) & Semi-Annual | Accuracy **$\pm 1.0\%$ or $\pm 0.010\text{ OD}$** ($0.0\text{–}2.5\text{ OD}$); Imprecision **`CV <= 1.0%`** across all $8$ optical channels | Clean optical lenses and halogen/LED source; realign 96-well plate carrier transport. |
| **3. `ELISA` Washer Dispense Volume Uniformity ($300\ \mu\text{L/well}$)** | Weekly (Gravimetric / Graduated Cylinder) | **$300\ \mu\text{L} \pm 5\%$** across all $8/12$ manifold needles; **zero blocked needles** | Sonicate/pass acupuncture cleaning wire through all $8/12$ dispense and aspiration needles; flush with $0.5\%$ NaOCl + `CLRW` water. |
| **4. `ELISA` Washer Residual Aspiration Volume (`CRITICAL`)** | Daily / Every Run | **Residual volume $\le 2.0\ \mu\text{L/well}$** ($96\text{-well}$ flat-bottom plate weight gain $\le 0.19\text{ g}$ after final aspiration) | Adjust manifold aspiration height ($0.2\text{ mm}$ from well bottom) and vacuum pump pressure; tap plate on lint-free blotter before adding `TMB` substrate (residual conjugate causes false-positive `ELISA` rows!). |

## Section 5: Mechanical `RPR / VDRL` Rotator, Water-Bath ($37^\circ\text{C} / 56^\circ\text{C}$) & `IIF` Fluorescence Microscope SOP
1. **Mechanical `RPR` / `VDRL` Orbital Rotator (`SOP 4.2.10` & `4.2.11`):**
   - **Rotational Speed:** Verify every morning with a digital tachometer / stopwatch: **$100 \pm 2\text{ RPM}$** for `RPR` ($8\text{ min}$ rotation) and **$180 \pm 2\text{ RPM}$** for `VDRL` ($4\text{ min}$ rotation).
   - **Circumscribing Circle Diameter:** Place a pen on the rotating platform against a millimeter grid card $\to$ Must circumscribe a **$19.0\text{ mm} \pm 1.0\text{ mm}$ ($3/4\text{ inch}$)** circle in the horizontal plane.
   - **Antigen Dispensing Needle Calibration:** Verify `RPR` $20\text{-gauge}$ blunt needle delivers **$60 \pm 2\text{ drops/mL}$** ($17\ \mu\text{L/drop}$) and `VDRL` $18\text{-gauge}$ needle delivers **$60 \pm 2\text{ drops/mL}$** vertically.
   - **Ambient Temperature Shield:** Serology room temperature must be maintained at **$23^\circ\text{C}\text{–}29^\circ\text{C}$** ($< 20^\circ\text{C}$ causes false-negative `RPR`; $> 30^\circ\text{C}$ causes false-positive `RPR`!).
2. **Epifluorescence Microscope (`470–490 nm` Blue LED / Mercury Excitation for `ANA HEp-2` `4.3.5` & `ANCA` `4.3.10`):**
   - Log LED/Mercury burner cumulative hours daily; verify dark-room ambient light obscurity, $20\times / 40\times$ Fluotar objectives, and **mandatory Positive (`Homogeneous AC-1` / `Speckled AC-4` Titre Control) + Negative Control wells** on every `HEp-2` slide run.
