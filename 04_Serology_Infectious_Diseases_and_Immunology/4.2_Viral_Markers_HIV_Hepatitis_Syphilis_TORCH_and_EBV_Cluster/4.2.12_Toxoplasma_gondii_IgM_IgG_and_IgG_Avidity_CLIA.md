## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Gestational Timing, Stability & HIL Rejection Matrix

### Pathobiology & The 2-Year `Toxoplasma IgM` Persistence Trap
- **Transmission Sources in India:** Ingestion of **undercooked mutton/pork/poultry containing bradyzoite tissue cysts**, OR ingestion of **unwashed raw vegetables/fruits/water contaminated with sporulated oocysts shed in cat feces**.
- **Why `Toxoplasma IgM` Alone Misleads Clinicians:** After an asymptomatic *Toxoplasma* exposure, `Toxo IgM` appears within **$5\text{ to }14\text{ days}$**, peaks at $1\text{ month}$, and **frequently remains positive for $12\text{ to }24\text{ MONTHS}$ ($1\text{–}2\text{ years}$!)** on modern sensitive CLIA/ELFA analyzers! Consequently, **$> 80\%$ of pregnant women who test positive for `Toxo IgM` in the 1st trimester were actually infected 6 to 18 months BEFORE becoming pregnant**!

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `Toxo IgG Avidity` Calculation & Six Sigma IQC

1. **MAGLUMI X3 CLIA Cutoffs & Grey-Zone Verification:**
   - **`Toxoplasma IgM`:** Non-Reactive $< 0.90\text{ COI}$ ($< 0.80\text{ AU/mL}$); **Equivocal / Grey-Zone $0.90\text{–}1.10\text{ COI}$**; Reactive $> 1.10\text{ COI}$.
   - **`Toxoplasma IgG` (Quantitative `IU/mL`):**
     - **Non-Reactive (Susceptible):** $< 4.0\text{ IU/mL}$ (or $< 1.6\text{–}3.0\text{ IU/mL}$ per specific kit master curve).
     - **Equivocal / Grey-Zone:** **$4.0\text{ to }8.0\text{ IU/mL}$**.
     - **Reactive (Immune / Exposed):** **$\ge 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$**.
2. **`Toxoplasma IgG Avidity Index (%)` SOP & Decision Cutoffs:**
   - **Minimum `IgG` Titer Required for Valid Avidity Testing:** Patient serum must have a reactive `Toxo IgG` above the assay's minimum avidity threshold (typically **$\ge 8.0\text{–}15.0\text{ IU/mL}$**); high-titer sera ($> 200\text{ IU/mL}$) must be pre-diluted into the linear working range before urea treatment so excess antibody does not artificially inflate the Avidity Index!
   - **Avidity Cutoffs:**
     - **Low Avidity ($< 30.0\%\text{–}40.0\%$):** Cannot exclude recent primary infection within the past $3\text{–}4\text{ months}$.
     - **Equivocal / Borderline Avidity ($40.0\%\text{–}59.9\%$):** Indeterminate maturation phase; repeat paired `Toxo IgG` in $2\text{–}3\text{ weeks}$.
     - **High Avidity ($\ge 60.0\%$):** **Definitively excludes primary *Toxoplasma* infection within the preceding $4\text{ months}$ ($16\text{ weeks}$)!** *(Note: Because anti-Toxoplasma therapy with Spiramycin delays avidity maturation, Low Avidity can persist for $> 6\text{–}9\text{ months}$ in treated patients, so **High Avidity rules OUT recent infection, whereas Low Avidity alone does NOT prove recent infection**—always confirm Low Avidity with a paired $2\text{–}3\text{ week}$ `IgG` titer check!).*

## 6. NABL & CLSI GP47 Critical ("Panic") Alerts & Maternal Spiramycin Window

- **Urgent Antenatal Notification:** Any **Low-Avidity ($< 30\%\text{–}40\%$) `Toxo IgG` with Reactive `Toxo IgM`** OR **documented `Toxo IgG` seroconversion from negative to positive during pregnancy** must be communicated promptly to the Obstetrician because early initiation of **Spiramycin within $3\text{ to }4\text{ weeks}$ of maternal seroconversion** reduces placental transmission by up to $60\%$!

## 7. Kinetic Course & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Why a Single High `Toxo IgG` Titer ($> 300\text{ IU/mL}$) Does NOT Prove Acute Infection:**
  - Healthy immunocompetent adults frequently maintain stable `Toxo IgG` titers of $200\text{ to }500\text{ IU/mL}$ for years after a past infection. Only a **$\ge 2\text{- to }3\text{-fold}$ rising `IgG` titer between two paired samples drawn $2\text{–}3\text{ weeks}$ apart** (tested in the same analytical run) or **Low `IgG Avidity`** indicates recent primary infection.
- **Smart LIMS Reflex Cascade:** Every antenatal specimen with **`Toxo IgM` $\ge 0.90\text{ COI}$ AND `Toxo IgG` $\ge 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$** automatically reflexes to **`Toxoplasma IgG Avidity Index (%)`** before final report release!
