## 2. Pre-Analytical Phlebotomy, Window-Period Timing, Stability & HIL Rejection Matrix

### Virological Biology of Core Antigen (`HBcAg`) & Why `Anti-HBc` Is Unique
- **Why Free `HBcAg` Is Not Detected in Serum:** The $21\text{ kDa}$ Hepatitis B Core Antigen (`HBcAg`) forms the internal icosahedral nucleocapsid completely enclosed inside the outer `HBsAg` lipid envelope, so free `HBcAg` does not circulate in serum. However, `HBcAg` is the **most immunogenic protein of HBV**, eliciting high-titer **`Anti-HBc IgM`** at the exact onset of acute hepatic transaminitis (`ALT` surge) and **`Anti-HBc IgG` (`Total`)** that **persists for the entire lifetime of the patient**!
- **The "Core Window Period" (Why Acute HBV Can Be `HBsAg`-Negative!):**
  - In $5\%\text{–}10\%$ of patients with severe or fulminant **Acute Hepatitis B**, massive immune lysis of infected hepatocytes clears circulating `HBsAg` rapidly **weeks before protective `Anti-HBs` appears**. During this **"Core Window Period"**, both `HBsAg` and `Anti-HBs` are **NEGATIVE**, and **`Anti-HBc IgM` is the ONLY positive serological marker of acute HBV**!

### Specimen Stability & Quantitative HIL Rejection Matrix
| Storage Condition | Maximum Stability | Interference Factor | Quantitative Rejection Threshold & Corrective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 3. Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Grey-Zone Verification & Six Sigma IQC

1. **Cutoff Index (`COI`) & High-Titer Acute vs Low-Titer Flare Stratification for `Anti-HBc IgM`:**
   - **`Anti-HBc IgM`:** Non-Reactive $< 0.90\text{ COI}$; **Equivocal / Grey-Zone $0.90\text{–}1.10\text{ COI}$**; Reactive $> 1.10\text{ COI}$.
   - **Important Clinical Nuance (High-COI Acute HBV vs Low-COI Reactivation Flare of Chronic HBV):**
     - In **True Acute Hepatitis B**, `Anti-HBc IgM` is strongly positive (**typically $\text{COI} > 5.0\text{ to }30.0+$** or $> 50\text{ PEI U/mL}$) with low-avidity `Anti-HBc IgG`.
     - During a **Severe Reactivation Flare of Chronic Hepatitis B** (often mimicking acute hepatitis), `Anti-HBc IgM` can transiently turn **weakly-to-moderately positive ($\text{COI } 1.1\text{–}5.0$)**!
2. **`Anti-HBc Total` Competitive Assay Protocol:**
   - Ensure correct directional interpretation of competitive inhibition assays (`S/CO` vs converted `COI`); re-test all Grey-Zone ($0.90\text{–}1.10$) and isolated `Anti-HBc Total (+)` samples in duplicate after recentrifugation.
3. **Six Sigma IQC & EQAS:**
   - Run Negative and Positive Controls daily for both assays ($\text{CV}_A \le 6.5\%$); participate in NABL-accredited EQAS.

## 6. NABL & CLSI GP47 Critical ("Panic") Alerts & Rituximab / Oncology Reactivation Warning

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL ONCOLOGY / RHEUMATOLOGY PRE-IMMUNOSUPPRESSION ALERT (`HBV REACTIVATION`):**
> Even after complete clinical recovery from Hepatitis B (`HBsAg` **Negative**, `Anti-HBc Total` **Positive**, with or without `Anti-HBs`), covalently closed circular DNA (**`cccDNA`**) remains latently integrated inside hepatocyte nuclei for life!
> - **High-Risk Drugs (`>10%–40%` Fatal Reactivation Risk):** B-cell depleting anti-CD20 monoclonal antibodies (**`Rituximab`, `Obinutuzumab`, `Ofatumumab`**), Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation (`HSCT`), anthracyclines, or high-dose corticosteroids ($\ge 20\text{ mg/day}$ Prednisone for $\ge 4\text{ weeks}$).
> - **Mandatory AASLD / EASL / INASL Rule:** All patients must be screened with **BOTH `HBsAg` AND `Anti-HBc Total`** before starting chemotherapy/biologics! Any patient with **`HBsAg (-) / Anti-HBc Total (+)`** receiving **Rituximab / Anti-CD20 or Stem Cell Transplant MUST receive prophylactic `Entecavir` or `Tenofovir`** (continued for $\ge 12\text{–}18\text{ months}$ after stopping Rituximab) to prevent fatal **Reverse Seroconversion / Fulminant HBV Reactivation**!

## 7. Kinetic Day-by-Day Rise/Peak/Persistence & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

- **Kinetics:** `Anti-HBc IgM` appears $1\text{–}2\text{ weeks}$ after `HBsAg` (coinciding with onset of elevated `ALT` and clinical symptoms), peaks at $4\text{–}8\text{ weeks}$, and declines below cutoff within **$3\text{ to }6\text{ months}$**. `Anti-HBc IgG` (`Total`) appears alongside `IgM` and remains positive for **life**.
- **Smart LIMS Reflex Cascade:**
  - In any patient presenting with **Acute Severe Hepatitis (`ALT > 500 U/L`) where `HBsAg`, `Anti-HAV IgM`, `Anti-HEV IgM`, and `Anti-HCV` are all NEGATIVE**, automatically reflex-recommend **`Anti-HBc IgM`** to catch **Fulminant Acute HBV in the Core Window Period**!
