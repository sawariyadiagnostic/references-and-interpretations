## 🔥 4.3a — STANDALONE: Quantitative `CRP`, `ASO / Anti-DNase B`, `RF` & `Anti-CCP`

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — ACUTE-PHASE REACTANTS & RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS SEROLOGY]**

1. **Quantitative C-Reactive Protein (`CRP` — Normal Adult: $< 5.0\text{–}6.0\text{ mg/L}$; Neonatal Sepsis Cutoff: $> 10.0\text{ mg/L}$):**
   - Synthesized by hepatocytes via $\text{IL-6}$ within **$4\text{–}6\text{ hours}$**, peaking at **$36\text{–}48\text{ hours}$** with a constant half-life of **$\sim 19\text{ hours}$**.
   - **Differential Tiers:** $< 5\text{ mg/L}$ (Normal); $6\text{–}40\text{ mg/L}$ (Viral fever, mild inflammation, pregnancy); $40\text{–}200\text{ mg/L}$ (Active bacterial infection, RA/Vasculitis/Crohn's flare, post-op Day 2–3); $> 200\text{ mg/L}$ (Severe bacterial sepsis, necrotizing pancreatitis, major burns). *(Note: Active SLE flares frequently exhibit disproportionately low/normal $\text{CRP}$ alongside high $\text{ESR}$ unless serositis or bacterial superinfection is present!).*

2. **Anti-Streptolysin O (`ASO`) & `Anti-DNase B` (Revised Jones Criteria for Acute Rheumatic Fever & PSGN):**
   - **Age-Specific ULN:** Adults $< 200\text{ IU/mL}$; School-Age Children ($5\text{–}15\text{ yrs}$) $< 240\text{–}300\text{ IU/mL}$.
   - **Kinetics & Skin Infection Caveat:** $\text{ASO}$ rises at $1\text{–}3\text{ weeks}$ and peaks at $3\text{–}5\text{ weeks}$ post-pharyngitis. **Streptococcal pyoderma/impetigo fails to elevate $\text{ASO}$ in $>60\%$ of cases** because dermal free cholesterol oxidizes/inhibits Streptolysin O $\rightarrow$ always order **`Anti-DNase B`** when evaluating Post-Streptococcal Glomerulonephritis (PSGN) after skin infection! **An isolated elevated $\text{ASO}$ without Major/Minor Jones Criteria NEVER diagnoses Acute Rheumatic Fever.**

3. **Rheumatoid Factor (`RF`) & `Anti-CCP (ACPA)` (2010 ACR/EULAR Rheumatoid Arthritis Scoring):**
   - **ACR/EULAR Serology Points:** Negative $\text{RF}$ & $\text{Anti-CCP} = 0\text{ pts}$; Low-Positive ($> 1\times$ to $\le 3\times\text{ULN}$) of either $= 2\text{ pts}$; **High-Positive ($> 3\times\text{ULN}$) of either $= 3\text{ pts}$** (out of $\ge 6/10$ points needed for definite RA).
   - **Specificity Shield:** $\text{Anti-CCP}$ is **$95\%\text{–}98\%$ specific** for erosive RA, whereas $\text{RF}$ is frequently false-positive in healthy elderly ($5\%\text{–}15\%$), Sjögren's syndrome ($70\%\text{–}90\%$), Chronic Hepatitis C/Cryoglobulinemia ($40\%\text{–}70\%$), Tuberculosis, Endocarditis, and Malaria. Conversely, **$20\%\text{–}25\%$ of genuine RA patients are seronegative**.

## 🦋 4.3b — STANDALONE: `ANA` (ICAP `AC-1` to `AC-29`), `Anti-dsDNA` & `ENA 17-Antigen Profile`

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — ANA ICAP PATTERNS, ANTI-dsDNA & ENA IMMUNOBLOT MATRIX]**

**Gold-Standard `HEp-2 IIF` ICAP Pattern-to-Antigen Reflex Guide:**
| ICAP Code & Staining Pattern | Target Autoantigens | Primary Clinical Associations & Mandatory Reflex Tests |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`AC-1` Nuclear Homogeneous** | $\text{dsDNA}$, Nucleosomes, Histones | **Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)**, Drug-Induced Lupus (`Anti-Histone`), Autoimmune Hepatitis Type 1. *Reflex: Quantitative `Anti-dsDNA`, `C3/C4`, `ENA`.* |
| **`AC-2` Nuclear Dense Fine Speckled (`DFS70`)** | $\text{DFS70 / LEDGF}$ ($70\text{ kDa}$) | **Negative Predictor of SARD:** When monospecific (without pathogenic ENA), strongly argues **against** SLE/SSc/Sjögren's in healthy individuals! |
| **`AC-3` Centromere** | $\text{CENP-A / CENP-B}$ | **Limited Cutaneous Systemic Sclerosis (CREST Syndrome)**, Primary Biliary Cholangitis (`PBC`). |
| **`AC-4` / `AC-5` Nuclear Fine & Coarse Speckled** | $\text{SS-A/Ro60, SS-B/La, Sm, U1-nRNP, Mi-2, Ku}$ | **SLE** (`Anti-Sm` is $99\%$ specific), **Mixed Connective Tissue Disease (`MCTD` — `U1-nRNP`)**, **Sjögren's Syndrome & Neonatal Lupus/Congenital Heart Block (`Ro/La`)**, Dermatomyositis (`Mi-2`). |
| **`AC-8` / `AC-9` / `AC-10` Nucleolar** | $\text{PM/Scl, Fibrillarin (U3-RNP), RNA Pol III}$ | **Diffuse Systemic Sclerosis (Scleroderma)**, Scleroderma-Myositis Overlap. |
| **`AC-29` DNA Topoisomerase I (`Scl-70`-like)** | $\text{DNA Topoisomerase I (Scl-70)}$ | **Diffuse Cutaneous Systemic Sclerosis (`dcSSc`)** with high risk of **Interstitial Lung Disease (`ILD`)**. |
| **`AC-19` / `AC-20` Cytoplasmic Speckled** | $\text{Jo-1}$ (tRNA Synthetases), $\text{Ribosomal P}$ | **Antisynthetase Syndrome / Polymyositis (`Anti-Jo-1`)**; **Neuropsychiatric SLE / Lupus Hepatitis (`Rib-P`)**. |
| **`AC-21` Cytoplasmic Reticular (`AMA-like`)** | $\text{AMA-M2}$ (Pyruvate Dehydrogenase $\text{E2}$) | **Primary Biliary Cholangitis (`PBC`)**. |

- **🛡️ `ANA` Methodology & Healthy Population Shield:** By **2019 ACR/EULAR SLE Criteria**, $\text{ANA} \ge 1:80$ on $\text{HEp-2 IIF}$ is the mandatory entry criterion. Solid-phase CLIA/ELISA CTD screens miss up to $15\%\text{–}25\%$ of nucleolar/cytoplasmic/mitotic autoantibodies. Conversely, low-titer $\text{ANA}$ ($1:80$) occurs in **$13\%$ of healthy individuals**; clinical correlation and reflex **17-Antigen ENA Profile + Quantitative `Anti-dsDNA`** are essential.

## 🌾 4.3c — STANDALONE: Celiac Serology (`ESPGHAN`), Antiphospholipid (`APS`) & `ANCA / Anti-GBM` Vasculitis

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — CELIAC ESPGHAN, APS 12-WEEK & ANCA/GBM CRITICAL SHIELD]**

1. **Celiac Disease Serology (`Anti-tTG IgA/IgG, EMA, DGP` — Gluten-Containing Diet Mandate):**
   - **Pre-Test Gluten Rule:** Patient **MUST be consuming gluten daily ($\ge 3\text{–}10\text{ g/day}$ in $\ge 1$ meal/day) for at least $6\text{ to }8\text{ weeks}$** prior to blood draw; testing after starting a Gluten-Free Diet (GFD) causes false-negative results!
   - **Selective $\text{IgA}$ Deficiency Check:** Occurs in $2\%\text{–}3\%$ of Celiac patients ($10\text{–}15\times$ general population). Always check **Serum Total $\text{IgA}$**; if $< 7\text{ mg/dL}$, rely on **`Anti-tTG IgG` and `Anti-DGP IgG`**.
   - **ESPGHAN 2020 No-Biopsy Criteria (Children):** Duodenal biopsy may be omitted in symptomatic children only if **`Anti-tTG IgA` is $\ge 10\times\text{ULN}$** AND confirmed by a positive **`Anti-Endomysial Antibody (EMA IgA)` on a second separate blood sample**.

2. **Antiphospholipid Syndrome (`aCL IgG/IgM` & `Anti-β2GPI IgG/IgM` — Sydney / 2023 ACR-EULAR Criteria):**
   - **12-Week Persistence Mandate:** Transient low-titer antiphospholipid antibodies are common during acute viral/bacterial/malarial fevers. Definite APS requires **persistent Medium-to-High Titer positivity ($\ge 40\text{ GPL/MPL}$ or $> 99\text{th percentile}$) on two or more occasions at least $12\text{ weeks}$ apart**, tested alongside **3-Step Coagulation Lupus Anticoagulant (`LA` — dRVVT/SCT)**.

3. **`ANCA` Vasculitis (`c-ANCA/PR3`, `p-ANCA/MPO`) & `Anti-GBM` (Goodpasture Syndrome):**
   - **2017 International Consensus:** High-quality **antigen-specific `Anti-PR3` (Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis [`GPA`]) and `Anti-MPO` (Microscopic Polyangiitis [`MPA`] / `EGPA`) immunoassays** are the primary first-line screening tests over ethanol-fixed neutrophil IIF alone.
   - **🚨 CRITICAL PANIC VALUE:** Any positive **`Anti-GBM`**, **`Anti-PR3`**, or **`Anti-MPO`** in a patient with hemoptysis or rapidly rising creatinine (**Pulmonary-Renal Syndrome / Rapidly Progressive Glomerulonephritis [`RPGN`]**) requires **immediate <30-minute critical telephone notification** to initiate emergency plasmapheresis/immunosuppression before irreversible dialysis dependency occurs!

## 🫁 4.3d — STANDALONE: `HLA-B27`, `Total/Specific IgE`, `Mantoux TST` & `IGRA (QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus)`

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — HLA-B27, ALLERGY IgE & TB IMMUNOLOGY (MANTOUX / IGRA)]**

1. **`HLA-B27` Typing (Real-Time PCR vs Flow Cytometry):**
   - **Methodology:** Real-Time PCR (exon 2/3 allele-specific amplification) avoids false-positive cross-reactivity with **`HLA-B7`, `HLA-B22`, `HLA-B40`, and `HLA-B42`** seen with monoclonal anti-HLA-B27 (`FD705/ABC-m3`) flow cytometry antibodies in Indian populations (which carry high `HLA-B*27:05` and `HLA-B*27:04` subtype frequencies).
   - **Genetic Carrier Shield:** Present in **$6\%\text{–}8\%$ of healthy North Indians**; $>90\%$ of healthy $\text{HLA-B27}(+)$ individuals never develop Ankylosing Spondylitis.

2. **Tuberculosis Immunology — `Mantoux TST` ($5\text{ TU}$) & 4-Tube `IGRA` (`QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus`):**
   - **QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus 4-Tube Subtraction Matrix:**
     - **Tube 1 (`Nil`):** Background $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ ($\text{IU/mL}$; must be $\le 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$).
     - **Tube 2 (`TB1` — $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell peptides from $\text{ESAT-6 / CFP-10}$)** & **Tube 3 (`TB2` — $\text{CD4}^+ + \text{CD8}^+$ cytotoxic T-cell peptides):** Positive if **`TB1 - Nil` OR `TB2 - Nil` is $\ge 0.35\text{ IU/mL}$ AND $\ge 25\%$ of `Nil` value**.
     - **Tube 4 (`Mitogen` — PHA Positive Control):** Must have **`Mitogen - Nil` $\ge 0.50\text{ IU/mL}$**; if $< 0.50\text{ IU/mL}$ with negative TB1/TB2, report as **Indeterminate (T-Cell Anergy / Pre-analytical delay / Immunosuppression)**.
   - **🛡️ MANDATORY WHO / ICMR / NTEP MEDICO-LEGAL SHIELD (MANTOUX & IGRA):**
     - **Cannot Differentiate Latent TB (`LTBI`) from Active TB Disease:** Approximately **$30\%\text{–}40\%$ of healthy Indian adults** have Latent TB sensitization and test positive on Mantoux and $\text{IGRA}$ for life. **Never diagnose or treat Active Tuberculosis based on Mantoux or $\text{IGRA}$ alone** (use `CBNAAT / GeneXpert MTB/RIF`, `Truenat`, `AFB Smear`, or `MGIT Liquid Culture`). Conversely, **$20\%\text{–}25\%$ of severe Active/Miliary/HIV TB cases test falsely negative (anergic)**! *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
