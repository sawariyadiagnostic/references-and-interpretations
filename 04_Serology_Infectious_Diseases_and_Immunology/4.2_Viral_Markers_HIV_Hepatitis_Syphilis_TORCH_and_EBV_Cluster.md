## ⚠️ 4.2a — STANDALONE: `HIV 1 & 2` 4th-Gen Screening Assay *(Statutory Indian Medico-Legal Shield)*

> [!CAUTION]
> **CRITICAL STATUTORY RULE FOR INDIAN LABORATORIES (NACO, HIV/AIDS ACT 2017 & NCDRC PRECEDENT):**
> 1. **NEVER report a single screening test (Rapid Card, Tri-Dot, ELISA, or Maglumi X3 CLIA) as `"HIV POSITIVE"`.** Always report the result value as **`"Non-Reactive"`** or **`"Initially Reactive / Indeterminate — Refer for Confirmatory Testing"`**.
> 2. Under **NACO Strategy III**, a diagnosis of HIV infection in an asymptomatic individual requires **THREE sequential reactive tests (`Assay 1 + Assay 2 + Assay 3`)** using three different antigen configurations/principles (or confirmatory **HIV-1/2 Western Blot / Line Immunoassay / Quantitative HIV-1/2 RNA Viral Load PCR**) coupled with mandatory pre- and post-test counseling (ICTC).

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — HIV 1 & 2 SCREENING INTERPRETATION & STATUTORY LEGAL SHIELD]**

**Clinical & Analytical Overview:**  
This assay is an **initial screening test** for antibodies to Human Immunodeficiency Virus Type 1 ($\text{HIV-1}$, Group M and Group O) and Type 2 ($\text{HIV-2}$) and **$\text{HIV-1 p24}$ core capsid antigen** (via 4th-Generation Combo CLIA/ECLIA/ELISA). Inclusion of $\text{p24}$ antigen shortens the diagnostic window period to **$14\text{–}18\text{ days}$** post-exposure.

**Interpretation of Screening Result:**
- **Non-Reactive (Negative) Screening Result ($\text{COI / S/CO} < 0.90\text{–}1.00$):** Indicates absence of detectable $\text{HIV-1/2}$ antibodies and $\text{p24}$ antigen at the time of collection. **However, a Non-Reactive result does NOT exclude recent $\text{HIV}$ exposure within the immunological "Window Period"** ($14\text{–}28\text{ days}$ for 4th-Generation $\text{Ag/Ab}$ Combo assays; up to **$3\text{ to }12\text{ weeks}$ [$90\text{ days}$]** for 3rd-Generation Antibody/Rapid tests). If recent high-risk exposure is suspected, repeat testing after **4 to 12 weeks** or immediate **Qualitative/Quantitative $\text{HIV-1}$ RNA PCR (NAT)** is recommended.
- **Initially Reactive / Borderline / Grey-Zone Screening Result ($\text{COI} \ge 0.90\text{–}1.00$):** **THIS IS NOT A CONFIRMED DIAGNOSIS OF HIV INFECTION.** Screening immunoassays are engineered with ultra-high sensitivity ($> 99.8\%$) for blood/clinical safety, which inherently produces **biological false-positive reactions** (especially at low Signal-to-Cutoff ratios $\text{S/CO } 1.0\text{–}15.0$) in low-prevalence populations.

**🛡️ MANDATORY STATUTORY MEDICO-LEGAL SHIELD (NACO GUIDELINES & HIV/AIDS ACT 2017):**
1. **Mandatory Confirmatory Testing Protocol (NACO Strategy III):** Per **National AIDS Control Organization (NACO, Govt. of India)** guidelines, **no clinical, personal, employment, surgical refusal, marital, or antiretroviral therapy decision may be made on a single reactive $\text{HIV}$ screening test**. Any initially reactive specimen must first be re-tested in duplicate on the same sample, and if repeatedly reactive, must undergo **confirmatory evaluation using two additional independent assays of different antigenic principles/manufacturers (`A1 + A2 + A3` NACO Strategy III / ICTC referral), $\text{HIV-1/2}$ Differentiation Immunoblot, and/or Quantitative $\text{HIV-1/2}$ RNA Viral Load (RT-PCR)** on a fresh venous blood sample.
2. **Documented Causes of Biological False-Positive $\text{HIV}$ Screening Reactions:** **Pregnancy (especially multiparous women)**, recent vaccinations (Influenza, Hepatitis B, Rabies, Tetanus, COVID-19), acute viral infections (Dengue, EBV, CMV, Viral Hepatitis), **Malaria**, **Tuberculosis / Leprosy**, autoimmune connective tissue disorders (SLE, Rheumatoid Factor, Antiphospholipid antibodies), polyclonal hypergammaglobulinemia, chronic renal failure/hemodialysis, multiple blood transfusions, or HAMA/heterophile antibodies.
3. **Infant Testing Caveat ($< 18\text{ Months}$):** Maternal $\text{IgG}$ antibodies cross the placenta; serological antibody tests in infants under 18 months reflect maternal exposure—definitive Early Infant Diagnosis (EID) requires **$\text{HIV-1}$ Total Nucleic Acid (TNA) / Proviral DNA PCR on Dried Blood Spot (DBS) or EDTA whole blood**.
4. **Statutory Confidentiality & Non-Discrimination Clause:** Issued in strict compliance with **Chapters IV & V of The HIV and AIDS (Prevention and Control) Act, 2017**. Disclosure is restricted exclusively to the tested individual (or legal guardian) and authorized healthcare providers directly involved in care. Denial of healthcare/surgical services based on HIV status is a punishable statutory offense. **Not valid for medico-legal purposes.**

## 🧬 4.2c — STANDALONE: Hepatitis C (`Anti-HCV`) & Acute Enteric Hepatitis (`Anti-HAV IgM` & `Anti-HEV IgM`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — ANTI-HCV REFLEX RNA & ACUTE HAV/HEV PREGNANCY SHIELD]**

1. **Hepatitis C Antibody (`Anti-HCV` — Mandatory Reflex `HCV RNA` Protocol):**
   - **Window Period:** $\text{Anti-HCV}$ takes **$6\text{ to }12\text{ weeks}$** to seroconvert (and may remain negative in hemodialysis/HIV).
   - **Reactive `Anti-HCV` Legal Shield:** **A reactive `Anti-HCV` test NEVER proves active Hepatitis C infection!** It cannot distinguish **(a) Active Viremic HCV**, **(b) Spontaneously Cleared or Post-DAA Cured Past HCV** ($\text{Anti-HCV}$ remains positive for life after cure!), or **(c) Biological False-Positive reactivity** (common when Maglumi/Architect $\text{S/CO} < 3.0\text{–}5.0$ in pregnancy or autoimmune disease). **Mandatory Next Step:** Confirm all reactive $\text{Anti-HCV}$ results with **Quantitative `HCV RNA Real-Time PCR`** before initiating DAA therapy.
2. **Acute Hepatitis A (`Anti-HAV IgM`) & Hepatitis E (`Anti-HEV IgM`):**
   - **🚨 CRITICAL OBSTETRIC ALERT FOR `ANTI-HEV IgM`:** Acute **Hepatitis E ($\text{HEV}$ Genotype 1)** in the **2nd or 3rd Trimester of Pregnancy** carries a **$15\%\text{ to }25\%$ maternal mortality rate from Fulminant Hepatic Failure (Acute Liver Failure)**, DIC, postpartum hemorrhage, and intrauterine fetal death. Any reactive `Anti-HEV IgM` in a pregnant woman is an **Immediate Critical Alert** requiring urgent hepatology/obstetric ICU coordination and Coagulation (`PT/INR`), `Bilirubin`, `ALT/AST`, and `Glucose` monitoring.

## 🔬 4.2d — STANDALONE: Syphilis Serology — Traditional vs CDC Reverse Algorithm (`VDRL/RPR` & `Anti-TP CLIA / TPHA`)

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — TRADITIONAL VS REVERSE SYPHILIS ALGORITHM & PROZONE SHIELD]**

| `Anti-TP CLIA / TPHA` (Specific Treponemal) | `VDRL / RPR` (Non-Treponemal Titer) | 2nd Treponemal Tie-Breaker (`TPPA / TPHA`) | Integrated Clinical Interpretation (Traditional & CDC Reverse Algorithm) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Non-Reactive (-)** | **Non-Reactive (-)** | Not Required | **No Serological Evidence of Syphilis** *(or incubating early primary chancre $<2\text{–}4\text{ wks}$).* |
| **Non-Reactive (-)** | **Reactive (+)** *(Usually $\le 1:4\text{–}1:8$)* | **Non-Reactive (-)** | **Biological False-Positive (BFP) `VDRL/RPR`!** Common in **Pregnancy**, Malaria, Dengue, SLE/APS, TB, Leprosy. Syphilis excluded. |
| **Reactive (+)** | **Reactive (+)** *(Report Endpoint Titer)* | Not Required | **Confirmed Active, Untreated, or Recently Treated Syphilis.** Monitor 4-fold $\text{RPR/VDRL}$ titer decline at $6\text{ & }12\text{ months}$ post-Penicillin. |
| **Reactive (+)** *(Reverse Screen)* | **Non-Reactive (-)** *(Discordant)* | **Reactive (+)** | **Confirmed Past Treated Syphilis ("Serofast Treponemal Scar")**, Late Latent Syphilis with waned reagin, or Early Primary Syphilis. |
| **Reactive (+)** *(Low COI)* | **Non-Reactive (-)** *(Discordant)* | **Non-Reactive (-)** | **False-Positive Treponemal Screen CLIA.** Syphilis excluded. |

- **🛡️ Prozone Dilution Mandate:** All strongly suspected Secondary Syphilis or antenatal samples must be tested at **$1:8\text{ and }1:16$ dilution** to prevent false-negative undiluted $\text{VDRL/RPR}$ due to antibody excess (**Prozone Phenomenon**).

## 🤰 4.2e — STANDALONE: Maternal `TORCH` Panel & `IgG Avidity`, `EBV` 4-Marker Panel & `H. pylori`

### **[LIMS COPY-PASTE BLOCK — TORCH AVIDITY, EBV STAGING & H. PYLORI ACG SHIELD]**

1. **TORCH (`Toxoplasma, Rubella, CMV, HSV-1/2`) & Mandatory `IgG Avidity` Shield:**
   - **Never counsel pregnancy termination on an isolated reactive `Toxo IgM` or `CMV IgM`!** $\text{IgM}$ antibodies frequently persist for **$6\text{ to }18\text{ months}$** ("chronic residual $\text{IgM}$") or arise from polyclonal false-positive cross-reactivity.
   - **Mandatory Reflex `Toxo IgG Avidity` & `CMV IgG Avidity` (Tested in 1st Trimester $\le 12\text{–}16\text{ Weeks}$):**
     - **High $\text{IgG}$ Avidity Index ($\ge 60\%$ /kit cutoff):** Confirms **past maternal infection acquired $> 4\text{ months}$ prior to testing** (pre-conception immunity; near-zero risk of primary vertical fetal transmission!).
     - **Low $\text{IgG}$ Avidity Index ($< 30\%\text{–}40\%$):** Suggests **recent primary maternal infection within the past $3\text{–}4\text{ months}$** $\rightarrow$ Refer to Fetal Medicine for serial fetal neurosonography and **Amniotic Fluid Real-Time PCR at $\ge 18\text{ weeks}$ gestation** (and $\ge 6\text{ weeks}$ post-maternal exposure).
   - **Rubella $\text{IgG}$ Protective Cutoff:** **$\ge 10\text{ IU/mL}$** confirms protective maternal immunity against Congenital Rubella Syndrome (CRS).
   - **$\text{HSV-1 / HSV-2}$ Serology:** Use **Type-Specific Glycoprotein-G (`gG-1` and `gG-2`) $\text{IgG}$**; serum $\text{HSV IgM}$ is non-type-specific, turns positive during recurrent flares, and cannot stage primary genital herpes.

2. **Epstein-Barr Virus (`EBV`) 4-Marker Staging Matrix (`VCA-IgM, VCA-IgG, EA-IgG, EBNA-IgG`):**
   | `VCA-IgM` | `VCA-IgG` | `EA-IgG` (Early Ag) | `EBNA-IgG` (Nuclear Ag) | EBV Clinical Stage Interpretation |
   | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
   | **(-)** | **(-)** | **(-)** | **(-)** | **EBV Naive / Susceptible** (No prior EBV infection). |
   | **(+)** | **($\pm$ / +)** | **($\pm$ / +)** | **(-)** | **Acute Primary Infectious Mononucleosis** *(Note: `EBNA-IgG` is ALWAYS negative during acute primary EBV and only appears 6–12 weeks later!).* |
   | **(-)** | **(+)** | **(-)** | **(+)** | **Past Resolved / Latent EBV Immunity** ($>90\%$ of healthy adults). |
   | **($\pm$)** | **(+ High)** | **(+ High)** | **(+)** | **EBV Reactivation** or Chronic Active EBV (correlate with Quantitative `EBV DNA PCR`). |

3. ***Helicobacter pylori* Serology vs Stool Antigen & Urea Breath Test (`ACG Guidelines Shield`):**
   - **Serum `H. pylori IgG/IgA` Cannot Confirm Cure:** Serum $\text{H. pylori IgG}$ antibodies remain positive for **6 to 24+ months after successful eradication ("serological scar")**. Per **ACG / Maastricht VI Guidelines**, active infection diagnosis and **Post-Treatment Test-of-Cure (performed $\ge 4\text{ weeks}$ after stopping antibiotics and $\ge 2\text{ weeks}$ after stopping PPIs)** must use **Monoclonal Stool Antigen Assay** or **$^{13}\text{C} / ^{14}\text{C}$ Urea Breath Test (`UBT`)**. *Not valid for medico-legal purposes.*
