### 2.1 Cellular Immunopathogenesis of M. tuberculosis Infection
The Interferon-Gamma Release Assay (IGRA) is an *ex-vivo* functional cellular immunological assay that quantifies cell-mediated immune sensitization against *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*. When a human host inhales *M. tuberculosis* bacilli, alveolar macrophages and dendritic cells phagocytose the mycobacteria, processing mycobacterial proteins into peptide fragments presented on cell surfaces. Sensitized T-lymphocytes differentiate into memory effector T-cells. Upon *in-vitro* re-exposure of whole blood to synthetic *M. tuberculosis*-specific peptide antigens, circulating memory T-cells rapidly degranulate and release high concentrations of **Interferon-Gamma ($\text{IFN-}\gamma$)** into the plasma. This secreted cytokine is metrologically quantified against the WHO International Standard in $\text{IU/mL}$.

### 2.2 The Genomic RD1 Advantage: 100% BCG Vaccine Independence
The cardinal clinical breakthrough of IGRA over the legacy Tuberculin Skin Test (TST / Mantoux) resides in genomic deletion biology:
1. **The RD1 Locus:** *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* possesses a specific $9.5\text{-kb}$ genomic region known as the **Region of Difference 1 (RD1)**, which encodes two highly immunogenic virulent proteins:
   - **ESAT-6** (Early Secreted Antigenic Target, $6\text{ kDa}$)
   - **CFP-10** (Culture Filtrate Protein, $10\text{ kDa}$)
2. **Genetic Deletion in BCG:** During the historic serial subculturing (230 passages between 1908 and 1921 by Calmette and Guérin), the entire RD1 segment was **irrevocably deleted** from the genome of the parent *Mycobacterium bovis* strain to create the attenuated *M. bovis* BCG vaccine.
3. **Absence in Environmental NTM:** Furthermore, RD1 is absent from virtually all environmental Non-Tuberculous Mycobacteria (NTM) found in soil and municipal water, including *M. avium-intracellulare* complex (MAC), *M. fortuitum*, *M. abscessus*, and *M. chelonae*.
   - *Sole Exceptions:* Only three uncommon pathogenic non-tuberculous mycobacteria retain RD1 genes: *M. kansasii*, *M. marinum*, and *M. szulgai*.
4. **100% Specificity Against BCG:** Because the synthetic peptides in QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus are derived exclusively from ESAT-6 and CFP-10, **IGRA has 100% specificity against prior BCG vaccination**. Unlike the Mantoux skin test—which causes extensive false-positive indurations for decades in BCG-vaccinated populations like Haryana and across India—a positive IGRA is never caused by BCG!

### 2.3 The 4-Tube Technology in QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus
QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus (QFT-Plus) employs a specialized 4-tube blood collection and stimulation system:
- **Tube 1 — Nil Control (Gray Cap):** Contains no antigens; provides an inert heparinized environment. Measures the patient's baseline circulating background levels of $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ (due to heterophile antibodies, circulating immune complexes, or non-specific cytokine storms). Must be $\le 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$ for a valid test.
- **Tube 2 — TB1 Antigen (Green Cap):** Coated with long synthetic peptides derived from ESAT-6 and CFP-10. These peptides are processed via the endosomal pathway and presented on **MHC Class II** molecules, specifically stimulating **CD4+ Helper T-lymphocytes**.
- **Tube 3 — TB2 Antigen (Yellow Cap):** Coated with long peptides PLUS an innovative set of short synthetic peptides derived from ESAT-6 and CFP-10. These short peptides bypass endosomal processing and bind directly to **MHC Class I** clefts, stimulating **BOTH CD4+ Helper T-cells AND CD8+ Cytotoxic T-lymphocytes**.
  - *Clinical Significance of CD8+ Stimulation:* CD8+ cytotoxic T-cell responses correlate strongly with recent mycobacterial transmission, higher bacterial load, active progression from latent to active TB disease, and retain robust cellular responsiveness in HIV-positive patients suffering from severe CD4+ lymphopenia.
- **Tube 4 — Mitogen Positive Control (Purple Cap):** Coated with **Phytohemagglutinin (PHA)**, a potent non-specific T-cell plant lectin mitogen. Evaluates the intrinsic functional viability and immunological competence of the patient's T-lymphocytes. Mitogen minus Nil must be $\ge 0.50\text{ IU/mL}$ to confirm immune responsiveness when antigen tubes are negative.

### 2.4 The Strict 16-Hour Pre-Analytical Window & Bedside Handling
Live T-lymphocytes are exceptionally sensitive to cold shock and prolonged ambient stasis. The following pre-analytical rules are strictly enforced at Sawariya Diagnostic Lab:
1. **Tube Fill Volume:** Draw exactly $1.0\text{ mL}$ of whole blood into each of the 4 tubes. The meniscus must rest within the black volumetric indicator band ($0.8\text{ to }1.2\text{ mL}$). Underfilling concentrates the dried antigens (causing non-specific stimulation); overfilling dilutes antigens and exhausts heparin (causing microclots).
2. **Bedside Inversion Protocol:** Immediately upon phlebotomy, invert all 4 tubes firmly **10 times** (or shake gently for 5 seconds) to ensure complete dissolution of the dried antigen film from the tube walls. Do not vortex or invert violently, as erythrocyte hemolysis releases intracellular proteases that degrade released $\text{IFN-}\gamma$.
3. **Ambient Transport (17–27°C):** Store and transport tubes strictly at room temperature ($17^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }27^\circ\text{C}$). **NEVER REFRIGERATE (2–8°C) OR FREEZE WHOLE BLOOD PRIOR TO THE 37°C INCUBATION STEP!** Cold shock triggers rapid apoptotic cell death in T-lymphocytes, leading to catastrophic Mitogen failure and false Indeterminate results.
4. **16-Hour Incubation Deadline:** Blood tubes must be placed upright into the $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ incubator within a **maximum of 16 hours** from the exact venipuncture timestamp. Extended delays (> 16 hours) lead to spontaneous lymphocyte degradation.
5. **Incubation Duration:** Incubate upright at $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ for **$16\text{ to }24\text{ hours}$** in a humidified or dry microbiological incubator. Following incubation, tubes can be held at $2^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }8^\circ\text{C}$ for up to 28 days or centrifuged immediately.

1. **Positive Result:**
   - $\text{Nil} \le 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$, **AND**
   - Either $(\text{TB1} - \text{Nil}) \ge 0.35\text{ IU/mL}$ **AND** $(\text{TB1} - \text{Nil}) \ge 0.25 \times \text{Nil}$,
   - **OR** $(\text{TB2} - \text{Nil}) \ge 0.35\text{ IU/mL}$ **AND** $(\text{TB2} - \text{Nil}) \ge 0.25 \times \text{Nil}$.
   - (Mitogen control value is irrelevant when antigen response is robustly positive).
2. **Negative Result:**
   - $\text{Nil} \le 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$, **AND**
   - Both $(\text{TB1} - \text{Nil}) < 0.35\text{ IU/mL}$ (or $\ge 0.35$ but $< 25\%$ of Nil) **AND** $(\text{TB2} - \text{Nil}) < 0.35\text{ IU/mL}$ (or $\ge 0.35$ but $< 25\%$ of Nil), **AND**
   - $(\text{Mitogen} - \text{Nil}) \ge 0.50\text{ IU/mL}$.
3. **Indeterminate Result — Type A (Mitogen Failure / Immunological Anergy):**
   - $\text{Nil} \le 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$, **AND**
   - Antigen tubes negative ($< 0.35\text{ IU/mL}$), **AND**
   - $(\text{Mitogen} - \text{Nil}) < 0.50\text{ IU/mL}$.
   - *Causes:* Severe host lymphopenia ($\text{ALC} < 500/\mu\text{L}$), advanced HIV/AIDS ($\text{CD4} < 100/\mu\text{L}$), high-dose corticosteroids/chemotherapy, or pre-analytical chilling/incubation delay beyond 16 hours.
4. **Indeterminate Result — Type B (High Background Nil):**
   - $\text{Nil} > 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$ (irrespective of antigen or mitogen values).
   - *Causes:* Endogenous circulating heterophile antibodies, severe systemic sepsis/cytokine storm, or in-vitro specimen bacterial contamination.
5. **The Borderline / Gray Zone ($0.20\text{ to }0.70\text{ IU/mL}$):**
   - Per WHO 2024 and CDC guidelines, results where either TB1-Nil or TB2-Nil falls between $0.20\text{ and }0.70\text{ IU/mL}$ (and especially $0.35\text{ to }0.50\text{ IU/mL}$) represent a physiological "wobble zone" where analytical imprecision and biological fluctuation can lead to spontaneous conversions or reversions. In low-risk individuals (e.g., annual healthcare worker screening), borderline positive results must be verified by repeat testing on a fresh specimen before committing the patient to months of chemoprophylaxis.

### 2.6 The Cardinal Clinical Directive & NTEP/WHO Active TB Shield
> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY CLINICAL DIRECTIVE (WHO & NTEP INDIA):**
> 1. **IGRA CANNOT DISTINGUISH LATENT TB INFECTION (LTBI) FROM ACTIVE TUBERCULOSIS DISEASE!** In high-burden countries like India, approximately 30% to 40% of the healthy adult population harbor latent *M. tuberculosis* infection with a positive IGRA. Therefore, a positive IGRA confirms immunological sensitization but **does NOT prove active tuberculosis**.
> 2. **ACTIVE TB EXCLUSION MANDATE:** Prior to prescribing TB Preventive Therapy (TPT; e.g., 3HP, 3HR, or 6H) in any IGRA-positive patient—especially before commencing Anti-TNF-α biologics or solid organ transplantation—the clinician **MUST rule out active pulmonary and extrapulmonary TB** via symptom evaluation, Chest Radiography (CXR) or HRCT, and microbiological testing (**Sputum CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra [SOP 6.1.8] & MGIT AFB Culture [SOP 6.1.7]**). Initiating single-agent TPT in a patient with unsuspected active TB breeds catastrophic acquired drug resistance!
> 3. **NEVER USE IGRA FOR TREATMENT MONITORING OR CURE:** Memory T-cells persist in lymphoid organs for years to decades following complete mycobacterial eradication. **IGRA remains positive indefinitely** after successful cure. Repeating IGRA to monitor response to Anti-Tubercular Therapy (ATT) or confirm cure is medically irrational, scientifically invalid, and legally indefensible.

## ⚙️ Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, Flash-CLIA / ELISA Chemistry & Instrumentation

### 3.1 Reagents, Calibrators & Analytical Reaction Chemistry
1. **Flash Chemiluminescence Technology (Snibe MAGLUMI X3):**
   - **Solid Phase (Capture):** Nano-magnetic microbeads coated with high-affinity murine monoclonal anti-human $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ antibodies.
   - **Tracer (Detection):** Amino-butyl-ethyl-isoluminol (ABEI) chemical label conjugated to a secondary monoclonal anti-human $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ antibody directed against a non-overlapping epitope (sandwich immunoassay).
   - **Signal Generation:** After automated magnetic separation and washing to eliminate unbound plasma proteins, Starter Reagent 1 (oxidizing agent) and Starter Reagent 2 (catalyst) are injected into the measuring cuvette. The resulting rapid flash chemiluminescent reaction emits light at $\lambda = 425\text{ nm}$, measured by a photomultiplier tube (PMT) in Relative Light Units (RLU). RLU is directly proportional to $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ concentration.
2. **Alternative Microplate ELISA Chemistry:**
   - 96-well microplate coated with murine anti-human $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ monoclonal antibody. Recombinant human $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ calibrators ($0.00, 0.25, 1.00, 4.00, 10.00\text{ IU/mL}$) generate a 4-parameter logistic (4-PL) calibration curve. Horseradish Peroxidase (HRP) conjugate and Tetramethylbenzidine (TMB) substrate produce a chromogenic blue reaction stopped with $0.5\text{ M } \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$, read at $450\text{ nm}$ (reference filter $620\text{–}650\text{ nm}$).

### 3.2 Universal Step-by-Step Operating Procedure
- **Stage 1 (Pre-Incubation & Whole-Blood Stimulation):**
  1. Inspect collection tubes: confirm blood volume is within the $0.8\text{–}1.2\text{ mL}$ black mark and all 4 tubes are present.
  2. Invert tubes $10\times$ immediately after draw. Confirm accession barcode matches LIMS request.
  3. Transfer tubes into a calibrated, validated incubator maintained strictly at $37.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$. Record incubator insertion timestamp.
  4. Incubate upright for $16\text{ to }24\text{ hours}$ continuously.
- **Stage 2 (Centrifugation & Gel Supernatant Harvesting):**
  1. Remove tubes from the incubator. Centrifuge all 4 tubes at $2,000\text{ to }3,000 \times g$ (recommended $2,500 \times g$) for $15\text{ minutes}$ in a swing-bucket centrifuge.
  2. Following centrifugation, the inert thixotropic polyester gel forms a complete barrier separating the sedimented erythrocytes/leukocytes from the supernatant plasma.
  3. Supernatant plasma can be sampled directly from the centrifuged collection tubes on automated analyzers or harvested into secondary cryovials (stable at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ for 28 days or $-20^\circ\text{C}$ for 1 year). Avoid disturbing the gel layer.
- **Stage 3 (Automated Analytical Run & Measurement):**
  1. Perform analyzer daily start-up: check wash buffers, system liquid, light check, and waste levels.
  2. Load primary tubes or harvested plasma aliquots into analyzer sample racks in strict order: `Nil (Gray)`, `TB1 (Green)`, `TB2 (Yellow)`, `Mitogen (Purple)`.
  3. Analyzer automatically dispenses $20\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of plasma + $20\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of magnetic microbeads + $50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of ABEI conjugate.
  4. Incubate at $37.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $15\text{ minutes}$, wash magnetic beads $3\times$, inject Starters 1 & 2, and measure PMT signal for 3 seconds.
- **Stage 4 (Calculation & LIMS Autoverification):**
  1. Read raw $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ concentrations ($\text{IU/mL}$) for each tube: $[\text{Nil}]$, $[\text{TB1}]$, $[\text{TB2}]$, $[\text{Mitogen}]$.
  2. Compute: $\Delta\text{TB1} = [\text{TB1}] - [\text{Nil}]$, $\Delta\text{TB2} = [\text{TB2}] - [\text{Nil}]$, and $\Delta\text{Mitogen} = [\text{Mitogen}] - [\text{Nil}]$.
  3. Evaluate against diagnostic criteria and transmit result with qualitative and quantitative parameters into LIMS.

### 3.3 Calibration Verification & Traceability Chain
- Multi-point calibration is executed using manufacturer-supplied calibrators traceable to the WHO 1st International Standard (NIBSC 87/586).
- Recalibration is mandatory upon: (a) reagent lot change, (b) major optical or pipetting maintenance, (c) failure of internal quality control rules, or (d) expiration of the 28-day calibration validity window.
- Curve fit acceptance criterion: $R^2 \ge 0.9990$ with back-calculated calibrator recovery within $\pm 10\%$ of nominal target.

### 3.4 Internal Quality Control & Daily Sigma Gate
- **Internal Tube Controls:** Every patient run contains its own built-in biological controls:
  - Background baseline control: $\text{Nil} \le 8.0\text{ IU/mL}$.
  - Host immunocompetence / viability control: $\text{Mitogen} - \text{Nil} \ge 0.50\text{ IU/mL}$.
- **Third-Party Liquid Controls:** Run two levels of external liquid quality control (Low: $0.30\text{–}0.60\text{ IU/mL}$; High: $2.00\text{–}5.00\text{ IU/mL}$) at the beginning of each testing batch.
- **Westgard Rules Applied:**
  - $1_{3s}$ Violation: Any control exceeds $\pm 3\text{ SD}$; run rejected immediately.
  - $2_{2s}$ Violation: Two consecutive controls exceed $+2\text{ SD}$ or $-2\text{ SD}$; systematic error; run rejected.
  - $R_{4s}$ Violation: Difference between two controls within run exceeds $4\text{ SD}$; random error; reject run.
  - $10_x$ Warning: 10 consecutive controls on one side of mean; systematic shift; recalibrate before next run.
- **Proficiency Testing / EQAS:** Mandatory participation in international EQAS programs (CAP Mycobacterial IGRA QF Survey, UK NEQAS, or CMC Vellore EQAS) with 100% concordance requirement.

### 3.5 Step-by-Step Microplate ELISA Bench Protocol (Small-Batch / Fallback Protocol)
1. **Reagent Equilibrations:** Equilibrate all ELISA kit components (antibody-coated plate, conjugate, substrate, stop solution, wash buffer) to room temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for $\ge 30\text{ minutes}$ prior to testing.
2. **Standard Curve Preparation:** Reconstitute lyophilized WHO-traceable human $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ standards with distilled water to yield calibrator points: $0.00, 0.25, 1.00, 4.00, 10.00\text{ IU/mL}$.
3. **Well Inoculation:** Dispense $50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of 1× Green Enzyme Conjugate working solution into each designated microplate well. Add $50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of harvested plasma (or standards/controls) into assigned wells. Mix gently for $1\text{ minute}$ on a microplate shaker at $500\text{ rpm}$.
4. **First Incubation:** Cover microplate with adhesive seal and incubate at room temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for **$120 \pm 5\text{ minutes}$** shielded from direct light.
5. **Automated Washing:** Wash wells $6\times$ using automated microplate washer (Bio-Rad PW40) with $400\text{ }\mu\text{L/well}$ of 1× Wash Buffer. Ensure complete aspiration with residual volume $< 2\text{ }\mu\text{L/well}$. Invert and firmly tap against absorbent lint-free paper.
6. **Substrate Reaction:** Pipette $100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of TMB Substrate into each well. Incubate in dark at room temperature for **$30 \pm 2\text{ minutes}$**.
7. **Reaction Termination & Reading:** Dispense $50\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ of $0.5\text{ M } \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ Stop Solution into each well (color converts from blue to yellow). Read dual absorbance at $450\text{ nm}$ against $620\text{–}650\text{ nm}$ reference filter within $5\text{ minutes}$.
8. **Analysis Validation:** Mean Optical Density ($\text{OD}$) of Calibrator 4 ($4.0\text{ IU/mL}$) must be $\ge 0.60$; $\text{OD}$ of Calibrator 0 ($0.0\text{ IU/mL}$) must be $\le 0.15$; correlation coefficient $r \ge 0.98$ for 4-PL curve.

### 5.2 Special Population Stratification
1. **Pediatric Patients (< 5 Years):** Immature T-cell responsiveness can lower total $\text{IFN-}\gamma$ yield, but the TB2 tube provides enhanced sensitivity via CD8+ short peptides. QFT-Plus is approved for children $\ge 2\text{ years}$ by WHO/CDC. In children $< 2\text{ years}$, concurrent TST and IGRA can be considered.
2. **Elderly Patients (> 65 Years):** Age-related immunosenescence reduces lymphocyte proliferation and increases Indeterminate Type A rates (up to 8–12%). Always evaluate Absolute Lymphocyte Count (ALC) on CBC before interpreting low-mitogen runs.
3. **Pregnancy (1st, 2nd, 3rd Trimester & Postpartum):** Safe and highly specific throughout pregnancy. Physiological Th2 shift during normal pregnancy slightly blunts cell-mediated immunity but does not alter the $0.35\text{ IU/mL}$ diagnostic cutoff. Screening is critical in high-risk pregnant women to prevent congenital TB transmission.
4. **Immunocompromised Patients (HIV, Solid Organ Transplant, Dialysis):** Risk of Indeterminate Type A increases linearly as CD4 count drops below $200/\mu\text{L}$. If QFT-Plus yields Indeterminate, reflex immediately to the **T-SPOT.TB assay** (which washes and normalizes cell count to $250,000\text{ PBMCs/well}$, rescuing sensitivity in lymphopenic hosts).

### 5.4 NTEP India Guidelines for TB Preventive Therapy (TPT) Regimens
Following active TB rule-out, eligible IGRA-positive individuals receive TPT under NTEP guidelines:
1. **3HP Regimen (Preferred for Adults & Children $\ge 2$ Years):** Weekly weight-banded **Rifapentine + Isoniazid** for 12 weeks (12 doses total). High adherence, lower hepatotoxicity profile.
2. **3HR Regimen (Alternative for Pediatrics & Household Contacts):** Daily **Rifampicin + Isoniazid** for 12 weeks (84 doses total). Recommended in pediatric household contacts.
3. **6H Regimen (Traditional Alternative):** Daily **Isoniazid** ($300\text{ mg/day}$ in adults, $10\text{ mg/kg/day}$ in children) + Pyridoxine ($25\text{–}50\text{ mg/day}$) for 6 months (168 doses).
4. **Baseline Liver Function Monitoring:** Check baseline Bilirubin, AST, and ALT (SOP 2.1.1). Repeat LFT if symptoms of drug-induced hepatotoxicity (anorexia, nausea, jaundice) develop. Withhold TPT if transaminases exceed $3\times$ upper limit of normal with symptoms or $5\times$ without symptoms.

## 🔬 Section 7: Longitudinal Kinetics, Differential Diagnosis & 6 Worked Clinical Scenarios

### 7.1 Longitudinal Kinetics & Immunological Memory
Following primary *M. tuberculosis* infection, sensitized T-lymphocytes undergo clonal expansion over **2 to 8 weeks** (the immunological window period). Once formed, central memory T-cells reside in bone marrow and lymph nodes indefinitely. Following complete microbiological cure with 6 months of ATT or 3 months of TPT, effector memory T-cells decline, but central memory cells persist. Consequently, **60% to 90% of cured patients remain IGRA-positive for life**.

### 10.2 Plain-Language Patient Summary (English)
> **What does your QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus (IGRA) test mean?**  
> This modern blood test checks whether your immune system (specifically T-lymphocytes) recognizes proteins from the tuberculosis (*M. tuberculosis*) bacteria.  
> - **BCG Vaccine Does Not Affect This Test:** Unlike the traditional Mantoux skin test, IGRA is **100% unaffected by childhood BCG vaccination**. A positive result is never a false-positive from your BCG shot!  
> - **If Your Result is Positive:** It means your body has encountered TB bacteria. However, this test **cannot tell whether the infection is 'Latent' (sleeping quietly without causing sickness) or 'Active' (causing symptoms and contagious)**. Your doctor will examine you, order a Chest X-ray, and test your sputum (GeneXpert) to make sure you do not have active disease before discussing preventive therapy.  
> - **Never Repeat This Test to Check if TB is Cured:** Your immune cells remember TB bacteria for many years. Even after you complete your full treatment and are completely cured, this test will stay positive. Do not repeat this test to check if medications worked!

### 10.3 Patient & Family Counseling Advisory in Hindi (हिंदी परामर्श)
> **क्वांटिफेरॉन-टीबी गोल्ड प्लस (IGRA) रक्त परीक्षण की जानकारी एवं परामर्श:**  
> 1. **यह टेस्ट क्या जांचता है?**  
>    यह आधुनिक रक्त परीक्षण यह पता लगाता है कि क्या आपके रक्त की श्वेत रक्त कोशिकाएं (T-कोशिकाएं) टीबी के बैक्टीरिया को पहचानती हैं या नहीं।  
> 2. **बचपन में लगे बीसीजी (BCG) के टीके का कोई असर नहीं:**  
>    पुराने मोंटू (Mantoux) चमड़ी वाले टेस्ट के विपरीत, इस रक्त टेस्ट पर बचपन में लगे बीसीजी टीके का **कोई गलत असर (फॉल्स-पॉजिटिव) नहीं पड़ता**।  
> 3. **पॉजिटिव (Positive) परिणाम का क्या अर्थ है?**  
>    पॉजिटिव परिणाम का मतलब है कि आपके शरीर में टीबी के बैक्टीरिया के प्रति प्रतिरक्षा स्मृति मौजूद है। **ध्यान रहे:** यह टेस्ट यह नहीं बता सकता कि टीबी "शांत/सुप्त" (Latent TB - जो बीमारी नहीं फैलाती) है या "सक्रिय" (Active TB - बीमारी के लक्षण)। इसलिए डॉक्टर टीबी की रोकथाम या इलाज शुरू करने से पहले छाती का एक्स-रे और बलगम की जांच (GeneXpert) करवाएंगे।  
> 4. **अति-महत्वपूर्ण चेतावनी (इलाज के बाद दोबारा टेस्ट न कराएं):**  
>    टीबी की दवाइयां पूरी होने और बीमारी पूरी तरह ठीक हो जाने के बाद भी यह टेस्ट जिंदगी भर पॉजिटिव रह सकता है क्योंकि शरीर की कोशिकाएं बैक्टीरिया को याद रखती हैं। **टीबी ठीक हुई या नहीं, यह जानने के लिए यह टेस्ट कभी दोबारा न कराएं!**
