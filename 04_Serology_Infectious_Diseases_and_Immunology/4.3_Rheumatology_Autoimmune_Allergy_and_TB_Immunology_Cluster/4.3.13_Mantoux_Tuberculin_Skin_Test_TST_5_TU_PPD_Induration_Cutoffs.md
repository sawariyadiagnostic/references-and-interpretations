## ⚙️ Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP, `CLSI EP15/EP06` Verification, Calibration, Six Sigma IQC & Comparability

1. **International Method Principle:**
   - **Assay Methodology:** In-Vivo Intradermal Mantoux Injection (0.1 mL of 5 TU PPD-S or Bioequivalent 2 TU PPD RT-23 with Tween-80) & Standardized Sokal Ballpoint-Pen Transverse Induration Measurement at 48–72 Hours (Purified Protein Derivative (PPD) Intradermal Test, 5 TU PPD-S / 2 TU PPD RT-23 with Tween-80, Type IV Cell-Mediated Hypersensitivity TB Screen, Latent Tuberculosis Infection (LTBI) Screen)
   - **Chemical / Immunological / Analytical Reaction Principle:**
     Primary Analytical Principle: Intradermal injection of 0.1 mL PPD into the volar surface of the left forearm (producing a 6–10 mm pale tense wheal) followed by transverse diameter measurement of palpable induration at 48–72 hours. Calibration: Standardized 2 TU PPD RT-23 (with Tween-80, bioequivalent to 5 TU PPD-S) + Sokal Ballpoint-Pen Transverse Measurement Protocol (Every patient (Verify 6–10 mm wheal at injection and read transversely across forearm long axis at 48–72h)). Reference/Confirmatory Standard: Interferon-Gamma Release Assay (IGRA — SOP 4.3.14) for LTBI; Sputum/Tissue AFB Culture (MGIT 960) & CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra (SOP 6.1.8) for Active TB.
2. **Universal Step-by-Step Operational Protocol (Hardware-Agnostic):**
   - **Step 1 (Pre-Procedure Cold-Chain & Vial Verification):** Verify $5\text{ TU}/0.1\text{ mL}$ PPD-S (`RT-23` with Tween-80) vial expiration, cold-chain storage ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$, protected from light), and open-vial date ($\le 30\text{ days}$); screen patient for prior severe bullous/necrotic `TST` reaction or documented active `TB`.
   - **Step 2 (Calibrated Intradermal Mantoux Injection):** Draw strictly **$0.1\text{ mL}$ ($5\text{ TU}$)** into a $1.0\text{ mL}$ graduated tuberculin syringe with a $27\text{-gauge}$ short-bevel needle; cleanse volar forearm $5\text{–}10\text{ cm}$ below elbow with $70\%$ alcohol, allow to dry, stretch skin taut, insert needle **bevel UP at a $5^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$ intradermal angle**, and inject slowly to raise a discrete **$6\text{–}10\text{ mm}$ pale tense wheal (`peau d'orange`)** (if subcutaneous with no wheal, repeat immediately $\ge 5\text{ cm}$ away!).
   - **Step 3 (Internal Quality Control & Six Sigma Gate):** Internal QC Material: Standardized PPD-S $5\text{ TU}$ potency lot certificate (`NIBSC` / `BCG Lab Guindy` / `Span`) + **Dual-Observer Blinded Induration Reading Competency (`CV < 1–2 mm`)** | Frequency: Every vial lot & quarterly staff competency | Westgard Rules: Wheal diameter $6\text{–}10\text{ mm}$ at $0\text{ min}$; reading strictly within $48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$.
   - **Step 4 ($48\text{–}72\text{ Hour}$ Sokal Ballpoint-Pen Transverse Induration Measurement):** At **$48\text{–}72\text{ hours}$**, palpate and demarcate the lateral margins of **firm palpable INDURATION (NEVER measure flat surrounding erythema!)** using the **Sokal ballpoint-pen method** across the **transverse axis** (perpendicular to the long axis of the forearm) and record in exact two-digit millimeters (e.g., `00 mm`, `08 mm`, `14 mm`).
3. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`), `LoD`/`LoQ` & Clinical Reportable Range (`CRR`):**
   - Analytical Measuring Range (AMR): 00 mm to 40+ mm (with mandatory documentation of vesiculation, bullae, ulceration, or lymphangitis)
4. **Method Verification (`CLSI EP15-A3` / `EP06-Ed2` / `EP28-A3c`):**
   - Prior to clinical go-live or after any future analyzer upgrade, verify precision ($5\text{ days} \times 5\text{ replicates}$ across 2 levels), trueness against EQAS/reference material, linearity across the `AMR` (`CLSI EP06`), and local reference interval transference on $n=20$ healthy reference individuals from Haryana (`CLSI EP28-A3c`; $\le 2/20$ outside limits passes transference).

### 5.2 Physiological, Obstetric, Acute Trauma/ICU & Environmental Modifiers
- **Cutaneous Anergy (False-Negative `00 mm` Induration in Severe/Disseminated Disease):** Up to **$25\%\text{–}50\%$ of patients with overwhelming active Miliary Tuberculosis, Tuberculous Meningitis, Advanced HIV (`CD4 < 200 cells/uL`), Severe Protein-Energy Malnutrition (`SAM`), Sarcoidosis, or recent live-attenuated viral infection/vaccination (`Measles`, `MMR`, `Varicella` within $4\text{–}6\text{ weeks}$)** exhibit complete cutaneous T-cell anergy (`00 mm` Mantoux)!
- **Prior `BCG` Vaccination & Non-Tuberculous Mycobacteria (`NTM`) Cross-Reactivity:** Because `PPD` is a crude culture filtrate sharing $> 200$ antigens with *M. bovis* `BCG` and environmental `NTM`, childhood `BCG` vaccination can cause false-positive induration ($5\text{–}14\text{ mm}$); prefer **`IGRA` (`QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus` `4.3.14`)** in `BCG`-vaccinated individuals.
- **The 'Booster Phenomenon' in Serial Healthcare Worker Screening:** In individuals with waned remote immunity, a first `TST` can stimulate memory T-cells so a repeat test $1\text{–}4\text{ weeks}$ later reads positive (Two-Step baseline testing prevents misdiagnosing a boosted reaction as a new occupational conversion).

## 🔬 Section 7: In-Vivo Pharmacokinetics ($t_{1/2}$), Pathophysiological Severity Grading, Differential Diagnosis & Smart LIMS Reflex Cascades

### 7.1 In-Vivo Kinetics & Turnover Dynamics
After primary *M. tuberculosis* exposure, T-cell sensitization takes **2 to 8 weeks (Window Period)** to convert the Mantoux test from negative to positive.

### 7.2 Pathophysiological Differential Diagnosis
- **Causes of Elevated / Positive / Abnormal-High Values:**
  05 – 09 mm Transverse Induration (Risk-Stratified Borderline / Positive in High-Risk Hosts): 5 – 9 mm Induration:
  • **POSITIVE** if the patient is **HIV-positive**, a **recent close household contact of an active pulmonary TB case**, an **organ transplant recipient**, on **immunosuppressive therapy (Prednisone ≥ 15 mg/day or Anti-TNF-α biologics)**, a **severely malnourished child**, or has **fibrotic Chest X-Ray lesions**. Exclude active TB via Chest X-Ray and CBNAAT GeneXpert before initiating TB Preventive Therapy (TPT).
  • **NEGATIVE / EQUIVOCAL** in immunocompetent adults without close TB contact (frequently reflects prior neonatal BCG vaccination or environmental non-tuberculous mycobacteria [NTM]; confirm with ESAT-6/CFP-10 Blood IGRA [4.3.14] if clarification is needed). | 10 – 14 mm Transverse Induration (Positive per NTEP India Standard Cutoff): POSITIVE TUBERCULIN SKIN TEST (≥ 10 mm — NTEP India Standard Cutoff for 2 TU PPD RT-23 / 5 TU PPD-S). Indicates delayed-type IV T-cell hypersensitivity to mycobacterial antigens.
  • **MANDATORY INTERPRETATION SHIELD**: A positive Mantoux test **CANNOT distinguish Latent Tuberculosis Infection (LTBI — present in ~30–40% of healthy adults in India) from Active Tuberculosis Disease**.
  • **Next Steps**: (1) If specificity confirmation is needed to rule out BCG/NTM cross-reactivity, perform **Blood IGRA (QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus 4.3.14)**; (2) Perform clinical symptom screening, Chest X-Ray, and microbiological testing (**CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra 6.1.8 & AFB Smear/Culture 6.1.7**) to rule out active TB before considering Latent TB Preventive Therapy (TPT). | ≥ 15 mm Transverse Induration (OR Any Reaction with Vesiculation / Bullae / Ulceration): STRONGLY POSITIVE TUBERCULIN SKIN TEST (≥ 15 mm or Bullous/Vesicular Reaction). Unlikely to be due to remote childhood BCG vaccination alone; strongly indicative of *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* sensitization (Latent TB Infection or Active TB). Perform thorough clinical, radiological (Chest X-Ray/CT), and microbiological (**CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra 6.1.8**) evaluation for active pulmonary or extrapulmonary tuberculosis. Do NOT repeat Mantoux testing in this patient in the future.
- **Causes of Decreased / Deficient / Abnormal-Low Values:**
  00 – 04 mm Transverse Induration (Non-Reactive): Negative Tuberculin Skin Test (Mantoux). In an immunocompetent individual outside the 2–8 week post-exposure window period, indicates absence of cell-mediated hypersensitivity to tuberculin PPD. **CRITICAL CLINICAL CAVEAT (ANERGY SHIELD)**: A negative Mantoux (00 mm) NEVER excludes active tuberculosis! Up to 25–50% of patients with severe active TB (Miliary TB, Tuberculous Meningitis, extensive cavitary TB), HIV co-infection, severe malnutrition, sarcoidosis, recent measles/live viral vaccination (< 4–6 weeks), or corticosteroid/biologic therapy exhibit cutaneous T-cell anergy. If active TB is suspected, perform CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra (6.1.8) and AFB Culture.

### 7.3 Smart LIMS Algorithmic Reflex & Diagnostic Cascade Rules

  - IF **Mantoux TST is Positive (≥ 5 mm in Immunocompromised/Contacts or ≥ 10 mm in Standard Indian Patients)** -> MANDATORY CLINICAL & RADIOLOGICAL RULE-OUT OF ACTIVE TB (**Chest X-Ray + Symptom Screen [cough > 2 weeks, fever, night sweats, weight loss] + Sputum/Gastric Aspirate CBNAAT GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra [6.1.8] & AFB Smear/Culture [6.1.7]**) BEFORE initiating **TB Preventive Therapy (TPT: 3HP [12 weekly doses of Isoniazid + Rifapentine], 3HR, or 6H)** (giving single/dual-drug TPT to a patient with undiagnosed active cavitary TB rapidly breeds multidrug-resistant TB!).
  - IF **False-Positive Mantoux from Prior BCG Vaccination or Non-Tuberculous Mycobacteria (NTM)** is suspected -> Reflex to **Blood IGRA (QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus — SOP 4.3.14)** (IGRA utilizes *ESAT-6* and *CFP-10* antigens encoded by the RD1 genomic region that is completely DELETED in all *Mycobacterium bovis* BCG vaccine strains!).
