### 5.2 Physiological, Obstetric, Acute Trauma/ICU & Environmental Modifiers
- **Ethnic & Geographic Baseline Prevalence in India:** `HLA-B27` is present in **$6\%\text{–}8\%$ of the healthy North Indian / Haryana population** (and $90\%\text{–}95\%$ of Ankylosing Spondylitis patients). Because only $5\%\text{–}10\%$ of `HLA-B27+` individuals ever develop Spondyloarthropathy, a positive result is **never diagnostic in isolation** without `ASAS` clinical/MRI sacroiliitis features.
- **Allelic Subtype Modifier (`HLA-B*27:05 / *27:04` vs Protective `*27:06 / *27:09`):** Subtypes **`B*27:05` and `B*27:04`** (predominant in India) are strongly disease-predisposing, whereas **`B*27:06`** (Southeast Asia) and **`B*27:09`** (Sardinia) are non-pathogenic.
- **Flow Cytometry `HLA-B7` Cross-Reactivity:** Monoclonal anti-HLA-B27 clones (`FD705`, `ABC-m3`, `GS145.2`) cross-react with **`HLA-B7`, `B22`, `B40`, and `B73`**, creating a 'gray-zone' mean fluorescence intensity (`MFI`) shift that **MUST be confirmed by Allele-Specific Real-Time PCR**.

### 7.2 Pathophysiological Differential Diagnosis
- **Causes of Elevated / Positive / Abnormal-High Values:**
  HLA-B27: POSITIVE (Detected — Confirmed by Allele-Specific Real-Time PCR): HLA-B27 ALLELE DETECTED. Confirms genetic susceptibility within the Spondyloarthritis (SpA) spectrum and fulfills the genetic entry criterion of the **ASAS (Assessment of SpondyloArthritis international Society) Classification Criteria** for Axial Spondyloarthritis (when accompanied by ≥ 2 clinical SpA features in patients < 45 years old with ≥ 3 months of back pain).
  • **Crucial Clinical Shield**: **~6% of the healthy North Indian population is HLA-B27 positive**, and **90–95% of healthy HLA-B27 carriers NEVER develop Ankylosing Spondylitis**. Therefore, a positive HLA-B27 must NEVER be used in isolation to diagnose Ankylosing Spondylitis without corroborating clinical features (inflammatory back pain, enthesitis, dactylitis, acute anterior uveitis, elevated CRP [4.3.1], or sacroiliitis on MRI/X-ray).
  • **Lifetime Rule**: Because HLA-B27 is a germline genetic trait, this test should **NEVER be repeated**.
- **Causes of Decreased / Deficient / Abnormal-Low Values:**
  HLA-B27: NEGATIVE (Not Detected by Real-Time PCR / Flow Cytometry): HLA-B27 Allele NOT Detected. Significantly lowers the post-test probability of classic Ankylosing Spondylitis (90–95% of AS patients are HLA-B27 positive). CAVEAT: A negative HLA-B27 does NOT completely exclude Spondyloarthritis—~5–10% of Ankylosing Spondylitis, ~15–25% of Non-Radiographic Axial Spondyloarthritis (nr-axSpA), and ~40–50% of Psoriatic Spondylitis and IBD-associated Enteropathic Spondylitis are HLA-B27 negative; correlate with Sacroiliac Joint MRI (STIR sequences) if clinical inflammatory back pain is present.

### 7.3 Smart LIMS Algorithmic Reflex & Diagnostic Cascade Rules

  - IF **Flow Cytometry MFI falls in the Indeterminate Gray Zone** -> MANDATORY AUTO-REFLEX to **Allele-Specific Real-Time PCR** on the same EDTA sample (the HLA-B7 CREG [Cross-Reactive Epitope Group: HLA-B7, B22, B27, B40, B42] causes partial monoclonal antibody binding on flow cytometry, whereas Real-Time PCR is 100% definitive!).
  - IF **HLA-B27 is Positive** in a patient < 45 years old with chronic inflammatory back pain (≥ 3 months) -> Evaluate per the **ASAS Clinical Arm Criteria** (HLA-B27 [+] PLUS ≥ 2 additional SpA features: inflammatory back pain, arthritis, enthesitis, uveitis, dactylitis, psoriasis, Crohn's/UC, good response to NSAIDs, family history of SpA, or **Elevated CRP [4.3.1]**) and recommend **Sacroiliac Joint MRI (STIR / Fat-Saturated T2 sequences for subchondral bone marrow edema / osteitis)**.

• Recommended Reflex Cascade:    - IF Flow Cytometry MFI falls in the Indeterminate Gray Zone -> MANDATORY AUTO-REFLEX to Allele-Specific Real-Time PCR on the same EDTA sample (the HLA-B7 CREG [Cross-Reactive Epitope Group: HLA-B7, B22, B27, B40, B42] causes partial monoclonal antibody binding on flow cytometry, whereas Real-Time PCR is 100% definitive!).   - IF HLA-B27 is Positive in a patient < 45 years old with chronic inflammatory back pain (≥ 3 months) -> Evaluate per the ASAS Clinical Arm Criteria (HLA-B27 [+] PLUS ≥ 2 additional SpA features: inflammatory back pain, arthritis, enthesitis, uveitis, dactylitis, psoriasis, Crohn's/UC, good response to NSAIDs, family history of SpA, or Elevated CRP [4.3.1]) and recommend Sacroiliac Joint MRI (STIR / Fat-Saturated T2 sequences for subchondral bone marrow edema / osteitis).
