## Section 3: Pre-Analytical Phase Control & Sample Integrity SOP

Pre-analytical errors account for $> 65\%$ of clinical serology discrepancies. Sawariya Diagnostic Lab enforces strict specimen collection, transport, centrifugation, and automated Hemolysis, Icterus, Lipemia (HIL) screening cutoffs.

### 1. Phlebotomy Tube Verification & Clot Retraction

```
[Venipuncture: Vacutainer Needle 21G/22G] ──► [Fill BD Vacutainer SST II Advance Gold-Top Gel Tube (4.0 mL)]
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
                                  [Gentle Inversion 5 to 6 Times (DO NOT SHAKE)]
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
                                  [Upright Clotting at Ambient Temp (20–25°C) for 30–60 Minutes]
                                  *(Strict Alert: Centrifugation < 30 min causes latent fibrin clots)*
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
                                  [Centrifugation in Swing-Out Rotor: 1,800 x g for 10 Minutes at 20–25°C]
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
                                  [Visual & Automated HIL Index Check -> Primary Tube Loading on Maglumi X3]
```

### 2. Automated & Visual HIL Rejection Thresholds

| Interfering Substance | Bench Rejection Cutoff | Analytical Mechanism of Interference | Impacted Serology Assays | Bench Action upon Threshold Breach |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hemolysis ($H$)** | $\text{Free Hb} \ge 500\text{ mg/dL}$ ($H \ge 3+$) | Pseudo-peroxidase activity; optical absorbance overlap at $450/630\text{ nm}$; quenching of acridinium ester chemiluminescent flashes | False reactive HIV/HBsAg; false depression of low-positive IgM | Reject specimen; log `REJ-HEM-01`; request fresh red-top or SST redraw |
| **Icterus ($I$)** | $\text{Total Bilirubin} \ge 20\text{ mg/dL}$ | Strong chromatic quenching of emitted blue-green photons ($420\text{–}460\text{ nm}$); competitive binding | Suppresses low-titre Anti-HCV and Dengue IgM/IgG signals | Log `REJ-ICT-02`; dilute sample $1:2$ if assay validated or note limitation |
| **Lipemia ($L$)** | $\text{Triglycerides} \ge 1,000\text{ mg/dL}$ | High-angle light scattering in turbidimetry; physical steric hindrance of antibody-antigen binding | Pseudo-elevation of CRP, RF, ASO; false non-reactive CLIA | Clear by high-speed centrifugation ($10,000 \times g \times 10\text{ min}$) or reject |
| **Fibrin Micro-Clots** | Visible threads / gel flakes | Aspiration probe occlusion; volume short-sampling; non-specific trapping of enzyme conjugates | Random erratic spikes in ELISA/CLIA; analyzer pipettor stall | Re-centrifuge serum at $3,000 \times g \times 10\text{ min}$; re-aspirate supernatant |

## Section 4: Internal Quality Control (IQC) Protocol (`NABL 112` & `CLSI C24-Ed4`)

Sawariya Diagnostic Lab enforces a rigorous multi-rule Westgard internal quality control protocol for both quantitative and qualitative serological assays in strict compliance with `NABL 112` and `CLSI C24-Ed4`.

### 1. Control Materials & Daily Run Sequence

1. **Commercial Multi-Analyte Controls:** Bio-Rad Lyphochek Immunoassay Plus Control (Levels 1, 2, and 3) run every 24 hours at 08:00 AM before commencing patient sample batches on the Snibe Maglumi X3 and ChemWell Analyzer.
2. **In-House Weak-Positive Serology Controls ("Grey-Zone Verifiers"):** Formulated by diluting characterized high-titer patient sera with negative human serum matrix to achieve a target Cutoff Index ($\text{COI}$) of $1.50\text{–}2.50$ for HIV, HBsAg, HCV, and Dengue NS1. These controls detect subtle analytical decay and optical drift long before standard manufacturer controls fail.
3. **Negative Controls:** Non-reactive serum matrix verified to yield $\text{COI} < 0.20$.

### 3. Out-of-Control Bench Troubleshooting Algorithm

When an IQC rule violation ($1_{3s}, 2_{2s}, R_{4s}$) triggers run rejection, the duty technician and laboratory pathologist execute the following 5-step containment protocol:
- **Step 1 (Immediate Instrument Auto-Lock):** LIMS automatically places all pending serology results in 'QC-HOLD' status. No patient results are authorized or printed.
- **Step 2 (Root-Cause Isolation):**
  - Inspect control vial for signs of evaporation, contamination, improper reconstitution, or storage temperature breach.
  - Verify reagent onboard stability, remaining test count, and expiration date.
  - Inspect optical flow cell, wash probes, fluidic lines for bubble formation or clogs.
- **Step 3 (Rerun Control):** Thaw and reconstitute a fresh, unopened aliquot of control material. Rerun Level 1 and Level 2.
- **Step 4 (Recalibration):** If fresh control fails, perform full 2-point or master curve recalibration using fresh calibrator vials.
- **Step 5 (Patient Sample Impact Assessment):** Once IQC rules pass ($Z < 1.5$), re-examine all patient samples processed since the last acceptable QC run. Document root cause, corrective action, and preventive action in `NABL-SER-LOG-03`.
