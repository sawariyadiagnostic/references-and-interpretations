## Section 1: Purpose & NABL Portal (`NABL 100 / NABL 112`) Application Readiness
This Master Departmental Pack fulfills two mandatory NABL accreditation functions for **Department 04: Serology, Infectious Diseases, Viral Markers & Autoimmune Immunology**:
1. **Turnkey `NABL 100` Portal Scope Submission Table:** Provides the complete parameter-by-parameter **Scope of Accreditation Matrix** (Analyte, Specimen Matrix, Examination Technique [`CLIA`, `ELISA`, `Immunoturbidimetry`, `IIF`, `Flocculation`, `ICT/RDT`], Cutoff/Reporting Units, Measurement Uncertainty / $C_5\text{–}C_{95}$ Gray-Zone Precision, and Controlled SOP Code).
2. **Statutory `HIV & AIDS Act 2017` / `NACO 3-Test Strategy`, Municipal `IDSP` Notifiable Disease Reporting & 10 Print-Ready Bench Registers (`NABL 112`):** Establishes the complete operational audit trail inspected during NABL On-Site Assessments.
