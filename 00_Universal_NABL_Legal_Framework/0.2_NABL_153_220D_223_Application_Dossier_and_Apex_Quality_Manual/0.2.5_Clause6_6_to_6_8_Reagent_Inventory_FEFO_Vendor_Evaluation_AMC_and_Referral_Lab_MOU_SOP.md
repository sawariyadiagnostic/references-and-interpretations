# 0.2.5 Clauses 6.6, 6.7 & 6.8 Reagent Inventory Management (`FEFO`), Lot Quarantine & Acceptance, Approved Vendor Evaluation, Analyzer `AMC/CMC` SLA & Referral Laboratory (`MOU`) Audit SOP

## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 6.6, 6.7 & 6.8 Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the end-to-end lifecycle of **Reagents and Consumables (`Clause 6.6`)**, **Service Agreements with Patients/Clinicians/Hospitals (`Clause 6.7`)**, and **Externally Provided Products and Services (`Clause 6.8` — Approved Vendors, Calibration Agencies, Biomedical `AMC/CMC` Providers, and NABL-Accredited Referral Laboratories)**.
2. **NABL 112 Specific Requirement:** NABL assessors physically inspect the cold-room/refrigerators (`2–8°C`) to verify that:
   - **Zero expired reagents, calibrators, controls, or vacutainers** are present anywhere in the laboratory.
   - Unverified newly received reagent lots are physically segregated with a **`RED "QUARANTINE — LOT VERIFICATION PENDING"` sticker** until lot-to-lot verification (`CLSI EP26-A` / `5 Pos + 5 Neg`) passes, after which a **`GREEN "LOT VERIFIED & APPROVED"` sticker** is affixed.
   - Every open vial carries **"Date Opened"**, **"Date Reconstituted"**, and **"Open-Vial Expiry Date"**.

## Section 2: Reagent & Consumable Receiving Inspection, Quarantine & `FEFO` Inventory Control (`Clause 6.6`)
1. **7-Point Receiving & Cold-Chain Inspection at Store Desk (`Upon Delivery`):**
   - Measure inside-box temperature of cold-chain shipments (`CLIA`, `Biochemistry`, `Coagulation`, `Control Sera`, `RDTs`, `Antibiotic Disks`) using a calibrated Infrared Thermometer: **Must arrive at $2.0^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8.0^\circ\text{C}$** (or frozen on dry ice $\le -15^\circ\text{C}$ for frozen plasmas/calibrators). **Reject immediately any shipment arriving $> 10^\circ\text{C}$ with melted gel packs!**
   - Verify remaining shelf-life is **$\ge 3\text{–}6\text{ months}$**, physical packaging/desiccant integrity, `COA` (`Certificate of Analysis`), `IFU` package insert version (check for any manufacturer method/reference-range change!), and `CDSCO` import/manufacturing license.
2. **Physical Segregation: Red Quarantine Sticker vs. Green Approved Sticker (`Clause 6.6.3`):**
   - **Step 1 (`RED QUARANTINE`):** Place newly received kit lots in the designated **"Quarantine Shelf"** inside the $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ refrigerator with a **Red Label (`QUARANTINE — DO NOT USE FOR PATIENTS`)**.
   - **Step 2 (`Lot-to-Lot Verification — CLSI EP26-A`):**
     - **Quantitative Assays (`Hematology`, `Biochemistry`, `CLIA`, `Coagulation`):** Run $2\text{ levels}$ of `IQC` + **$2\text{–}3$ retained patient specimens** (spanning low, normal, and high decision levels) in parallel on the **Old Lot** and **New Lot**. Acceptance: inter-lot `% Difference` **$\le \frac{1}{2} \times \text{CLIA } TE_a$** (or $\le 1.5 \times CV_A$).
     - **Qualitative/Semi-Quantitative Serology (`RDT / ELISA`):** Run **$5\text{ Characterized Positives (incl. 2 Weak-Pos)} + 5\text{ Characterized Negatives}$** (`SOP 4.1.11` — $100\%$ concordance required).
     - **Microbiology Media & Stains:** Perform `pH`, $5\%$ sterility ($48\text{h}$), and `ATCC` Growth Promotion / Inhibition (`SOP 6.1.11`).
   - **Step 3 (`GREEN RELEASE & FEFO`):** Affix the **Green Label (`LOT VERIFIED — Date & Sign`)** and place behind older lots following strict **First-Expiry-First-Out (`FEFO`)** rotation.

## Section 3: Approved Vendor Qualification & Annual 100-Point Re-Grading (`Clause 6.8.1 & 6.8.3`)
No diagnostic reagent, calibrator, control, vacutainer, or calibration service may be purchased from an unapproved vendor. Vendors are scored annually on a **100-Point Objective Matrix**:

| Evaluation Criterion | Weight (`100 Pts`) | Mandatory Qualification Requirement | Scoring Metric |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Regulatory & Quality Certification** | **$30\text{ Pts}$** | `CDSCO` License + `ISO 13485` / `CE-IVD` / `US-FDA` (for kits) or **`NABL ISO/IEC 17025`** (for calibration agencies) or **`NABL ISO 15189`** (for referral labs) | $30\text{ pts}$ if full certificates valid; $0\text{ pts}$ (Disqualified) if expired/missing. |
| **2. Cold-Chain Integrity & Packaging on Delivery** | **$25\text{ Pts}$** | $100\%$ of deliveries arrive within $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ with data-logger/intact phase-change packs | Deduct $5\text{ pts}$ per cold-chain excursion. |
| **3. Lot Consistency, Long Expiry ($\ge 6\text{m}$) & `COA/IFU` Supply** | **$25\text{ Pts}$** | Minimal lot switches ($\le 2\text{–}3\text{ lots/year}$); zero lot-verification failures | Deduct $5\text{ pts}$ per short-expiry ($< 3\text{m}$) or failed lot. |
| **4. Delivery Lead Time & Biomedical Application/Service SLA** | **$20\text{ Pts}$** | Routine supply $\le 48\text{h}$; emergency breakdown engineer on-site **$\le 4\text{–}12\text{ hours}$** | **Grade A ($\ge 85\text{ pts}$):** Preferred; **Grade B ($70\text{–}84\text{ pts}$):** Conditional; **Grade C ($< 70\text{ pts}$):** Blacklisted. |

## Section 4: Service Agreements (`Clause 6.7`) & NABL-Accredited Referral Laboratory `MOU` Governance (`Clause 6.8.2`)
1. **Clinical Service Agreements (`Clause 6.7`):**
   - For walk-in patients, the signed/barcoded **Test Requisition Form (`TRF`) & Invoice Receipt** constitutes the binding service agreement (specifying tests ordered, accredited vs `#` status, price, and committed `TAT`).
   - For institutional/hospital clients, a written **Hospital Service Level Agreement (`SLA`)** defines specimen transport criteria, `STAT` vs routine `TAT`, critical read-back contacts, and billing terms. Any contract variation impacting diagnostic quality triggers immediate notification to the clinical user.
2. **Referral Laboratory Selection, `MOU` & Annual Audit (`Clause 6.8.2` & `NABL 112`):**
   - **Rule 1 (`Mandatory NABL ISO 15189:2022 Accreditation`):** Specialized send-out tests (`HPLC Vitamins`, `LC-MS/MS`, `NGS NIPT`, `MGIT 960 TB Culture`) are referred **exclusively to NABL-accredited reference laboratories** whose current NABL Scope of Accreditation explicitly lists the referred parameter.
   - **Rule 2 (`Report Integrity — Zero Alteration`):** The referring laboratory **never alters** the numerical result, units, biological reference interval, or pathologist interpretation of the referral laboratory (`SOP 0.1.3`), and clearly identifies the referral status (`#` per `NABL 133`) while retaining responsibility for communicating results to the patient/clinician.
