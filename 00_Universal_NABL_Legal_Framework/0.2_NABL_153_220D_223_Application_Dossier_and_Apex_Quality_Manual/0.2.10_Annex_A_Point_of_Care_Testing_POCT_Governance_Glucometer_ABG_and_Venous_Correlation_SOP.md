# 0.2.10 Normative Annex A Point-of-Care Testing (`POCT`) Governance, Capillary Glucometer / `ABG` / Rapid Card Oversight, Daily Liquid QC & Monthly Venous-vs-Capillary Split-Sample Correlation (`CLSI POCT12-A3`) SOP

## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & ISO 15189:2022 Normative Annex A Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To establish complete institutional governance, operator training and competency lock, daily 2-level liquid Internal Quality Control (`IQC`), monthly Central-Laboratory split-sample comparability, and clinical contraindication shields for all **Point-of-Care Testing (`POCT`)** devices operated by or under the oversight of the laboratory, in strict compliance with **ISO 15189:2022 Normative Annex A (`Additional Requirements for Point-of-Care Testing`, superseding `ISO 22870:2016`)**, **CLSI POCT04**, **CLSI POCT12-A3**, and **NABL 112**.
2. **Scope of `POCT` Devices Governed:**
   - **Capillary Blood Glucose Meters (`Glucometers` — Glucose Dehydrogenase [`GDH-FAD`] / Glucose Oxidase [`GOx`] Plasma-Calibrated Strips):** Used ONLY for (a) pre-OGTT fasting safety screening before administering a $75\text{g}$ oral glucose load (`SOP 2.1.5`), (b) immediate evaluation of phlebotomy vasovagal syncope (`SOP 0.2.6`), or (c) emergency bedside/OPD triage when specifically requested.
   - **Point-of-Care Cartridge Blood Gas / Electrolyte Analyzers (`ABG / iCa2+ / Lactate` — `SOP 2.7.1`)** and **Emergency Lateral-Flow Rapid Cassettes (`UPT`, `Troponin/Malaria/Dengue` where applicable)**.

---

## Section 2: `POCT` Governance Structure & Multidisciplinary Responsibility (`ISO 15189:2022 Annex A.2`)
1. **Laboratory Director & Designated `POCT` Coordinator (`Clause A.2.1 & A.2.2`):**
   - The **Laboratory Director** retains ultimate responsibility for the quality, device selection, verification, and clinical appropriateness of all `POCT` performed within the facility or satellite collection centers.
   - The **Quality Manager / Clinical Biochemist** serves as the designated **`POCT` Coordinator**, responsible for:
     1. Maintaining the **Master `POCT` Device Inventory** (Make, Model, Serial #, Location, Strip Lot # & Expiry).
     2. Approving only **enzymatically specific (`GDH-FAD` or `Hexokinase`-calibrated, whole-blood hematocrit-compensated `20%–60% Hct`) hospital-grade glucometers** (strictly prohibiting `GDH-PQQ` strips that suffer lethal false-high interference from icodextrin/maltose/galactose!).
     3. Auditing daily `POCT` liquid `IQC` logs and conducting **Monthly Split-Sample Comparability** against the central laboratory's **`Bonavera Chem-400` Hexokinase Reference Method (`SOP 2.1.2`)**.
2. **Operator Training, Competency Assessment & Authorization (`Clause A.3`):**
   - Only phlebotomists, nurses, or technologists who have completed documented training and passed the **Annual `POCT` Practical Competency & Blinded Split-Sample Check (`SOP 0.1.12`)** are authorized to perform `POCT`.

## Section 3: Daily 2-Level Liquid `POCT` Control & Strip Lot Verification (`CLSI POCT12-A3`)
1. **Daily Pre-Use 2-Level Aqueous/Matrix Control Gate (`Every Morning Before Any Patient Fingerstick`):**
   - Test **Level-1 (Hypoglycemic/Low-Normal Control: $\approx 45\text{–}80\text{ mg/dL}$)** and **Level-2 (Hyperglycemic Control: $\approx 250\text{–}350\text{ mg/dL}$)** on every active glucometer.
   - Both levels MUST fall within the manufacturer's lot-specific acceptable range (`PASS`). **If either control fails, quarantine the glucometer immediately (`DO NOT USE`)** and use central laboratory venous plasma glucose (`Bonavera Chem-400`).
2. **Strip Canister Storage & Open-Vial Dating:**
   - Store glucometer test strips in their original desiccant vial at $4^\circ\text{C}\text{–}30^\circ\text{C}$; write **"Date Opened"** and **"90-Day Open-Vial Expiry"** on the vial; recap immediately after removing a strip.

---

## Section 4: Monthly Central-Lab (`Bonavera Chem-400`) vs. `POCT` Glucometer Split-Sample Comparability (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7.4 & CLSI POCT12-A3`)
Every month (and on every new test-strip lot), compare **at least 2 to 5 paired capillary fingerstick (`POCT`) and simultaneous venous NaF-Oxalate plasma (`Bonavera Chem-400` Hexokinase `2.1.2`) specimens** spun within $< 15\text{ minutes}$:

| Central Lab Reference Venous Plasma Glucose (`Bonavera Chem-400`) | Mandatory `CLSI POCT12-A3 / ISO 15197:2013` Acceptance Limit for `POCT` Glucometer | Mandatory Action if Outside Tolerance |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **At Glucose $< 100\text{ mg/dL}$ ($< 5.55\text{ mmol/L}$)** | **Within $\pm 12\text{ mg/dL}$** *(ISO 15197 max $\pm 15\text{ mg/dL}$)* of `Bonavera Chem-400` | Replace test strip vial, clean optical sensor window, verify patient Hematocrit ($20\%\text{–}60\%$), and re-test; decommission meter if bias persists. |
| **At Glucose $\ge 100\text{ mg/dL}$ ($\ge 5.55\text{ mmol/L}$)** | **Within $\pm 12.0\%$** *(ISO 15197 max $\pm 15.0\%$)* of `Bonavera Chem-400` | Replace test strip vial and re-verify with 2-level liquid controls; decommission meter if bias $> \pm 12\%$. |

## Section 5: Mandatory Clinical Contraindications & Reporting Rules for Capillary `POCT` Glucose
1. **When Capillary Fingerstick `POCT` Is Strictly Contraindicated (`Draw Venous/Arterial Blood Instead!`):**
   - **Peripheral Circulatory Shock, Severe Hypotension, Sepsis, or Vasopressor Therapy:** Peripheral vasoconstriction causes severe local tissue glucose extraction $\to$ **Capillary fingerstick glucose is falsely LOW ($30\text{–}50\text{ mg/dL}$ lower than true central venous/arterial glucose!)**.
   - **Diabetic Ketoacidosis (`DKA`) or Hyperosmolar Hyperglycemic State (`HHS`)**, severe generalized edema (`Anasarca`), or extreme Hematocrit (**$\text{Hct} < 20\%$ causes falsely HIGH glucometer readings; $\text{Hct} > 60\%$ [neonatal polycythemia] causes falsely LOW glucometer readings** on non-hematocrit-corrected strips!).
2. **Mandatory Report Distinction & Critical Value Confirmation (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.4.1 & Annex A`):**
   - Every `POCT` result entered into the `LIMS` or patient record must be explicitly labeled **`"Point-of-Care Capillary Whole-Blood (Plasma-Equivalent) Glucose"`** to distinguish it from accredited Central-Lab Venous Plasma Hexokinase Glucose (`2.1.2`).
   - **Diagnostic Prohibition (`ADA / WHO / ICMR`):** Capillary `POCT` glucometry must **NEVER be used alone to establish a formal diagnosis of Diabetes Mellitus or Gestational Diabetes (`GDM`)** — all diagnostic cutoffs (`FPG >= 126 mg/dL`, `2h OGTT >= 200 mg/dL`) require accredited venous plasma analysis on `Bonavera Chem-400`!
   - **Critical `POCT` Alert (`< 50 mg/dL` or `> 400 mg/dL`):** Initiate immediate clinical notification (`<= 15 min`) AND draw a simultaneous **Venous NaF-Oxalate Plasma tube** for confirmatory central-laboratory analysis (`SOP 2.1.2`).
