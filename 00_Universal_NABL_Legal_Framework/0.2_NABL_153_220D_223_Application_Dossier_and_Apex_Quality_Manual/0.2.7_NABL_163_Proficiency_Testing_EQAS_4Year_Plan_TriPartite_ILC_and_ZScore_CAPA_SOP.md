## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & NABL 163 / ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.3.7.3 Regulatory Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To define the laboratory's **4-Year Rolling Proficiency Testing (`PT`) / External Quality Assessment Scheme (`EQAS`) Participation Plan**, **Zero-Collusion Routine-Integration Protocol**, **Tri-Partite Inter-Laboratory Comparison (`ILC` with $\ge 2$ NABL-accredited peer laboratories)** for parameters where accredited `PT` is unavailable, and **Quantitative `Z-Score / SDI` Root-Cause Investigation (`CAPA`) Workflow**.
2. **NABL 163 Mandatory Pre-Application & Maintenance Rules:**
   - **Rule 1 (`Pre-Application Prerequisite`):** Prior to submitting `NABL 153`, the applicant laboratory MUST have satisfactorily participated in **at least ONE completed `PT / EQAS` trial** from a `NABL / ILAC MRA ISO/IEC 17043` accredited PT provider (or `NABL`-recognized national reference provider: **`AIIMS New Delhi ISHTM`, `CMC Vellore ACBI`, `IAMM EQAS`, `RML/NIMHANS/NRL`, `Bio-Rad EQAS`**) for each applied discipline (`Hematology`, `Clinical Biochemistry`, `Immunoassay`, `Serology`, `Clinical Pathology`, `Microbiology`).
   - **Rule 2 (`4-Year 100% Scope Coverage`):** Every single accredited parameter on the laboratory's `NABL 100 / 153` Scope of Accreditation MUST be covered by either **Accredited `PT / EQAS`** or **Documented Tri-Partite `ILC`** at least once every year (and $100\%$ across the 4-year accreditation cycle).

## Section 3: Mandatory Routine-Integration & Anti-Collusion Protocol (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.7.3`)
1. **Integration into Routine Patient Workflow:**
   - Reconstituted/thawed `EQAS` samples are assigned a standard routine `LIMS` barcode and tested by the **routine bench technologist on duty** using the exact same reagent lot, calibration curve, and single-replicate run protocol as patient specimens.
2. **Strict Prohibitions (`Zero Special Treatment`):**
   - **NEVER** run an `EQAS` sample in triplicate/multiplicate unless patient samples are routinely run in triplicate!
   - **NEVER** recalibrate an analyzer or open a brand-new `IQC` vial specifically before running an `EQAS` specimen!
   - **NEVER** communicate, compare, or share `EQAS` results with another laboratory, reagent vendor engineer, or peer before the official `EQAS` submission deadline (`Anti-Collusion Declaration` signed on every cycle!).

## Section 4: Tri-Partite Inter-Laboratory Comparison (`ILC`) Protocol for Parameters Without Commercial `EQAS` (`NABL 163`)
For any accredited parameter where an accredited commercial `PT/EQAS` scheme is unavailable or temporarily disrupted (e.g., **`ESR`, `Sickling Test`, `Direct/Indirect Coombs`, `G6PD`, `Semen Analysis`, `CSF / Serous Body Fluid Cell Counts`, `Urine Rothera/Hay/Fouchet/Bence-Jones`, `ADA`, `Fructosamine`, `Ammonia`, `Hanging Drop`**), the Quality Manager executes a **Quarterly / Semi-Annual Split-Sample `ILC` Exchange** with **at least TWO (`>= 2`) Peer NABL `ISO 15189:2022` Accredited Laboratories (`Lab B` & `Lab C`)**:
1. **Specimen Preparation & Blinded Cold-Chain Dispatch:**
   - Select **2 to 3 fresh, de-identified patient specimens** (spanning normal and pathological levels) within $< 2\text{ hours}$ of collection; aliquot into identical coded tubes (`ILC-2026-Q1-S1, S2`) and dispatch simultaneously at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ to Peer NABL Lab B and Peer NABL Lab C for analysis within the analyte's stability window (`SOP 0.1.9`).
2. **Statistical Evaluation Criteria (`Tri-Partite ILC`):**
   - **Quantitative Analytes:** Calculate the Consensus Mean ($\bar{X}$) of the 3 NABL laboratories (`Our Lab`, `Lab B`, `Lab C`) and compute the **Percentage Deviation (`% Bias`)** against **CLIA / EFLM Total Allowable Error ($TE_a$)**:
     $$\text{\% Difference} = \frac{|X_{\text{Lab}} - \bar{X}_{\text{Consensus}}|}{\bar{X}_{\text{Consensus}}} \times 100 \le \mathbf{\frac{1}{2} \times \text{TE}_a} \quad \text{(PASS)}$$
   - **Qualitative / Semi-Quantitative / Microscopic Examinations:** Must achieve **$100\%$ diagnostic category concordance** (or within $\pm 1\text{ grade}$ / Rümke 95% binomial envelope).

## Section 5: Quantitative `Z-Score / SDI` Evaluation & Mandatory Unsatisfactory `CAPA` Protocol
When monthly/quarterly `EQAS` summary reports arrive:
$$\text{Standard Deviation Index (`SDI` or `Z-Score`)} = \frac{X_{\text{Lab Result}} - \mu_{\text{Peer Method Group Mean}}}{\text{SD}_{\text{Peer Method Group}}}$$
- **$|Z| \le 2.0$ (`Satisfactory — Green`):** Acceptable performance; sign and file.
- **$2.0 < |Z| < 3.0$ (`Warning — Yellow`):** Alert signal! Check Levey-Jennings `IQC` chart for emerging `4:1s` or `10:x` bias; **mandatory `CAPA` required if two consecutive `EQAS` cycles exceed $|Z| > 2.0$ on the same analyte**.
- **$|Z| \ge 3.0$ or Qualitative Discordance (`Action / Unsatisfactory — Red`):** **Immediate Mandatory `CAPA` Investigation (`NABL-LOG-12`) within $48\text{ hours}$:**
  1. Audit transcription/unit-conversion error (`mg/dL` vs `mmol/L`), reconstitution volume/water quality, cold-chain receipt, `IQC` Z-score on the day of analysis, and calibration factor ($K$) drift.
  2. Re-assay the retained $-20^\circ\text{C}$ `EQAS` aliquot (`stored for 30 days`) after corrective recalibration.
  3. Evaluate clinical impact on patient samples run during that period (`SOP 0.2.9` Recall Assessment).
