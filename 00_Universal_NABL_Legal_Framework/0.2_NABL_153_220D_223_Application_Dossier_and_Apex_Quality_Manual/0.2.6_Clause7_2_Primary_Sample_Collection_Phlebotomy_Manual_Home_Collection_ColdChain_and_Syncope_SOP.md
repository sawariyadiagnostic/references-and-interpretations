## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.2 Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To serve as the standalone **Primary Sample Collection & Phlebotomy Manual** required by **ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 7.2.1 to 7.2.7**, **CLSI GP41-Ed7 (`Venipuncture`)**, **CLSI GP42-Ed7 (`Capillary Blood Specimen Collection`)**, and **NABL 112**, governing:
   - Patient & Clinician Pre-Examination Information Directory (fasting, diurnal timing, drug/biotin washout, abstinence).
   - Mandatory **Two-Unique-Identifier Verification** at the phlebotomy chair/bedside before drawing blood (`Zero Unlabeled Tube Policy`).
   - Standardized Adult Venipuncture ($\le 60\text{-second}$ tourniquet rule, `CLSI GP41` Order of Draw), Pediatric/Neonatal Heel-Stick & Fingerstick Collection, Paired Blood Culture Asepsis ($70\%\text{ IPA} + 2\%\text{ Chlorhexidine}$), and Non-Blood Primary Sample Collection ($24\text{h}$ Urine, Clean-Catch Midstream Urine, Stool, Sputum, Semen, Swabs).
   - **Home-Collection & Satellite Collection Center Insulated Cold-Chain (`2.0°C–8.0°C` PCM Vaccine Carrier + Calibrated Digital Min/Max Data Logger) Validation & Transport Log**.
   - **Phlebotomy Adverse Reaction (`Vasovagal Syncope / Seizure / Hematoma / Accidental Arterial Puncture / Nerve Injury`) First-Aid Emergency Protocol**.
2. **Mandatory Availability (`Clause 7.2.4.1`):** A controlled physical or digital copy of this Manual MUST be present at **every phlebotomy workstation** and inside **every Home-Collection Technician's kit bag**.

| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |

Patient remains seated at rest. | Exact Timed Draw (`120 min` post-meal / `0, 60, 120 min` OGTT) | Drawing at $90\text{ min}$ or $150\text{ min}$ or ambulatory exercise falsifies glycemic peak/clearance. |

## Section 3: Standardized 10-Step Venipuncture (`CLSI GP41`), Pediatric Capillary Draw (`CLSI GP42`) & $24\text{h}$ Urine Protocol
1. **The 10-Step Venipuncture Protocol (`CLSI GP41-Ed7`):**
   - **Step 1 (`Positive Patient ID`):** Ask the patient to state their **Full Name and Age/Date of Birth** (never ask a "Yes/No" question!) and match against the `TRF` and printed barcode labels (**Minimum 2 Unique Identifiers: Full Name + Unique Lab Barcode ID/DOB**).
   - **Step 2 (`Positioning`):** Seat the patient comfortably in a phlebotomy chair with safety armrests (or supine on a couch if anxious/pediatric/history of syncope). **Never draw blood from a standing patient!**
   - **Step 3 (`Hand Hygiene & Gloves`):** Perform alcohol hand rub ($70\%\text{ IPA}$) and don clean single-use nitrile gloves.
   - **Step 4 (`Vein Selection & <= 60-Second Tourniquet Rule`):** Apply single-use/disinfected tourniquet $7.5\text{–}10\text{ cm}$ above the antecubital fossa (median cubital > cephalic > basilic vein). **Release the tourniquet within $\le 60\text{ seconds}$ as soon as blood flows into the first tube!** (*Prolonged tourniquet $> 60\text{ sec}$ causes hemoconcentration, elevating Total Calcium, Albumin, Protein, Lipids, Enzymes, and Potassium by $+5\%\text{ to }+15\%$!*). Instruct the patient **NOT to pump their fist** (fist-pumping elevates Potassium by $+1.0\text{ mmol/L}$ and Lactate!).
   - **Step 5 (`Antisepsis`):** Cleanse the venipuncture site with $70\%$ Isopropyl Alcohol in concentric circles ($5\text{ cm}$ diameter) and **allow to air-dry completely for $30\text{ seconds}$** (wet alcohol hemolyzes red cells and stings!). *Note: For Blood Cultures (`6.1.2`), use $70\%\text{ IPA}$ followed by $2\%\text{ Chlorhexidine Gluconate}$ ($30\text{s}$ dry); for Blood Ethanol (`2.7.5`), use non-alcoholic Aqueous Povidone-Iodine / Benzalkonium.*
   - **Step 6 (`Sterile Needle Verification`):** Show the intact sterile seal of the $21\text{G}/22\text{G}$ closed evacuated multi-sample needle (or winged butterfly set) to the patient before opening.
   - **Step 7 (`CLSI GP41 Order of Draw — SOP 0.1.2`):**
     $$\mathbf{(1)\text{ Blood Culture}} \to \mathbf{(2)\text{ Light-Blue } 3.2\%\text{ Citrate } (9:1\text{ Fill Mark})} \to \mathbf{(3)\text{ Red Plain / Gold SST}} \to \mathbf{(4)\text{ Green Heparin}} \to \mathbf{(5)\text{ Lavender }\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}} \to \mathbf{(6)\text{ Grey NaF-Oxalate}}$$
   - **Step 8 (`Gentle Inversion`):** Gently invert each tube in a figure-of-8 immediately upon removal ($3\text{–}4\times$ for Citrate; $5\text{–}8\times$ for `SST`; $8\text{–}10\times$ for `EDTA`/Heparin/Fluoride). **NEVER shake vigorously!**
   - **Step 9 (`Needle Safety & Bedside Barcoding`):** Activate safety shield and discard needle immediately into the **White Translucent Puncture-Proof Sharps Container (`SOP 0.1.14`)** (never recap with two hands!). **Affix barcode labels onto the tubes IN FRONT OF THE PATIENT before they leave the chair** and record the exact **Time of Collection (`hh:mm`)** and **Phlebotomist Initials**.
   - **Step 10 (`Post-Puncture Hemostasis`):** Apply sterile cotton/gauze pressure for $2\text{–}3\text{ minutes}$ ($5\text{ min}$ if on Aspirin/Warfarin/DOACs) with the arm straight (do NOT bend the elbow, which causes antecubital hematomas!), apply an adhesive bandage, and verify the patient feels well before standing.
2. **Pediatric Heel-Stick & Fingerstick Capillary Order of Draw (`CLSI GP42-Ed7`):**
   - Warm heel ($40\text{–}42^\circ\text{C}$ moist towel for $3\text{ min}$); puncture ONLY the **medial or lateral plantar surface of the heel** using a depth-controlled retractable neonatal lancet ($\le 1.0\text{ mm}$ preterm; $\le 2.0\text{ mm}$ term — never puncture the central arch or posterior curvature to prevent calcaneal osteomyelitis!).
   - **Wipe away the FIRST drop of blood** with dry sterile gauze (contains interstitial tissue fluid and thromboplastin!).
   - **Capillary Order of Draw (OPPOSITE to Venipuncture!):** **(1) Blood Gas Capillary** $\to$ **(2) Lavender Micro-$\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ (`CBC` first to prevent platelet clumping!)** $\to$ **(3) Green Heparin** $\to$ **(4) Red/Gold Serum Micro-Tube**.
3. **Patient Instruction for $24\text{-Hour}$ Timed Urine Collection (`SOP 2.3.10 & 2.3.12`):**
   - At $07:00\text{ AM}$ on Day 1, **empty the bladder completely into the toilet and DISCARD this first morning specimen** (record exact start time: $T_{\text{start}} = 07:00\text{ AM}$).
   - Collect **EVERY single drop of urine passed over the next $24\text{ hours}$** (day and night) into the $3\text{–}5\text{ L}$ amber graduated jug kept refrigerated ($2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ / cool pack).
   - At exactly $07:00\text{ AM}$ on Day 2 ($T_{\text{end}}$), empty the bladder one final time **INTO the collection jug**, mix thoroughly, and transport to the laboratory immediately (alongside a simultaneous blood draw for Creatinine Clearance if ordered).

## Section 4: Home-Collection & Satellite Cold-Chain (`2.0°C–8.0°C`) Validation & Vasovagal Syncope Emergency Protocol
1. **Home-Collection Insulated Vaccine-Carrier (`2–8°C`) Protocol (`Clause 7.2.5 & 7.2.6`):**
   - Every home-collection bag is a rigid, leak-proof **Polyurethane-Insulated Carrier** containing conditioned **Phase-Change Gel Packs (`4°C`)**, a **Sponge Tube-Separator Rack** (so blood tubes NEVER directly touch frozen ice packs, which causes freeze-hemolysis!), a **Calibrated Digital Min/Max Stem Thermometer**, and a **Mini Pocket Spill Kit (`1% NaOCl` + Absorbent Pads)**.
   - Maximum transit time from patient's home to central laboratory centrifugation: **$\le 2\text{ hours}$** ($\le 45\text{ min}$ if unspun serum/plasma; portable field centrifuge used for trips $> 90\text{ min}$). Upon arrival at Central Accessioning, record the **Arrival Cooler Temperature ($2.0^\circ\text{C}\text{–}8.0^\circ\text{C}$)** in `NABL-PHL-LOG-01`.
2. **Emergency Management of Phlebotomy Vasovagal Syncope (Fainting), Seizure or Hematoma (`Clause 4.3 & ISO 15190`):**
   - **Immediate Action (`Syncope / Pre-Syncope: Pallor, Diaphoresis, Bradycardia, Dizziness`):**
     1. **Release the tourniquet and withdraw the needle immediately** (activate sharps safety shield).
     2. Lower the patient's head and recline the phlebotomy chair into the **Trendelenburg / Supine Position with legs elevated $30^\circ$** above heart level; loosen tight collar/clothing; apply a cool compress to the forehead/neck.
     3. Summon the **Laboratory Director / On-Duty Medical Officer (`MD`)** immediately; monitor radial pulse, blood pressure, and capillary blood glucose; keep the patient supine for **$\ge 15\text{–}20\text{ minutes}$** with oral glucose/electrolyte drink once fully conscious; log every event in `NABL-PHL-LOG-02`.
