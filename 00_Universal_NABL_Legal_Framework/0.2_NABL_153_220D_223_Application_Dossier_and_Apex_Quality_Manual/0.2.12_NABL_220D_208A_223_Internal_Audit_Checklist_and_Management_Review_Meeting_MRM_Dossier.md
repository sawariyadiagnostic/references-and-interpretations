### 1.4 The 3-Pronged Audit Methodology (`Horizontal` + `Vertical` + `Witnessed Trial`) & Internal Audit Process `FMECA` (`ISO 22367`)
Every internal audit executed under this SOP combines three complementary investigative vectors required by **`NABL 220D`**, **`NABL 208A`**, and **`NABL 223`**:
1. **Prong 1 — Horizontal System Audit (`NABL 223` Clause-by-Clause Verification):** Evaluates a single systemic requirement (e.g., `Clause 6.5` Metrological Traceability, `Clause 6.6.3` Lot-to-Lot Reagent Verification, or `Clause 7.4.1.6` Critical Value Read-Back) horizontally across all 6 departments to verify uniform QMS implementation.
2. **Prong 2 — Vertical Sample Traceability Audit (`14-Point Specimen Lifecycle Forensic Trace`):** Selects actual completed patient accessions at random from the LIMS (specifically targeting Critical Values, Reflex/Repeat tests, Amended Reports, and Positive Serology/Culture results) and traces every physical and digital footprint backward to the exact phlebotomist, vacutainer lot, centrifuge spin, reagent lot, calibrator trace, `IQC` run, and pathologist `DSC` signature.
3. **Prong 3 — Live Bench Witnessed Examination (`NABL 208A` On-Site Trial):** The auditor stands at the bench and witnesses a technologist performing an actual examination from specimen preparation to result entry, scoring procedural adherence against the controlled Level-3 SOP and conducting a 5-question oral competency defense.
