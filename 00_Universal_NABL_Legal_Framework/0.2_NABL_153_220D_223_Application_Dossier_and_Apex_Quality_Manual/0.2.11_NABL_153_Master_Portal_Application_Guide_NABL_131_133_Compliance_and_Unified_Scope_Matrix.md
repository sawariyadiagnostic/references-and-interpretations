## Section 2: `NABL 131` Terms & Conditions Compliance Undertaking (`ISO 15189:2022`)
```text
================================================================================
UNDERTAKING OF COMPLIANCE WITH NABL 131 (TERMS & CONDITIONS FOR ACCREDITATION)
================================================================================
We, the Legal Entity Owner and Laboratory Director, hereby undertake to comply
with all 26 clauses of NABL 131, specifically committing to:
1. Maintain total compliance with ISO 15189:2022, NABL 112, NABL 133 & NABL 163
   at all times and grant NABL assessors unannounced/scheduled access to all
   laboratory premises, LIMS databases, bench registers, and personnel.
2. Claim accreditation ONLY for the specific diagnostic parameters and matrices
   explicitly granted on our NABL Scope of Accreditation, and prefix any
   non-accredited or referred parameter with '#' per NABL 133.
3. Participate continuously in NABL 163-compliant PT/EQAS and Tri-Partite ILC
   programs and submit timely CAPA for any unsatisfactory performance.
4. Notify NABL in writing within <= 15 days of any change in Legal Status,
   Ownership, Premises Location, Laboratory Director, Authorized Signatories,
   or Major Primary Analytical Equipment (MEK-1503, Chem-400/200, Maglumi X3).
5. Pay all prescribed NABL application, assessment, and annual surveillance/
   accreditation fees on time and never use accreditation in a manner that
   brings NABL into disrepute.
```

## Section 3: `NABL 133` Policy on Use of NABL Symbol & Distinction of Non-Accredited Parameters (`#` Rule)
1. **Pre-Accreditation Phase (`Before Grant of NABL Certificate`):**
   - While the application (`NABL 153`) is under processing, **the NABL Symbol / Logo and the phrase "NABL Accredited Laboratory" are strictly disabled in the `LIMS` report header**.
2. **Post-Accreditation Phase (`After Grant of NABL Certificate & Scope`):**
   - The official NABL Symbol (with exact Certificate Number `MC-XXXX` below the logo, maintaining exact aspect ratio and minimum height per `NABL 133`) is enabled ONLY on reports containing **at least one accredited parameter**.
   - **Mandatory Non-Accredited Parameter Distinction (`#` Symbol Rule — `NABL 133`):**
     - Whenever a single patient report contains **both** NABL-accredited parameters (e.g., `CBC`, `LFT`, `KFT`, `TSH`) and **non-accredited / referred / historical parameters** (e.g., `# BT/CT`, `# HPLC Vitamin B1`, `# Referred NIPT`), every non-accredited parameter MUST be prominently prefixed with **`#`** (or `*`) and accompanied by the bold footnote (`SOP 0.1.1`):
       > **`#` Indicates test parameter is NOT covered under our NABL ISO 15189:2022 Accredited Scope (or performed at a NABL-Accredited Referral Laboratory).**
   - **Reports Containing Zero Accredited Parameters:** Must be printed on **Plain Laboratory Letterhead WITHOUT the NABL Symbol or `MC-XXXX` claim**.
