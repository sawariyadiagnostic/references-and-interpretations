## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.6 Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To standardize the validation, cybersecurity access control (`RBAC`), bidirectional analyzer interface verification (`ASTM E1394 / HL7`), auto-verification & calculated-equation locking (`CLSI AUTO15`), uneditable electronic audit trail tracking, daily 3-2-1 encrypted backup + quarterly restoration drills, and **Manual Paper Downtime Contingency & Post-Recovery Back-Entry Verification** required by **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.6 (`Control of Data and Information Management`)**, **Clause 8.4 (`Control of Records`)**, and **NABL 112**.
2. **Scope:** Covers the Laboratory Information Management System (`LIMS`), bidirectional middleware interfaces connected to **`Nihon Kohden MEK-1503`**, **`Bonavera Chem-400 & Chem-200`**, **`Snibe Maglumi X3`**, Coagulometer, `ISE`, and Urine Reader, as well as encrypted PDF/WhatsApp/Email/Portal patient report delivery channels.

---

## Section 2: Pre-Go-Live & Post-Update `LIMS` Validation Protocol (`CLSI AUTO08 / AUTO11`)
Before initial deployment—and after **any software patch, formula modification, reference range update, or new analyzer interface integration**—the Quality Manager and IT Administrator execute and document the **5-Point `LIMS` Validation Protocol**:
1. **Bidirectional `ASTM / HL7` Interface Transmission Accuracy (`20-Sample End-to-End Check`):**
   - Transmit $20$ barcoded test orders across `Hematology`, `Biochemistry`, and `Maglumi X3 CLIA` from `LIMS` $\to$ Analyzer $\to$ `LIMS` $\to$ Final PDF Report.
   - Verify **$100\%$ exact character-for-character concordance** between the raw analyzer printout (`instrument screen/thermal tape`) and the final rendered `LIMS` PDF report across:
     - Numeric values, decimal rounding rules, `UCUM` units, `< LoQ` / `> AMR` inequality signs (`< 0.005`, `> 1000`), and `HIL` / histogram flags.
2. **Mathematical Equation & Suppression Gate Verification (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.6`):**
   - Verify all calculated parameters (`MCV/MCH/MCHC`, `ANC/ALC/AEC/AMC/ABC`, `INR`, `CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR`, `Friedewald/Martin-Hopkins LDL-C`, `Payne Corrected Calcium`, `Anion Gap`, `BUN`, `Indirect Bilirubin`, `Globulin & A:G Ratio`, `R-Factor`, `FIB-4`, `eAG`, `TSAT %`, `HOMA-IR`, `ROMA Index`, `Free Androgen Index`) against a handheld scientific calculator across **Low, Normal, and High/Boundary inputs** (`SOP 2.8 LOG-09`).
   - Verify **Hard Suppression Gates:** Confirm that entering `Triglycerides = 415 mg/dL` automatically **blocks** Friedewald `LDL-C` output (`CALC-SUPPRESSED`), and that entering `Patient Age = 8 Years` automatically switches `CKD-EPI 2021` to **Pediatric Schwartz eGFR**.
3. **5-Dimensional Biological Reference Interval & Critical Flag Boundary Check:**
   - Test dummy patients across **Neonatal (`0–28d`), Pediatric (`1m–17y`), Adult Male, Adult Female, Geriatric (`>=65y`), and Pregnancy (`T1/T2/T3`)** profiles to confirm the `LIMS` pulls the exact age/sex/pregnancy-stratified reference interval and triggers bold **`L / H`**, **`CRITICAL LOW (!!)`**, and **`CRITICAL HIGH (!!)`** alerts at the exact cutoffs defined in `SOP 0.1.5` and all 273 standalone SOPs.

## Section 3: Cybersecurity, Role-Based Access Control (`RBAC`) & Uneditable Electronic Audit Trail (`Clause 7.6.2 & 7.6.3`)
| User Role Tier | Permitted `LIMS` Privileges | Strictly Blocked (`Read-Only / Prohibited`) Actions | Password & Session Security |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Front-Desk / Phlebotomist** | Patient registration, `TRF` scan upload, barcode printing, sample collection timestamping, receipt printing. | **Zero access** to enter/edit test results, view confidential `HIV` results (`NABL-SER-LOG-04`), or alter reference ranges. | Unique User ID + Strong Password ($12+\text{ chars}$, rotated every $90\text{d}$); auto-lock after $10\text{ min}$ idle. |
| **2. Bench Technologist (`MLT`)** | Sample accessioning, rejection logging (`REJ-CODE`), viewing analyzer interface queue, entering manual results/dilutions (`Maker / First-Level Technical Entry`). | **Cannot authorize/release final reports**; cannot embed Authorized Signatory digital signature; cannot alter master SOPs/equations. | Unique User ID; all manual edits logged in Audit Trail. |
| **3. Authorized Signatory (`MD Pathology / Biochemistry / Microbiology`)** | Reviewing `IQC` & delta-checks (`RCV %`), adding clinical interpretation comments, **Second-Level Final Authorization & Digital Signature Release**, issuing Amended Reports (`SOP 0.1.3`). | Cannot sign reports outside their `NABL 112` specialty scope (`NABL-LOG-04`); cannot delete historical audit trail entries. | Encrypted Token / Two-Factor / Biometric Signatory Pin; **Zero shared logins!** |
| **4. Quality Manager / LIMS Admin** | Master test dictionary configuration (with Lab Director co-signature), `QI` extraction, audit trail inspection, encrypted database backup. | Cannot authorize clinical patient reports unless also a qualified `MD` Signatory. | Dual-Control Administrative Log. |

- **Immutable Electronic Audit Trail (`Clause 8.4.2`):** Whenever any result is modified prior to or after authorization, the `LIMS` permanently records: **`[Original Value] -> [New Value] | [User ID] | [Exact Date & Timestamp hh:mm:ss] | [Mandatory Reason for Change]`**. Post-release modifications automatically generate an **`[AMENDED REPORT — SUPERSEDES REPORT DATED ...]`** header (`SOP 0.1.3` & `NABL-LOG-14`).

## Section 4: Daily 3-2-1 Encrypted Backup, Quarterly Restoration Drill & Manual Paper Downtime Contingency (`Clauses 7.6.4 & 7.6.6`)
1. **The 3-2-1 Encrypted Backup Rule:**
   - **Real-Time (`RAID-1` Mirrored SSDs)** on the primary server + **Automated Daily `23:00 PM` Full Encrypted (`AES-256`) Database Backup** to (1) an isolated local External NAS/Hard Drive AND (2) a secure India-region Cloud Vault (`AWS Mumbai / Azure India` — `DPDP Act 2023` compliant).
   - **Mandatory Quarterly Backup Restoration Drill (`Every 3 Months`):** Restore the backup database onto a sandbox workstation and verify 5 random historical patient reports (`100% integrity required`).
2. **Manual Paper Downtime Contingency Protocol (`If LIMS / Server / Network Fails > 15 Minutes`):**
   - **Step 1:** Activate the **Emergency Paper Downtime Kit** (pre-numbered Manual Accession Register, pre-printed Carbonless Duplicate Departmental Worksheets, and Manual Report Templates).
   - **Step 2:** Analyzers (`MEK-1503`, `Chem-400/200`, `Maglumi X3`) continue operating in **Standalone Local Memory Mode** (each stores $\ge 1,000\text{–}10,000$ results onboard) and print results on thermal/local printers.
   - **Step 3:** Urgent `STAT` and Critical Value results are verified directly from the analyzer printout by the `MD` Signatory and telephoned/issued on signed Emergency Manual Report Forms.
   - **Step 4 (`Post-Recovery Reconciliation`):** Once `LIMS` is restored, batch-retransmit stored analyzer results via `ASTM` interface (or double-verify any manual back-entry by two staff members) and log the downtime episode in `NABL-LIMS-LOG-01`.
