## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.3 / ISO 15190:2020 Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To define the architectural zoning, unidirectional workflow separation, access restriction, environmental monitoring (temperature, relative humidity, **Lux illumination intensity**, **air changes per hour [`ACH`]**, and **acoustic noise level [`dBA`]**), and emergency safety station verification (**Plumbed/Self-Contained ANSI Z358.1 Eye-Wash Stations, Safety Showers, Fire Extinguishers, and First-Aid Kits**) required under **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.3**, **ISO 15190:2020 (`Medical Laboratories — Requirements for Safety`)**, and **NABL 112**.
2. **Scope:** Applies to all physical zones of the diagnostic facility (`Reception/Waiting`, `Phlebotomy & Sample Collection Rooms`, `Sample Accessioning & Centrifugation`, `Hematology/Biochemistry/Immunoassay Core Lab`, `Serology/Microscopy`, `Microbiology/AFB/CBNAAT BSL-2+ Containment Suite`, `Clean Media Prep Room`, and `Biohazard Autoclave/BMW Storage Room`).

---

## Section 2: Unidirectional 4-Zone Architectural Segregation & Access Control (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.3.2 & 6.3.3`)
To prevent cross-contamination of patient specimens, protect patients/visitors from biohazards, and shield sensitive analytical activities (`Microscopy`, `Molecular CBNAAT`, `Clean Media Prep`) from vibration and aerosols, the laboratory is physically partitioned into **4 Access-Controlled Zones**:

| Facility Zone | Rooms / Sections Included | Access Restriction Level (`Signage & Lock`) | Unidirectional Airflow & Physical Separation Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Zone 1: Public & Pre-Examination Zone (`Clean`)** | Patient Reception, Waiting Area, Disabled-Friendly Patient Restroom (with Emergency Call Bell!), Adult/Pediatric Phlebotomy Cubicles, Dedicated Private Andrology (`Semen`) Collection Room | **Public / Escorted Patient Access** | Physically separated from analytical benches; ventilated at $22\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$; Phlebotomy couch & Syncope Kit within arm's reach (`SOP 0.2.6`). |
| **Zone 2: Administrative, Signatory & Clean Store Zone (`Clean`)** | Laboratory Director & Signatory Reporting Cabins, Server/UPS Room, Clean Reagent/Consumable Cold-Room, Clean Media Preparation Room (`Autoclave #1`), Staff Rest/Locker Area | **Staff ID Badge Access Only** (*Zero food/drink in technical zones; eating permitted ONLY in Staff Rest Room!*) | Positive air pressure relative to analytical benches; **Clean Media Autoclave #1** strictly segregated from infected waste (`SOP 6.1.11`). |
| **Zone 3: Core Analytical Zone (`BSL-2 Restricted`)** | Central Accessioning & Capped-Bucket Centrifugation, Hematology & Coagulation (`MEK-1503`), Clinical Biochemistry (`Chem-400/200` & `ISE`), Immunoassay (`Maglumi X3`), Serology (`ELISA/RPR`), Dark-Room `IIF` Microscopy, Clinical Pathology (`Urine/Stool/Semen/Body Fluids`) | **Biometric / RFID Authorized Lab Personnel Only** (`International Biohazard Symbol + Authorized Entry Only`) | Smooth, impervious, seamless epoxy/vinyl flooring with coved corners (`1% NaOCl` chemical-resistant); minimum **$1.2\text{–}1.5\text{ m}$ clear aisle width**; centrifuges placed $\ge 1.0\text{ m}$ away from analytical balances and hematology/coagulation optical analyzers to prevent vibration artifact! |
| **Zone 4: High-Containment Microbiology, `AFB` & Decontamination Zone (`BSL-2+ Negative Flow`)** | Bacteriology Culture & `AST` Room, Mycobacteriology (`AFB Smear` & `CBNAAT GeneXpert`) Room with **Class II Type A2 Biosafety Cabinet (`BSC`)**, Dedicated Washing & Biohazard Pre-Treatment **Autoclave #2**, Locked 4-Color `BMW` Storage Room | **Strict Restricted Access (`Microbiology Staff Only`; N95 Respirator + Gown Required)** | Self-closing interlocking door with vision panel; **Unidirectional negative airflow ($\ge 6\text{–}12\text{ ACH}$)** exhausting away from public areas; dedicated hand-free foot/sensor washbasin and **Emergency Eye-Wash Station** inside the suite. |

## Section 4: Biosafety Equipment, Emergency Eye-Wash Station, Fire Safety & Ergonomics (`ISO 15190:2020`)
1. **Emergency Eye-Wash Station & Drench Shower (`ANSI Z358.1` / `ISO 15190 Clause 10.4`):**
   - Installed within **$\le 10\text{ seconds}$ unobstructed travel distance ($\le 15\text{ meters}$)** of all chemical/acid/alkali, staining (`Carbol Fuchsin / Acid-Alcohol`), and biohazard specimen handling areas.
   - **Mandatory Weekly Flushing Protocol (`Every Monday Morning`):** Activate the hands-free push-paddle/foot-pedal eye-wash station for **$3\text{ full minutes}$** to flush stagnant water, rust, and *Acanthamoeba / Pseudomonas* biofilm from the supply line; verify dual aerated water streams rise evenly ($15\text{–}30\text{ cm}$) with tepid potable water.
2. **Fire Detection, `ABC / CO2` Extinguishers & Chemical Storage (`ISO 15190 Clause 8 & 11`):**
   - Ceiling smoke/heat detectors in all rooms; **`CO2` / Clean-Agent Extinguishers ($4.5\text{ kg}$)** beside analyzer/UPS panels (never use dry-powder or water on live electronics!) and **`ABC` Extinguishers** in corridors.
   - Flammable solvents (**Acetone-free Methanol for Leishman stain, Absolute Ethanol, Xylene, Acid-Alcohol**) are stored in a **Yellow Flame-Proof Metal Safety Cabinet** ($< 50\text{ L}$ total laboratory working stock, away from heat/autoclaves) with a **Material Safety Data Sheet (`MSDS / SDS`) Binder** accessible at the bench.
