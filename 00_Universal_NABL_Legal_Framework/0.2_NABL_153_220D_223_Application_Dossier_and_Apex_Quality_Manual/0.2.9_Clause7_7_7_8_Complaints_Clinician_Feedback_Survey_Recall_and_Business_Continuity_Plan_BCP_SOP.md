## Section 1: Purpose, Scope & ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 7.5, 7.7, 7.8 & 8.6 Mandate
1. **Purpose:** To establish standardized procedures for:
   - **Patient & Referring Clinician Feedback Surveys (`Clause 8.6.2`)** with quantitative Net Promoter Score (`NPS`) / Customer Satisfaction (`CSAT %`) analysis.
   - **Impartial Complaint Receipt, Root-Cause Investigation & $\le 48\text{-Hour}$ Resolution (`Clause 7.7`)**.
   - **Nonconforming Work (`NCW`) Halt, Clinical Risk Grading & Erroneous Released Report Recall (`Clause 7.5`)**.
   - **Mandatory `Clause 7.8` Continuity and Emergency Preparedness Planning (`Business Continuity Plan — BCP`)**, ensuring zero interruption to life-critical diagnostic services during analyzer breakdowns, power/UPS/HVAC failures, reagent shortages, natural disasters, or pandemic surges.

---

## Section 2: Patient & Referring Clinician Satisfaction Survey Protocol (`Clause 8.6.2`)
1. **Ongoing Patient Feedback (`Reception Drop-Box & WhatsApp/QR Digital Survey` — Reviewed Monthly):**
   - Evaluates 5 parameters on a **`1 (Poor)` to `5 (Excellent)` Likert Scale**: (1) Reception courtesy & billing transparency, (2) Waiting time ($< 15\text{ min}$ target), (3) Phlebotomist skill & painless single-prick collection, (4) Cleanliness & washroom hygiene, and (5) Timely report delivery (`TAT`).
2. **Semi-Annual / Annual Referring Clinician & Hospital Ward Survey (`Minimum 25–50 Clinicians`):**
   - Evaluates 6 clinical parameters (`1 to 5 Scale`): (1) Analytical reliability & clinical correlation of results, (2) Adequacy of test menu & 5D reference intervals, (3) Clarity of pathologist auto-comments & reflex guidance, (4) Speed of `STAT` turnaround & $\le 30\text{-minute}$ Critical Value telephone calls, (5) Accessibility of Pathologist/Biochemist/Microbiologist for clinical consultation, and (6) Overall satisfaction.
   - **Mandatory Action Threshold:** Any score **$\le 3/5$** on any single survey item triggers a logged **Preventive/Corrective Action (`CAPA` in `NABL-LOG-12`)** within $7\text{ days}$, and the aggregated `CSAT %` ($\text{Target} \ge 92\%$) is reviewed in the **Annual Management Review Meeting (`SOP 0.2.12`)**.

## Section 3: Impartial 48-Hour Complaint Handling & Erroneous Report Recall (`Clauses 7.7 & 7.5`)
1. **Complaint Handling Workflow (`Clause 7.7.1–7.7.3`):**
   - **Step 1 (`Receipt & Acknowledgment <= 2 Hours`):** Log every verbal, telephone, email, WhatsApp, or clinician complaint in `NABL-LOG-13` (`SOP 0.1.15`) and issue a courteous acknowledgment to the complainant.
   - **Step 2 (`Independent Investigation — Zero Self-Review`):** Per `ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.7.3 b`, investigation and resolution MUST be conducted by the **Quality Manager / Laboratory Director (or a signatory not involved in the original laboratory activity)** to guarantee impartiality.
   - **Step 3 (`Technical Re-Check & Resolution <= 48 Hours`):** Retrieve the retained specimen from the $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ / $-20^\circ\text{C}$ archive, audit `IQC` and pre-analytical `HIL`/tube integrity, re-test in duplicate on Primary and Backup analyzers (at zero cost to the patient!), and communicate the transparent outcome in writing within **$\le 48\text{ hours}$**.
2. **Nonconforming Work (`NCW`) Halt & Erroneous Released Report Recall (`Clause 7.5`):**
   - Whenever an analytical error is discovered *after* patient results have been released (e.g., delayed discovery of calibration drift, reagent deterioration, `EQAS |Z| >= 3.0`, or sample mix-up):
     1. **Immediate Halt:** Stop testing on the affected assay/analyzer immediately.
     2. **Look-Back Identification:** Identify all patient specimens analyzed since the last known valid `IQC` / calibration event.
     3. **Immediate Recall & Clinician Notification (`<= 30 Minutes` if Clinically Significant):** Telephone the ordering clinician and patient immediately to **recall the erroneous report**, re-analyze the retained specimens on a verified backup system/reagent lot, and issue a formal **`[AMENDED REPORT — SUPERSEDES REPORT DATED ...]`** (`SOP 0.1.3` & `NABL-LOG-14`).

## Section 4: Mandatory `Clause 7.8` Business Continuity & Emergency Preparedness Plan (`BCP`)
Per **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.8**, the laboratory maintains, tests annually, and executes the following **6-Scenario Continuity & Emergency Response Matrix**:

| Emergency / Disruption Scenario | Immediate Primary Failover (`0 – 2 Hours`) | Extended Continuity Fallback (`> 2 – 48 Hours`) | Recovery Verification Gate Before Resuming Normal Operations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Primary Biochemistry Analyzer Breakdown (`Bonavera Chem-400`)** | Immediately switch all routine & `STAT` chemistry tubes to the **In-House Backup Analyzer (`Bonavera Chem-200` + Standalone `ISE`)** (`Weekly comparability pre-verified per SOP 2.1.12`). | If both `Chem-400` and `Chem-200` experience simultaneous failure, dispatch samples at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ to our **Tie-Up Peer NABL-Accredited Backup Laboratory (`MOU` in `SOP 0.2.5`)** after notifying clinicians/patients (`#` flagged). | Biomedical engineer repair report + Calibration + **2-Level `IQC` (`PASS`)** + **5-Sample Split Comparability** against `Chem-200`. |
| **2. Primary Hematology Analyzer Breakdown (`Nihon Kohden MEK-1503`)** | Switch `CBC` to Backup Hematology Analyzer / Manual Validated Methods (**Spun Micro-Hematocrit `PCV`, Cyanmethemoglobin/SLS `Hb`, Neubauer Chamber `WBC/PLT`, Manual 200-Cell Leishman `PBS/DLC`, and Manual `200 mm` Westergren `ESR`** per `SOP 1.4`). | Route high-volume non-urgent `CBC` tubes to **Tie-Up Peer NABL-Accredited Hematology Lab (`MOU`)** if breakdown exceeds $> 6\text{ hours}$. | Background check (`Hb <= 0.1, WBC <= 0.2, PLT <= 5`) + **3-Level `CBC/ESR IQC` (`PASS`)** (`SOP 1.1.15`). |
| **3. Primary `CLIA` Immunoassay Analyzer Breakdown (`Snibe Maglumi X3`)** | Hold non-urgent routine hormones/vitamins at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ (or $-20^\circ\text{C}$); immediately route **`STAT` Cardiac (`hs-cTnI, NT-proBNP`), Sepsis (`PCT`), and Emergency `β-hCG`** to **Tie-Up Peer NABL-Accredited `CLIA` Lab (`<= 2h` `STAT` SLA)**. | Engineer on-site within $\le 4\text{–}12\text{h}$ (`AMC/CMC SLA`); transfer batch routine serum aliquots at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ to Peer NABL Lab if downtime $> 12\text{h}$. | `PMT` Dark/Light Check + `Starter 1+2` Prime + `RFID` 2-Point Calibration + **Multi-Level `IQC` (`PASS`)** (`SOP 3.1.12`). |
| **4. Grid Power Failure / `UPS` or Diesel Generator (`DG`) Fault** | **Online Sine-Wave `UPS` (`2–4 Hour` Battery Bank)** maintains uninterrupted power to all analyzers and `LIMS` with zero reboot; automatic `DG` changeover in $< 15\text{ seconds}$. | Keep all $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ refrigerators and $-20^\circ\text{C}$ freezers **strictly closed** (maintains temperature for $4\text{–}6\text{h}$); if outage $> 4\text{h}$, transfer reagents/kits into pre-conditioned **`2–8°C` Insulated Vaccine Chests** or backup clinic fridge. | Verify all fridge/freezer Min/Max temperatures (`NABL-LOG-07`) and run 2-level `IQC` across all analyzers after power stabilization. |
| **5. Type-I `CLRW` Water Purification Plant Breakdown (`Conductivity > 2.0 uS/cm`)** | Switch immediately to **Emergency Stock of Sealed Commercial Type-I / HPLC-Grade Sterile Deionized Water Carboys (`2 x 20 Liters` maintained in store)** (`SOP 0.1.13 & 2.1.12`). | Replace RO/Deionization resin cartridges within $24\text{ hours}$. | Verify post-service water conductivity **$\le 1.0\ \mu\text{S/cm}$** and run Cuvette Cell Blank + Calcium/Iron/ALP `IQC`. |
| **6. LIMS / Internet Outage, Fire/Chemical Spill, or Epidemic Surge** | Activate **Manual Paper Downtime Kit (`SOP 0.2.8 Sec 4`)** for LIMS outage; execute **`ISO 15190` Evacuation / `1% NaOCl` Spill Containment (`SOP 0.1.14`)** for safety events; activate cross-trained backup staff shift for dengue/fever surge. | Restore LIMS from **3-2-1 Cloud/NAS Backup (`SOP 0.2.8`)**. | Complete post-event reconciliation and log in `NABL-BCP-LOG-01`. |
