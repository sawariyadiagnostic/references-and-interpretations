## Section 1: Laboratory Profile, Scope of Services & Top-Management Quality Policy Statement (`ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 5.1–5.5`)
1. **Legal Entity & Clinical Mission:**
   - The Laboratory operates as a legally registered, independent multi-disciplinary medical diagnostic center committed to providing **clinically accurate, metrologically traceable, timely, and patient-safe diagnostic information** across six core laboratory medicine disciplines: **(01) Hematology, Immunohematology & Coagulation**, **(02) Clinical Biochemistry & Electrolyte/Blood-Gas Analysis**, **(03) Flash Chemiluminescence (`CLIA`) Immunoassay, Endocrinology, Vitamins, Acute Cardiac/Sepsis & Oncology Biomarkers**, **(04) Infectious Disease Serology, Viral Markers (`HIV/HBV/HCV/Syphilis/TORCH`) & Autoimmune Immunology (`ANA HEp-2 IIF / ENA / ANCA`)**, **(05) Clinical Pathology, Urinalysis, `WHO 6th Edition` Andrology & Serous/`CSF` Body Fluid Cytology**, and **(06) Medical Microbiology, Aerobic/Automated Culture & `CLSI M100` Susceptibility, Mycobacteriology (`AFB / CBNAAT`) & Mycology**.
2. **Signed Quality Policy Statement (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 5.5` & `Clause 8.2`):**
   > *"We at the Laboratory are committed to good professional practice, total impartiality, absolute patient data confidentiality, and the highest standard of analytical and clinical excellence in compliance with **ISO 15189:2022**, **NABL 112**, **NABL 131/133/163**, and statutory Indian healthcare regulations (**Clinical Establishments Act, Consumer Protection Act 2019, HIV & AIDS Act 2017, PC & PNDT Act 1994, DPDP Act 2023, and Bio-Medical Waste Rules 2016**). We ensure that every examination is performed using verified methods, higher-order metrological traceability, Six-Sigma Internal Quality Control (`IQC`), accredited Proficiency Testing (`PT/EQAS`), and risk-based patient safety shields (`ISO 22367:2020`), with continual improvement driven by measurable Quality Indicators (`QIs`)."*
   - **Signed:** `Laboratory Director (MD Pathology)` & `Quality Manager` | **Date:** `15-Oct-2026`.

## Section 2: Clause-by-Clause Level-1 Policy Declarations (`ISO 15189:2022 Clauses 4.1 through 8.9 + Annex A`)

### 2.1 Clause 4: General Requirements (`4.1 Impartiality`, `4.2 Confidentiality`, `4.3 Requirements Regarding Patients`)
- **4.1 Impartiality Policy:** Laboratory activities are structured and managed to safeguard total clinical and analytical impartiality. Commercial, financial, referral, or administrative pressures are strictly prohibited from influencing test selection, analytical execution, reference interval flagging, or diagnostic interpretation. The laboratory prohibits fee-splitting, kickbacks, or volume-linked inducements. All personnel sign an annual **Impartiality & Conflict-of-Interest Declaration (`SOP 0.2.2`)**, and impartiality risks are tracked in the **Master FMEA Risk Register (`SOP 0.1.10`)**.
- **4.2 Confidentiality Policy:** All patient clinical, demographic, genetic, and test data are legally protected confidential medical records (`DPDP Act 2023`, `IT Act 2000`). Access to the `LIMS` is restricted by individual role-based credentials (`SOP 0.2.8`). Reports are released solely to the patient or authorized treating clinician. Statutory confidentiality shields govern **HIV testing (`HIV & AIDS Act 2017` — `SOP 4.2.2` & `NABL-SER-LOG-04`)** and **Prenatal screening (`PC & PNDT Act 1994` — `SOP 3.5.6` & `NABL-IMM-LOG-08`)**.
- **4.3 Requirements Regarding Patients Policy:** The well-being, safety, dignity, and rights of every patient are the paramount consideration (`SOP 0.2.2`). The laboratory provides clear pre-examination instructions (`SOP 0.2.6`), obtains documented informed consent for invasive/specialty procedures, enforces a **Pediatric/Neonatal Minimum Blood-Volume Collection Policy** to prevent iatrogenic anemia, and maintains an active **Vasovagal Syncope & Phlebotomy First-Aid Response Protocol (`SOP 0.2.6`)**.

### 2.2 Clause 5: Structural and Governance Requirements (`5.1 Legal Entity` to `5.6 Risk Management`)
- **5.1–5.4 Legal Entity, Laboratory Director & Authority:** The laboratory holds valid statutory registrations (`SOP 0.2.3`) and is directed by a full-time, `NMC`-registered **Laboratory Director (`MD Pathology`)** supported by specialty Authorized Signatories (`MD Biochemistry`, `MD Microbiology`) and a designated **Quality Manager** who has independent authority to halt non-conforming analytical runs (`SOP 0.1.12` & `SOP 0.2.3`).
- **5.5–5.6 Quality Objectives, Quality Indicators (`QIs`) & Risk Management (`ISO 22367`):** The laboratory monitors **24 quantitative Pre-Analytical, Intra-Analytical, and Post-Analytical Quality Indicators monthly (`SOP 0.2.3`)** and maintains a living **Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (`FMEA`) Risk Register (`SOP 0.1.10`)** across all 273 tests.

### 2.3 Clause 6: Resource Requirements (`6.1–6.8`: Personnel, Facilities, Equipment, Traceability, Reagents & Referral Labs)
- **6.1–6.2 Personnel:** All staff meet `NABL 112` qualification criteria, undergo onboarding, Ishihara color-vision screening, `HBV` immunization (`Anti-HBs >= 10 mIU/mL`), and semi-annual/annual **6-Point Six-Sigma Competency Assessment (`SOP 0.1.12`)**.
- **6.3 Facilities & Biosafety (`ISO 15190:2020`):** The facility maintains unidirectional clean-to-dirty zoning, biometric access restriction, controlled temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) and humidity ($30\text{–}60\%$), verified bench illumination ($\ge 500\text{–}1,000\text{ Lux}$), weekly emergency eye-wash flushing (`SOP 0.2.4`), and `BMW Rules 2016` compliance (`SOP 0.1.14`).
- **6.4–6.5 Equipment & Metrological Traceability (`ISO 17511`):** Primary and backup analyzers (`Nihon Kohden MEK-1503`, `Bonavera Chem-400 & Chem-200`, `Snibe Maglumi X3`, Coagulometer, `ISE`, `ELISA`, Microscopes, Autoclaves, `Class II A2 BSC`) undergo `IQ/OQ/PQ`, annual `NPL/NABL`-traceable calibration (`SOP 0.1.13`), daily startup/PM checks (`SOPs 1.1.15, 1.2.10, 1.3.10, 2.1.12, 2.3.14, 3.1.12, 4.1.11, 5.1.7, 6.1.11`), and higher-order reference traceability (`NIST SRM / WHO NIBSC / IFCC / ATCC`).
- **6.6–6.8 Reagents, Service Agreements & Externally Provided Services:** Every new reagent/kit lot undergoes pre-use lot verification (`CLSI EP26-A` / `5 Pos + 5 Neg` `SOP 0.2.5 & 4.1.11`) before moving from Quarantine (`Red Sticker`) to Active Use (`Green Sticker`) under `FEFO` control. Referral tests are sent **exclusively to NABL `ISO 15189:2022` accredited referral laboratories** under signed `MOUs` (`SOP 0.2.5`) and flagged per `NABL 133` (`SOP 0.1.3 & 0.2.11`).

### 2.4 Clause 7: Process Requirements (`7.1–7.8`: Pre-Examination, Examination, Validity [`IQC/EQAS`], Post-Examination, `LIMS` & `BCP`)
- **7.1–7.2 Pre-Examination & Phlebotomy:** Governed by the **Primary Sample Collection Manual (`SOP 0.2.6`)** and **`CLSI GP41` Order-of-Draw / Quantitative `HIL` Rejection Matrix (`SOP 0.1.2`)**.
- **7.3 Examination Processes, Method Verification (`EP15`), `MU %` (`ISO/TS 20914`) & Validity (`IQC / NABL 163 EQAS`):** Every examination follows its 10-section standalone SOP (`Folders 01–06`), verified prior to go-live per `CLSI EP15/EP06/EP28` (`SOP 0.1.8`), monitored daily by Westgard Sigma `IQC` (`SOP 0.1.6`), and validated externally through the **4-Year `NABL 163` `PT/EQAS` & Tri-Partite `ILC` Program (`SOP 0.2.7`)**.
- **7.4–7.8 Post-Examination, `LIMS` (`Cl. 7.6`), Complaints (`Cl. 7.7`) & Business Continuity (`Cl. 7.8`):** Critical values are communicated with documented read-back within **$\le 30\text{ minutes}$ (`SOP 0.1.5`)**; `LIMS` interfaces and equations are validated per `CLSI AUTO08/AUTO15` with daily encrypted backups (`SOP 0.2.8`); complaints are resolved within $48\text{ hours}$ (`SOP 0.2.9`); and emergency operational continuity is guaranteed by the **Clause 7.8 Business Continuity Plan (`SOP 0.2.9`)** and **Normative Annex A `POCT` Governance (`SOP 0.2.10`)**.
