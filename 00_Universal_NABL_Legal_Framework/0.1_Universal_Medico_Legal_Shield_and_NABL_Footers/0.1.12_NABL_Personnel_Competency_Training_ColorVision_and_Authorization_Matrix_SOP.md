## 🧪 Section 2: Pre-Employment Medical Screening, **Ishihara Color-Vision Test** & Hepatitis-B Immunization (`NABL 112` & `ISO 15190`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Classic NABL Assessor Checkpoint:** During every NABL On-Site Assessment, the Lead Assessor inspects the **Personnel Files (`Clause 6.2.5`)** of bench technicians and pathologists for two mandatory medical records: **(1) Ishihara Color-Blindness Screening Certificate** (essential for reading Leishman peripheral smears, Ziehl-Neelsen AFB slides, Gram stains, urine dipsticks, and visual hemolysis/icterus indices) and **(2) Hepatitis-B Vaccination & Anti-HBs Titer Record ($> 10\text{ mIU/mL}$)**!

| Personnel Health & Pre-Onboarding Gate | Mandatory Protocol & Acceptance Criterion | Action if Deficient / Non-Compliant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Ishihara 38-Plate Color-Vision Screening** | Performed at onboarding for all Pathologists, Microbiologists, Technologists, and Phlebotomists by an Ophthalmologist / Medical Officer. | Any staff with red-green color deficiency is **restricted from independent visual colorimetry, dipstick reading, ZN/Gram microscopy, and visual `HIL` grading** (assigned only to automated digital readout workflows with dual sign-off). |
| **2. Hepatitis-B Surface Antibody (`Anti-HBs`) & Immunization (`0, 1, 6 Months`)** | Mandatory 3-dose Recombinant Hep-B vaccination at onboarding + quantitative `Anti-HBs` titer check ($4\text{–}8\text{ weeks}$ post-3rd dose) on `Snibe MAGLUMI X3`. Protective threshold: **$\ge 10\text{ mIU/mL}$**. | Non-responders ($< 10\text{ mIU/mL}$) receive a repeat 3-dose booster series and counseling on post-exposure HBIG prophylaxis (`SOP 0.1.13`). |
| **3. Primary Qualification & Degree Verification (`NABL 112`)** | Verify original degree/diploma certificates, State Paramedical Council / NMC registration, and prior experience certificates. | Unverified personnel are strictly barred from handling patient specimens or LIMS sign-out. |

## 📊 Section 4: The 6-Point Direct Competency Assessment Protocol (`ISO 15189 Clause 6.2.2` & `CLSI QMS03`)

> [!CAUTION]
> **Frequency Mandate (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.2.2`):** Every technical staff member and phlebotomist MUST undergo formal documented Competency Assessment:
> 1. **Prior to independent work** after initial onboarding training;
> 2. **Every 6 Months during the first year** of employment; and
> 3. **Annually (every 12 months) thereafter** (or immediately if an `EQAS` failure or `CAPA` traces to bench execution).

Each staff member is evaluated across all **6 International Competency Methods (`CLSI QMS03-Ed4`)**:

| Competency Method | Assessment Mechanism in Our Laboratory | Minimum Passing Score / Target |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Direct Observation of Routine Bench Work** | Assessor watches the technician perform `MEK-1503` / `Chem 400` / `Maglumi X3` startup, pipette calibration check, Leishman staining, or `CLSI GP41` venipuncture using a 10-point checklist. | **$100\%$ adherence** to critical safety & SOP steps |
| **2. Monitoring of Recording & Reporting (`ALCOA+`)** | Audit of 10 recent entries made by the technician in the `IQC Log`, `Critical Value Register`, and `Equipment PM Log`. | **$100\%$ legibility & accuracy** (zero uninitialed alterations) |
| **3. Review of Work Records (`IQC` & `L-J` Troubleshooting)** | Present the technician with 3 simulated Westgard violations ($1_{3s}$, $2_{2s}$, $R_{4s}$) and `HIL` flags (`H-Index = 120 mg/dL` on $\text{K}^+$) to verify correct troubleshooting action. | **$\ge 90\%$ score** |
| **4. Blinded Unknown Sample / Split-Sample Testing** | Technician analyzes **3 blinded previously-characterized patient samples or EQAS aliquots** across their assigned bench (`CBC`, `Biochemistry`, `Immunoassay`, or `Serology`). | Quantitative results within **$\pm 0.5 \times \text{TE}_a\%$**; Qualitative **$100\%$ concordant** |
| **5. Inter-Observer Microscopic Morphology Concordance** | **5 blinded stained slides** (`Peripheral Blood Smear` differential/morphology, `Urine Sediment`, `Gram Stain`, `ZN AFB Smear`) read independently by the Technologist/Pathologist against the Reference Consensus. | Cell counts within $95\%$ CI; Diagnostic flags **$100\%$ concordant** |
| **6. Written / Oral Technical & Problem-Solving Quiz** | 20-question technical examination covering pre-analytical interferences, critical values, biosafety spill response, and analyzer flags. | **$\ge 80\%$ ($16/20$)** (If $< 80\%$, mandatory re-training & re-test within $15\text{ days}$) |

## 🧬 Section 5: Continuing Professional Education (`CME / CNE` — Minimum 12 Trainings / Year)

To satisfy **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.2.4**, the Quality Manager maintains an **Annual Training Calendar** delivering at least **1 structured training session per month**:
- **Q1 (Jan–Mar):** `ISO 15189:2022` QMS Overview; `CLSI GP41` Order-of-Draw & Pre-Analytical `HIL` Rejection; Biosafety, Needle-Stick PEP & `BMWM 2016` Waste Segregation.
- **Q2 (Apr–Jun):** `Nihon Kohden MEK-1503` Histogram/Scattergram Flags & EDTA Pseudothrombocytopenia; `Bonavera Chem 400/200` Calibration & NADH Depletion; `Snibe MAGLUMI X3` Flash-CLIA & Biotin/Hook Interference.
- **Q3 (Jul–Sep):** Westgard Six Sigma Rules & `EQAS` Root-Cause Analysis; Measurement Uncertainty (`MU`) & `RCV %` Delta Checks; `CLSI GP47` 30-Minute Critical Value Read-Back Drill.
- **Q4 (Oct–Dec):** Urinalysis & Body Fluid Microscopy; Microbiology Gram/AFB & `CLSI M100` AST; `CPA 2019` Medico-Legal Documentation & 48-Hour Complaint De-Escalation.

## 🚨 Section 6: Mandatory Contents of Individual NABL Personnel Files (`ISO 15189 Clause 6.2.5`)

Every staff member has a dedicated **Indexed Personnel Dossier (`FILE-HR-XX`)** containing 10 mandatory tabs:
1. Appointment Letter, Confidentiality & Impartiality Undertaking (`Cl. 4.1 & 4.2`), and Specimen Signature / Initials Sheet;
2. Job Description (`JD`) detailing exact responsibilities, authorities, and deputization backups;
3. Verified Copies of Educational Degrees/Diplomas & State Council / NMC Registration Certificates;
4. **Ishihara Color-Vision Test Certificate** & **Hepatitis-B Vaccination + `Anti-HBs` Titer Report**;
5. Onboarding Induction Checklist (4-week supervised bench rotation);
6. **Certificate of Competence & Functional Authorization Matrix** signed by the Laboratory Director;
7. Six-Monthly / Annual **6-Point Competency Assessment Records** (including blinded sample & slide concordance sheets);
8. Monthly Continuing Education (`CME`) Attendance & Quiz Score Sheets;
9. Record of any Needle-Stick / Exposure Incidents or Bench Error Counseling (`Blame-Free Just Culture`);
10. Annual Performance Appraisal & Training Needs Identification (`TNI`).

## 🔬 Section 7: Deputization & Absence Backup Matrix (`ISO 15189 Clause 5.4`)

To ensure uninterrupted NABL compliance during leave or emergencies:
- **Laboratory Director / Consultant Pathologist Backup:** Locum / Associate NABL-qualified **MD Pathologist** registered on the NABL Scope of Authorized Signatories (`NABL 112`). *(Note: If the sole Authorized Signatory for a discipline is unavailable and no NABL-approved backup signatory is present, reports for that discipline cannot bear the NABL symbol per `NABL 133`).*
- **Quality Manager Backup:** Deputy Quality Manager (Senior Technical Supervisor trained in `ISO 15189:2022`).
- **Bench Technologist Cross-Training:** Every technologist is cross-trained and competency-certified on at least **two departments** (e.g., Hematology + Clinical Pathology; Biochemistry + Immunoassay).
