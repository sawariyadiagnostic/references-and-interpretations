## ⚙️ Section 3: Hardware-Agnostic Analytical SOP for Interference Detection & Neutralization

When an immunoassay or immunoturbidimetric result is clinically discordant (e.g., $\text{TSH}$ and $\text{FT4}$ both elevated, or $\beta\text{-hCG}$ unexpectedly low in a molar pregnancy), execute this **4-Step Reference Laboratory Confirmatory SOP**:

1. **Step 1 — Serial Dilution Linearity Check ($1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:100$) for Hook Effect & Heterophile Antibodies:**
   - Dilute patient serum with manufacturer zero-calibrator/diluent and re-assay on `Snibe MAGLUMI X3` or `Bonavera Chem 400`.
   - **High-Dose Hook Effect Present:** The $1:10$ or $1:100$ diluted sample yields a **paradoxically much higher** raw signal and final multiplied concentration than the neat (undiluted) sample!
   - **Heterophile / HAMA Interference Present:** Serial dilutions show **non-linear recovery** ($> \pm 20\%$ deviation from expected proportionality).
2. **Step 2 — Polyethylene Glycol (`PEG 6000`) Precipitation SOP for Macro-Analytes (`Macro-Prolactin`, `Macro-AST`, `Macro-Amylase`, `Macro-TSH`):**
   - Mix **$200\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ Patient Serum** + **$200\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ freshly prepared $25\%\text{ (w/v) PEG 6000}$** in phosphate-buffered saline ($\text{pH } 7.4$).
   - Vortex for $10\text{ seconds}$, incubate at room temperature ($20\text{–}25^\circ\text{C}$) for $10\text{ minutes}$, and centrifuge at $1,500\text{–}3,000 \times g$ for $15\text{ minutes}$.
   - Assay the clear supernatant and multiply result by **$2$** (dilution factor):
     $$\text{PEG Recovery (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Supernatant Concentration} \times 2}{\text{Unprecipitated Neat Concentration}}\right) \times 100$$
   - **Interpretation:** Recovery **$< 40\%$** confirms predominance of biologically inactive high-molecular-weight IgG-complexed **Macro-Analyte (e.g., Macroprolactinemia)**; Recovery **$> 60\%$** confirms true monomeric hyperprolactinemia!
3. **Step 3 — Heterophile Blocking Tube (`HBT`) / Alternate Platform Check:**
   - Incubate serum in a commercial Heterophile Blocking Tube for $1\text{ hour}$ or re-test on an alternate methodology utilizing a different species antibody clone (e.g., chimeric/goat/rabbit vs murine).
