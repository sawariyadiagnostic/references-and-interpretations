## 🧪 Section 2: Pre-Analytical Reference Individual Selection Criteria (`CLSI EP28-A3c`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why Reference Interval Verification Fails When Pre-Analytics Are Ignored:** Under **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.3.5** and **CLSI EP28-A3c**, a laboratory cannot simply copy manufacturer kit-insert normal ranges without verifying that they fit the local population (**Haryana / North India**). However, if the $20$ healthy reference volunteers are not screened for occult iron/$\text{B}_{12}$ deficiency, subclinical hypothyroidism, fatty liver, or fasting status, false "outliers" will invalidate the verification!

| Verification Cohort | Mandatory Inclusion Criteria ($n=20$ per Partition) | Mandatory Exclusion Criteria (`CLSI EP28-A3c` Questionnaire) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Healthy Adult Males & Non-Pregnant Females ($18\text{–}60\text{y}$)** | Ambulatory, normotensive ($\text{BP} < 130/85$), $\text{BMI } 18.5\text{–}24.9\text{ kg/m}^2$, $10\text{–}12\text{h}$ overnight fast, seated posture for $15\text{ min}$ prior to morning draw ($7\text{–}10\text{ AM}$). | Acute infection/fever within $14\text{ days}$, diabetes, thyroid disease, anemia/thalassemia trait, alcohol $> 14\text{ units/wk}$, tobacco smoking, oral contraceptives/steroids/biotin, blood donation within $90\text{ days}$. |
| **Pediatric (`CALIPER`) & Geriatric Partitions** | Direct transference from validated **CALIPER / WHO / IFCC** reference studies using identical analytical traceability (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.3.5`), supplemented by indirect LIMS outpatient verification (`refineR`). | Hospitalized/ICU patients or febrile emergency pediatric draws. |
| **Maternity (1st, 2nd, 3rd Trimester)** | Singleton uncomplicated gestation with USG-confirmed gestational age, normal OGTT, normotensive, receiving routine antenatal care. | Gestational diabetes (GDM), pre-eclampsia, twin gestation, or active bleeding. |

## ⚙️ Section 3: Step-by-Step Mathematical SOP for Method Verification (`CLSI EP15-A3`, `EP06-Ed2`, `EP28-A3c`)

Before any new test, analyzer (`MEK-1503`, `Chem 400/200`, `Maglumi X3`), or reagent brand is placed into clinical service, the Quality Manager & Pathologist execute this **4-Pillar Pre-Go-Live Verification Protocol**:

### 3.1 Pillar 1 — `CLSI EP15-A3` $5 \times 5$ Precision & Trueness (Bias) Verification
1. **Experimental Design:** Select **2 or 3 concentration levels** of certified third-party QC / reference material (Level-1 Low/Normal, Level-2 High/Pathological). Assay **5 replicates per day for 5 operating days** ($N = 25$ total measurements per level).
2. **One-Way ANOVA Calculations:**
   - **Within-Run Repeatability ($s_r$):**
     $$s_r = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{d=1}^{5} \sum_{i=1}^{5} (x_{di} - \bar{x}_d)^2}{5 \times (5 - 1)}}$$
   - **Between-Run Variance ($s_b^2$):**
     $$s_b^2 = \max\left(0, \frac{\frac{\sum_{d=1}^{5} (\bar{x}_d - \bar{\bar{x}})^2}{5 - 1} - \frac{s_r^2}{5}}{1}\right)$$
   - **Within-Laboratory Intermediate Precision ($s_{wl}$ and $CV_{wl}\%$):**
     $$s_{wl} = \sqrt{s_r^2 + s_b^2} \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad CV_{wl}\% = \left(\frac{s_{wl}}{\bar{\bar{x}}}\right) \times 100$$
3. **Acceptance Criteria:**
   - $CV_r\% \le \text{Manufacturer Claimed Repeatability CV}$ (or Upper Verification Limit $\text{UVL}$);
   - $CV_{wl}\% \le \text{Desirable } CV_A\%$ ($0.5 \times CV_I$) and $\text{Bias}\% = \left|\frac{\bar{\bar{x}} - \text{Target}}{\text{Target}}\right| \times 100 \le \text{Desirable } B\%$.

### 3.2 Pillar 2 — `CLSI EP06-Ed2` Linearity (`AMR`) & Clinical Reportable Range (`CRR` Auto-Dilution) Verification
1. **Analytical Measurement Range (`AMR`):** The range of analyte values that the analyzer can measure **directly without any dilution or concentration** (`LoQ` to Upper Limit of Linearity `ULOL`).
   - *Protocol:* Prepare **5 to 7 equidistant admixtures** ($0\%, 20\%, 40\%, 60\%, 80\%, 100\%$) of a High Pool (near `ULOL`) and Low Pool (near `LoQ`), run in duplicate/triplicate, and verify linear regression slope ($0.95\text{–}1.05$), $R^2 \ge 0.990$, and deviation from linearity $\le 0.5 \times \text{TE}_a\%$.
   - **(2) Clinical Reportable Range (`CRR`):** The extended upper range achieved via **validated automated or manual dilution** ($1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:100$) using manufacturer-approved diluent ($0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$ or zero-calibrator).

### 3.3 Pillar 3 — `CLSI EP28-A3c` Binomial $n=20$ Reference Interval Verification
1. Collect specimens from **$n = 20$ qualified healthy local reference individuals** (Haryana adult cohort).
2. Analyze under routine conditions and count how many of the $20$ results fall outside the candidate reference interval ($2.5\text{th to }97.5\text{th}$ percentile):
   - **$\le 2$ out of $20$ results ($\le 10\%$) outside the interval:** **PASS!** The candidate reference interval is statistically verified at the $95\%$ confidence level and adopted into LIMS.
   - **$3$ or $4$ out of $20$ results outside:** Collect a **second set of $n = 20$ healthy reference subjects**. If $\le 2$ of the second $20$ fail (or $\le 4$ out of combined $40$), **PASS**.
   - **$\ge 5$ out of $20$ (or $> 4$ out of $40$) outside:** **FAIL!** The manufacturer's reference interval does not match the local population; establish a local reference interval ($n \ge 120$ non-parametric per `CLSI EP28-A3c`) or adopt validated ICMR Indian Reference Intervals.

## 🧬 Section 5: Qualitative & Semi-Quantitative Method Verification (`CLSI EP12-A2` — $2 \times 2$ Concordance)

For qualitative serology (`HIV`, `HBsAg`, `HCV`, `Syphilis`, `Dengue NS1/IgM`, `Widal/TyphiDot`, `Malaria RDT`) and microscopy:
1. Test a panel of **minimum 20 known Positive** (including weak/borderline positives near the cutoff index `COI = 1.0`) and **20 known Negative** specimens (sourced from EQAS archives, reference lab split samples, or commercial verification panels).
2. Calculate **Positive Percent Agreement (`PPA` / Diagnostic Sensitivity)** and **Negative Percent Agreement (`NPA` / Diagnostic Specificity)**:
   $$\text{PPA (\%)} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN} \times 100 \ge 98\% \qquad \text{NPA (\%)} = \frac{TN}{TN + FP} \times 100 \ge 98\%$$
3. Verify the **Cutoff Repeatability Zone ($C_5\text{ to }C_{95}$ Gray Zone):** Any patient sample yielding a signal-to-cutoff ratio (`S/CO` or `COI`) between **$0.85\text{ and }1.15$** must be centrifuged at $10,000 \times g$ for $10\text{ min}$, re-tested in duplicate, and reported as **`Borderline / Indeterminate — Advise Confirmatory Testing`**.

## 🚨 Section 6: Critical Substrate Depletion & Carryover Safeguards (`Bonavera Chem 400 / 200`)

> [!CAUTION]
> **The NADH Substrate Depletion Trap in Acute Viral Hepatitis (`ALT / AST > 2,000 U/L`):** In UV kinetic enzymatic assays (`ALT`, `AST`, `LDH`), if patient enzyme activity is massive ($> 2,500\text{ U/L}$), all $\text{NADH}$ in the cuvette is consumed during the $60\text{-second}$ pre-incubation lag phase *before* the photometer starts taking kinetic readings! Unchecked, a $4,000\text{ U/L}$ `ALT` can falsely report as **"$12\text{ U/L}$ (Normal)"**!
- **Mandatory Analyzer Safeguard:** Enable **Initial Absorbance (`A0`) Depletion Flagging** on `Bonavera Chem 400` and `Chem 200` (if initial $340\text{ nm}$ absorbance $< 0.800\text{ Abs}$, auto-trigger immediate **$1:10$ dilution rerun**).

## 🔬 Section 7: Lot-to-Lot Reagent Crossover & Carryover Verification Kinetics

1. **Sample-to-Sample Carryover Check (`CLSI EP10`):**
   - Run **3 consecutive aliquots of a High Sample ($H_1, H_2, H_3$)** followed immediately by **3 consecutive aliquots of a Low Sample ($L_1, L_2, L_3$)** (especially critical for `β-hCG`, `HBsAg`, `PSA`, `Ferritin`, `Bilirubin`, `Calcium`):
     $$\text{Carryover (\%)} = \left(\frac{L_1 - L_3}{H_3 - L_3}\right) \times 100 \quad \left[\text{Acceptance Limit: } \le 1.0\%\text{ (and } L_1 - L_3 < 3 \times SD_{\text{low}}\text{)}\right]$$
2. **New Reagent Lot Crossover (`5-Sample Bridge`):**
   - Before retiring an old reagent lot, run **5 patient samples** + **2 levels of IQC** on both Old Lot and New Lot; verify mean bias $\le 0.5 \times \text{TE}_a\%$.

## ⚠️ Section 8: Method Verification Pitfalls + ISO 22367 FMEA Risk Table

| Verification Pitfall | Technical Consequence | Mandatory Control |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reporting exact numbers below `LoQ` (e.g., reporting `TSH = 0.0001` or `PSA = 0.002`)** | Noise below the Limit of Quantitation (`LoQ` where $CV_A > 20\%$) is statistically meaningless and misleads clinicians (e.g., biochemical recurrence of prostate cancer). | Configure LIMS to censor values below `LoQ` as **`< [LoQ Value]`** (e.g., `< 0.005 µIU/mL`). |
| **Reporting extrapolated numbers above `AMR` without dilution** | Beer-Lambert photometric non-linearity or CLIA detector saturation causes severe underestimation above `AMR`. | LIMS auto-holds any raw value $> \text{AMR}$ until a validated dilution within `CRR` is executed. |

| **`FMEA-0.1.8-A`: Extreme ALT/AST (>3,000 U/L) reported as normal due to NADH substrate exhaustion** | 9 | 2 | 1 | **18** | Enable `A0` substrate depletion absorbance threshold on `Bonavera Chem 400/200` + mandatory $1:10$ auto-dilution whenever Bilirubin $> 5\text{ mg/dL}$ with unexpectedly normal ALT/AST. |
