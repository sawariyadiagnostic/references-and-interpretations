## 🧪 Section 2: Master NABL 129 Auxiliary Equipment Calibration Schedule (`ISO/IEC 17025` Traceable)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why NABL Assessors Inspect Auxiliary Equipment Calibration Certificates (`NABL 129`):** Even if your main analyzers (`MEK-1503`, `Bonavera 400`, `Maglumi X3`) are calibrated daily with kit calibrators, **every manual dilution, lyophilized control reconstitution, ESR timer, coagulation water-bath, and serum centrifugation** depends on auxiliary equipment! Under **NABL 129**, all auxiliary measuring equipment MUST be calibrated annually by an **NABL-accredited (`ISO/IEC 17025`) calibration laboratory** with unbroken traceability to **NPL (National Physical Laboratory, India) / SI Units**!

| Auxiliary Equipment Category | Calibration Parameter & Verification Range | External `ISO/IEC 17025` Calibration Frequency | In-House Intermediate Verification SOP & Acceptance Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Variable & Fixed Micropipettes ($5\text{–}100\text{ }\mu\text{L}$ & $100\text{–}1,000\text{ }\mu\text{L}$)** | Volumetric Accuracy (Bias $\%$) & Repeatability ($CV\%$) via Gravimetric Method (`ISO 8655`) | **Every 6 to 12 Months** (NABL `ISO 17025` Lab) | **Quarterly In-House Gravimetric Check:** Weigh $10$ dispenses of distilled water on an analytical balance ($1\text{ }\mu\text{L} = 1.0\text{ mg}$ at $20^\circ\text{C}$, $Z$-factor $= 1.0029$). **Pass:** Accuracy within **$\pm 1.5\%$**, Imprecision **$CV \le 0.8\%$**. |
| **2. Laboratory Centrifuges (Serum/Plasma, Urine & Coagulation PPP)** | Rotational Speed (`RPM` / `RCF` $\times g$) via Optical Tachometer + Digital Timer ($\text{sec/min}$) | **Annually (Every 12 Months)** | **Semi-Annual Tachometer & Platelet-Poor Plasma (`PPP`) Check:** Verify speed within **$\pm 5\%$ of target RPM** (`1,500–2,000 x g` for $10\text{ min}$ for SST; Citrate `PPP` must yield residual $\text{PLT} < 10 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$). |
| **3. Digital Thermometers & Data-Loggers (Fridges, Freezers, Incubators, Water Baths, Room)** | Temperature Accuracy across $-20^\circ\text{C}, 4^\circ\text{C}, 25^\circ\text{C}, 37^\circ\text{C}, 56^\circ\text{C}$ | **Annually (Every 12 Months)** | Apply the **`Correction Factor`** from the `ISO 17025` Calibration Certificate directly on the thermometer label; max permissible error **$\le \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$** ($\le \pm 0.2^\circ\text{C}$ for $37^\circ\text{C}$ water bath). |
| **4. Autoclave Pressure Gauge, Temp Sensor & Timer** | Temperature ($121^\circ\text{C}$), Pressure ($15\text{ psi}$), Thermal Mapping & Timer | **Annually (Every 12 Months)** | **Every Load:** Chemical indicator tape; **Weekly/Monthly:** `Geobacillus stearothermophilus` biological spore strip (`SOP 0.1.13`). |
| **5. Digital Stopwatches / ESR & Coagulation Timers** | Time Interval Accuracy ($1\text{ min to }60\text{ min}$) | **Annually (Every 12 Months)** | Monthly comparison against synchronized NTP atomic clock time (max drift **$\le \pm 1\text{ sec/hour}$**). |

## ⚙️ Section 3: Primary Analyzer Lifecycle Governance (`IQ / OQ / PQ`, Labeling & Breakdown Protocol — `ISO 15189 Cl. 6.4`)

1. **Equipment Master Inventory & Unique Labeling (`Clause 6.4.3 & 6.4.9`):**
   - Every instrument and auxiliary device bears a permanent waterproof **Equipment Calibration & Status Label** displaying:
     - `Unique Equipment ID` (e.g., `EQ-HEM-01` for `MEK-1503`, `EQ-BIO-01` for `Bonavera Chem 400`, `EQ-BIO-02` for `Bonavera Chem 200`, `EQ-IMM-01` for `Snibe MAGLUMI X3`);
     - `Date of Last Calibration / PM`, `Due Date of Next Calibration / PM`, and `Calibration Factor / Status: ACTIVE`.
2. **4-Step Installation & Commissioning Qualification (`IQ / OQ / PQ`):**
   - **IQ (Installation Qualification):** Vendor engineer + Quality Manager verify serial numbers, electrical earthing ($\text{N-E} < 2\text{ V}$), UPS linkage, ambient temp, and plumbing/drainage.
   - **OQ (Operational Qualification):** Vendor demonstrates optical absorbance linearity, pipettor precision, background noise (`0` counts on `MEK-1503`), and calibration curve validity.
   - **PQ (Performance Qualification):** Laboratory executes in-house **`CLSI EP15-A3 / EP06 / EP28` Verification (`SOP 0.1.8`)** before reporting the first patient sample!
3. **Defective / Out-of-Service Equipment Quarantine (`Clause 6.4.7`):**
   - Any instrument failing calibration, `IQC`, or mechanical safety is immediately unplugged/locked in LIMS and tagged with a bold red sign: **`[OUT OF SERVICE — DO NOT USE | WORKLOAD ROUTED TO BACKUP PLATFORM]`** until repaired by the service engineer and **re-verified with Calibration + 2-Level IQC** prior to clinical return!

## 📊 Section 4: Clinical Laboratory Reagent Water (`CLRW` / Type-I Deionized Water) Quality SOP (`CLSI C03-A4`)

Because `Bonavera Chem 400`, `Bonavera Chem 200`, `Snibe MAGLUMI X3` wash buffers, and lyophilized control reconstitutions depend on ultrapure water, tap or unverified RO water must NEVER be used:

| Water Quality Parameter | `CLRW` / Type-I Target Specification (`CLSI C03-A4`) | Monitoring Frequency | Corrective Action if Out of Spec |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Electrical Resistivity / Conductivity** | **Resistivity $\ge 10\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$** (Conductivity **$\le 0.1\text{ }\mu\text{S/cm}$** at $25^\circ\text{C}$; Max $\le 1.0\text{ }\mu\text{S/cm}$ for general wash) | **Daily** (`9:00 AM` inline meter log) | Replace mixed-bed deionization (`DI`) resin cartridge and $0.22\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ final filter immediately. |
| **2. pH & Chemical Purity (Calcium / Chloride / Silicates)** | $\text{pH } 5.5\text{–}7.5$; Zero detectable $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ or $\text{Cl}^-$ blank absorbance on `Bonavera Chem 400` | **Weekly** (Run water as sample for $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ / $\text{Cl}^-$ / `ALP` blank) | Regenerate/replace DI cartridges if reagent blank absorbance drifts. |
| **3. Microbial Colony Count (`CFU/mL`)** | **$< 10\text{ CFU/mL}$** (Cultured on Nutrient/Blood Agar at $37^\circ\text{C}$ for $48\text{h}$) | **Monthly** (Microbiology QC check) | Sanitize water storage tank & tubing with $0.1\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite / $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ and replace $0.22\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ membrane filter. |

## 🧬 Section 5: Reagent, Kit & Consumable Receipt, Cold-Chain Inspection & `CLSI EP26` Lot-to-Lot Acceptance Testing (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.6`)

> [!CAUTION]
> **Absolute NABL Rule (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.6.3`):** Never load a newly delivered reagent box or new reagent lot directly onto `MEK-1503`, `Bonavera Chem 400/200`, or `Maglumi X3` for patient testing without **(A) Physical & Cold-Chain Receipt Inspection** and **(B) Pre-Use Lot Acceptance Verification**!

1. **Step 1 — Delivery Cold-Chain & Expiry Inspection (`At Reception`):**
   - Measure the internal temperature of the courier thermocol box using an **Infrared (`IR`) Thermometer**: refrigerated kits (`Maglumi X3` CLIA kits, Chemistry enzymes, Hematology controls, Rapid cards requiring $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$) must arrive at **$2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$** (reject delivery if ice packs are completely melted and box temp $> 15^\circ\text{C}$!).
   - Verify **Lot Number, Manufacturing Date, Remaining Shelf-Life ($\ge 3\text{–}6\text{ months}$)**, and intact box seal.
2. **Step 2 — Pre-Use New Lot Acceptance Verification (`CLSI EP26-Ed2` & `ISO 15189 Cl. 6.6.3`):**
   - **For Quantitative Reagents (`MEK-1503`, `Chem 400/200`, `Maglumi X3`):** Before the old reagent lot is exhausted, calibrate the **New Lot**, run **Level-1 and Level-2 IQC** (must fall within $\pm 2\text{ SD}$), and run **2 to 5 previously tested patient samples** spanning normal and pathological values on both **Old Lot and New Lot**.
     - *Acceptance Criterion:* Mean lot-to-lot difference must be **$\le 0.33 \times \text{TE}_a\%$** (or $\le 0.5 \times \text{TE}_a\%$).
   - **For Qualitative Rapid Cards / Serology Kits (`HIV`, `HBsAg`, `HCV`, `Dengue NS1/IgM`, `Malaria`, `Urine hCG`) & Stains (`Leishman`, `Gram`, `ZN AFB`):** Test **1 Known Positive Control/Sample** and **1 Known Negative Control/Sample** from the archive before placing the new box into routine use!
3. **Step 3 — `FEFO` Inventory & Open-Vial Labelling (`Cl. 6.6.4`):**
   - Arrange all refrigerator shelves by **FEFO (*First-Expiry, First-Out*)**.
   - Every opened reagent bottle, calibrator, control, or stain bottle MUST have a waterproof sticker stating: **`Date Opened: [DD-MM-YYYY] | On-Board/Open Expiry Date: [DD-MM-YYYY] | Opened By: [Initials]`**.

## 🚨 Section 6: Converting Centrifuge `RPM` to Relative Centrifugal Force (`RCF` in $\times g$)

Because `CLSI GP44-A4` specifies centrifugation in **$\text{RCF}$ ($\times g$)** rather than `RPM` (since `RPM` varies with rotor radius $r$ in $\text{cm}$), verify your laboratory centrifuge setting using the international nomogram equation:

$$\mathbf{\text{RCF }(\times g) = 1.118 \times 10^{-5} \times r_{\text{cm}} \times (\text{RPM})^2}$$

- **Example (`r = 12 cm` rotor):**
  - **Serum `SST` / Red-Top Separation ($1,500 \times g$ for $10\text{ min}$):** Set speed to **$\approx 3,350\text{ RPM}$**.
  - **Coagulation $3.2\%$ Citrate Platelet-Poor Plasma (`PPP`, $2,000 \times g$ for $15\text{ min}$):** Set speed to **$\approx 3,850\text{ RPM}$** (yielding residual $\text{PLT} < 10 \times 10^3/\mu\text{L}$ so platelet phospholipid factor 3 does not shorten `aPTT` or mask Lupus Anticoagulant!).
  - **Urine Sediment Examination ($400 \times g$ for $5\text{ min}$):** Set speed to **$\approx 1,730\text{ RPM}$** (spinning urine at $3,500\text{ RPM}$ shatters hyaline/cellular casts!).

## 🔬 Section 7: Annual Supplier & Referral Lab Evaluation SOP (`ISO 15189 Clauses 6.6.5 & 6.8`)

Once every 12 months, the Quality Manager evaluates all critical vendors (Reagent distributors for `Nihon Kohden`, `Bonavera`, `Snibe Maglumi`, EQAS providers, Calibration agencies, and Referral Labs) on a **100-Point Scorecard**:
- Cold-chain delivery integrity ($30\text{ pts}$); Remaining shelf-life $\ge 6\text{ months}$ ($20\text{ pts}$); Lot-to-lot consistency ($20\text{ pts}$); Emergency engineer breakdown response time $\le 24\text{ hours}$ ($20\text{ pts}$); Documentation/COA/MSDS completeness ($10\text{ pts}$). Vendors scoring **$< 70\%$** are placed on probation or replaced.
