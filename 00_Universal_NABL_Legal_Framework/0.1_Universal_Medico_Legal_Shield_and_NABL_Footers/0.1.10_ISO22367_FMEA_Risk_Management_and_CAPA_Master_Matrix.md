## 🧪 Section 2: Pre-Analytical, Analytical & Post-Analytical Error Taxonomy (`IFCC WG-LEPS`)

Per the International Federation of Clinical Chemistry Working Group on Laboratory Errors and Patient Safety (**IFCC WG-LEPS**) and **ISO 22367:2020**, total laboratory risk is partitioned across the three phases of the Total Testing Process (`TTP`):

| Phase of Total Testing Process (`TTP`) | Share of Total Lab Errors | Top 5 High-Risk Failure Modes to Monitor Monthly (`ISO 15189 Clause 8.8`) | Target Quality Indicator (`QI`) Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Pre-Analytical Phase** | **$60\%\text{ – }70\%$** | 1. Unlabeled/mislabeled primary tube (`Zero Tolerance`); 2. Hemolyzed serum (`H-Index` $> 50\text{ mg/dL}$); 3. Clotted $\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ or Citrate tube; 4. Under-filled Citrate tube ($< 90\%$); 5. Wrong Order-of-Draw ($\text{K}_2\text{-EDTA}$ carryover). | Overall Sample Rejection Rate **$< 1.0\%$**; Misidentification Rate **$0.00\%$** |
| **2. Analytical Phase** | **$10\%\text{ – }15\%$** | 1. Out-of-control IQC run released; 2. Unrecognized EDTA platelet clumping; 3. NADH substrate depletion in severe hepatitis; 4. High-dose Hook Effect / Biotin interference; 5. Unverified bias between Primary & Backup analyzer. | Unacceptable IQC Repeat Rate **$< 2.0\%$**; EQAS Concordance **$\ge 95\%$** |
| **3. Post-Analytical Phase** | **$15\%\text{ – }25\%$** | 1. Delayed Critical ("Panic") Value call ($> 30\text{ mins}$); 2. Manual transcription typo on non-interfaced test; 3. Incorrect age/sex reference partition applied; 4. Amended report issued without recalling prior report; 5. Missing statutory legal footer. | Critical Notification $\le 30\text{m}$ Rate **$\ge 98\%$**; Amended Report Rate **$< 0.1\%$** |

## ⚙️ Section 3: Quantitative `ISO 22367:2020` FMEA Scoring Scales & Risk Priority Number (`RPN`) SOP

To eliminate subjective guesswork required by **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 8.5**, every failure mode across all **273 standalone test SOPs (`298` total files across `25` clusters)** is scored on a **$1\text{–}10$ scale** across three orthogonal dimensions:

$$\mathbf{\text{Risk Priority Number (RPN)} = \text{Severity }(S) \times \text{Occurrence }(O) \times \text{Detectability }(D)} \quad [\text{Scale: } 1 \text{ to } 1,000]$$

| Score ($1\text{–}10$) | **1. Severity of Patient Harm ($S$)** | **2. Probability of Occurrence ($O$)** | **3. Likelihood of Failure Escaping Detection ($D$)** |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$9\text{ – }10$** | **Catastrophic / Critical Harm:** Death, permanent organ injury, wrong transfusion, unnecessary surgery/chemo, or missed MI/sepsis. | **Frequent:** $> 1\text{ in }100$ samples ($> 1\%$) if uncontrolled. | **Undetectable ($9\text{–}10$):** No analyzer flag, no IQC flag, no delta check; error reaches patient unnoticed. |
| **$6\text{ – }8$** | **Moderate / Significant Harm:** Inappropriate prescription (e.g., wrong thyroxine/antibiotic dose), delayed therapy, or repeat invasive draw. | **Occasional:** $1\text{ in }500$ to $1\text{ in }2,000$ samples ($0.05\%\text{–}0.2\%$). | **Moderate Detection ($5\text{–}7$):** Detected only by manual technician/pathologist visual review or clinical query. |
| **$3\text{ – }5$** | **Minor Inconvenience:** Turnaround time (`TAT`) delay or free non-urgent recollection before treatment starts. | **Rare:** $1\text{ in }5,000$ to $1\text{ in }20,000$ samples. | **High Detection ($2\text{–}4$):** Caught automatically by Westgard IQC, `HIL` index, or LIMS Delta Check. |
| **$1\text{ – }2$** | **Negligible / No Clinical Impact:** Cosmetic formatting or internal clerical log note without patient impact. | **Remote / Almost Never:** $< 1\text{ in }50,000$ samples. | **Certain Detection ($1$):** Hard software interlock physically blocks report release. |

### `RPN` Action Thresholds:
- **$\text{RPN} \ge 100$ (or ANY Severity $S = 9\text{–}10$ with $D \ge 3$):** **UNACCEPTABLE RISK (Red)** — Immediate structural/software engineering control mandatory before operating!
- **$\text{RPN } 40\text{–}99$:** **ALARP / Moderate Risk (Yellow)** — Implement procedural checklist, LIMS warning rule, and monthly audit monitoring.
- **$\text{RPN} < 40$:** **Acceptable Residual Risk (Green)** — Controlled via routine SOP and annual review.

## 🧬 Section 5: Non-Conforming Event (`NCE`) & 5-Whys Root Cause Analysis (`CAPA`) Workflow (`ISO 15189 Cl. 8.7`)

Whenever a laboratory error, IQC/EQAS failure, critical delay, or clinician/patient complaint occurs, execute this **5-Step Closed-Loop CAPA Protocol**:

1. **Step 1 — Immediate Containment & Correction (`Within 1 Hour`):**
   - Halt affected analytical run or recall erroneous report; quarantine defective reagent/control lot; perform patient look-back to identify any other affected specimens; issue **`[AMENDED REPORT]`** (`SOP 0.1.3`) if any released patient result was impacted.
2. **Step 2 — Event Classification & Severity Grading (`CLSI QMS11`):**
   - Classify phase (`Pre-Analytical`, `Analytical`, `Post-Analytical`) and assign **Clinical Impact Score** (`Near-Miss Caught In-House` vs `Released Without Clinical Harm` vs `Sentinel Event`).
3. **Step 3 — Root Cause Analysis (`5-Whys` & `Ishikawa 6M Fishbone` — *Manpower, Machine, Method, Material, Milieu, Measurement*):**
   - *Rule:* **"Human Error / Staff Carelessness" is NEVER an acceptable final root cause!** Ask *Why* 5 times until the underlying **system or software barrier deficit** is exposed.
4. **Step 4 — Corrective Action (Prevents Recurrence of This Event) & Preventive Action (Prevents Occurrence Across Other Tests):**
   - Prioritize **Hard Engineering / LIMS Software Controls** over passive "re-training memos."
5. **Step 5 — 30-Day & 90-Day CAPA Effectiveness Verification (`ISO 15189 Clause 8.7.1 e`):**
   - A CAPA cannot be closed on the day it is written! The Quality Manager audits the process at **$30\text{ days}$ and $90\text{ days}$** to verify mathematically that the recurrence rate is **$0.0\%$** and $\text{RPN}_{\text{final}} < 40$.

## 🚨 Section 6: 48-Hour Clinician/Patient Complaint Resolution SOP (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 7.9` & `CPA 2019`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Turning a Dissatisfied Patient/Doctor into a Lifelong Advocate (And Preventing Consumer Court Litigation):** Over 95% of diagnostic laboratory consumer complaints escalate to legal notices ONLY because the laboratory staff responded defensively, dismissively, or refused to re-check the sample. Follow this **Golden 48-Hour De-Escalation Protocol**:

1. **Hour 0 (Immediate Reception Empathy):** Listen calmly without arguing. Log the query in the `Complaint & Feedback Register`. Immediately reassure the patient/clinician: *"Thank you for bringing this to our attention. Under our NABL ISO 15189 quality protocol, we safely preserve all blood samples at $2\text{–}8^\circ\text{C}$ for 48 hours specifically for complimentary verification."*
2. **Hour 1 to Hour 4 (Technical Investigation):**
   - Retrieve the archived primary specimen; check barcode identity, `MCV`/`ABO` fingerprint, serum `HIL` indices, and EDTA smear.
   - Re-assay the archived tube in duplicate on both the **Primary Analyzer** and **Backup Platform** alongside a fresh **Complimentary Recollection Sample** (if the patient is available).
3. **Hour 4 to Hour 24 (Transparent Pathologist Consultation & Resolution):**
   - The Consultant Pathologist speaks directly with the referring clinician/patient, sharing the `IQC` chart, duplicate verification numbers, and biological `RCV %` / pre-analytical explanation (or issuing a transparent Amended Report + full fee refund if a true discrepancy is found).

## 🔬 Section 7: Monthly NABL Quality Indicators (`QI`) Dashboard (`ISO 15189 Clause 8.8.2`)

| Quality Indicator ID | Monthly Metric Formula | Target Benchmark | Action Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **`QI-PRE-01` (Sample Rejection Rate)** | $(\text{Rejected Samples} \div \text{Total Samples Received}) \times 100$ | **$< 1.0\%$** | $> 1.5\%$ triggers phlebotomy/runner audit |
| **`QI-PRE-02` (Hemolysis Rate in Chemistry)** | $(\text{Samples with H-Index } \ge 50 \div \text{Total Serum Samples}) \times 100$ | **$< 1.5\%$** | $> 2.0\%$ triggers needle/centrifuge/transit review |
| **`QI-ANA-01` (IQC Failure Rate)** | $(\text{IQC Runs Violating Westgard Reject} \div \text{Total IQC Runs}) \times 100$ | **$< 2.0\%$** | $> 3.0\%$ triggers analyzer PM / calibration audit |
| **`QI-ANA-02` (EQAS Acceptable Z-Score %)** | $(\text{EQAS Results with } \lvert Z \rvert \le 2.0 \div \text{Total EQAS Challenges}) \times 100$ | **$\ge 95.0\%$** | Any $\lvert Z \rvert \ge 3.0$ triggers immediate `CAPA` |
| **`QI-POST-01` (Critical Value Call $\le 30\text{m}$)** | $(\text{Critical Calls in } \le 30\text{m} \div \text{Total Critical Values}) \times 100$ | **$\ge 98.0\%$** | Any call $> 30\text{m}$ triggers incident review |
| **`QI-POST-02` (Turnaround Time `TAT` Compliance)** | $(\text{Reports Released Within Promised TAT} \div \text{Total Reports}) \times 100$ | **$\ge 95.0\%$** | $< 90\%$ triggers workflow bottleneck analysis |
| **`QI-POST-03` (Amended Report Rate)** | $(\text{Amended Reports Issued} \div \text{Total Reports Released}) \times 100$ | **$< 0.10\%$** | Every single analytical amendment triggers `RCA` |
