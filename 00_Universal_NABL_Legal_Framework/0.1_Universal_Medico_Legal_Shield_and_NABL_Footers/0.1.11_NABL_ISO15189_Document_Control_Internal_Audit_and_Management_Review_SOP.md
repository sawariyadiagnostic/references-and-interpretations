Every policy, SOP, work instruction, and log sheet must follow a unified numbering system with zero uncontrolled/obsolete copies on laboratory benches!

## ⚙️ Section 3: Step-by-Step SOP for Document Control (`Cl. 8.3`), Internal Audit (`Cl. 8.8.3`) & Management Review (`Cl. 8.9`)

### 3.1 Document Creation, Unique Coding, Approval & Revision Control (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 8.3.2`)
1. **Unique Document Header Block:** Every controlled document must display:
   - `Document Title` & `Unique Document Control ID` (e.g., `SOP-HEM-1.1.1`);
   - `Version Number` (e.g., `v2.1`), `Effective Date`, `Next Review Date` ($\le 12\text{ months}$), and `Page X of Y`;
   - **Dual Sign-Off:** *Prepared/Reviewed By* (`Quality Manager / Technical Supervisor`) and *Approved/Authorized By* (`Laboratory Director / Consultant Pathologist — MD Pathology`).
2. **Controlled Distribution & Obsolete Document Quarantine (`Cl. 8.3.2 f–g`):**
   - Only documents bearing the **`[CONTROLLED COPY]`** watermark/header in the LIMS or official Departmental SOP Binder may be used at workbenches.
   - Whenever a document is revised (e.g., `v2.0` $\rightarrow$ `v2.1`), the Quality Manager immediately withdraws all physical/electronic copies of `v2.0` from benches, stamps a single archival copy as **`[OBSOLETE — RETAINED FOR HISTORICAL REFERENCE ONLY]`** with the retirement date, and files it in the `Obsolete Document Archive` (`5-year` retention).
3. **Handwritten Amendments Prohibition:** Unauthorized handwritten pen/pencil changes on printed SOPs or reference range tables are **strictly prohibited** (`Zero Tolerance` NABL NC).

### 3.2 Annual Internal Audit Program (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 8.8.3` & `CLSI QMS15`)
1. **Frequency & Scope:** Conducted **at least once every 12 months** (or semi-annually across staggered departments) covering **all clauses of ISO 15189:2022 (`Clauses 4 to 8`)** and **all pre-analytical, analytical, and post-analytical activities** across Folders `00` to `06`.
   - **(2) Auditor Independence & Qualification (`Cl. 8.8.3.2`):**
   - Internal audits must be conducted by personnel trained in **ISO 15189:2022 Internal Auditing (4-day NABL/ISO 15189 IA Certificate)**;
   - **Impartiality Rule:** *Personnel shall NOT audit their own routine bench activities!* (e.g., The Biochemistry Supervisor or an external NABL-certified lead auditor audits Hematology, while the Consultant Pathologist/Hematology lead audits Biochemistry/Reception).
3. **Horizontal + Vertical ("Witness Trail") Audit Methodology:**
   - During each departmental audit, the internal auditor picks **3 random released patient reports** from the prior 6 months and traces their complete **"Birth-to-Delivery" Witness Trail**:
     $$\text{TRF / Barcode Time} \longrightarrow \text{Phlebotomy & Temp Log} \longrightarrow \text{Reagent Lot & Calibration} \longrightarrow \text{Same-Day IQC L-J Chart} \longrightarrow \text{Raw Analyzer Printout/Flag} \longrightarrow \text{Pathologist Sign-Out & Critical Call Log}$$
4. **Closure of Internal Audit Non-Conformities (`NCs`):** All Major/Minor NCs identified during Internal Audit must have Root Cause Analysis (`5-Whys`), Corrective Action, and Documentary Evidence submitted within **$15\text{–}30\text{ days}$** (`SOP 0.1.10`).

### 3.3 Annual Management Review Meeting (`MRM` — `ISO 15189:2022 Clause 8.9`)
1. **Frequency & Quorum:** Chaired by the **Laboratory Director**, attended by the **Quality Manager, Technical Supervisors, and Phlebotomy Lead** at least **once every 12 months** (not exceeding a 12-month gap).
   - **(2) Mandatory 14 Management Review Inputs (`Clause 8.9.2`):**
   - Status of actions from previous Management Reviews;
   - Changes in internal/external issues, test volume, staffing, or equipment (`MEK-1503`, `Bonavera 400/200`, `Maglumi X3`);
   - Fulfillment of Quality Objectives & Monthly Quality Indicators (`QI-PRE`, `QI-ANA`, `QI-POST`);
   - Feedback and complaints from clinicians, patients, and staff (`Cl. 7.9`);
   - Results of Internal Audits (`Cl. 8.8.3`) and External NABL Assessments;
   - Status of Non-Conformities and `CAPA` effectiveness (`Cl. 8.7`);
   - Results of `IQC` (Westgard Sigma metrics), `EQAS / PT` Z-scores, and Inter-Laboratory/Inter-Instrument Comparability (`Cl. 7.3.7`);
   - Evaluation of Referral Laboratories (`Cl. 6.8`) and Reagent/Equipment Suppliers (`Cl. 6.6`);
   - Review of the `ISO 22367` FMEA Risk Register (`Cl. 8.5`);
   - Adequacy of human resources, staff competency, and environmental/biosafety controls.
3. **Mandatory Management Review Outputs (`Clause 8.9.3`):** Formal minutes recording decisions on QMS improvement, resource allocation, equipment upgrades, and next-year Quality Objectives with target dates and responsible owners.

## 🧬 Section 5: Impartiality (`Cl. 4.1`), Confidentiality (`Cl. 4.2`) & Patients' First Policy (`Cl. 4.3`)

1. **Declaration of Impartiality & Conflict of Interest (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 4.1`):**
   - Laboratory management and all diagnostic personnel sign an annual **Impartiality & Zero-Inducement Undertaking**: analytical test selection, reporting, and interpretation are governed strictly by scientific evidence and clinical guidelines—free from any commercial, financial, or external pressure.
2. **Data Confidentiality & `DPDP Act 2023` Compliance (`Clause 4.2`):**
   - Patient results are released solely to the patient, authorized representative, or referring clinician via authenticated channels (password/OTP-protected PDF, LIMS portal, or verified clinician contact).

## 🚨 Section 6: Critical NABL Desktop & On-Site Assessment Non-Conformity (`NC`) Prevention Rules

1. **Never Backdate a QC, Temperature, or Maintenance Log:** NABL assessors cross-check handwritten temperature/PM logs against analyzer electronic error logs and staff attendance registers (`ALCOA+` audit). If a staff member was on leave on Tuesday, their signature must never appear on Tuesday's refrigerator log!
2. **Never Leave Uncontrolled Sticky Notes or Old Reference Range Sheets on Walls:** Every single chart taped to a wall or analyzer must carry a `Document Control ID` and `Controlled Copy` stamp.

## 🔬 Section 7: `ALCOA+` Data Integrity & Electronic LIMS Backup SOP (`ISO 15189 Cl. 7.6 & 8.4`)

All paper and electronic records must satisfy **`ALCOA+`** (*Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available*):
- **Paper Log Corrections:** If a technician makes a writing mistake in a physical register, **NEVER use white correction fluid (Whitener), erasers, or black-out scribbling!** Draw a **single neat horizontal line** through the incorrect entry (`~~4.2~~ 4.5`), write the correct value alongside, and sign with **initials and date** (`ISO 15189 Clause 8.4.2`).
- **LIMS Daily Backup (`3-2-1 Rule`):** Automated daily encrypted database backup at `11:30 PM` to (1) Primary Server, (2) External NAS/Hard Drive, and (3) Offsite Encrypted Cloud, with quarterly restore-verification testing.
