### 1.1 Architectural Three-Zone Functional Separation & Access Control (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.3.2` & `ISO 15190 Cl. 6`)
To prevent cross-contamination between patient reception areas, routine analytical benches, and decontamination zones, the laboratory facility enforces a strict **3-Zone Architectural Hierarchy**:

### 1.2 Comprehensive Environmental, Cold-Chain, Ergonomic & Electrical Monitoring Matrix (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.3.1–6.3.4`)
Environmental conditions directly govern analytical accuracy: optical interference filters (`Bonavera Chem 400/200`), photomultiplier tubes (`Snibe MAGLUMI X3`), and aperture impedance sensors (`MEK-1503`) exhibit thermal drift if room temperature exceeds **$26^\circ\text{C}$**, while refrigerated calibrators and enzymes lose catalytic activity if cold-chain units exceed **$8^\circ\text{C}$**. All sensors must hold valid **`ISO/IEC 17025` NABL-traceable calibration certificates**.

### 1.3 Personal Protective Equipment (`PPE`), Aerosol Control & Facility Safety Hygiene (`ISO 15190:2020 Cl. 7 & Cl. 12`)
1. **Standardized `PPE` Donning & Doffing Sequence (`CDC / WHO / ISO 15190`):**
   - **Donning Sequence (Before Entering Analytical Zone 2/3):**
     1. Perform hand hygiene (`WHO 6-Step Technique` with $70\%$ alcohol rub for $20\text{–}30\text{ sec}$ or soap and water for $40\text{–}60\text{ sec}$);
     2. Don dedicated **fluid-resistant, long-sleeved, knee-length laboratory coat** (buttoned completely; street coats/shawls prohibited);
     3. Don **surgical mask or N95/FFP2 respirator** (mandatory in Zone 3 / `AFB` staining / spill cleanup);
     4. Don **protective safety goggles or face shield** (when uncapping tubes, operating centrifuges, or preparing disinfectants);
     5. Don **powder-free nitrile examination gloves** pulled snugly over the wrist cuffs of the laboratory coat.
   - **Doffing Sequence (Before Exiting Analytical Zone — *Most Contaminated to Least Contaminated*):**
     1. Peel off **nitrile gloves** using the glove-in-glove technique without touching bare skin $\rightarrow$ dispose into **Red Bin**;
     2. Perform interim hand hygiene;
     3. Remove **goggles/face shield** from the rear headband (clean with $70\%$ alcohol wipe);
     4. Unbutton and remove **laboratory coat** folding outer surface inward, and hang on designated Zone-2/3 rack (**never wear laboratory coats into Zone 1 reception, washrooms, or staff tea/dining rooms!**);
     5. Remove **mask/respirator** by the rear straps without touching the front filter $\rightarrow$ dispose into **Yellow Bin**;
     6. Perform final **hand washing with antimicrobial soap and running water for $40\text{–}60\text{ seconds}$**.
2. **Centrifuge & Sample Decapping Aerosol Containment (`ISO 15190 Cl. 14.3`):**
   - All specimen centrifugation must use **centrifuges equipped with sealed safety buckets/rotors and safety-interlocked lids** that cannot be opened while the rotor is spinning.
   - Balanced paired tubes must be inspected for hairline cracks prior to loading.
   - Allow the centrifuge rotor to come to a complete rest plus a **$2\text{–}5\text{ minute}$ settling pause** before opening the lid during routine runs. If a tube breakage is suspected inside a centrifuge, **keep the lid closed for a mandatory $30\text{ minutes}$** to allow aerosols to settle before opening the sealed safety bucket inside a Biosafety Cabinet or under full `PPE`.
3. **Emergency Eye-Wash Station (`ANSI/ISEA Z358.1`) & Chemical Hygiene (`SDS/MSDS`):**
   - Hands-free **plumbed Emergency Eye-Wash Stations** must be accessible within **$10\text{ seconds}$ unobstructed travel distance ($\le 15\text{ meters}$)** from every analytical and staining bench.
   - **Weekly Flushing Verification:** Every Monday at `09:00 AM`, the Safety Officer activates each eye-wash station for **$3\text{ minutes}$** to clear stagnant pipeline water, verifies dual-stream symmetry and tepid water temperature ($16\text{–}38^\circ\text{C}$), and logs the check in `NABL-SAF-REG-01`.
   - **Material Safety Data Sheet (`SDS / MSDS`) Binder & Labeling:** A physical and digital `SDS` binder covering all laboratory reagents (stains, calibrators, acids/alkalis, alcohols, sodium hypochlorite) must be maintained at the bench with **GHS Pictograms and NFPA 704 Hazard Diamonds** clearly displayed on secondary containers. When diluting laboratory reagents, always follow the standard chemical hygiene rule of **adding reagent slowly to water** while wearing eye protection.

## 🧴 Section 2: Surface Decontamination & Spill Management SOP (`ISO 15190:2020 Cl. 12.6` & `WHO Biosafety Manual 4th Ed.`)

### 2.1 Daily Preparation of Fresh $0.5\%$ & $1.0\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite ($\text{NaOCl}$) Solutions
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why Daily Fresh Preparation Is Mandatory:** Aqueous Sodium Hypochlorite ($\text{NaOCl}$) solutions undergo rapid photolytic and thermal decomposition once diluted with tap water, losing $> 40\%$ of their available chlorine potency within **$24\text{ hours}$**. Every morning at **`08:30 AM`**, fresh working solutions must be prepared in **opaque, dark-colored plastic containers** labeled with: `Solution Strength (% & ppm)`, `Date & Time of Preparation`, `Expiry Date & Time (24 Hours)`, and `Prepared By (Initials)`.

The stoichiometric dilution formula used by bench technologists is:
$$V_1 \times C_1 = V_2 \times C_2 \implies V_1\text{ (Volume of Commercial Stock Bleach)} = \frac{C_2\text{ (Desired Working \%)} \times V_2\text{ (Final Volume)}}{C_1\text{ (Stock Available Chlorine \%)}}$$

$$\text{Parts of Water per } 1\text{ Part Stock Bleach} = \left(\frac{\%\text{ Available Chlorine in Stock}}{\%\text{ Desired Working Solution}}\right) - 1$$

| Commercial Stock Strength ($C_1$) | **To Prepare `0.5% NaOCl` (`5,000 ppm` Available Chlorine)**<br>*(Routine Daily Workbench Wipe & General Non-Blood Surface Disinfection)* | **To Prepare `1.0% NaOCl` (`10,000 ppm` Available Chlorine)**<br>*(Biological Specimen Spills, Phlebotomy Table Accidents & `BMWM` Tube/Slide Soak)* | **To Prepare `2.0% NaOCl` (`20,000 ppm` Available Chlorine)**<br>*(Large Volume / High-Protein Biological Spills)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stock $= 4.0\%$ Available Chlorine** | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 7\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$125\text{ mL Stock} + 875\text{ mL Water}$**)* | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 3\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$250\text{ mL Stock} + 750\text{ mL Water}$**)* | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 1\text{ part Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$500\text{ mL Stock} + 500\text{ mL Water}$**)* |
| **Stock $= 5.0\%$ Available Chlorine** | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 9\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$100\text{ mL Stock} + 900\text{ mL Water}$**)* | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 4\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$200\text{ mL Stock} + 800\text{ mL Water}$**)* | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 1.5\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$400\text{ mL Stock} + 600\text{ mL Water}$**)* |
| **Stock $= 10.0\%$ Available Chlorine** | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 19\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$50\text{ mL Stock} + 950\text{ mL Water}$**)* | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 9\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$100\text{ mL Stock} + 900\text{ mL Water}$**)* | **$1\text{ part Stock} + 4\text{ parts Water}$**<br>*(For $1,000\text{ mL}$: **$200\text{ mL Stock} + 800\text{ mL Water}$**)* |

- **Disinfectant Selection by Surface & Matrix Type:**
  - **Blood, Serum, Plasma & Urine Spills:** Freshly prepared **$1.0\%\text{ NaOCl}$ (`10,000 ppm`)** with **$\ge 20\text{–}30\text{ minutes}$** contact time.
  - **Mucoid / Sputum / `AFB` Microscopy Area Spills:** Because dense mucoproteins in sputum can partially neutralize free chlorine, use **$5.0\%\text{ Phenolic / Certified Tuberculocidal Disinfectant}$** (or **$2.0\%\text{ NaOCl}$** with extended **$30\text{ minutes}$** contact time).
  - **Precision Metallic & Optical Surfaces (`MEK-1503` probes, `Bonavera 400/200` sample arms, `Maglumi X3` carousels, Centrifuge Rotors):** Because sodium hypochlorite corrodes stainless steel and anodized aluminum, always follow any hypochlorite wipe on metal surfaces with a **clean water wipe and $70\%\text{ Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) / Ethanol}$** wipe.

### 2.3 Step-by-Step Surface Decontamination & Spill Response Workflow (`Minor < 10 mL` vs `Major > 10 mL`)

```
[1. ALERT & CORDON] ---> Warn nearby colleagues; place 'CAUTION: SPILL' sign; allow 15-30 min settling if centrifuge/high-energy spill.
        |
[2. DON FULL PPE] -----> Double Nitrile Gloves + Fluid-Resistant Gown + Eye Goggles + Mask/N95 + Shoe Covers.
        |
[3. COVER SPILL] ------> Gently place Absorbent Paper Towels over the entire spill from outer perimeter inward (NEVER pour disinfectant directly onto uncovered liquid!).
        |
[4. POUR CONCENTRICALLY] -> Pour freshly prepared 1.0% NaOCl (10,000 ppm) starting from the OUTER MARGIN moving INWARD in concentric circles until saturated.
        |
[5. CONTACT TIME] -----> Leave undisturbed for MANDATORY 20 TO 30 MINUTES contact time.
        |
[6. MECHANICAL PICKUP] -> Use Forceps/Scoop to transfer broken glass into BLUE Box; transfer soaked paper towels into YELLOW Bag.
        |
[7. SECONDARY WIPE] ---> Mop/wipe surface with fresh 1.0% NaOCl, rinse with water + 70% Alcohol (if metallic), doff PPE, wash hands, and log in NABL-SAF-REG-02.
```

## 🩺 Section 3: Occupational Health, Needlestick / Splash First-Aid & `< 2-Hour` Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (`PEP`) SOP (`NACO / WHO / CDC`)

### 3.1 Immediate First-Aid Protocol (`Minute 0 to Minute 15` — At the Point of Exposure)

> [!CAUTION]
> **Critical First-Aid Prohibitions (`NACO & WHO Guidelines`):**
> 1. **NEVER squeeze, milk, suck, or scrub a needlestick puncture wound!** Squeezing or scrubbing creates local micro-capillary inflammation and increases percutaneous absorption.
> 2. **NEVER apply caustic agents (undiluted bleach/hypochlorite, absolute alcohol, or harsh scrub solutions) directly into an open puncture wound or mucous membrane!**

| Exposure Route | Immediate Step-by-Step First-Aid Action (`Minute 0–15`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Percutaneous Injury** *(Needlestick, Lancet, Scalpel, or Broken Glass Puncture)* | 1. Immediately remove gloves carefully and hold the injured site under **gentle running tap water**;<br>2. Wash the wound and surrounding skin gently with **mild liquid soap and running water for $5\text{ minutes}$** without scrubbing;<br>3. Allow spontaneous bleeding if present, but **do not squeeze or milk the finger**;<br>4. Dry gently with a sterile gauze pad and cover with a waterproof sterile adhesive dressing. |
| **Ocular Splash** *(Blood, Body Fluid, or Chemical Splash into Eyes)* | 1. Proceed immediately ($\le 10\text{ seconds}$) to the **`ANSI Z358.1` Emergency Eye-Wash Station**;<br>2. Hold both eyelids open with thumb and index finger and irrigate continuously with **sterile normal saline ($0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$) or clean tepid running water for a full $15\text{ minutes}$**, rolling the eyeballs in all directions;<br>3. Remove contact lenses (if worn) **only after** the initial $1\text{–}2\text{ minutes}$ of flushing. |
| **Oral / Nasal Mucous Membrane or Non-Intact Skin Splash** | 1. Spit out any fluid immediately; rinse the mouth or nasal passage repeatedly with **clean running water or normal saline for $10\text{–}15\text{ minutes}$** (do not swallow);<br>2. Wash affected non-intact/eczematous skin with mild soap and running water for $5\text{ minutes}$. |

### 3.2 Baseline STAT Serology & `< 2-Hour` Golden Window Clinical Referral (`Minute 15 to Minute 120`)
1. **Immediate Notification & STAT Source/Staff Testing (`Minute 15–60`):**
   - Report the exposure immediately to the **Quality Manager / Biosafety Officer and Laboratory Director (`MD Pathology`)**.
   - With appropriate clinical consent and strict privacy coding (`HIV & AIDS Act 2017`), run **complimentary STAT baseline serology** on the **Source Patient Sample** and the **Exposed Staff Member's Baseline Sample (`Day 0`)** on `Snibe MAGLUMI X3` / WHO-prequalified rapid assays:
     - **`HIV-1/2` (4th Gen Ag/Ab Combo)**
     - **`HBsAg` (Hepatitis B Surface Antigen)** + Exposed Staff **`Anti-HBs` Quantitative Titer ($\text{mIU/mL}$)**
     - **`Anti-HCV` (Hepatitis C Antibody)**
   - For female staff of reproductive age considering `PEP`, perform a baseline **Urine/Serum $\beta\text{-hCG}$ Pregnancy Test**, **Serum Creatinine / `eGFR`**, and **ALT/AST** (without delaying the first dose of `PEP`).
2. **NACO Antiretroviral Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (`HIV PEP` — Ideal Initiation $\le 2\text{ Hours}$, Maximum Cutoff $\le 72\text{ Hours}$):**
   - If the Source Patient tests **HIV Reactive**, is known HIV-positive, or is of **unknown HIV status with high clinical/epidemiological risk**, administer the first dose of `HIV PEP` from the laboratory's emergency starter pack **within $\le 2\text{ hours}$** under the supervision of the designated Occupational Health / ART Physician:

| Clinical Attribute | NACO / WHO Standard Adult `HIV PEP` Regimen |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Preferred 3-Drug Fixed-Dose Combination (`TLD`)** | **Tenofovir Disoproxil Fumarate (`TDF 300 mg`) + Lamivudine (`3TC 300 mg`) + Dolutegravir (`DTG 50 mg`)** |
| **Dosing Schedule & Duration** | **One single fixed-dose tablet orally once daily for `28 Continuous Days`** *(full 28-day compliance is mandatory)* |
| **Time Window to First Dose** | **Golden Window: $\le 2\text{ Hours}$ post-exposure** *(Maximum permissible initiation window: **$\le 72\text{ Hours}$**)* |
| **Alternative Regimen** *(If `TDF` is contraindicated due to renal impairment $\text{eGFR} < 50\text{ mL/min}$)* | **Zidovudine (`AZT 300 mg`) + Lamivudine (`3TC 150 mg`) twice daily + Dolutegravir (`DTG 50 mg`) once daily** *(or Lopinavir/Ritonavir `LPV/r 400/100 mg BD` or Atazanavir/Ritonavir `ATV/r 300/100 mg OD` for 28 days)* |
| **Emergency Starter Kit Location** | Locked, temperature-monitored (`< 25°C`) **`EMERGENCY PEP STARTER KIT`** (containing a 7-day `TLD` starter pack) kept in the Laboratory Director's / Quality Manager's cabin with 24×7 access key. |

### 3.3 Hepatitis B (`HBV`) Post-Exposure Prophylaxis Matrix & Longitudinal Serological Follow-Up
All laboratory personnel must undergo pre-employment **`HBV` 3-dose vaccination ($0, 1, 6\text{ months}$)** and documented **`Anti-HBs` titer verification ($\ge 10\text{ mIU/mL}$)**. Following an occupational exposure, `HBV` management follows the `CDC / WHO` stratification matrix:

| Exposed Staff `HBV` Vaccination & `Anti-HBs` Responder Status | **Action if Source Patient is `HBsAg POSITIVE` or Unknown High-Risk** | **Action if Source Patient is Documented `HBsAg NEGATIVE`** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Previously Vaccinated & Documented Responder**<br>($\text{Anti-HBs} \ge 10\text{ mIU/mL}$) | **No `HBIG` or vaccine treatment required** *(Protective immunity confirmed; log baseline titer).* | **No treatment required.** |
| **2. Unvaccinated or Incompletely Vaccinated** | Administer **`HBIG 0.06 mL/kg` IM immediately (within $\le 24\text{ hours}$, max $7\text{ days}$)** in one deltoid + **Initiate/Complete 3-Dose `HBV` Vaccine Series ($0, 1, 6\text{ months}$)** simultaneously in the **opposite deltoid**. | **Initiate / Complete 3-Dose `HBV` Vaccine Series ($0, 1, 6\text{ months}$).** |
| **3. Vaccinated with Unknown `Anti-HBs` Titer** | Perform STAT `Anti-HBs` quantitative assay:<br>• **If $\ge 10\text{ mIU/mL}$:** No treatment required.<br>• **If $< 10\text{ mIU/mL}$:** Administer **`1 Dose of HBIG (0.06 mL/kg IM)` + `1 Booster Dose of HBV Vaccine`** immediately, and recheck `Anti-HBs` after $1\text{–}2\text{ months}$. | Perform `Anti-HBs` assay; if $< 10\text{ mIU/mL}$, administer `HBV` vaccine booster and recheck titer in $1\text{–}2\text{ months}$. |
| **4. Documented Non-Responder After Two Complete 3-Dose Series** ($\text{Anti-HBs} < 10\text{ mIU/mL}$) | Administer **`2 Doses of HBIG (0.06 mL/kg IM)`**: First dose immediately within $\le 24\text{ hours}$, and Second dose **$1\text{ month}$ later**. | **No treatment required.** |

- **Mandatory Longitudinal Serological Follow-Up Schedule (`NABL-SAF-REG-03`):**
  - **`Baseline (Day 0 / Within 2 Hours)`:** `HIV-1/2 Ag/Ab`, `HBsAg`, `Anti-HBs` titer, `Anti-HCV`, `CBC`, `ALT/AST`, `Serum Creatinine`.
  - **`Week 2 & Week 4` (If on 28-Day `TLD PEP`):** `CBC`, `ALT/AST`, `Serum Creatinine`, and clinical adherence/tolerability check.
  - **`6 Weeks Post-Exposure`:** Repeat `HIV-1/2` (4th Gen), `HBsAg`, `Anti-HCV` (plus `HCV RNA` if source was `HCV` positive), and `ALT`.
  - **`3 Months (12 Weeks) Post-Exposure`:** Repeat `HIV-1/2`, `HBsAg`, and `Anti-HCV`.
  - **`6 Months (24 Weeks) Post-Exposure`:** Final clearance `HIV-1/2`, `HBsAg`, and `Anti-HCV` serology.

### 4.2 Pre-Treatment Autoclave Verification (`Chemical + Weekly Geobacillus stearothermophilus ATCC 7953 QC`)
To satisfy **BMWM Rules 2016 Schedule II** and **ISO 15189:2022 Clause 6.4**, the dedicated **Pre-Treatment Waste Autoclave** (kept strictly separate from the clean media preparation autoclave) undergoes 3-tier verification recorded in `NABL-SAF-REG-05`:
1. **Physical Parameter Verification (Every Cycle):**
   - Gravity-displacement autoclave cycle must achieve **$121^\circ\text{C}$ at $15\text{ psi}$ ($1.05\text{ kg/cm}^2$) for a minimum sterilization hold time of $45\text{–}60\text{ minutes}$** (ensuring core thermal penetration through dense waste loads).
2. **Chemical Indicator Verification (Every Load):**
   - Place **Class 1 Autoclave Indicator Tape** on the exterior of every load and a **Class 4 / Class 5 Multi-Parameter Chemical Indicator Strip** at the geometric center of the waste bag. Complete uniform color change to black/dark-brown is verified before transferring the bag to the Yellow stream.
3. **Biological Spore Indicator Verification (Weekly — Mandatory NABL & HSPCB Requirement):**
   - Every **Saturday**, place a self-contained **`Geobacillus stearothermophilus` (`ATCC 7953`, $\ge 10^6\text{ spores}$) Biological Indicator Vial/Strip** inside the center of a representative waste load and run the standard $121^\circ\text{C} / 15\text{ psi}$ cycle.
   - Upon completion, crush the inner media ampoule of the autoclaved test vial alongside an **un-autoclaved Positive Control Vial** from the same lot and incubate both at **$56^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }60^\circ\text{C}$ for $24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$**:
     - **Pass Criterion:** Test Vial remains **Purple (`No Spore Growth / Sterile`)**, while Positive Control Vial turns **Yellow (`Turbid / Acid Production from Spore Viability`)**.
     - **Failure Protocol:** If the Test Vial turns yellow, quarantine the autoclave immediately, re-autoclave all waste loads processed since the last valid test using the backup autoclave or chemical disinfection, service the steam trap/thermocouple, and obtain **3 consecutive passing spore tests** before returning the unit to service.

### 4.3 Liquid Chemical/Effluent Treatment (`ETP`), `< 48-Hour` Storage Rule, Barcoded `CBWTF` Handover & Annual `Form-IV`
1. **Liquid Waste & Analyzer Effluent Neutralization (`BMWM Rules 2016 Schedule II — Clause 8`):**
   - Liquid waste generated from automated analyzers (`MEK-1503`, `Bonavera Chem 400/200`, `Maglumi X3`) and staining sinks is routed into a dedicated **Liquid Effluent Pre-Treatment / Neutralization Tank (`ETP`)** dosed with **`1%–2%` Sodium Hypochlorite** ($\ge 30\text{ min}$ retention contact time) and pH-adjusted to comply with `CPCB` discharge limits (**$\text{pH } 6.5\text{–}9.0$**, $\text{TSS} \le 100\text{ mg/L}$, $\text{BOD} \le 30\text{ mg/L}$, $\text{COD} \le 250\text{ mg/L}$, Oil & Grease $\le 10\text{ mg/L}$, and $\ge 99.99\%$ microbial reduction in Bio-Assay test) before discharge into the municipal sewer.
   - All hematology reagents used on `MEK-1503` are **Cyanide-Free** and **Azide-Free (or ultra-low preservative)**; sinks are flushed copiously with water daily.
2. **The $\frac{3}{4}$ Fill Rule, Barcode Scanning & Maximum `<= 48-Hour` On-Site Storage Limit (`BMWM Rule 8(7)`):**
   - All 4 color-coded waste bags/containers are sealed with goose-neck ties when **$\frac{3}{4}$ (`75%`) full**, weighed on a calibrated digital platform scale (`in kg`), affixed with the **`HSPCB/CPCB` QR/Barcode Sticker**, scanned into the official tracking portal, and handed over to the authorized **Common Bio-Medical Waste Treatment Facility (`CBWTF`)** GPS-tracked vehicle **daily (and never stored on-site beyond $48\text{ hours}$)**.
3. **Statutory Record Retention, Website Display & Annual `Form-IV` Return (`BMWM Rule 13`):**
   - Maintain the `Daily Bio-Medical Waste Generation & Handover Register (NABL-SAF-REG-04)` for a mandatory **5-Year Retention Period**.
   - Submit the **Annual Report in `Form-IV`** to the **Haryana State Pollution Control Board (`HSPCB`) on or before `30th June` every year** covering the preceding calendar year (`Jan 1 to Dec 31`), and display monthly/annual `BMWM` records and valid `HSPCB` Authorization Certificate (`Form-III`) on the laboratory governance noticeboard/website.
