## 🧪 Section 2: Facility Zoning, Access Control & Daily Environmental / Cold-Chain Monitoring Matrix (`ISO 15189 Cl. 6.3`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why Environmental Logs Are Inspected at Every Single NABL Audit:** Optical photometers (`Bonavera Chem 400/200`), chemiluminescence photomultiplier tubes (`Snibe MAGLUMI X3`), and impedance counters (`MEK-1503`) experience severe calibration drift if room temperature exceeds **$26^\circ\text{C}$**, and refrigerated reagents/controls degrade rapidly if refrigerator temperature rises above **$8^\circ\text{C}$** during overnight power fluctuations!

### 2.1 Laboratory Physical Access & Functional Separation (`Cl. 6.3.2`)
1. **3-Zone Access Segregation:**
   - **Zone 1 (Public / Patient Zone):** Reception, waiting lounge, wheelchair-accessible washroom, and dedicated well-ventilated Phlebotomy & Sample Collection Cubicles (equipped with reclining phlebotomy chair, emergency resuscitation/syncope kit, and handwash station).
   - **Zone 2 (Restricted Analytical Zone — `AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY` Biohazard Signage):** Hematology, Clinical Biochemistry, Immunoassay (`Maglumi X3`), Serology, and Clinical Pathology workbenches.
   - **Zone 3 (High-Containment / Decontamination Zone):** Microbiology Culture & AFB Staining Room (equipped with `Class II Type A2 Biosafety Cabinet`), Autoclave / Pre-Treatment Disinfection Area, and Locked Bio-Medical Waste Holding Area.

### 2.2 Mandatory Twice-Daily (`9:00 AM` & `5:00 PM`) Environmental & Cold-Chain Thresholds

| Monitored Zone / Equipment | Traceable Calibrated Sensor | Acceptable Operating Range | Action Threshold & Immediate Corrective Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Analytical Laboratories (`Hematology`, `Biochemistry`, `Immunoassay`)** | NABL-traceable Digital Thermo-Hygrometer (`Min/Max` memory) | **Temp:** $18^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }25^\circ\text{C}$<br>**Humidity:** $30\%\text{ to }60\%\text{ RH}$ | If $> 26^\circ\text{C}$: Adjust dual split-ACs; pause `Maglumi X3` / `Chem 400` calibration until room stabilizes $\le 25^\circ\text{C}$, then verify `IQC`. |
| **Reagent &Archived Sample Refrigerators (`2–8°C`)** | Calibrated Digital Probe submerged in $50\text{ mL}$ Glycerol/Glycol buffer vial | **$2.0^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }8.0^\circ\text{C}$** *(Target $4\text{–}6^\circ\text{C}$)* | If $< 2^\circ\text{C}$ (freezing risk to whole-blood controls/latex kits) or $> 8^\circ\text{C}$ for $> 1\text{ hr}$: Transfer kits to backup fridge; run 2-level `IQC` on all affected reagents before patient testing. |
| **Control / Calibrator / Serum Archive Deep Freezer (`-20°C`)** | Calibrated Low-Temp Digital Probe (`Min/Max` alarm) | **$-15.0^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }-25.0^\circ\text{C}$** | If warmer than $-10^\circ\text{C}$: Inspect frozen control aliquots for thawing; discard any thawed single-use control vials (`SOP 0.1.6`). |
| **Microbiology Bacteriological Incubator (`37°C`)** | Calibrated Digital Probe | **$35.0^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }37.0^\circ\text{C}$** ($\pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$) | If outside $35\text{–}37^\circ\text{C}$: Adjust thermostat and verify with ATCC quality control strains. |
| **Online Sine-Wave UPS & Dedicated Earthing** | Panel Voltmeter (`Neutral-to-Earth`) | **$\text{N-E Voltage} < 2.0\text{ V AC}$**; Backup $\ge 4\text{ Hours}$ | `N-E Voltage > 3 V` causes erraticISE (`Na+/K+`) and PMT optical noise; service earth pit immediately. |

## ⚙️ Section 3: Statutory Bio-Medical Waste Management (`BMWM Rules 2016` — `CPCB` & `HSPCB` Haryana) SOP

Every workbench and phlebotomy cubicle must maintain the **4 Color-Coded, Foot-Operated, Non-Chlorinated Barcoded Bins** connected to the authorized **Common Bio-Medical Waste Treatment Facility (`CBWTF`)** in Haryana:

| Bin Color & Biohazard Label | Exact Laboratory Waste Permitted in Category (`BMWM Schedule I`) | Mandatory Pre-Treatment at Laboratory Before CBWTF Handover | What Must NEVER Enter This Bin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🟡 YELLOW BIN**<br>*(Non-chlorinated plastic bag, barcoded)* | • **Soiled Cotton Swabs**, gauze, band-aids, and filter papers contaminated with blood/body fluids;<br>• **Discarded Microbiology Culture Plates/Tubes**, stocks, and specimen containers after pre-treatment;<br>• **Expired/Discarded Chemical or Liquid Reagents** (captured separately for chemical neutralization). | **Microbiology cultures, stocks, and infectious media MUST be autoclaved in-house** at **$121^\circ\text{C}$ ($15\text{ psi}$) for $\ge 30\text{ minutes}$** (or microwaved/chemically disinfected with freshly prepared **$1\%\text{–}2\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite** for $30\text{ min}$) before placing in Yellow bag! | **NO** plastic vacutainer tubes, **NO** gloves, **NO** syringes, **NO** needles or glass slides! |
| **🔴 RED BIN**<br>*(Non-chlorinated plastic bag / container, barcoded)* | **Contaminated Recyclable Plastic Waste:**<br>• Used **Plastic Vacutainer Tubes** (`EDTA`, `SST`, `Fluoride`, `Citrate`) after clot/blood pre-treatment;<br>• Used **Plastic Pipette Tips**, plastic reaction cuvettes (`Bonavera` / `Maglumi`), plastic urine/stool containers, and **Nitrile/Latex Gloves**. | Immerse contaminated plastic tubes/tips/cups in **freshly prepared $1\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite ($\text{NaOCl}$) solution for $\ge 30\text{ minutes}$** (or autoclave in autoclavable bags) prior to draining and sealing in Red bag. | **NO** cotton swabs, **NO** metal needles, **NO** broken glass or glass microscope slides! |
| **⚪ WHITE TRANSLUCENT**<br>*(Puncture-proof, leak-proof, tamper-proof container)* | **Waste Metal Sharps:**<br>• Phlebotomy **Hypodermic Needles**, butterfly needles, lancets, scalpels, and blades. | Fill container up to $\frac{3}{4}$ capacity with **$1\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite**; use electric needle-hub cutter/destroyer if applicable; seal permanently when **$\frac{3}{4}$ full**. | **NO** plastic syringe barrels (barrels go to Red Bin) and **NO** glass slides! |
| **🔵 BLUE CARDBOARD / PUNCTURE-PROOF BOX**<br>*(Barcoded)* | **Glassware Waste:**<br>• Used **Glass Microscope Slides** (`PBS`, `Gram`, `ZN AFB`, `Wet Mount`), glass coverslips, Westergren glass pipettes, Neubauer coverslips, and broken glass vials/ampoules. | **Soak all stained/infectious glass slides and pipettes in $1\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite for $\ge 30\text{ minutes}$** before transferring into the puncture-proof Blue box! | **NO** metal needles, **NO** plastic tips, **NO** cotton swabs! |

> [!CAUTION]
> **Maximum Holding Time Rule (`BMWM Rules 2016`):** Bio-medical waste bags must be tied when **$\frac{3}{4}$ full**, weighed, scanned via the **CPCB/HSPCB Barcode Tracking App**, logged in the `Daily Bio-Medical Waste Register (in kg)`, and handed over to the authorized CBWTF vehicle **within $\le 48\text{ hours}$**!

## 📊 Section 4: Daily Preparation of Fresh $1\%$ & $10\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite ($\text{NaOCl}$) Disinfectant

> [!WARNING]
> **Why Commercial Bleach Dilution Math Is Tested by NABL Assessors:** Commercial liquid bleach in India typically contains **$4\%$ to $5\%$ available chlorine** (whereas industrial sodium hypochlorite contains **$10\%$ available chlorine**). Once diluted with water, hypochlorite loses potency after **$24\text{ hours}$**! Therefore, fresh working solutions MUST be prepared every morning at `8:30 AM` in opaque labeled containers displaying `Date/Time of Preparation` and `Expiry (24 Hours)`:

$$\text{Parts of Water to Add to } 1\text{ Part Stock Bleach} = \left(\frac{\%\text{ Available Chlorine in Stock Bleach}}{\%\text{ Desired Working Solution}}\right) - 1$$

| Stock Bleach Strength | **To Make `1%` Working Solution** *(Routine Bench Wipe, Tube/Slide Soak, Minor Spill)* | **To Make `2%` Working Solution** *(High-Risk Viral / Mycobacterial / Major Blood Spill)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stock $= 4\%$ Available Chlorine** | **$1\text{ part Bleach} + 3\text{ parts Water}$** (e.g., $250\text{ mL Bleach} + 750\text{ mL Water} = 1\text{ Liter}$) | **$1\text{ part Bleach} + 1\text{ part Water}$** ($500\text{ mL Bleach} + 500\text{ mL Water}$) |
| **Stock $= 5\%$ Available Chlorine** | **$1\text{ part Bleach} + 4\text{ parts Water}$** (e.g., $200\text{ mL Bleach} + 800\text{ mL Water} = 1\text{ Liter}$) | **$1\text{ part Bleach} + 1.5\text{ parts Water}$** ($400\text{ mL Bleach} + 600\text{ mL Water}$) |
| **Stock $= 10\%$ Available Chlorine** | **$1\text{ part Bleach} + 9\text{ parts Water}$** (e.g., $100\text{ mL Bleach} + 900\text{ mL Water} = 1\text{ Liter}$) | **$1\text{ part Bleach} + 4\text{ parts Water}$** ($200\text{ mL Bleach} + 800\text{ mL Water}$) |

*(Note: For металлиc surfaces on `MEK-1503`, `Bonavera 400/200`, `Maglumi X3`, and centrifuge rotors where hypochlorite causes corrosion, wipe with **$70\%$ Isopropyl Alcohol / Ethanol** after cleaning!).*

## 🧬 Section 5: Biological Spill Management SOP (`ISO 15190:2020`) — The 7-Step Spill Kit Protocol

Every laboratory room must keep a labeled **`BIOHAZARD SPILL KIT`** containing: Nitrile gloves, disposable gown, N95 mask/goggles, absorbent paper towels, forceps + dustpan/brush (for broken glass), freshly relatable **$1\% / 2\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite**, and Yellow/Blue biohazard bags.

1. **Step 1 — Alert & Cordon:** Warn nearby staff immediately; place the `CAUTION: BIOHAZARD SPILL` sign and allow aerosols to settle ($5\text{–}10\text{ min}$ if tube broke inside a centrifuge—**never open a centrifuge lid for $30\text{ minutes}$ after an audible tube breakage!**).
2. **Step 2 — Don PPE:** Put on double nitrile gloves, lab coat/apron, eye protection, and mask.
3. **Step 3 — Contain & Cover (Prevent Aerosol Splash!):** *Never pour hypochlorite directly onto an uncovered pool of blood!* First cover the entire blood/fluid spill gently with **absorbent paper towels / gauze** starting from the outer perimeter inward.
4. **Step 4 — Pour Disinfectant Inward:** Pour freshly prepared **$1\%$ to $2\%$ Sodium Hypochlorite** concentrically from the **outer edge of the paper towels toward the center** until saturated.
5. **Step 5 — Contact Time (`20–30 Minutes`):** Leave undisturbed for **$20\text{ to }30\text{ minutes}$** of mandatory virucidal/bactericidal contact time.
6. **Step 6 — Safe Retrieval:** Use **mechanical forceps / plastic scoop** (never bare hands!) to pick up any broken glass into the **Blue Puncture-Proof Box**, and place soaked paper towels into the **Yellow Biohazard Bag**.
7. **Step 7 — Final Rinse & Log:** Wipe the area with water + $70\%$ alcohol, remove PPE, wash hands, and record the event in the `Biological Spill & Incident Register`.

## 🚨 Section 6: Needle-Stick Injury (`NSI`) & Mucocutaneous Splash Emergency `PEP` Protocol (`Within 2 Hours!`)

> [!CAUTION]
> **What to Do Immediately After a Needle-Stick or Blood Splash (`NACO / WHO Guidelines`):**
> - **DO NOT SQUEEZE, SUCK, OR SCRUB THE WOUND!** Squeezing or milking a needle-stick finger causes micro-capillary trauma and pushes viral particles deeper into the bloodstream!
> - **DO NOT APPLY BLEACH OR UNDILUTED DETTOL/SAVLON INTO AN OPEN PUNCTURE WOUND!**

1. **Immediate First Aid (`Minute 0–5`):**
   - **Skin Puncture:** Wash immediately under **running tap water and mild soap** for **$5\text{ minutes}$**; apply $70\%$ alcohol or $0.5\%$ chlorhexidine/povidone-iodine.
   - **Eye / Mucous Membrane Splash:** Irrigate immediately at the **Emergency Eye-Wash Station** with copious sterile normal saline ($0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$) or clean running water for **$10\text{–}15\text{ minutes}$** with eyelids held open.
2. **Immediate Source & Staff Serology (`Minute 15–60` — Complimentary STAT Run on `Snibe MAGLUMI X3` / Rapid):**
   - Test the **Source Patient Sample** (and baseline **Exposed Staff Sample**) STAT for **`HIV-1/2` (`4.2.1`)**, **`HBsAg` (`4.2.2`)**, and **`Anti-HCV` (`4.2.3`)**; verify staff member's baseline `Anti-HBs` titer.
3. **Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (`PEP` Initiation — Ideal `< 2 Hours`, Max `< 72 Hours`):**
   - **HIV Exposure:** If source is HIV-reactive or high-risk unknown, initiate **28-day Antiretroviral `PEP` (`Tenofovir 300 mg + Lamivudine 300 mg + Dolutegravir 50 mg [TLD]` once daily)** within **$2\text{ hours}$** under Medical Officer supervision.
   - **HBV Exposure:** If source is `HBsAg` Positive and exposed staff is unvaccinated / `Anti-HBs < 10 mIU/mL`, administer **Hepatitis-B Immunoglobulin (`HBIG 0.06 mL/kg IM`) within $24\text{ hours}$** + Day-0 Hep-B vaccine at opposite deltoid.
   - **Follow-Up Serology:** Repeat staff `HIV`, `HBsAg`, and `Anti-HCV` at **$6\text{ weeks}$, $3\text{ months}$, and $6\text{ months}$**.

## 🔬 Section 7: Autoclave Sterilization & Spore-Strip (`Geobacillus stearothermophilus`) QC Verification

1. **Physical Parameter Log (Every Cycle):** Record temperature (**$121^\circ\text{C}$**), pressure (**$15\text{ psi}$**), and holding time (**$\ge 30\text{ minutes}$ for waste; $15\text{ minutes}$ for clean media**).
2. **Chemical Indicator (`Every Load`):** Autoclave indicator tape / Bowie-Dick Class-4/5 chemical strip must show complete color change.
3. **Biological Indicator (`Weekly / Monthly — NABL Mandatory`):** Incubate a **`Geobacillus stearothermophilus` ($10^6$ spores) biological indicator ampoule** placed at the center of the waste load at $56\text{–}60^\circ\text{C}$ for $24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$ alongside an un-autoclaved positive control. **Zero growth (no yellow color shift) confirms $100\%$ sterilization efficacy!**
