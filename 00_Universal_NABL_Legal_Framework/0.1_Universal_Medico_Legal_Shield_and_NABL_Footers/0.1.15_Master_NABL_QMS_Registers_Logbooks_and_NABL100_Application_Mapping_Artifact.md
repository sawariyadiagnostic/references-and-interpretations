### 0.3 Forensic Record Integrity Rules (`ISO 15189:2022 Clause 8.4` & `ALCOA+` Standard)

Every physical logbook and electronic register listed in this artifact operates under seven mandatory forensic rules:
1. **Pagination & Spine Authentication:** All physical registers must be hard-bound, pre-numbered sequentially (`Page 001 of 200`), and stamped on Page 001 with the **Laboratory Control Certificate** signed by the Quality Manager and Laboratory Director stating the exact start date, total page count, and assigned bench location. Loose-leaf sheets or spiral pads are strictly prohibited unless controlled as numbered batch worksheets under `SOP-GOV-0.1.11`.
2. **Indelible Ink & Prohibition of Obliteration:** All manual entries must be recorded contemporaneously in permanent blue or black ballpoint ink. The use of correction fluid (*Whitener*), correction tape, erasers, pencils, or erasable pens is a **Class-I Critical Data-Integrity Non-Conformity**.
3. **Single-Line Strikethrough Correction Protocol (`SLSID`):** Any erroneous manual entry must be corrected exclusively by drawing a **single horizontal line** through the incorrect text so that the original entry remains 100% legible (`~~4.2~~ 4.8`), writing the correct value alongside, and appending the **Operator Initials, Date, Time, and Error Code** (`TE` = Transcription Error; `CE` = Calculation Error; `WE` = Wrong Column Entry).
4. **Mandatory Monthly "NIL" Certification:** For event-driven registers (`NABL-LOG-10` NCW, `NABL-LOG-12` Amended Reports, `NABL-LOG-13` Complaints, `NABL-LOG-19/SAF-01` Needle-Stick Injury), if zero events occur during a calendar month, the Technical Supervisor must write `"NIL EVENTS RECORDED FOR THE MONTH OF [MM/YYYY]"` across the next available row on the last working day of the month and counter-sign with the Quality Manager. Blank uncertified months are treated by NABL assessors as unmaintained records.
5. **Dual-Tier Supervisory Review Cadence:** Every operational bench logbook (`Temp`, `IQC`, `PM`, `CLRW Water`, `Critical Alerts`, `Sample Rejection`) requires **Weekly Technical Supervisor Verification** and **Monthly Quality Manager / Pathologist Audit Sign-off**.
6. **Electronic Record Immutability (`BSA 2023 Sec. 63` & `21 CFR Part 11` Principles):** Electronic registers in LIMS must capture the unique user ID, role-based biometric/OTP authentication, server-synchronized NTP timestamp (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS IST`), pre-edit value, post-edit value, and mandatory reason for modification.
7. **Statutory & Accreditation Retention Hierarchy:** All quality and technical records are retained in tamper-protected, pest-controlled, fire-safe archives (and daily 3-2-1 encrypted cloud/NAS backups) for the maximum duration mandated by **NABL 112**, **CPA 2019** (minimum 3 years for adult routine litigation shield), **PCPNDT Act 1994** (2 years or until final disposal of court proceedings), or **Medico-Legal/Histopathology/Pediatric** mandates (5 to 10+ years).
