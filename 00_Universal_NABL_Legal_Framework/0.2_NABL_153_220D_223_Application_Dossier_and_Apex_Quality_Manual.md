## Section 1: Purpose & International 4-Tier Quality Management System (`QMS`) Architecture
This Master Cluster (`0.2`) integrates the **Level-1 Apex Quality Manual**, **Level-2 Quality System Procedures (`QSPs` across Clauses 4.1 to 8.9 + Normative Annex A)**, **Level-3 Technical & Equipment SOPs (`287 Standalone SOPs` across Folders `00` to `06`)**, and **Level-4 Live Bench Registers & Turnkey `NABL 153 / 220D / 223` Portal Application Forms (`74 Live Bench Registers + Audit Dossiers`)** into a single cohesive, audit-ready accreditation framework.

### 1.1 The 4-Tier ISO 15189:2022 & NABL Documentation Pyramid
| Tier Level | Document Classification | Purpose & Regulatory Function during NABL Assessment | Controlled Document Series in Repository | Total Verified Files / Logs |

## Section 3: End-to-End Chronological NABL Accreditation Application Roadmap (`Day -90 to Certificate Grant`)
| Phase & Timeline | Operational Milestone (`ISO 15189:2022` & `NABL 100 / 153`) | Mandatory Documentary Evidence Generated from This Repository | Responsible Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Milestone 1 (`Day -90 to -30`)** | **QMS Implementation, Method Verification (`CLSI EP15`), `MU %` Calculation (`ISO/TS 20914`), Equipment `NPL` Calibration & `PT/EQAS` Enrollment (`NABL 163`)** | Populate 30-day `IQC` (`SOP 0.1.6`), complete 5-day $\times$ 5-replicate `EP15` sheets (`SOP 0.1.8`), calibrate equipment (`SOP 0.1.13`), and obtain at least **1 completed `EQAS` cycle + `Tri-Partite ILC` round (`SOP 0.2.7`)**. | Laboratory Director, Quality Manager & Dept Heads |
| **Milestone 2 (`Day -30 to -15`)** | **Complete 1 Full Internal Audit Cycle (`All Clauses 4.1–8.9 + All 6 Depts`) & Conduct 1 Formal Management Review Meeting (`MRM`)** | Execute internal audit using **`SOP 0.2.12` (`NABL 223` Checklist)**, close all raised internal `NCs` with root-cause `CAPA`, and sign the **Annual `MRM` Minutes (`SOP 0.2.12 Sec 4`)**. | Certified Internal Auditors & Lab Director |
| **Milestone 3 (`Day 0 — Portal Upload`)** | **Online Submission of `NABL 153` on NABL Medical Portal (`WP`) + Payment of Application Fee** | Upload **`0.2.1` (`Level-1 Apex Quality Manual + NABL 220D Matrix`)**, copy-paste the **Unified Scope Table from `0.2.11`**, upload statutory PDFs (`0.2.3`), `EQAS` certificates (`0.2.7`), and signed `NABL 131 / 133` (`0.2.11`). | Laboratory Director & Quality Manager |
| **Milestone 4 (`Day +15 to +30`)** | **Stage 1: NABL Lead Assessor Desktop Adequacy Audit (`NABL 220D`)** | Zero Adequacy Deficiencies expected because **`0.2.1`** pre-maps every single `NABL 220D` question to exact controlled SOP and Logbook IDs. | NABL Lead Assessor & Quality Manager |
| **Milestone 5 (`Day +45 to +60`)** | **Stage 2: Pre-Assessment Visit (`NABL 208A`)** | Review of physical facility zoning (`0.2.4`), live bench registers (`0.1.15` & Dept Packs `1.4–6.2`), internal audit & `MRM` (`0.2.12`), and readiness for final witness assessment. | NABL Lead Assessor & Lab Director |
| **Milestone 6 (`Day +75 to +90`)** | **Stage 3: Final On-Site & Witness Assessment (`NABL 223` & `NABL 112`)** | Technical assessors witness live test execution against our **273 Standalone Test SOPs (`Sections 1–10`)** and **14 Equipment/Media/Stain SOPs**, plus Authorized Signatory interviews. | NABL Assessment Team & All Dept Signatories |
